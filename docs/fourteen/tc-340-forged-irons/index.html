About the Fourteen TC-340 Forged

The TC-340 Forged pushed Fourteen's one-piece forging toward maximum forgiveness rather than tour shaping, released in Japan on 21 March 2018 (some Japanese retail listings give 23 March) with the company's own claim of the lowest and deepest centre of gravity yet achieved in a Fourteen forged iron. Each S35C soft-steel head is forged seven separate times to hollow out a deep undercut structure behind the face, with a thick topline, generous offset and an unusually wide sole on the longer irons - MyGolfSpy's first-look coverage aimed it squarely at "an already loyal Fourteen consumer who has lost some swing speed but still wants to bag forged irons... with maximum forgiveness and launch." The head range runs #5 through a 46-degree P/A wedge (no #4 head was produced), with the #6-PW five-club set the standard build and #5 and P/A sold as separate additions; stock shafts were the Nippon N.S.PRO 950GH HT in steel or Fourteen's own FT-26i graphite. Nickel-chrome satin finish, right- and left-hand.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Fourteen TC-340 Forged 7-iron?

The Fourteen TC-340 Forged 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 37.95 inches.

Who is the Fourteen TC-340 Forged for?

MyGolfSpy's first-look framed it as built for golfers who have lost swing speed but still want a forged Fourteen iron - maximum forgiveness and launch from a one-piece forging, rather than a tour-shaped players iron.

Is there a 4-iron in the Fourteen TC-340 Forged lineup?

No. Fourteen made this head only from #5 through a 46-degree P/A wedge; the standard set is #6 through pitching wedge (five clubs), with the #5 and P/A sold as separate additions.

When was the Fourteen TC-340 Forged released?

In Japan on 21 March 2018 (some Japanese retail listings record 23 March 2018), forged from S35C soft carbon steel seven separate times per head.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2018, the Fourteen TC-340 Forged is a current super game improvement set in the Fourteen range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
