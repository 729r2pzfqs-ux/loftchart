About the Fourteen TC-7 Forged

The TC-7 Forged is a players cavity back from Fourteen Golf, the Tokyo club-design house founded in 1981, sold as a US-market model through Fourteen Golf USA — it drew attention from spring 2021 and most American coverage treats it as a 2022 release. Each semi-compact head is forged from a single piece of S25C soft carbon steel with an undercut cavity that lifts launch and forgiveness while keeping minimal offset and a modern topline. Lofts are moderate for the class — a 31-degree 7-iron and a 44-degree pitching wedge across a 4-PW, right-hand-only chart — and in MyGolfSpy's 2022 Most Wanted players iron test the TC-7 rated highest for feel, forgiveness and distance, though the overall title went to the New Level 623-CB. Sold at $199 per club with Nippon steel stock shafts; still listed by Fourteen Golf USA as of mid-2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Fourteen TC-7 Forged 7-iron?

The Fourteen TC-7 Forged 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a 37-inch standard length.

Is the Fourteen TC-7 forged?

Yes. Each head is forged from a single piece of S25C soft carbon steel with an undercut cavity back, finished in nickel chrome.

Did the Fourteen TC-7 win MyGolfSpy's 2022 Most Wanted players iron test?

No — the overall title went to the New Level 623-CB — but the TC-7 rated highest in the field for feel, forgiveness and distance.

Who makes Fourteen golf clubs?

Fourteen Golf is a Japanese maker founded in Tokyo in 1981 by designer Takamitsu Takebayashi; it designed irons for other brands before launching its own line in 2002, and the TC-7 is sold in America through Fourteen Golf USA.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2021, the Fourteen TC-7 Forged is a current players set in the Fourteen range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
