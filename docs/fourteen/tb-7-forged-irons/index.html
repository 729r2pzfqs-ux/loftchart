About the Fourteen TB-7 Forged

The TB-7 Forged applies Fourteen's "Theater Blade" concept — a hidden cavity whose back wall curves smoothly into the blade rather than stepping down, for a soft forged feel without a visible cavity line — to a smaller, more compact head than the TB-5 Forged the design started with. Fourteen released it in Japan on 21 April 2022 with a "not too easy, not too difficult" brief, and reviewer Hiroichiro Yoshida's company-published test called it more operable and accurate than the larger, more forgiving TB-5, at the cost of some of that model's forgiveness — a straighter, less-offset neck and a compact head aimed at mid-to-low handicaps rather than the TB-5's wider range. The two models share an identical loft ladder (a 30-degree 7-iron, 42-degree pitching wedge) so they blend into a combo set, and Fourteen brought it to overseas markets, including the US, from September 2022. The factory chart runs #4 through pitching wedge with a nickel-chrome satin finish, made in Japan.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Fourteen TB-7 Forged 7-iron?

The Fourteen TB-7 Forged 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a factory length of 37.5 inches with the N.S.PRO 950GH HT R shaft — identical loft and lie to the larger TB-5 Forged.

How does the Fourteen TB-7 differ from the TB-5?

Both use the same 'Theater Blade' hidden-cavity forging and share one loft ladder, but the TB-7 has a smaller, more compact head with a straighter, less-offset neck, trading some of the TB-5's forgiveness for operability and accuracy - Fourteen's own reviewer described it as more of a 'muscle back' feel.

When was the Fourteen TB-7 Forged released?

In Japan on 21 April 2022, reaching overseas markets including the US from September 2022.

Is the Fourteen TB-7 Forged good for beginners?

No - reviewers note it favors amateur and professional golfers who prioritize feel and workability over the utmost forgiveness, unlike Fourteen's more forgiving TB-5.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Fourteen TB-7 Forged is a current players set in the Fourteen range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
