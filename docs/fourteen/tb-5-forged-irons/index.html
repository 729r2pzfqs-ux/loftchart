About the Fourteen TB-5 Forged

The TB-5 Forged is Fourteen's "Theater Blade" — an S20C soft-carbon forging whose back conceals the cavity step behind a smooth blade-like curtain, so the club reads as a players iron at address while carrying hidden perimeter help. Fourteen released it in Japan on 6 November 2020, aimed at golfers in the 85-96 mph driver-speed range, with a universal sole grind for all lies and a nickel-chrome satin finish; US sales through Fourteen Golf USA followed in late 2021, which is why most American coverage treats it as a 2021-2022 model. The factory chart runs a five-club 6-PW core set with the 5-iron sold separately, on Japanese-market lengths (37.5-inch 7-iron) with a 30-degree 7-iron and 42-degree pitching wedge; a 46-degree P/A wedge extended the set in some markets. The second-generation TB-5 Forged ('25), released 8 November 2024, replaced it, though Fourteen kept the original's left-handed version in the range.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Fourteen TB-5 Forged 7-iron?

The Fourteen TB-5 Forged 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a factory length of 37.5 inches on the Japanese standard build.

What is the Theater Blade structure on the Fourteen TB-5?

The back face hides the cavity step behind a smooth blade-style surface — like a theatre curtain in front of the machinery — so the iron looks like a compact blade at address while playing with cavity-back forgiveness.

Is the Fourteen TB-5 Forged a blade?

It looks like one but is not a true muscle back: the S20C forging conceals a cavity, and Fourteen aimed it at average golfers with driver speeds of 85-96 mph rather than at tour players.

When was the Fourteen TB-5 Forged released?

In Japan on 6 November 2020, with US sales following in late 2021. The second-generation TB-5 Forged ('25) replaced it on 8 November 2024.

Finding a used set

The Fourteen TB-5 Forged ran from 2020 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
