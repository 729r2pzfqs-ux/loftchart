About the Cleveland Launcher UHX

The Launcher UHX is a progressive utility-hollow iron set announced on 16 September 2019 and at retail in North America from 4 October 2019, replacing the Launcher CBX in Cleveland's game-improvement slot. The 4 through 7 irons are hollow-bodied with a high-strength steel face insert and a V-shaped sole for distance and forgiveness, while the 8-iron through pitching wedge switch to a conventional cavity back for control. Lofts are strong, with a 30-degree 7-iron and a 44-degree pitching wedge, and gapping stretches from 3 degrees in the long irons to 5 degrees in the wedges. The stock set runs 4-iron through pitching wedge, with an optional 49-degree DW gap wedge to extend it, and matching hollow utility singles were sold down to an 18-degree 3-iron. Specs below are the standard men's steel build; offsets are converted from the published millimetre figures, and the D2 swing weight is the steel value — graphite builds run D1.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland Launcher UHX 7-iron?

The Cleveland Launcher UHX 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 63 degrees and a length of 37 inches.

Which Launcher UHX irons are hollow?

The 4 through 7 irons use a hollow body with a high-strength steel face insert for distance and forgiveness; the 8-iron through pitching wedge are conventional cavity backs for control. Cleveland also sold hollow UHX utility singles in 18, 20 and 23 degrees.

When were the Cleveland Launcher UHX irons released?

Cleveland announced them on 16 September 2019 alongside the Launcher HB Turbo, and they reached North American retail on 4 October 2019.

Could the Launcher UHX set be extended beyond the pitching wedge?

Yes — Cleveland offered an optional 49-degree DW gap wedge with the same hollow construction as the low-lofted clubs, on the stock Dynamic Gold DST 98 steel shaft.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland Launcher UHX ran from 2019 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
