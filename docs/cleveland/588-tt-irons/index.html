About the Cleveland 588 TT

The 588 TT (Tour Trajectory) is the most compact of the three cast irons in Cleveland's 2013 588 relaunch, sitting above the mid-size 588 MT and the ultra-high-launch 588 Altitude and below the forged 588 CB and MB. It pairs a shorter blade, thinner topline and less offset with the same strong loft ladder Cleveland published for the MT — 31 degrees at the 7-iron and 44 at the pitching wedge — for a more penetrating flight aimed at better players who still want cast-cavity forgiveness and distance. The standard set was eight pieces, 4-iron through the 49-degree DW gap wedge, in right and left hand. Steel lengths are below; graphite builds were 0.25 inch longer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland 588 TT 7-iron?

The Cleveland 588 TT 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 63 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

What is the difference between the Cleveland 588 TT and 588 MT?

They share the same published lofts, lies, lengths and offsets, but the TT (Tour Trajectory) uses a more compact head with a thinner topline for a flatter, more penetrating flight, while the MT (Mid Trajectory) is larger and more forgiving.

What clubs came in the Cleveland 588 TT set?

The standard set was eight pieces from the 4-iron to the 49-degree DW gap wedge; a 3-iron and 54-degree sand wedge were also available, in right and left hand.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland 588 TT ran from 2013 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
