About the Cleveland ZipCore XL

The ZipCore XL replaced the Launcher XL in January 2024 as Cleveland's oversize distance iron and is the first Cleveland iron to carry the ZipCore technology from its wedges: in the 8-iron through sand wedge, heavy steel in the hosel and heel is swapped for a lightweight vibration-damping core that centres the CG on the strike. The 4- through 7-irons instead get an AI-designed MainFrame face — a variable network of grooves, channels and cavities milled into the back of the face — and the whole set uses the large cavity-back XL head with perimeter weighting, plus an 8-gram Action Mass CB counterweight in the grip end. HydraZip face blasts and loft-specific grooves split the set the same way, reducing spin in the 4i-7i and boosting it in the 8i-SW. Lofts carry over from the Launcher XL almost unchanged — a very strong 29-degree 7-iron and 43-degree pitching wedge — but each iron is built about a quarter-inch longer than the Launcher XL standard build. Set options run 4-PW, 5-PW or 5-PW plus the 48-degree gap wedge, with a 54-degree sand wedge offered right hand only.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland ZipCore XL 7-iron?

The Cleveland ZipCore XL 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

What changed between the Cleveland Launcher XL and the ZipCore XL?

Lofts and lies carried over essentially unchanged, but the construction changed: the ZipCore XL drops the hollow long irons for a cavity back throughout, adds the wedge-derived ZipCore hosel core in the 8-iron through sand wedge and an AI-designed MainFrame face in the 4- through 7-irons. Each club is also built about a quarter-inch longer than the Launcher XL standard build.

What clubs come in a stock Cleveland ZipCore XL set?

Cleveland's set builder offers 4-PW, 5-PW or 5-PW plus the 48-degree gap wedge, in right or left hand. The 54-degree sand wedge is offered right hand only.

Are the Cleveland ZipCore XL lofts strong?

Yes. The 29-degree 7-iron and 43-degree pitching wedge are roughly a club stronger than a traditional iron at the same number, which is why the set extends to a 48-degree gap wedge and 54-degree sand wedge.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland ZipCore XL ran from 2024 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
