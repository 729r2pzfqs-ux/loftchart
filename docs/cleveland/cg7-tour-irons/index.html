About the Cleveland CG7 Tour

The CG7 Tour is the compact, lower-offset version of Cleveland's CG7 cavity-back, aimed at better players. It shares the CG7 technology package — a full-cavity 360-degree Gelback visco-elastic insert for vibration damping, progressively sized Dynamic Micro-Cavities to move the centre of gravity, and heel/toe muscle weighting — in a smaller head with a Tour-style sole and noticeably less offset (0.115 inches at the 7-iron). Lofts are traditional for its era, with a 34-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge. A Black Pearl finish version was sold alongside the standard chrome finish. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland CG7 Tour 7-iron?

The Cleveland CG7 Tour 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

What is the pitching wedge loft of the Cleveland CG7 Tour irons?

The CG7 Tour pitching wedge is 46 degrees, with a 64.5-degree lie and a 35.5-inch steel length.

How is the CG7 Tour different from the standard CG7?

The CG7 Tour uses a more compact head, a Tour-style sole and much less offset — 0.115 inches at the 7-iron — while keeping the same Gelback insert and Micro-Cavity technology as the standard CG7.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland CG7 Tour ran from 2009 to 2010 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
