About the Cleveland CG Gold

The CG Gold is the traditional-loft half of Cleveland's 2007 "Red or Gold" fitting concept: golfers with mid-to-slow tempos were pointed to the Gold, with its conventional lofts (33.5-degree 7-iron, 45-degree pitching wedge) and flighted Actionlite steel shafts designed to launch long irons higher and flight scoring clubs down, while faster swingers got the stronger-lofted CG Red. The cavity back carries Cleveland's Gelback damping insert, and colour-coded hosel markings denote flat, standard or upright lie. Cleveland published the set 2-iron through sand wedge, with the 50-degree wedge labelled DW; steel lengths below, graphite 0.25 inch longer. It was reviewed well as a soft-feeling, easy-launching game-improvement iron and ran until the CG7 generation arrived in 2009.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland CG Gold 7-iron?

The Cleveland CG Gold 7-iron has a loft of 33.5 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

What is the difference between Cleveland CG Gold and CG Red irons?

They are two halves of Cleveland's 2007 fitting concept: the Gold carries traditional lofts with flighted shafts for mid-to-slow tempos, while the Red is about a degree and a half stronger through the scoring clubs and half an inch longer-playing in character, for faster swingers seeking distance.

What shafts came in Cleveland CG Gold irons?

The stock steel shaft was the Royal Precision Actionlite Flighted, whose kickpoint shifts through the set to launch long irons higher and flight short irons down; a CG Gold graphite was offered in A, R and S flexes.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland CG Gold ran from 2007 to 2009 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
