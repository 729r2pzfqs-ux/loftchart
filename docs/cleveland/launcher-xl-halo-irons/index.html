About the Cleveland Launcher XL Halo

The Launcher XL Halo is a hybrid-shaped iron: a hollow, wide-soled head with a very low centre of gravity, aimed at players who struggle to get long irons airborne. Cleveland built it long — 39.125 inches at the 4-iron — with strong lofts, a 30-degree 7-iron, and it ships in graphite as the reference build, where the set measures D0 against D1 in steel. Cleveland stamps the 49.5-degree wedge DW; it is listed here as GW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland Launcher XL Halo 7-iron?

The Cleveland Launcher XL Halo 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 63.0 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

When were Cleveland Launcher XL Halo irons released?

Cleveland announced them on 18 August 2021; the graphite version went on sale on 20 August 2021 and the steel version on 17 September 2021.

What is the DW in a Launcher XL Halo set?

The distance wedge at 49.5 degrees, which sits between the 44-degree pitching wedge and the 54.5-degree sand wedge. It is listed here as GW.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland Launcher XL Halo ran from 2021 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
