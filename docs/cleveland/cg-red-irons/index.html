About the Cleveland CG Red

The CG Red is the distance half of Cleveland's 2007 "Red or Gold" fitting concept, aimed at players with faster, more aggressive tempos. Its lofts run about a degree stronger than the companion CG Gold through the scoring clubs — 34 degrees at the 7-iron and 46 at the pitching wedge — on the same cast cavity platform with Cleveland's Gelback damping insert and colour-coded hosel markings for flat, standard or upright lies. Cleveland published the CG Red 2-iron through pitching wedge in steel, with True Temper Dynamic Gold the stock shaft. Compared with the flighted-shaft, traditional-loft Gold, the Red trades a little height for speed and distance; both were replaced by the CG7 generation in 2009.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland CG Red 7-iron?

The Cleveland CG Red 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 36.75 inches.

Should I choose Cleveland CG Red or CG Gold irons?

Cleveland's own fitting rule was tempo: aggressive, faster swingers were pointed to the Red, with its slightly stronger lofts and Dynamic Gold shafts, while smoother or slower tempos got the Gold with traditional lofts and flighted Actionlite shafts.

What clubs were available in the Cleveland CG Red set?

Cleveland published the CG Red from the 2-iron through the pitching wedge, all available in left hand as well; there was no factory gap or sand wedge in the Red spec table.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland CG Red ran from 2007 to 2009 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
