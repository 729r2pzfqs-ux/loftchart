About the Cleveland 588 Altitude

The 588 Altitude is the easiest-to-hit member of Cleveland's 2013 588 iron range, built for golfers with moderate swing speeds who struggle to flight the ball. Every head is fully hollow, placing the centre of gravity at its lowest and deepest point for maximum launch, with a forged face for ball speed and feel and a bendable hosel that allows fitting adjustments of up to 2 degrees flat or 3 upright. Lofts are traditional — 31 degrees at the 7-iron, 44 at the pitching wedge — because the design flights the ball easily without strong lofts, and the set is built light, about half an inch longer than standard with an 85-gram steel shaft. Lengths and the D3 swing weight below are the steel build; graphite builds run a quarter-inch longer at D2. The gap and sand wedge complete the set at D4.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland 588 Altitude 7-iron?

The Cleveland 588 Altitude 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 63 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

Are the Cleveland 588 Altitude irons adjustable?

The hosel is bendable, so a fitter can adjust lie up to 2 degrees flat or 3 degrees upright, and loft up to 2 degrees in either direction.

Who are the Cleveland 588 Altitude irons for?

They target golfers with moderate swing speeds who need help launching the ball: every head is fully hollow with a low, deep centre of gravity, and the set is built long and light with an 85-gram steel or 55-gram graphite shaft.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland 588 Altitude ran from 2013 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
