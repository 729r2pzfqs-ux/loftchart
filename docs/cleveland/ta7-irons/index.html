About the Cleveland TA7

The TA7 was the best-known model of Cleveland's Tour Action iron family of the early 2000s — a mid-size cavity-back whose patented micro-cavity lightened the topline and dropped the centre of gravity, moving weight to the perimeter while keeping a modern heel-to-toe blade length and a progressive offset. It combined that with Cleveland's Vibration Absorbing System and was offered in standard and gunmetal finishes, with a separate TA7 Tour version (2003) offering a more compact head and traditional lofts. Lofts on the standard TA7 are mildly strong for the era — a 33.5-degree 7-iron and 45-degree pitching wedge — and the range ran from a 16-degree 1-iron to a 60-degree lob wedge with True Temper's Sensicore Release as the stock shaft. The Tour Action line ended when Cleveland's CG series arrived from 2004. Note: this site's research places the TA7 in 2002 (manufacturer-era spec databases and period listings agree), though some timelines date the Tour Action family's origins to the late 1990s.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland TA7 7-iron?

The Cleveland TA7 7-iron has a loft of 33.5 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel length.

What is the difference between the Cleveland TA7 and TA7 Tour?

The standard TA7 is the mid-size micro-cavity model with slightly strong lofts (33.5-degree 7-iron), while the TA7 Tour of 2003 is a more compact, minimal-offset version with traditional lofts (35-degree 7-iron) aimed at better players.

When did the Cleveland TA7 come out?

Specification databases and period listings place it in 2002, with the TA7 Tour following in 2003; the Tour Action line was replaced by Cleveland's CG series from 2004.

What is the micro-cavity on the Cleveland TA7?

A patented hollow running along the topline that removes weight from the top of the head, lowering the centre of gravity and freeing mass for the perimeter — the TA7's signature feature, paired with Cleveland's Vibration Absorbing System for feel.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland TA7 ran from 2002 to 2004 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
