About the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged

The TA1 Form Forged sat at the top of Cleveland's Tour Action iron range — "the ultimate in muscle-back performance" in the company's own words — and was sold alongside the slightly-cavitied TA3 Form Forged from 2000. It is a classic forged 1-PW muscle back with fully traditional lofts (36-degree 7-iron, 48-degree pitching wedge) and no published offset, on True Temper Dynamic Gold stock with Tri-Gold, Rifle and Sensicore custom options. A GunMetal satin finish variant was added alongside the standard chrome. It remained in the line until Cleveland's CG1 blade took its slot in 2004. Note that some lists date the TA1 to 1998; Cleveland's own site first shows the Form Forged models in 2000, which matches compiled stock-shaft records.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cleveland TA1 Form Forged 7-iron?

The Cleveland TA1 Form Forged 7-iron has a loft of 36 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 36.75 inches.

Are the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged irons blades?

Yes — Cleveland marketed the TA1 FF as 'the ultimate in muscle-back performance', a pure forged blade sold 1-iron through pitching wedge, with the TA3 Form Forged as its slightly more forgiving sibling.

What shafts came in the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged?

True Temper Dynamic Gold (X100, S300, R300) was standard, with Tri-Gold, Royal Precision Rifle and Dynamic Gold Sensicore available as custom options.

Finding a used set

The Cleveland TA1 Form Forged ran from 2000 to 2004 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
