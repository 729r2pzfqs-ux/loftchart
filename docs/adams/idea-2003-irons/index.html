About the Adams Idea (2003)

The original Idea set launched Adams' integrated hybrid-iron concept: the stock men's make-up replaces the long irons with a 17-degree 3 i-Wood and a 21-degree 4 i-Wood, runs hollow-back mid irons through the middle of the set and finishes with cavity-back short irons. A 25-degree 5 i-Wood was offered with identical loft, lie and length to the 5-iron so the two could be swapped without changing the gapping. Lofts are traditional for the era, with a 33-degree 7-iron and a 45-degree pitching wedge, and matching GW, SW and LW extended the set. Only the iron clubs are listed below; the 3 i-Wood (17 degrees, 58.5-degree lie, 40.5 inches) and 4 i-Wood (21 degrees, 59.25-degree lie) are hybrids rather than irons. The set was retroactively referred to as the Idea a1 once the a2 generation arrived in 2005, and the name was revived for an unrelated hollow-body set in 2023.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an original Adams Idea 7-iron?

The 2003 Adams Idea 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 37.5-inch length.

What clubs come in an original Adams Idea set?

The stock men's set replaces the long irons with a 17-degree 3 i-Wood and a 21-degree 4 i-Wood, then runs 5-iron through pitching wedge. A 25-degree 5 i-Wood could be swapped for the 5-iron, and matching GW, SW and LW heads extended the set.

Is the original Adams Idea the same as the Idea a1?

Yes. After the Idea a2 arrived in 2005, Adams' own site referred to the original set as the Idea a1. It is unrelated to the hollow-body Adams Idea irons that TaylorMade revived the name for in 2023.

Finding a used set

The Adams Idea (2003) ran from 2003 to 2005 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
