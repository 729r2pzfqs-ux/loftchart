About the Adams Idea a3 OS

The Idea a3 OS is the oversize, maximum-forgiveness half of the third-generation Idea hybrid-iron combo set, sold alongside the standard Idea a3 from 2007. Where the base a3 keeps five conventional irons, the a3 OS integrates six progressively shaped hybrids - 19-, 22- and 25-degree Boxer hybrids plus 28-, 32- and 36-degree iron-like hybrids - ahead of just two cavity-back short irons, the 9-iron and pitching wedge, for a wide-sole, high-MOI set Adams marketed as its "easiest-to-hit hybrid irons ever." That is two more hybrid clubs than the four-hybrid Idea a2 OS it replaced, and four more than the three-hybrid standard Idea a3. Golf Digest's 2008 Hot List praised Adams for pushing the hybrid-iron concept further by "increasing the number of wood like hybrid and iron-like hybrids and leaving just two irons in the set."

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Adams Idea a3 OS 7-iron?

The Adams Idea a3 OS '7' is a 32-degree iron-like hybrid rather than a conventional iron, with a 61.75-degree lie and a 37.5-inch length.

What clubs come in a stock Adams Idea a3 OS set?

Eight clubs: 19-, 22- and 25-degree Boxer hybrids (3H, 4H, 5H), three iron-like hybrids numbered 6, 7 and 8 (28, 32 and 36 degrees), then a 40-degree 9-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge. A senior's set was also offered.

How does the Idea a3 OS differ from the standard Idea a3 and the Idea a2 OS?

The a3 OS replaces six clubs with hybrid or iron-like hybrid heads and leaves only the 9-iron and pitching wedge as conventional irons, versus three hybrids in the standard Idea a3 and four hybrid clubs in the previous Idea a2 OS. Golf Digest's 2008 Hot List singled out the a3 OS for pushing the hybrid-iron count further than earlier Adams sets.

Finding a used set

The Adams Idea a3 OS ran from 2007 to 2008 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
