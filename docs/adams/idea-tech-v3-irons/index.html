About the Adams Idea Tech V3

The Idea Tech V3 is Adams' 2010 hybrid-iron set, sold in two eight-club configurations: a Forged set of 4, 5 and 6 hybrids and a 7 mid-hybrid ahead of four oversized cavity-back forged irons from 8-iron to gap wedge, and an All Hybrid set that runs hollow hybrid-iron heads all the way down (with a 49.5-degree GW in place of the forged set's 50). The hybrids carry tungsten weighting in stainless steel heads with ultra-thin faces, finished in nickel-chromium plate with PVD black accents. Lofts run 33 degrees at the 7 and 45 at the pitching wedge, and 2 and 3 hybrids, SW and LW extended the range. The forged-set clubs from the 7 mid-hybrid down are listed below; the 4-6 hybrids (22, 25 and 29 degrees) are hybrids rather than irons. It won a 2011 Golf Digest Hot List gold award and retailed around 799.95 USD before the Idea Tech V4 replaced it in late 2011.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Adams Idea Tech V3 7-iron?

33 degrees, with a 62.25-degree lie and a 37.5-inch length. The 7 in this set is a mid-hybrid head rather than a conventional iron.

What clubs come in a stock Adams Idea Tech V3 set?

Eight clubs in either configuration: 4, 5 and 6 hybrids and a 7 mid-hybrid, then 8-iron through gap wedge - forged cavity-back irons in the Forged set, or hollow hybrid-iron heads in the All Hybrid set.

Are Adams Idea Tech V3 irons forged?

In the Forged configuration the 8-iron through gap wedge are oversized cavity-back forged irons; the hybrids and the All Hybrid configuration's heads are cast stainless steel with tungsten weighting.

Finding a used set

The Adams Idea Tech V3 ran from 2010 to 2011 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
