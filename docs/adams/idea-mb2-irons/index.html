About the Adams Idea MB2

The Idea MB2 is Adams' 2011 muscle-back blade, forged from 8620 carbon steel and left in a raw, unplated finish that rusts with use. Brass weight plugs in the sole lower the centre of gravity, which Adams pitched at shot shaping and control rather than forgiveness. The stock set runs 3-iron through pitching wedge - the only set in Adams' 2012 line-up to include a 3-iron - with a 50-degree gap wedge available, and lofts follow a classic blade ladder with a 34-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge. It sat above the Idea Pro a12 and Idea Black CB2 as the best-player option in the range and retailed around 999.95 USD in steel.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Adams Idea MB2 7-iron?

The Adams Idea MB2 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch length.

What clubs come in a stock Adams Idea MB2 set?

The stock set is 3-iron through pitching wedge, with a 50-degree gap wedge available. It was the only set in Adams' 2012 line-up to include a 3-iron.

Do Adams Idea MB2 irons rust?

Yes, by design. The forged 8620 carbon steel heads are left with a raw, unplated finish that develops rust with use, which Adams said preserved groove volume and feel.

Finding a used set

The Adams Idea MB2 ran from 2011 to 2012 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
