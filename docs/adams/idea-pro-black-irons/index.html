About the Adams Idea Pro Black

The Idea Pro Black is a traditionally-shaped muscle-back blade, forged from carbon steel and finished in black nickel ion plating, that Adams brought to the PGA, Champions and Nationwide Tours for the 2009 season before a full retail release that spring. Adams' own materials rated it just 40% on its forgiveness scale with a narrow sole and constant, minimal offset, putting it at the workability end of the "Idea Pro Black" family - a year later Adams added the Idea Pro Black CB1 as a cavity-back, perimeter-weighted companion for players wanting some of the blade's look with more forgiveness, and retailer and site navigation began labelling this original blade "Idea Pro Black MB" to keep the two apart. It is the same muscle-back model that the Idea MB2's own predecessor field calls "Adams Idea Pro Black MB."

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Adams Idea Pro Black 7-iron?

The Adams Idea Pro Black 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch length.

What clubs come in a stock Adams Idea Pro Black set?

The set was offered in two configurations, 3-PW or 4-GW, all built on KBS Black Tour steel shafts in regular, stiff or extra-stiff flex.

Is the Adams Idea Pro Black the same iron as the Idea Pro Black MB?

Yes. Adams released it in spring 2009 simply as the Idea Pro Black. Once the company later reused the Idea Pro Black name for a hybrid, its own site navigation began calling this iron the Idea Pro Black MB to tell the two product lines apart, but the specs, shape and construction are identical.

Finding a used set

The Adams Idea Pro Black ran from 2009 to 2011 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
