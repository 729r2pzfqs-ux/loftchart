About the Adams Idea Pro Gold

The Idea Pro Gold is the second generation of Adams' forged players' hybrid-iron set, released in mid-2008. The eight-club stock make-up pairs 20-degree 3 and 23-degree 4 Idea Pro Gold Boxer hybrids with six forged 8620 carbon steel irons from the 5-iron to the pitching wedge, now finished in mirror chrome with a thin topline and sole. Against the original Pro Forged the irons sit about a degree more upright through the set and the 5-iron strengthens to 27 degrees, while the 7-iron stays at 34 degrees and the pitching wedge at 46. Only the irons are listed below; the two Boxer hybrids are not irons. 1, 2 and 5 hybrids and forged 3, 4, GW, SW and LW heads were available custom, and the set listed at 899 USD in steel and 1,099 USD in graphite.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Adams Idea Pro Gold 7-iron?

The Adams Idea Pro Gold 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch length.

What clubs come in a stock Adams Idea Pro Gold set?

Eight clubs: 20-degree 3 and 23-degree 4 Boxer hybrids, then forged 5-iron through pitching wedge. 1, 2 and 5 hybrids and forged 3, 4, GW, SW and LW heads were available through custom order.

What changed from the Idea Pro Forged to the Idea Pro Gold?

The hybrids adopted Adams' Boxer shaping, the forged irons gained a mirror-chrome finish and roughly one degree more upright lie through the set, the 5-iron strengthened from 26 to 27 degrees, and the stock iron shaft changed from True Temper Black Gold to Rifle Project X Flighted.

Finding a used set

The Adams Idea Pro Gold ran from 2008 to 2009 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
