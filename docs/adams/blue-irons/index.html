About the Adams Blue

The Blue was Adams Golf's last new iron release, launched on 3 April 2015 as a super-game-improvement set sold alongside a matching driver, fairway wood and hybrid. The 8-club stock set runs 5-iron through sand wedge; rather than offer a 4-iron, Adams steered golfers toward the companion Blue hybrid (19-25 degrees) for the long-iron slots. Velocity Slot Technology in the sole lets the cast, one-piece face flex more at impact for higher ball speed on mishits, paired with a low, rearward center of gravity, a thick top line and generous offset aimed at high-handicap and beginner golfers. It reached shops only months before TaylorMade quietly stopped developing new Adams products and shut down adamsgolf.com, marking the effective end of the Adams iron line.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Adams Blue 7-iron?

31.5 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length (37 inches in graphite).

What clubs come in a stock Adams Blue iron set?

Eight clubs: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, PW, AW and SW. Adams did not offer a 4-iron head in the Blue line, pairing the set instead with the separate Blue hybrid (19-25 degrees) for the long-iron slots.

Was the Adams Blue the last iron model Adams Golf released?

As far as can be verified, yes. Launched 3 April 2015, it appears to be the final new Adams-branded iron line before TaylorMade stopped developing new Adams products and took down adamsgolf.com around 2016, ahead of adidas selling both TaylorMade and Adams to KPS Capital Partners in 2017.

Finding a used set

The Adams Blue ran from 2015 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
