About the Adams Idea a3

The Idea a3 is the third-generation Idea hybrid-iron combo set and the first built around Adams' Boxer hybrid shaping, announced on 8 May 2007. The stock eight-club make-up is a 19-degree 3 Boxer hybrid, 22-degree 4 hybrid and 25-degree 5 hybrid in place of the long irons, then two hollow-back mid irons and three cavity-back short irons from the 6-iron to the pitching wedge. Adams claimed the Boxer hybrids carried up to 70 percent higher MOI than rival hybrids. Lofts stay traditional with a 33-degree 7-iron and a 45-degree pitching wedge, and a 2 hybrid, 4- and 5-irons and GW, SW and LW heads were available through custom fitting. Only the iron clubs are listed below; the stock hybrids are Boxer hybrids rather than irons. The men's set listed at 699.99 USD in steel and 799.99 USD in graphite.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Adams Idea a3 7-iron?

The Adams Idea a3 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch length.

What clubs come in a stock Adams Idea a3 set?

Eight clubs: 19-, 22- and 25-degree Boxer hybrids replace the 3-, 4- and 5-irons, followed by hollow-back 6- and 7-irons and cavity-back 8-iron through pitching wedge. A 2 hybrid, conventional 4- and 5-irons and GW, SW and LW heads were custom options.

What is Boxer Technology on the Idea a3?

Boxer is Adams' rounded, high-MOI hybrid head shape introduced with the a3 generation. Adams claimed the a3's Boxer hybrids had up to 70 percent higher MOI than other leading hybrids of the time.

Finding a used set

The Adams Idea a3 ran from 2007 to 2008 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
