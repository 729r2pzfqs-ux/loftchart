About the Adams Idea a7 OS

The Idea a7 OS is the maximum-forgiveness half of the 2009 a7 generation, announced alongside the standard Idea a7 on 6 August 2009. The stock eight-club make-up is three full hybrids (19-degree 3, 22-degree 4 and 25-degree 5), two iron-like mid-hybrids (28-degree 6 and 32-degree 7) and three short irons (8-iron, 9-iron and pitching wedge), with a 2 hybrid, GW, SW and LW offered to extend the set. Lofts use Adams' gapping ladder with a 32-degree 7 and a 44-degree pitching wedge. The 6 and 7 below are the mid-hybrid heads Adams numbered as part of the iron set; the 2-5 hybrids are not listed. The set won Golf Digest Hot List gold in the super-game-improvement category in 2010 and 2011 and listed at 699.99 USD in steel and 799.99 USD in graphite.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Adams Idea a7 OS 7-iron?

32 degrees, with a 62.25-degree lie and a 37.5-inch length. Note that the 7 in this set is an iron-like mid-hybrid rather than a conventional iron.

What clubs come in a stock Adams Idea a7 OS set?

Eight clubs: 3, 4 and 5 hybrids, 6 and 7 mid-hybrids, then 8-iron, 9-iron and pitching wedge. A 17-degree 2 hybrid plus GW, SW and LW heads were offered to extend the set.

What is the difference between the Idea a7 and the Idea a7 OS?

The a7 uses two hybrids and a hollow 5-iron ahead of conventional cavity-back irons with a 33-degree 7-iron; the a7 OS replaces five clubs with hybrid or mid-hybrid heads and runs one degree stronger, with a 32-degree 7. The OS version won Hot List super-game-improvement gold in 2010 and 2011.

Finding a used set

The Adams Idea a7 OS ran from 2009 to 2011 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
