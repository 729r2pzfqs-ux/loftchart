About the Bridgestone Tour B X-CBP

The Tour B X-CBP is the pocket-cavity model of Bridgestone's 2018 Tour B iron range — a forged soft-carbon-steel body with a press-formed SAE 8655 chrome-molybdenum face over a pocket cavity, giving a players profile with more launch and forgiveness than the Tour B X-CB it sat beside. It was released in Japan in autumn 2018 (the JDM stock set was 5-PW at 116,640 yen with N.S.PRO 950GH or MODUS3 TOUR105 steel shafts, the 4-iron a custom order) and was sold quietly in the US market, where retailers list it as a 2019 model; it never had the retail push of the X-CB and remained little known in the States. Lofts are moderately strong for a players iron of the era, with a 31-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge. Lengths below are the stock lightweight-steel build; Dynamic Gold, MODUS3 120/130 and KBS builds ran a quarter-inch shorter. The 222CB+ of 2022 took over the pocket-cavity slot in Bridgestone's line.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone Tour B X-CBP 7-iron?

The Bridgestone Tour B X-CBP 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch length in the stock lightweight-steel build.

What is the difference between the Bridgestone Tour B X-CB and X-CBP?

The X-CB is a one-piece forged cavity-back with traditional lofts (32-degree 7-iron), while the X-CBP adds a pocket cavity behind a press-formed SAE 8655 chrome-molybdenum face, slightly more offset, a wider sole and lofts about a degree stronger (31-degree 7-iron, 44-degree pitching wedge) for more launch and forgiveness.

When did the Bridgestone Tour B X-CBP come out?

It was released in Japan in autumn 2018 as part of the 2018 Tour B iron range, and US retailers sold it as a 2019 model without a major launch.

Are the Bridgestone Tour B X-CBP irons forged?

The body is forged from soft carbon steel, but the face is a separate press-formed piece of high-strength SAE 8655 chrome-molybdenum steel covering a pocket cavity — a two-piece construction rather than a one-piece forging.

Finding a used set

The Bridgestone Tour B X-CBP ran from 2018 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
