About the Bridgestone Tour B JGR HF1

The JGR Hybrid Forged — later relabelled Tour B JGR HF1 once Bridgestone folded it into the Tour B line — was announced at the January 2016 PGA Merchandise Show and reached US retail on 4 March 2016 at $699 (steel) or $749 (graphite), Bridgestone's pitch for players who found ordinary super-game-improvement irons too harsh. The forged, hybrid-inspired head has a wide sole running well behind the face, a hollow body with the crown cut away like a hybrid, and a 1.5 mm forged face — Bridgestone's thinnest ever — that flexes at impact for extra ball speed, with a rubber insert behind the face damping vibration and pushing weight to the perimeter for a low, deep center of gravity. Stock lofts are strong for the category (a 26-degree 7-iron) and the stock set ran 6 through a 44-degree first pitching wedge, with 5-iron and two additional wedges — a 44-degree second pitching wedge and 50-degree approach wedge — available individually. Stock shafts were Nippon N.S. Pro Zelos 8 steel or UST Mamiya Recoil graphite. The more compact Tour B JGR HF2 succeeded it in 2018.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone Tour B JGR HF1 7-iron?

The Bridgestone Tour B JGR HF1 7-iron has a loft of 26 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 38-inch length.

Is the Bridgestone JGR Hybrid Forged the same club as the Tour B JGR HF1?

Yes. It launched in March 2016 simply as the JGR Hybrid Forged; Bridgestone later folded it into its Tour B branding and its own site refers to it as the Tour B JGR HF1 once the HF2 arrived in 2018.

What makes the JGR Hybrid Forged irons distinctive?

A forged, hybrid-styled head with the crown cut away, a wide digging-resistant sole, and a 1.5 mm forged face — Bridgestone's thinnest at the time — that flexes at impact for added ball speed, with a rubber insert behind the face for feel and perimeter weighting.

What clubs came in a stock JGR Hybrid Forged (Tour B JGR HF1) set?

The standard set ran 6-iron through a 44-degree first pitching wedge (six pieces), with the 5-iron, a second 44-degree wedge and a 50-degree approach wedge available individually.

Finding a used set

The Bridgestone Tour B JGR HF1 ran from 2016 to 2018 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
