About the Bridgestone 222CB+

The 222CB+ is the semi-large forged cavity-back of Bridgestone's 22-series, announced by Bridgestone Sports on 2 August 2022 and released in Japan on 9 September 2022 alongside the compact 221CB and the custom-order 220MB muscle-back. Every iron in the set carries a Dual Wide Pocket Cavity that removes about 11 grams from the head, and a Gravity Control Design that thickens the face toward the sole for a lower centre of gravity and higher launch, while the impact area stays thick for a soft forged feel. It is the largest and most forgiving of the three models, with a 79.0-millimetre 7-iron blade length and lofts roughly one degree stronger than the 221CB through the middle irons. The JDM stock set is 5-PW with the 4-iron a custom-order single (no factory length is published for it); the US market received the model in December 2024 as a 5-PW set. The 242CB+ announced in August 2024 succeeded it in Japan, though the 222CB+ remained on sale in the US after the December 2024 launch. Lengths below are the stock N.S.PRO 950GH neo steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone 222CB+ 7-iron?

The Bridgestone 222CB+ 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch length in the stock N.S.PRO 950GH neo steel build.

What is the difference between the Bridgestone 222CB+ and 221CB?

The 222CB+ is the semi-large model: it carries a Dual Wide Pocket Cavity in every iron rather than only the long irons, a lower centre of gravity, a 79.0 mm 7-iron blade length against the 221CB's 76.3 mm, slightly stronger mid-iron lofts (31-degree 7-iron versus 32) and lighter stock shafts.

When did the Bridgestone 222CB+ come out?

Bridgestone Sports announced it on 2 August 2022 and released it in Japan on 9 September 2022. It reached the United States in December 2024, when Bridgestone re-entered the US club market.

Was the Bridgestone 222CB+ replaced?

In Japan, yes — the 242CB+ announced in August 2024 succeeded it. In the United States the 222CB+ was still current after the December 2024 launch.

Finding a used set

The Bridgestone 222CB+ ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
