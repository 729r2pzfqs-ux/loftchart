About the Bridgestone 221CB

The 221CB is a one-piece forged S20C soft carbon steel players cavity-back, announced by Bridgestone Sports on 2 August 2022 and released in Japan on 9 September 2022 alongside the larger 222CB+ and the custom-order 220MB muscle-back. It uses Bridgestone's Tour Contact Sole with cut leading and trailing edges, a Gravity Control Design that thickens the face toward the blade for spin, and dual-pocket cavities in the 4- and 5-irons for launch and forgiveness. In Japan the stock set is 5-PW with the 4-iron as a custom-order single; when Bridgestone re-entered the US club market in December 2024 the 221CB was sold there as a 4-PW set, and it remains in the US line even though the 241CB succeeded it in Japan in 2024. Lengths in this table are the N.S.PRO MODUS3 TOUR120 build per Bridgestone's 60-degree measurement, with the 4-iron length from the US retail spec.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone 221CB 7-iron?

The Bridgestone 221CB 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 36.75-inch length in the stock MODUS3 TOUR120 steel build.

When did the Bridgestone 221CB come out?

Bridgestone Sports announced the 221CB on 2 August 2022 and released it in Japan on 9 September 2022. It only reached the United States in December 2024, when Bridgestone re-entered the US club market with the 220 MB, 221 CB and 222 CB+ at $1,050 for a 4-PW set.

What is the difference between the Bridgestone 221CB and 222CB+?

The 221CB is the compact players cavity-back with dual-pocket cavities only in the 4- and 5-irons, while the 222CB+ is a semi-large forged cavity-back with a dual wide pocket cavity in every iron, a lower centre of gravity, slightly weaker long-iron lofts (21-degree 4-iron) and lighter stock shafts.

Was the Bridgestone 221CB replaced?

In Japan, yes — the 241CB announced in August 2024 is its successor and keeps the same loft structure. In the United States the 221CB remained the current players cavity-back after the December 2024 US launch.

Finding a used set

The Bridgestone 221CB ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
