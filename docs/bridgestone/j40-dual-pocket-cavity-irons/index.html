About the Bridgestone J40 Dual Pocket Cavity

The J40 Dual Pocket Cavity was the more forgiving of Bridgestone's two J40 forged irons, announced in September 2011 and at US retail from October 2011 alongside the compact J40 Cavity Back. The midsize head is premium-forged from 1020 mild carbon steel and carries a revamped version of the dual pocket cavity Bridgestone introduced on the J38 — two milled pockets that push discretionary weight to the perimeter for a higher MOI and a mid-to-high launch — while keeping traditional lofts (34-degree 7-iron, 46-degree pitching wedge) and modest offset for its "professionals to mid handicap amateurs" target. Like the Cavity Back it was sold right hand only as a 5-PW set at a $699 street price ($799 MSRP), with the 3- and 4-iron available individually, on a stock Project X flighted steel shaft with a Golf Pride Tour Velvet grip; Bridgestone also offered J40 Combo sets mixing the two heads. Offsets in the table are converted from Bridgestone's published millimetre figures (3.2 mm at the 3-iron tapering to 1.9 mm at the pitching wedge). The J40 generation was succeeded by the J15 forged iron family introduced at the January 2015 PGA Merchandise Show.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone J40 Dual Pocket Cavity 7-iron?

The Bridgestone J40 Dual Pocket Cavity 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 63-degree lie, a 36.75-inch length and a D1 swing weight.

What is the difference between the Bridgestone J40 Dual Pocket Cavity and the J40 Cavity Back?

Both are forged from 1020 mild carbon steel, but the Dual Pocket Cavity is the midsize, higher-launching model with two milled perimeter-weighting pockets and lofts one degree stronger through most of the set (34-degree 7-iron versus 35), while the Cavity Back is the compact better-player head with a V-cut cavity.

When were the Bridgestone J40 Dual Pocket Cavity irons made?

Bridgestone announced the J40 line in September 2011 with US retail from October 2011, and it remained current until the J15 forged iron family was introduced in January 2015.

Finding a used set

The Bridgestone J40 Dual Pocket Cavity ran from 2011 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
