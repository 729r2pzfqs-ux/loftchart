About the Bridgestone Tour B X-CB

The Tour B X-CB was the players cavity-back in Bridgestone's Tour B iron line, announced for the US market on 22 February 2017 alongside the Tour B X-Blade; the equivalent Japanese-market Tour B X-CB had debuted the previous autumn, and Bridgestone's US site files the model under its 2017 archive. The head is forged by Endo Manufacturing from 1020 carbon steel with a slightly larger profile and wider sole than the X-Blade, a forged weight pad behind the impact zone, and mid-iron lofts three degrees stronger than the blade (32-degree 7-iron against the X-Blade's 35). The US stock set was 4-PW at $1,200 with a 3-iron available, sold with Nippon's Modus3 Tour steel shaft as stock and ten further options at no upcharge. A second-generation Tour B X-CB announced in Japan in 2018 succeeded it there, and Bridgestone's next US-market players cavity-back was the 221CB in December 2024.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone Tour B X-CB 7-iron?

The Bridgestone Tour B X-CB 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 36.75-inch standard steel length.

What is the difference between the Bridgestone Tour B X-CB and X-Blade?

Both are Endo-forged from 1020 carbon steel, but the X-CB is a cavity-back with a slightly larger profile, wider sole and lower centre of gravity, and its 6-, 7- and 8-irons are three degrees stronger than the X-Blade's (28/32/36 versus 31/35/39 degrees).

When did the Bridgestone Tour B X-CB come out?

Bridgestone announced the Tour B X-CB for the US market on 22 February 2017 at $1,200 for a 4-PW set; the equivalent Japanese-market model had appeared in late 2016, and a second-generation Tour B X-CB followed in Japan in 2018.

Are the Bridgestone Tour B X-CB irons forged?

Yes. The heads are forged from 1020 carbon steel by Endo Manufacturing, with a forged weight pad positioned behind the impact zone.

Finding a used set

The Bridgestone Tour B X-CB ran from 2017 to 2018 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
