About the Bridgestone 220 MB

The 220 MB is Bridgestone's soft-iron-forged muscle-back, announced by Bridgestone Sports on 2 August 2022 as the B-Limited 220MB and released in Japan on 9 September 2022 as a custom-order-only single (3-iron through PW, 24,200 yen per club) alongside the 221CB and 222CB+ cavity-backs. The head is forged from S20C soft iron with a Tour Groove Design milled to 0.02-millimetre precision for spin consistency, and it carries the smallest blade length of the three 22-series irons (74.4 mm at the 7-iron against the 221CB's 76.3 mm). When Bridgestone re-entered the US club market in December 2024 the 220 MB became a stock US offering at $1,050 for a 4-PW set with Nippon Modus3 Tour 120 shafts, and it remains current. Lofts and lies below are Bridgestone's published figures; lengths are the US retail build (the JDM release lists no lengths because every club was built to order), and no length was published for the custom-order 3-iron.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone 220 MB 7-iron?

The Bridgestone 220 MB 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch length in the stock US Modus3 Tour 120 build.

When did the Bridgestone 220 MB come out?

Bridgestone Sports announced it as the B-Limited 220MB on 2 August 2022 and released it in Japan on 9 September 2022 as a custom-order model. It reached the United States as a stock 4-PW set in December 2024, when Bridgestone re-entered the US club market.

Are the Bridgestone 220 MB irons forged?

Yes. The heads are forged in Japan from S20C soft iron, with a precision-milled Tour Groove Design that Bridgestone specifies to 0.02 millimetres for spin consistency.

How big is the Bridgestone 220 MB head?

It is the smallest of Bridgestone's 22-series irons — 74.4 millimetres of blade length at the 7-iron, against 76.3 mm for the 221CB and 79.0 mm for the 222CB+.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Bridgestone 220 MB is a current blade set in the Bridgestone range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Bridgestone Tour B X-Blade. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
