About the Bridgestone 241CB

The 241CB is the players cavity-back in Bridgestone's B-Series tour iron line, announced by Bridgestone Sports on 6 August 2024 and released in Japan on 6 September 2024 as the successor to the 221CB. It is a one-piece forged S20C soft carbon steel head with a muscle-cavity back that thickens the metal directly behind the impact area, and a dual-cut "Tour Contact Sole" with relieved leading and trailing edges refined from tour feedback. The stock set is 5-iron through pitching wedge with a 22-degree 4-iron offered as a custom-order single; the lengths in this table are the N.S.PRO MODUS3 TOUR120 build measured by the Japanese 60-degree method (the MODUS3 TOUR105 build is a quarter inch longer per club). Bridgestone publishes face progression rather than offset, so the offset key is omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone 241CB 7-iron?

The Bridgestone 241CB 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch length in the stock MODUS3 TOUR120 steel build (Japanese 60-degree measurement).

What clubs come in a stock Bridgestone 241CB set?

The stock set is 5-iron through pitching wedge (six clubs). A 22-degree 4-iron is available as a custom-order single with N.S.PRO MODUS3 TOUR120 or TOUR105 shafts.

Did the 241CB replace the Bridgestone 221CB?

Yes. Bridgestone's August 2024 announcement of the B-Series 241CB and 242CB+ presented them as the successors to the 2022 221CB and 222CB+, keeping the same 32-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge lofts.

Is the Bridgestone 241CB forged?

Yes. The head is one-piece forged from S20C soft carbon steel, with a muscle-cavity back and a dual-cut Tour Contact Sole.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Bridgestone 241CB is a current players set in the Bridgestone range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Bridgestone 221CB. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
