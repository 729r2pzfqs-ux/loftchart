About the Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged

The J15 Driving Forged (J15DF) was unveiled alongside the J15 Cavity Back, J15 Dual Pocket Forged and J15 Muscle Back on 7 January 2015 ahead of that year's PGA Merchandise Show, part of what Bridgestone called its deepest iron line-up ever. It is built from two pieces of premium-forged carbon steel fused together to hide a cavity between the front and rear faces, with Bridgestone's Flex Action Speed Technology (F.A.S.T.) letting the face flex for extra ball speed and a mid Sure Contact Sole for consistent turf interaction; Bridgestone aimed it at "professionals to mid handicap amateurs," the same target statement it used for the companion J15CB. Loft, lie, length and swing weight are identical to the J15 Cavity Back (32-degree 7-iron, 46-degree pitching wedge), with slightly less bounce throughout. It was sold right hand only as a 4-PW set at $879 with a 3-iron available, on a stock True Temper DG Pro shaft. Bridgestone's own archive shows no direct successor once the J15 generation gave way to the Tour B line in 2017.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged 7-iron?

The Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch length — identical to the J15 Cavity Back.

What is the difference between the Bridgestone J15DF and J15CB?

Both are two-piece forged carbon steel irons with the same loft, lie, length and swing weight ladder, but the J15DF's hidden cavity and Flex Action Speed Technology face are pitched for extra ball speed, while the J15CB is the more traditional compact cavity-back of the pair.

What clubs came in a stock Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged set?

Bridgestone sold it right hand only as a 4-PW set at $879, with the 3-iron available separately, on a stock True Temper Dynamic Gold Pro shaft.

When was the Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged introduced?

It was unveiled on 7 January 2015 alongside the J15 Cavity Back, Dual Pocket Forged and Muscle Back irons, ahead of that year's PGA Merchandise Show.

Finding a used set

The Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged ran from 2015 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
