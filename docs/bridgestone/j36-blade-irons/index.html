About the Bridgestone J36 Blade

The J36 Blade was the muscle-back of Bridgestone's J36 forged line, aimed at "professionals to low handicap amateurs." The head is premium-forged from 1020 mild carbon steel with a high center of gravity for a penetrating ball flight, a thin top line and sole, and a compact profile with progressive offset for shot-shaping. It shares Bridgestone's J36 Cavity Back's exact loft, lie, length and swing-weight ladder (35-degree 7-iron, 47-degree pitching wedge, D3 throughout) with the same Rifle Project X flighted steel shaft and Golf Pride Tour Velvet grip as stock, sold right hand only as a 3-iron through pitching wedge. The Condition of Competition rule made the J36 irons non-conforming for professional play from 1 January 2010. Bridgestone did not carry a pure muscle-back forward into the J38 generation that followed — the next Bridgestone blade documented in this database is the 2015 J15 Muscle Back.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone J36 Blade 7-iron?

The Bridgestone J36 Blade 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, with a 63-degree lie, a 36.75-inch length and a D3 swing weight.

Are Bridgestone J36 Blade irons forged?

Yes. The heads are premium-forged from 1020 mild carbon steel with a high center of gravity for a penetrating flight, a thin top line and sole, and a compact profile.

What is the difference between the Bridgestone J36 Blade and the J36 Cavity Back?

Both share the identical published loft, lie, length and swing-weight ladder, but the Blade is a true muscle-back with a thin top line and compact profile for low-handicap players, while the Cavity Back adds a progressive-offset cavity for more forgiveness.

What clubs came in a stock Bridgestone J36 Blade set?

It was sold right hand only as a 3-iron through pitching wedge set, on a stock Rifle Project X flighted steel shaft.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2008, the Bridgestone J36 Blade is a current blade set in the Bridgestone range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Bridgestone J33 Forged Blade. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
