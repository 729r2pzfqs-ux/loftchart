About the Bridgestone B-FORGED 100CB

The B-FORGED 100CB is the players cavity-back in Bridgestone's tour iron line, announced by Bridgestone Sports on 29 July 2026 and released in Japan on 4 September 2026 as the successor to the 241CB, under a new B-FORGED name that drops the year-based numbering. It is a forged S20C soft carbon steel head with a thicker section behind the impact area than the 241CB, a "Neo Tour Contact Sole" with added relief towards the toe and heel, and face milling on the pitching wedge. Lofts and lies are unchanged from the 241CB, with a 32-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge. The stock set is 5-iron through pitching wedge with a 22-degree 4-iron offered as a custom-order single; the lengths in this table are the N.S.PRO MODUS3 TOUR120 build measured by the Japanese 60-degree method (the MODUS3 TOUR105 DUAL FLOW build is a quarter inch longer per club). Bridgestone publishes face progression rather than offset, so the offset key is omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone B-FORGED 100CB 7-iron?

The Bridgestone B-FORGED 100CB 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch length in the stock MODUS3 TOUR120 steel build (Japanese 60-degree measurement).

Did the lofts change between the Bridgestone 241CB and the B-FORGED 100CB?

No. The lofts and lies are the same club for club, from a 25-degree 5-iron through a 32-degree 7-iron to a 46-degree pitching wedge, and the MODUS3 TOUR120 lengths are also unchanged.

What clubs come in a stock Bridgestone B-FORGED 100CB set?

The stock set is 5-iron through pitching wedge (six clubs). A 22-degree 4-iron is available as a custom-order single.

When was the Bridgestone B-FORGED 100CB released?

Bridgestone Sports announced it on 29 July 2026 and it went on sale in Japan on 4 September 2026.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Bridgestone B-FORGED 100CB is a current players set in the Bridgestone range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Bridgestone 241CB. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
