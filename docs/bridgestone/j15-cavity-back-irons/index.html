About the Bridgestone J15 Cavity Back

The J15 Cavity Back (J15CB) was the better-player iron of Bridgestone's J15 forged family, unveiled on 7 January 2015 ahead of the PGA Merchandise Show as part of what Bridgestone called its deepest iron line ever, alongside the J15 Driving Forged, Dual Pocket Forged and cast models. The head is premium-forged carbon steel with a compact profile aimed at professionals through mid-handicap amateurs, and its wide Sure Contact Sole is relieved for consistent turf interaction — unusually forgiving for a players iron of its size. Lofts are traditional (32-degree 7-iron, 46-degree pitching wedge) and identical to the Tour B X-CB that replaced it in 2017; it was sold 4-PW with a 3-iron available, in right and left hand, with True Temper's Dynamic Gold Pro as the stock shaft. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Bridgestone J15CB 7-iron?

The Bridgestone J15 Cavity Back 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch length.

Are the Bridgestone J15CB irons forged?

Yes. The J15 Cavity Back is premium-forged from carbon steel, part of the all-forged J15 iron family Bridgestone launched in January 2015.

Who are the Bridgestone J15CB irons for?

Bridgestone aimed them at professionals through mid-handicap amateurs: a compact forged players head whose wide, relieved Sure Contact Sole adds unusual forgiveness through the turf for the category.

What replaced the Bridgestone J15CB?

The Tour B X-CB, announced for the US market in February 2017, took over the players cavity-back slot and kept the identical 20-to-46-degree loft structure.

Finding a used set

The Bridgestone J15 Cavity Back ran from 2015 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
