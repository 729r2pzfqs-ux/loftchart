About the Wishon 565MC

The 565MC is Tom Wishon's second-generation update to his 560MC forged cavity back, introduced in January 2016 and announced on GolfWRX with a cosmetic change designer Tom Wishon explained directly in the thread: the cavity badge medallion was dropped in favor of a fully CNC-machined surface milling pattern across the cavity floor and muscle weight, made possible by advances in the forging and machining process since the original 560MC. A center cavity half-moon weighting increases solid, on-center feel and adds a very slight increase in trajectory versus the 560MC, while the fully CNC-machined back cavity holds a precise centre of gravity position at every loft. Low offset (2 mm throughout the 5-9 irons) and a slightly thinner topline than the 560MC keep the look compact and traditional; early buyers on GolfWRX noted the head carries comparatively little bounce. Double nickel chrome pearl plating with a glass bead blast highlight, USGA/R&A-compliant CNC grooves. Sold as bare heads or custom builds (3-PW/AW) through Wishon-authorized clubmakers rather than retail, at $140-225 per head depending on volume.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wishon 565MC 7-iron?

The Wishon 565MC 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 61 degrees and a standard length of 37 inches.

How does the Wishon 565MC differ from the 560MC?

It is Tom Wishon's second-generation update: the cavity badge medallion is replaced by a fully CNC-machined surface milling pattern, a center cavity half-moon weighting adds solid feel and a touch more trajectory, and the topline runs slightly thinner than the original 560MC.

How do you buy Wishon 565MC irons?

As bare heads or custom-built sets through Wishon-authorized clubmakers - there is no off-the-rack retail version - priced from $140 to $225 per head depending on volume, available 3-iron through pitching wedge and approach wedge in right hand only.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2016, the Wishon 565MC is a current players set in the Wishon range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Wishon 560MC. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
