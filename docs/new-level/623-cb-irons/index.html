About the New Level 623-CB

The 623-CB is the cavity-back companion to New Level's 623-M muscle iron, teased by the company in late October 2021 and at retail from January 2022 — record the announcement year, 2021, against the 2022 retail date. It keeps the 623 family's compact shape, thin topline and minimal offset but swaps the muscle for a forged cavity, adding stability for what New Level pitches as a player's iron with a little help. Lofts sit one degree weaker than the 623-M throughout: a 34-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge across the stock 4-PW run, with 2- and 3-irons offered to order. The model's profile rose sharply when it finished first overall in MyGolfSpy's 2022 Most Wanted player's-iron test. New Level sells direct and through fitters and builds each set to order from its shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded; the 702-CB took over the cavity-back slot when the 702 series arrived in 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a New Level 623-CB 7-iron?

The New Level 623-CB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37 inches.

How does the 623-CB differ from the 623-M?

Same compact 623 shaping, thin topline and lie/length ladder, but the CB is a cavity back rather than a muscle back and its lofts are one degree weaker at every club — 34 versus 33 degrees at the 7-iron — trading a little flight for stability.

When did the New Level 623-CB come out?

New Level announced it in late October 2021 and it reached retail in January 2022. It went on to win MyGolfSpy's 2022 Most Wanted player's-iron test.

Finding a used set

The New Level 623-CB ran from 2021 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
