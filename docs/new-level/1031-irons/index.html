About the New Level 1031

The 1031 Forged was the more forgiving half of New Level Golf's 2018 launch lineup, sold alongside the compact 902 by the direct-to-consumer maker Eric Burch founded in Scottsdale, Arizona. The mid-size head is forged from 1020 carbon steel and carries noticeably more offset than the 902 — a near-constant 4.8 mm across the set against the 902's taper from 3.5 to 2 mm — with slightly stronger long-iron lofts (22 degrees at the 4-iron) that converge on the same 32-degree 7-iron before finishing at a 44-degree pitching wedge. Hooked On Golf Blog reviewed the set in August 2018, its first season. The chart runs 4-PW, right hand only, in chrome; New Level assembled every set to order from its shaft menu, so no stock shaft or swing weight is recorded. The model left the range as New Level rebuilt its iron line around the 902-PD/902-OS generation in 2021, and its factory spec sheet now sits on the company's Archives page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a New Level 1031 7-iron?

The New Level 1031 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie, 4.8 mm of offset and a 37-inch length.

What is the difference between the New Level 1031 and the 902?

Both launched in 2018 with a 32-degree 7-iron, but the 1031 is the friendlier head: a mid-size profile forged from 1020 carbon steel with roughly twice the offset of the compact 902, and a 44-degree pitching wedge to the 902's 45.

Is the New Level 1031 forged?

Yes. The head is forged from 1020 carbon steel; New Level positioned it as a forged iron with game-improvement levels of help.

Is the New Level 1031 still in production?

No. It left the line around New Level's 2021 range refresh, and its spec sheet now lives on the company's Archives page.

Finding a used set

The New Level 1031 ran from 2018 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
