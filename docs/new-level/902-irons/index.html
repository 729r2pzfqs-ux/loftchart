About the New Level 902

The 902 Forged was the iron that launched New Level Golf, the direct-to-consumer maker founded in Scottsdale, Arizona in 2018 by longtime club builder Eric Burch. The head is forged from 1025 carbon steel, then CNC-milled to its final state for club-to-club consistency, and carries a hollow-body design to lift launch and forgiveness while presenting a compact players profile. Lofts are traditional for the class — a 32-degree 7-iron and 45-degree pitching wedge across a 4-PW, right-hand-only chart in chrome — with modest offset that tapers from 3.5 mm at the 4-iron to 2 mm in the scoring clubs. New Level's own spec sheet is headed 2018, and the company built every set to order from its shaft menu, so no stock shaft or swing weight is recorded. The 902-PD Forged of 2021 was built as its enhanced sequel, and the original's spec sheet now sits on New Level's Archives page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a New Level 902 7-iron?

The New Level 902 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie, 2.5 mm of offset and a 37-inch length.

Is the New Level 902 forged?

Yes — forged from 1025 carbon steel and then CNC-milled to its final state, with a hollow body for launch and forgiveness.

What replaced the New Level 902?

The 902-PD Forged of 2021, a hollow-body sequel with the same 32-degree 7-iron loft, sold alongside the larger 902-OS.

Did the New Level 902 come in left hand?

No. The factory spec sheet lists the 902 as right hand only, in chrome.

Finding a used set

The New Level 902 ran from 2018 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
