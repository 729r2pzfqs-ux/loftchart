New Level Golf Club Specifications

New Level Golf sells forged irons direct and through fitters, building every set to order from its own shaft menu. Founder Eric Burch came to the company from club fitting, and the 623 series put the brand on the map when it won MyGolfSpy's 2022 players-iron test.
