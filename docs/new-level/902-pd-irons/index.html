About the New Level 902-PD

The 902-PD Forged was the hollow-body players-distance iron from New Level Golf, the direct-to-consumer maker in Scottsdale, Arizona, built as the sequel to the company's original 902 Forged with a progressive design — wider soles in the long irons, thinner toplines in the scoring clubs. The head is forged from 1025 carbon steel and hollow to raise launch and forgiveness, yet the lofts are traditional for the category: a 32-degree 7-iron and a 44-degree pitching wedge across a 4-PW, right-hand-only chart. New Level's own spec sheet is headed 2021 and the model was in MyGolfSpy's 2022 Most Wanted players distance test by early 2022, where it finished fifth overall and ranked best for forgiveness. Like the 902-OS it was retired around the launch of the 2025 702 series, its spec sheet moving to New Level's Archives page; every set was assembled to order from a shaft menu, so no stock shaft or swing weight is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a New Level 902-PD 7-iron?

The New Level 902-PD 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie, 2.5 mm of offset and a 37-inch length.

Is the New Level 902-PD a hollow-body iron?

Yes. It is forged from 1025 carbon steel with a hollow body for launch and forgiveness — unlike its one-piece-forged stablemate, the 902-OS.

How did the New Level 902-PD do in testing?

It finished fifth overall in MyGolfSpy's 2022 Most Wanted players distance iron test and was ranked best in the category for forgiveness.

Is the New Level 902-PD still in production?

No. New Level replaced its iron range with the 702 series in 2025, removed the 902-PD product page and moved its spec sheet to the company's Archives page.

Finding a used set

The New Level 902-PD ran from 2021 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
