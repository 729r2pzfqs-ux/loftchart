About the New Level 623-M

The 623-M was the tour iron of New Level's early lineup — the model founder Eric Burch cites as the company's first iron played on professional tours — and it was in reviewers' hands by December 2018. The head is a compact muscle design forged from 1020 carbon steel and CNC milled after forging, with a thin topline, minimal offset, a slightly wider-than-average sole and a milled tuning slot in the muscle that lowers the centre of gravity for more launch than a conventional blade. Lofts are traditional: a 33-degree 7-iron and a 45-degree pitching wedge across the stock 4-PW composition. New Level sells direct and through fitters and builds every set to order from a large shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded. The standard model left the range as the 702 series took over in 2025; a matte-black 623-M "Dead Golfer Edition", a 2023 collaboration with TRUE Linkswear built to these same standard specs, remained on newlevelgolf.com after the standard chrome model was gone.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a New Level 623-M 7-iron?

The New Level 623-M 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37 inches.

Is the New Level 623-M a blade?

Nearly — it is a compact forged muscle back with a thin topline and minimal offset, but a milled tuning slot in the muscle and a slightly wider sole lower the centre of gravity, so it launches higher and plays a touch more forgiving than a conventional blade.

What is the 623-M Dead Golfer Edition?

A 2023 limited collaboration with TRUE Linkswear: the same 623-M head in a matte black finish, built only to the standard loft and lie specs listed here because bending the finished head would blemish the coating.

Finding a used set

The New Level 623-M ran from 2018 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
