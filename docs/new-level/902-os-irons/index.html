About the New Level 902-OS

The 902-OS Forged was the oversized players-distance model from New Level Golf, the direct-to-consumer maker in Scottsdale, Arizona, built as a larger heel-to-toe version of its original 902 Forged iron for players wanting the balance between a player's iron and forgiveness. The head is one-piece forged carbon steel — 1020 on New Level's own per-club spec table, though retail copy says soft 1025 — and carries a removable precision-weighting screw in the back of the iron that New Level's builders use to control swing weight - the company publishes no stock swing weights because it has no stock configuration, and every set is assembled to order from a shaft menu (KBS Tour Lite, KBS Tour, Nippon Modus and True Temper Dynamic Gold options among others) rather than with a single stock shaft. Lofts are moderately strong for the category, with a 31-degree 7-iron and 43-degree pitching wedge, and heads ran 4-iron through a 47-degree approach wedge at about $130 per club. It placed 3rd overall in MyGolfSpy's 2023 players distance iron test, tying as best for feel. New Level marketed it as a brand-new offering for 2022; the model was retired around the launch of the 2025 702 series, after which its spec sheet moved to New Level's Archives page with only leftover left-handed stock listed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a New Level 902-OS 7-iron?

The New Level 902-OS 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie, 2.5 mm of offset and a 37-inch length.

What clubs came in a New Level 902-OS set?

Heads ran 4-iron through pitching wedge plus a 47-degree approach wedge, sold per club (around $130 each) and built to order - New Level had no fixed stock set configuration.

Is the New Level 902-OS a hollow-body iron?

No. It is a one-piece forged carbon-steel head (1020 per New Level's spec table) with a removable weighting screw in the back for swing-weight control. New Level's hollow-body model of that generation was the 902-PD.

Is the New Level 902-OS still in production?

No. New Level replaced its iron range with the 702 series in 2025 and moved the 902-OS spec sheet to its Archives page; only leftover left-handed stock remained listed after that.

Finding a used set

The New Level 902-OS ran from 2022 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
