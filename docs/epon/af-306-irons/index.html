About the Epon AF-306

The AF-306 is Epon's compact deep-cavity players iron, released on 26 March 2022 to mark the 45th anniversary of the Epon golf brand. Epon is the house label of Endo Manufacturing in Tsubame, Niigata, and the head is forged as one piece from S20C soft carbon steel with a tungsten weight forged into the toe side - 33 percent more tungsten than the preceding AF-305, with a 10 percent narrower sole and a fractionally shorter head. Lofts are traditional for the category, with a 32-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge. Epon sells heads through its Niigata direct store and authorized fitters, built to order with no fixed stock shaft; the lengths below are the standard steel assembly lengths from the Japanese retail spec chart, and offsets are converted from Epon's published gooseneck figures in millimetres.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Epon AF-306 7-iron?

The Epon AF-306 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 36.75-inch standard steel length.

Who makes Epon AF-306 irons?

Epon is the house brand of Endo Manufacturing in Tsubame, Niigata, Japan - a forging house that has also made heads for many major OEMs. The AF-306 is forged in-house from S20C carbon steel with a tungsten weight forged into the toe.

Is the Epon AF-306 a blade?

No. It is a compact deep-cavity players iron. The one-piece forging carries a tungsten inner weight on the toe side to raise the moment of inertia while keeping a compact, minimal-offset profile.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Epon AF-306 is a current players set in the Epon range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Epon AF-305. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
