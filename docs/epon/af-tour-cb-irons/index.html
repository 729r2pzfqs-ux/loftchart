About the Epon AF-Tour CB

The AF-Tour CB is a forged half-cavity players iron from Epon's Tour line, built as a more forgiving companion to the muscle-back AF-Tour - sharing a similar loft ladder but carrying more offset - from a single S20C billet with a nickel-chrome plated finish. A workshop blog post dated 18 February 2015 covers a completed AF-Tour CB fitting, and Ginone Golf's custom-order catalogue files the model under its 2015 product year, which is earlier than some later marketplace descriptions imply. The AF-Tour CB2 succeeded it, appearing in Ginone Golf's 2023 product year. Heads are built to order with no fixed stock shaft; lengths below are the standard steel assembly lengths from the Japanese retail spec chart. Epon's gooseneck/offset figures for this model were not found in an accessible source, so the offset key is omitted here rather than estimated.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Epon AF-Tour CB 7-iron?

The Epon AF-Tour CB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees, and a standard steel length of 36.5 inches.

How does the AF-Tour CB differ from the Epon AF-Tour?

The AF-Tour CB is a half-cavity version of the muscle-back AF-Tour, sharing a similar loft ladder but built with more offset for extra forgiveness.

When was the Epon AF-Tour CB released?

Around February 2015 - a workshop blog documented a completed custom build that month, and Ginone Golf's catalogue files the model under its 2015 product year.

Finding a used set

The Epon AF-Tour CB ran from 2015 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
