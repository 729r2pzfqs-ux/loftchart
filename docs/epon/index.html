Epon Golf Club Specifications

Epon is the house brand of Endo Manufacturing, the Tsubame forging specialist that has made heads for many major OEMs since entering golf in the late 1970s - the AF-306 marked the brand's 45th anniversary in 2022. Clubs are sold head-first through Epon's Niigata direct store and authorized fitters, so published charts cover loft, lie and gooseneck with recommended assembly lengths rather than a single stock build.
