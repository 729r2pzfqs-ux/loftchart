About the Epon AF-307

The AF-307 is Epon's compact forged cavity back, announced on 31 January 2025 and on sale from 21 February 2025 as the replacement for the AF-306. Epon is the house label of Endo Manufacturing in Tsubame, Niigata. The head is forged from S20C carbon steel with tungsten placed on both the toe side and the neck side, which Epon says raises the moment of inertia while shortening the centre-of-gravity distance for easier face rotation; the face is taller than before and the blade is shaped to look lower at the neck. Against the AF-306 the 9-iron and pitching wedge are one degree stronger, at 40 and 45 degrees, with the 4- through 8-irons unchanged. The figures below are Epon's own chart for the N.S.PRO MODUS3 TOUR 105 (S) steel build; offsets are converted from Epon's millimetre figures.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Epon AF-307 7-iron?

The Epon AF-307 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch length in the N.S.PRO MODUS3 TOUR 105 steel build.

How do the Epon AF-307 lofts differ from the AF-306?

The 4-iron through 8-iron are the same, from 23 to 36 degrees. The AF-307 9-iron is 40 degrees against 41, and the pitching wedge is 45 degrees against 46. Lies and offsets are unchanged.

When was the Epon AF-307 released?

Epon announced it on 31 January 2025 and put it on sale on 21 February 2025 through its Niigata direct store and authorised dealers.

Is the Epon AF-307 forged?

Yes. It is forged from S20C carbon steel, with tungsten weights on the toe and neck sides of the head.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Epon AF-307 is a current players set in the Epon range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Epon AF-306. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
