About the Epon AF-705

The AF-705 is a game-improvement iron in Epon's numbered "AF-7xx" distance line, forged from an S20C billet with an undercut pocket cavity and progressively larger offset than Epon's players and players-distance models to add forgiveness and easier launch. It predates the AF-706 that followed it - Epon's own AF-706 launch material describes the AF-706 as the brand's first hollow-construction iron, which means the AF-705 itself is a conventional one-piece forging rather than a hollow-body design. Ginone Golf's custom-order catalogue places the AF-705 in its 2017 product year. Heads are built to order with no fixed stock shaft; lengths below are the standard steel assembly lengths from the Japanese retail spec chart, and offsets are converted from the published gooseneck figures in millimetres. A 56-degree SW head extends the stock 5-PW set to fill the gap to the sand wedge, matching the wedge-extension pattern Epon carried into the AF-706.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Epon AF-705 7-iron?

The Epon AF-705 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees, and a standard steel length of 37 inches.

Is the Epon AF-705 a hollow iron?

No. It is a conventional one-piece forged pocket-cavity iron. Epon's own material for the later AF-706 identifies that model, not the AF-705, as the brand's first hollow-construction iron.

What wedges match the Epon AF-705 set?

Because the pitching wedge is 43 degrees, Epon offered a 49-degree AW and a 56-degree SW in the same head style to complete the set down to the sand wedge.

Finding a used set

The Epon AF-705 ran from 2017 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
