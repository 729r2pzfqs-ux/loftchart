About the Wilson Staff Model Blade (2024)

The second Staff Model Blade was announced on 11 January 2024 with a new Staff Model CB, both forged from 8620 carbon steel with Wilson's Fluid Feel hosel and extra mass in the toe. Lofts, lies and lengths are unchanged from the 2019 original, with a 34-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge. The stock set was 4-iron to pitching wedge with True Temper Dynamic Gold Mid 115 shafts; a retailer table also lists 2- and 3-irons, which are not recorded here because only one source gives them. Wilson replaced the model with the 2026 Staff Model Blade.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Staff Model Blade (2024) 7-iron?

The 2024 Staff Model Blade 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

Did the lofts change from the 2019 Staff Model Blade?

No. The 4-iron through pitching wedge run 23, 26, 30, 34, 38, 42 and 46 degrees on both, with the same lies and lengths.

How does the 2024 Staff Model Blade differ from the 2024 Staff Model CB?

Lofts, lies and lengths are the same. The Blade is a muscle back with slightly less offset in the longer irons, 0.10 inches at the 4-iron against 0.11 on the CB.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the 2024 Staff Model Blade?

46 degrees.

Finding a used set

The Wilson Staff Model Blade (2024) ran from 2024 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
