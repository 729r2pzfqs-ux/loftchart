About the Wilson Staff C300 Forged

The C300 Forged is the compact version of Wilson's C300, keeping the Power Hole idea — urethane-filled slots cut through the sole and topline so the face can flex further — in a forged head with a thinner topline. Lofts are moderately strong for a forged iron at 33 degrees for the 7-iron, and the set is built long, at 38.75 inches for the 4-iron.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Staff C300 Forged 7-iron?

The Wilson Staff C300 Forged 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

When were Wilson Staff C300 Forged irons released?

They were on sale from 22 January 2018, alongside the cast C300.

What are the Power Holes on a Wilson C300 Forged?

Urethane-filled slots cut through the sole and topline around the face. They let the face flex further at impact while the urethane keeps the feel and sound in check.

Finding a used set

The Wilson Staff C300 Forged ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
