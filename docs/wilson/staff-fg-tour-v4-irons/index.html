About the Wilson Staff FG Tour V4

The FG Tour V4 is Wilson's forged players cavity back of the mid-2010s, with a traditional loft chart — 35 degrees at the 7-iron, weaker than almost anything sold beside it — and a set that runs 4-iron through gap wedge. Lie angles step in irregular increments, from 60 degrees at the 4-iron to 64.25 at the gap wedge, rather than the even half-degree progression most makers use.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Staff FG Tour V4 7-iron?

The Wilson Staff FG Tour V4 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

When were Wilson Staff FG Tour V4 irons made?

They were revealed in 2014 and reached retail from January 2015, and were replaced by the FG Tour V6 in 2016.

Are FG Tour V4 lofts weak?

By modern standards, yes — deliberately so. The 35-degree 7-iron is a traditional players loft, five degrees weaker than a distance iron of the same period.

Finding a used set

The Wilson Staff FG Tour V4 ran from 2014 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
