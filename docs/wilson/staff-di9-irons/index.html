About the Wilson Staff Di9

Announced on 27 August 2008 for the 2009 season, the Di9 is Wilson Staff's distance iron built around a deliberate 3.5-degree loft strategy: every gap from the 4-iron to the gap wedge is exactly 3.5 degrees, with an approach wedge slotted in to keep short-iron gapping even. The low-profile, wide-sole head adds a longer hosel for higher MOI, a toe-biased cavity that widens the sweet spot, and a urethane "shock trap" insert in the heel to damp mishit vibration. The stock wide-tip True Temper TX-99 steel shaft was the lightest Wilson had produced at 99 grams.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Staff Di9 7-iron?

The Wilson Staff Di9 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.5 inches.

What is the Di9's 3.5-degree loft strategy?

From the 4-iron down to the gap wedge every loft gap is exactly 3.5 degrees, and Wilson added a 46-degree approach wedge between the pitching wedge and gap wedge so distance gaps stay even through the scoring clubs.

When did the Wilson Staff Di9 irons come out?

Wilson announced them on 27 August 2008 as its powerful, forgiving distance iron for 2009, and the Di11 replaced them in 2010.

Finding a used set

The Wilson Staff Di9 ran from 2008 to 2010 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
