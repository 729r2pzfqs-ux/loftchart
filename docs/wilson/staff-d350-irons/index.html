About the Wilson Staff D350

The D350 was the superlight companion to the Staff D300 in Wilson's 2017 distance line, built around the same Speed Sole thin-face technology but with lighter head, shaft and grip weights while keeping a standard D2 swing weight. It was sold primarily as a hybrid-iron blend: the stock iron set is 5-iron through gap wedge, with matching hybrids offered in place of the long irons and an approach and sand wedge available to extend the set. Lofts match the D300 through most of the bag, with a 31-degree 7-iron and a 43-degree pitching wedge. Figures below are the standard men's specification for the 5-GW irons.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Staff D350 7-iron?

The Wilson Staff D350 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.5-inch steel-shaft length.

What is the difference between the Wilson D300 and D350 irons?

The D350 is the superlight version of the D300: same Speed Sole distance technology and essentially the same lofts, but with lighter head, shaft and grip weights, and it was sold as a hybrid-iron blend with matching hybrids replacing the long irons.

What clubs come in a stock Wilson Staff D350 set?

The stock iron set is 5-iron through gap wedge. Matching hybrids were offered in 3 through 6 to fill the top of the bag, and a 55-degree sand wedge was available to extend the bottom.

Finding a used set

The Wilson Staff D350 ran from 2017 to 2019 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
