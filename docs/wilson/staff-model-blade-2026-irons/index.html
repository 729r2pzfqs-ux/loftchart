About the Wilson Staff Model Blade (2026)

The second-generation Staff Model Blade was announced 27 January 2026 alongside a refreshed Staff Model CB and the all-new hollow-body Staff Model XB, with the three positioned as one forged family designed together rather than separate projects; US retail followed in early February 2026. The head is forged from 8620 carbon steel with a precision-milled face and Wilson's Fluid Feel Hosel, which strips mass from the hosel and shifts it toward the toe. Compared with the 2019 Blade, the profile is more compact with a shorter blade length and noticeably tighter offset (0.105 inches at the 3-iron tapering to 0.045 at the pitching wedge, versus an unpublished offset on the original), and the bright chrome finish is replaced by a brushed satin look. Lofts, lies and lengths carry over unchanged from the 2019 model, with a 34-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge. The stock set is 3-iron through pitching wedge (eight clubs); a seven-piece steel set lists at $1,399.99 / £1,169. Figures below are the standard men's steel-shaft specification.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a 2026 Wilson Staff Model Blade 7-iron?

The 2026 Staff Model Blade 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length — unchanged from the original 2019 Staff Model Blade.

What is different about the 2026 Wilson Staff Model Blade versus the 2019 original?

Lofts, lies and lengths carry over unchanged, but the 2026 version has a more compact head with a shorter blade length, noticeably tighter offset through the set, and a brushed satin finish in place of the original's bright chrome.

What clubs come in a 2026 Wilson Staff Model Blade set?

The stock set is 3-iron through pitching wedge, eight clubs, sold as a set for $1,399.99 (about £1,169) in steel.

When was the 2026 Wilson Staff Model Blade released?

Wilson announced the Blade alongside the refreshed CB and new XB on 27 January 2026, with US retail following in early February 2026.

Production status

Introduced in 2026, the Wilson Staff Model Blade (2026) has no confirmed end date on file. It replaced the Staff Model Blade (2019). LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the Wilson catalogue — check with Wilson or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
