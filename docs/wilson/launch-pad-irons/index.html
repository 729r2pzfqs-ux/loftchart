About the Wilson Launch Pad

The original Launch Pad is Wilson's lightweight, anti-chunk iron for new golfers and chronic fat-hitters, announced on 10 December 2019 and in shops from 13 January 2020, so it sold as Wilson's 2020 line. A hollow construction supports a thin face, and the wide "Launch Pad" sole is designed to float over the turf instead of digging. Lofts are deliberately moderate for the category — a 30-degree 7-iron and a 44-degree pitching wedge — to keep flight high. The stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge; the 49-degree gap wedge and 55-degree sand wedge are custom options. Figures below are Wilson's published men's specification with the steel-shaft D0 swing weight; the graphite build was listed at C9.5.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Launch Pad 7-iron?

The Wilson Launch Pad 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.5-inch steel-shaft length.

What clubs come in a stock Wilson Launch Pad set?

The stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge. A 49-degree gap wedge and a 55-degree sand wedge were offered as custom options to match.

Are the Wilson Launch Pad lofts strong?

No. Wilson kept the lofts moderate for a super-game-improvement iron — a 30-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge — so the wide-soled, hollow heads launch high rather than chasing distance.

When did the Wilson Launch Pad irons come out?

Wilson announced them on 10 December 2019 and they reached shops on 13 January 2020, selling as the 2020 line until the Launch Pad 2 replaced them in early 2022.

Finding a used set

The Wilson Launch Pad ran from 2019 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
