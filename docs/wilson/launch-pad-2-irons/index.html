About the Wilson Launch Pad 2

The Launch Pad 2 is the second generation of Wilson's lightest, easiest-launching iron family, announced in early 2022 as part of a full Launch Pad line of driver, woods, hybrids and irons aimed at new golfers and players who struggle with contact. Weight is removed and mass lowered for a lower centre of gravity, and the wide sole is optimised for turf interaction to resist digging and chunking. Lofts are moderate rather than chased for distance — a 30-degree 7-iron and a 44-degree pitching wedge — which keeps flight high. Wilson's spec table runs 5-iron through sand wedge; the stock set is 5-PW plus gap wedge, with the 55-degree sand wedge a custom option offered right hand only. Stock builds were the KBS Max Ultralite steel shaft or Project X Evenflow graphite with a Wilson Staff midsize grip; the published table carries two unlabelled swing-weight rows, D1 and D0, matching the steel and graphite builds. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Launch Pad 2 7-iron?

The Wilson Launch Pad 2 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37.5-inch length.

What clubs come in a stock Wilson Launch Pad 2 set?

The stock set is 5-iron through pitching wedge plus a 49-degree gap wedge, in right or left hand. A 55-degree sand wedge is available as a custom option, right hand only.

Are the Wilson Launch Pad 2 lofts strong?

No — Wilson kept them moderate to preserve height. The 30-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge are one to two degrees weaker than most distance-focused game-improvement irons of 2022, such as the 29-degree 7-iron on the Cleveland Launcher XL.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Wilson Launch Pad 2 is a current super game improvement set in the Wilson range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Wilson Launch Pad. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
