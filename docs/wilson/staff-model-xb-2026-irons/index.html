About the Wilson Staff Model XB (2026)

The Staff Model XB is an all-new hollow-body model announced 27 January 2026 alongside a refreshed Staff Model Blade and Staff Model CB, positioned as a players-distance iron with a tour-inspired profile rather than a successor to any earlier Wilson Staff line. It is forged from 8620 carbon steel with a hollow-body construction that shifts weight to the perimeter for higher MOI, an internal urethane injection to damp vibration, and tungsten weighting; Wilson's Fluid Feel Hosel and a precision-milled face carry over from the Blade and CB. Lofts are meaningfully stronger than the Blade/CB pair — a 32-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge, versus 34 and 46 on those models — while lies and lengths match the rest of the 2026 family. The stock set runs 2-iron through gap wedge (ten clubs, with the 2-iron offered right-handed only); the retailer spec table lists this as a 4-PW stock configuration for pricing purposes, with a seven-piece steel set at $1,399.99 / £1,169. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Staff Model XB 7-iron?

The Staff Model XB 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length — two degrees stronger than the 34-degree 7-iron on the companion 2026 Staff Model Blade and CB.

Is the Staff Model XB a new model or a relaunch of an older Wilson iron?

It is an all-new model, introduced alongside the refreshed Staff Model Blade and CB on 27 January 2026. It has no prior-generation predecessor in Wilson's lineup.

What clubs come in a Wilson Staff Model XB set?

The model is built from 2-iron (right-handed only) through a 50-degree gap wedge, ten clubs in total, with 4-PW as the retailer's stock configuration and a seven-piece steel set priced at $1,399.99 (about £1,169).

How is the Staff Model XB different from the Staff Model CB?

The XB is a hollow-body construction with an internal urethane insert and tungsten weighting for higher MOI, aimed at a players-distance category, while the CB is a solid forged cavity back. The XB also runs meaningfully stronger lofts — a 32-degree 7-iron versus the CB's 34 degrees.

Production status

Introduced in 2026, the Wilson Staff Model XB (2026) has no confirmed end date on file. LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the Wilson catalogue — check with Wilson or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
