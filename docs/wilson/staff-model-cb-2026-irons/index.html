About the Wilson Staff Model CB (2026)

The second-generation Staff Model CB was announced 27 January 2026 as part of a refreshed forged Staff Model family alongside the Blade and the new hollow-body XB, with US retail following in early February 2026. The head is forged from 8620 carbon steel with a precision-milled face and Wilson's Fluid Feel Hosel, which strips mass from the hosel and shifts it toward the toe; the compact cavity-back shape carries a shorter blade length, softened edges and a tighter offset than the 2020 original (0.110 inches at the 3-iron tapering to 0.040 at the gap wedge), plus a brushed satin finish in place of the earlier chrome look. Sources describing the 2026 model do not repeat the 2020 version's Tri-Brace stabiliser or tungsten toe-weighting language, so those features are not assumed to carry over. Lofts, lies and lengths are unchanged from the 2020 CB, with a 34-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge. The stock set runs 3-iron through gap wedge (nine clubs); a seven-piece steel set lists at $1,399.99 / £1,169. Figures below are the standard men's steel-shaft specification.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a 2026 Wilson Staff Model CB 7-iron?

The 2026 Staff Model CB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length — the same numbers as the 2020 Staff Model CB and the 2026 Staff Model Blade.

What clubs come in a 2026 Wilson Staff Model CB set?

The model is built from 3-iron through a 50-degree gap wedge (nine clubs), with 4-PW as the typical stock set makeup and a seven-piece steel set priced at $1,399.99 (about £1,169).

How is the 2026 Staff Model CB different from the 2020 version?

Lofts, lies and lengths are unchanged, but the 2026 CB has a more compact head, tighter offset through the set, and a brushed satin finish rather than chrome. Descriptions of the 2026 model do not repeat the 2020 version's Tri-Brace stabiliser or tungsten toe-weighting claims.

How is the 2026 Staff Model CB different from the 2026 Staff Model Blade?

Both share the same 34-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge lofts and lengths, but the CB is a cavity back with a touch more offset and forgiveness and adds a 50-degree gap wedge that the Blade does not have.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Wilson Staff Model CB (2026) is a current players set in the Wilson range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Wilson Staff Model CB (2024). New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
