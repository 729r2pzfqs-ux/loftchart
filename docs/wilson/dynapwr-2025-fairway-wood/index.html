About the Wilson Dynapwr

Wilson's 2025 Dynapwr fairway woods launched in February 2025 with the second-generation Dynapwr drivers. The Carbon is the player's head — carbon crown, 30 g of low-forward tungsten and a six-way adjustable hosel — in five lofts from a 13.5° 3+ wood; the Max is the bonded, higher-launching forgiveness head with a 12 g rear weight, in three lofts. As of October 2026 the line is still current, with no 2027 replacement announced.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the 2025 Wilson Dynapwr fairway woods come in?

The Dynapwr Carbon comes in 13.5, 15, 16.5, 18 and 21 degrees; the Dynapwr Max in 15, 18 and 21.

Is the Wilson Dynapwr fairway wood adjustable?

Only the Carbon, which carries Wilson's six-way adjustable hosel. The Max has a bonded hosel and plays at its stamped loft.

What is the difference between the Dynapwr Carbon and Max fairway woods?

The Carbon is the lower-spinning player's head — carbon crown, 30 grams of forward tungsten, adjustable hosel, flatter lies. The Max is the bonded game-improvement head with a rear weight for high launch, at 50 dollars less.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Wilson Dynapwr is the current Wilson fairway wood line as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Wilson Dynapwr (2023). New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above; the hosel settings extend each head beyond its stamped loft, so a loft between two stock options is usually reachable from either. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
