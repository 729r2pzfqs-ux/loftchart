About the Wilson Staff D200

The D200 continued Wilson's superlight D-series game-improvement line, keeping the large undercut cavity of the D100 but thinning the cavity bottom so the face could flex faster and adding weight pods in the heel and toe that Wilson claimed raised MOI by five percent. The sole was raised and narrowed slightly compared with the D100. Lofts are moderate-strong for the class, with a 31-degree 7-iron and a 43-degree pitching wedge, and the 47-degree gap wedge fills the large gap to the 55-degree sand wedge. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Staff D200 7-iron?

The Wilson Staff D200 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.5-inch steel-shaft length.

What clubs come in a stock Wilson Staff D200 set?

The core set is 4-iron through pitching wedge, with a 47-degree gap wedge and a 55-degree sand wedge available to complete the bottom of the bag.

What changed from the Wilson D100 to the D200?

The D200 kept the D100's large undercut cavity but thinned the cavity bottom for more face flex, added heel and toe weight pods for about five percent more MOI, and raised and narrowed the sole slightly.

Finding a used set

The Wilson Staff D200 ran from 2015 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
