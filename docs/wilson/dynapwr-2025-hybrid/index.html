Hosel adjustment

The Wilson Dynapwr has a glued, non-adjustable hosel — the loft printed on the head is the loft it plays. Loft and lie can only be altered by a clubmaker, so pick the stock loft to match your launch rather than planning to tune it later.

About the Wilson Dynapwr

Wilson's 2025 Dynapwr hybrid launched in February 2025 with the Dynapwr woods — a single compact head with an extended toe, in five lofts from a 17° 2-hybrid to a 28° 6-hybrid, plus lightweight LITE builds of the 4-, 5- and 6-hybrids. The hosel is glued, so each head plays at its stamped loft. As of October 2026 the line is still current, with no replacement announced.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the 2025 Wilson Dynapwr hybrid come in?

Five lofts: 17, 19, 22, 25 and 28 degrees — the 2-hybrid through 6-hybrid. LITE builds are offered in the 22, 25 and 28 degree heads.

Is the Wilson Dynapwr hybrid adjustable?

No — the hosel is glued and each head plays at its stamped loft. In the 2025 Dynapwr family only the drivers and the Carbon fairway carry an adjustable hosel.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Wilson Dynapwr is the current Wilson hybrid line as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Wilson Dynapwr (2023). New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above, and with no adjustable hosel the stock loft is the one to get right. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
