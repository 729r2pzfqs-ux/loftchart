About the Wilson Staff D9 Forged

The D9 Forged is the players-distance companion to the cast Staff D9, launched in 2022 with a two-piece forged 8620 carbon steel head. It carries Wilson's urethane-filled Power Holes in an asymmetric, heel-biased layout adapted for the forged construction, in a slimmer head with less offset than the standard D9. Lofts are about three degrees weaker than the cast D9 through the mid irons — a 30.5-degree 7-iron and a 44-degree pitching wedge against 27 and 42 — with a 5-PW stock set and a 4-iron and 49-degree gap wedge available to match. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Wilson Staff D9 Forged 7-iron?

The Wilson Staff D9 Forged 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

What is the difference between the Wilson Staff D9 and D9 Forged?

The standard D9 is a cast stainless steel game-improvement iron with strong lofts (27-degree 7-iron). The D9 Forged is a forged 8620 carbon steel players-distance iron with a slimmer head, less offset and lofts about three degrees weaker, including a 30.5-degree 7-iron.

What clubs come in a stock Wilson Staff D9 Forged set?

The stock set is 5-iron through pitching wedge. A 21.5-degree 4-iron and a 49-degree gap wedge were available to extend the set.

Finding a used set

The Wilson Staff D9 Forged ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
