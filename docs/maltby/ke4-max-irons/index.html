About the Maltby KE4 Max

The KE4 Max sits at the forgiving end of Maltby's long-running KE4 line, cast from 17-4 stainless with an extremely thick topline, a wide sole and generous offset built for maximum forgiveness rather than shot-shaping. The GolfWorks' own Maltby MPF ratings chart dates it to 2022 with an MPF of 959 - Maltby's own "Ultra Game Improvement" tier - and the company advertises the model as carrying the strongest lofts in its premium iron range: a 30-degree 7-iron and 43-degree pitching wedge across a 4-GW factory chart, with a matching 48-degree gap wedge. A 4-way radiused sole varies bounce and sole width by club for turf interaction across lofts, and a vertical centre of gravity of 0.758 inches keeps launch high through the set. Sold as bare heads or built-to-order paks (commonly 4-PW or 4-GW) through The GolfWorks, with custom loft and lie available at no charge on professionally assembled sets; a separate KE4 MAX-F forged model shares the name but uses a different, forged construction.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Maltby KE4 Max 7-iron?

The Maltby KE4 Max 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a standard length of 37 inches.

Is the Maltby KE4 Max forged or cast?

Cast - 17-4 stainless steel, in Maltby's own 'Ultra Game Improvement' tier. A separate KE4 MAX-F model uses a forged 1025C carbon steel construction and sits in the Super Game Improvement category instead.

How forgiving is the Maltby KE4 Max?

Very: the GolfWorks' own MPF chart rates it 959 at the 6-iron, among the highest ratings in Maltby's premium iron range, with an extremely thick topline, wide sole and generous offset built for maximum forgiveness.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Maltby KE4 Max is a current super game improvement set in the Maltby range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
