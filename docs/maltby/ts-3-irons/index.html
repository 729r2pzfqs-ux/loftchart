About the Maltby TS-3

The TS-3 joined Maltby's Tour Series in 2023 as the highest-MPF head in the family: it measures 849 at the 6-iron on Maltby's Playability Factor scale, deep in the Super Game Improvement band — the classification recorded here — even though it wears a diminutive, players-style profile with smooth lines and only a moderate topline. The trick is construction: each head is true-forged from 1025C carbon steel in a five-step precision process with a tungsten weight co-forged low and toward the toe, and the cavity design progresses through the set, shifting mass toe-ward and low in the long irons and toward neutral in the scoring clubs, over progressive .780-to-.800-inch sole widths. The CNC-milled face carries Maltby's multi-groove pattern, and lofts are moderate for the forgiveness on offer — a 32-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge across a 4-GW chart in satin mirror nickel-chrome, right hand only. The GolfWorks sells it as bare heads or built-to-spec custom sets, so the lengths below are the company's published standard men's steel assembly lengths. A TS-3.5 and a DBM black finish followed in 2025 without retiring it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Maltby TS-3 7-iron?

The Maltby TS-3 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees and a lie of 62.5 degrees, with 0.110 inches of offset and a 37-inch length on The GolfWorks' standard steel build.

Why is the Maltby TS-3 classed as super game improvement?

Because of its measured forgiveness rather than its looks: it rates 849 on Maltby's MPF scale — Super Game Improvement territory — thanks to co-forged tungsten toe weighting and progressive cavities, despite a compact profile with a moderate topline.

Is the Maltby TS-3 forged?

Yes — true-forged from 1025C carbon steel in a five-step precision process, with the tungsten toe weight co-forged into the head rather than glued or screwed in.

When did the Maltby TS-3 come out?

It appeared with The GolfWorks' 2023 Maltby releases and remains on sale; a TS-3.5 head and a DBM black-finish version were added in 2025 alongside it.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Maltby TS-3 is a current super game improvement set in the Maltby range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
