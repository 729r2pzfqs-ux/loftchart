About the Maltby TS-1 IM

The TS-1 IM - "Injected Muscle" - extends Maltby's Tour Series hollow-forged concept with a stronger MS300 super maraging steel face (2.1 mm, chosen for higher elongation and yield strength than standard maraging) welded to a forged 1025C carbon steel body, with the same internal tungsten weight low and toward the toe as the original TS-1 and a polymer-filled void behind the face for added ball speed. The GolfWorks' own Maltby MPF ratings chart dates it to 2023 with an MPF of 728, just above the original TS-1's 711 - both inside Maltby's Super Game Improvement playability band despite the compact, tour-style head. Precision five-step forged, Pearl Chrome finish, a universal hosel accepting .370 parallel or .355 taper shafts, and optional custom loft/lie within +/-2 degrees at no charge on professionally assembled sets. Sold as bare heads or built-to-order paks (3-PW/GW, 4-PW/GW or 5-PW/GW) through The GolfWorks rather than off the rack.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Maltby TS-1 IM 7-iron?

The Maltby TS-1 IM 7-iron has a loft of 32.5 degrees and a lie of 62 degrees, with 0.090 inches of offset and a 4-degree bounce.

What does 'IM' mean in the Maltby TS-1 IM?

Injected Muscle - a polymer-filled void behind a 2.1 mm MS300 super maraging steel face, welded to a forged 1025C carbon steel body with an internal tungsten toe weight, aimed at more ball speed than the original TS-1.

How forgiving is the Maltby TS-1 IM?

The GolfWorks' own MPF chart rates it 728 at the 6-iron - inside Maltby's Super Game Improvement playability band - despite a compact, tour-style profile, slightly higher than the original TS-1's 711.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Maltby TS-1 IM is a current game improvement set in the Maltby range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Maltby TS-1. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
