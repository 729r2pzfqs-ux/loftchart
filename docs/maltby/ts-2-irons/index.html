About the Maltby TS-2

The TS-2 opened Maltby's Tour Series in 2018 as the distance half of the pair the compact TS-1 completed a few months later. It is a three-piece, true billet-forged hollow iron — a 2 mm 1025C carbon steel face and 1025C body with an internal tungsten weight positioned low and toward the toe, precision-welded and, from the 4-iron through 9-iron, injection-filled with dense polymer behind the whole face to boost deflection and feel. Lofts run a degree to a degree and a half stronger than the TS-1 (30-degree 7-iron, 44-degree pitching wedge) with more offset and a larger blade, which is what pushes it into the players-distance class; on Maltby's own MPF forgiveness scale it rates 727 at the 6-iron, in the Super Game Improvement band. The 4-GW chart comes in satin polished chrome, right hand only, on a universal hosel accepting .370 parallel or .355 taper shafts. The GolfWorks sells it as bare heads or built-to-order custom sets, so the lengths below are the company's published standard men's steel assembly lengths. Still in the line as of 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Maltby TS-2 7-iron?

The Maltby TS-2 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees and a lie of 62.5 degrees, with 0.125 inches of offset and a 37-inch length on The GolfWorks' standard steel build.

What is the difference between the Maltby TS-1 and TS-2?

Both are billet-forged hollow designs with tungsten toe weighting, but the TS-2 is the distance head: lofts one to two degrees stronger (30 against 32 at the 7-iron), more offset (0.140 against 0.120 inches at the long end) and a larger profile, while the TS-1 keeps the more compact players shape.

Is the Maltby TS-2 forgiving?

By Maltby's own measurement, yes — it rates 727 MPF at the 6-iron, inside the Super Game Improvement band, despite looking like a players iron.

Is the Maltby TS-2 still available?

Yes. As of 2026 The GolfWorks still sells TS-2 heads and custom builds, more than seven years after its 2018 introduction.

Finding a used set

The Maltby TS-2 ran from 2018 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
