About the Maltby STi2

The STi2 is a precision-cast 431 stainless iron Maltby markets as landing in its Super Game Improvement design category despite a mid-sized, "confidence-building" profile at address rather than an oversized cavity-back look. An undercut cavity keeps the centre of gravity low and deep (0.729 inches), a Variable Face Thickness design boosts ball speed on off-centre strikes, and Silica Gel damping in the 4-through-7 irons cuts vibration; 431 stainless was chosen specifically for a softer, more forged-like feel than typical cast alloys. The GolfWorks' own Maltby MPF ratings chart dates it to 2020 with an MPF of 772, and progressive offset runs from 0.138 inches in the 4-iron down to 0.098 inches in the scoring clubs. Lofts span a 32-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge across a 4-GW factory chart. Sold as bare heads or built-to-order paks through The GolfWorks; the STi2 GEN 2 replaced it in the catalog in 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Maltby STi2 7-iron?

The Maltby STi2 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a standard length of 37 inches.

Why did Maltby use 431 stainless steel for the STi2?

431 stainless is softer than the more common 17-4 stainless used in many cast irons, chosen specifically to give the STi2 a feel closer to a forged iron despite being cast.

What replaced the Maltby STi2?

The STi2 GEN 2, which took over the same Super Game Improvement slot in The GolfWorks' catalog around 2025.

Finding a used set

The Maltby STi2 ran from 2020 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
