About the Maltby TS-4

The TS-4 is the blade of Maltby's Tour Series — a classic muscle-back profile released through The GolfWorks in 2023 that hides thoroughly modern internals. Each head is true billet-forged from 1025C carbon steel in a precision multi-step process; in the 3-iron through 8-iron a tungsten weight is co-forged low and toward the toe and CMC (Ceramic Matrix Composite), which Maltby pioneered in this iron, is positioned inside the head to fine-tune the centre of gravity and soften feel. That mix is why a traditional-looking blade rates 755 at the 6-iron on Maltby's MPF forgiveness scale, a figure normally seen from mid-size cavity backs. The face and sole are CNC-milled, the face with Maltby's multi-groove pattern, and lofts are modern for a blade — a 33-degree 7-iron and 45.5-degree pitching wedge across a 3-GW chart in pearl chrome, right hand only, on a universal hosel taking .370 parallel or .355 taper shafts. Sold as bare heads or built-to-spec custom sets, so the lengths below are The GolfWorks' published standard men's steel assembly lengths. A DBM black-finish version followed in 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Maltby TS-4 7-iron?

The Maltby TS-4 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees and a lie of 62.5 degrees, with 0.100 inches of offset and a 37-inch length on The GolfWorks' standard steel build.

Is the Maltby TS-4 a real blade?

In profile, yes — a classic muscle back at address. Internally it is unusually engineered: tungsten is co-forged into the low toe and CMC (Ceramic Matrix Composite) is placed inside the #3-#8 heads, giving it an MPF rating of 755, far above a conventional blade.

What is CMC in the Maltby TS-4?

Ceramic Matrix Composite — a lightweight material Maltby first used in the TS4, positioned inside the forged head alongside the tungsten to optimise the centre of gravity and enhance feel.

When was the Maltby TS-4 released?

In 2023 through The GolfWorks, per Maltby's own MPF ratings database; the DBM (Diamonized Black Metal) finish version followed in 2025, and both remain on sale.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Maltby TS-4 is a current blade set in the Maltby range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
