About the Maltby MMB-17

The MMB-17 - Modified Muscle Back - is Maltby's forged blade-style iron softened just enough for a wider market: a weight relief pocket and inverted muscle design in the 3-through-7 irons, tungsten toe weighting in the 3-, 4- and 5-irons, and Maltby's MG multi-groove face, all forged from 8620 carbon steel for a soft feel. The GolfWorks' own Maltby MPF ratings chart dates it to 2013 with an MPF of 714, in the Super Game Improvement band by Maltby's own measurement despite reading as a traditional blade at address. Lofts run a 39-degree 8-iron and 47-degree pitching wedge with a matching 51-degree gap wedge, both wedges built to the same 291-gram head weight; a "DBM Black Forged" special finish was also sold under the PF701+ name. Sold as bare heads or built-to-order sets (commonly 4-PW or 4-P plus approach wedge) through The GolfWorks rather than off the rack.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Maltby MMB-17 7-iron?

Based on the confirmed 6-iron (31 degrees) and 8-iron (39 degrees) anchor points on the manufacturer-format chart, the 7-iron loft is 35 degrees, following the even 4-degree-per-club progression documented from the 6-iron through the gap wedge.

What does MMB stand for in the Maltby MMB-17?

Modified Muscle Back - a forged blade-style shape softened with a weight relief pocket, inverted muscle design in the 3-through-7 irons and tungsten toe weighting in the long irons, aimed at a wider range of players than a pure blade.

How forgiving is the Maltby MMB-17?

The GolfWorks' own MPF chart rates it 714 at the 6-iron - inside Maltby's Super Game Improvement playability band - despite its traditional blade-style look at address.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2013, the Maltby MMB-17 is a current blade set in the Maltby range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
