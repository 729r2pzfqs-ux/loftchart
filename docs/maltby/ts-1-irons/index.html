About the Maltby TS-1

The TS-1 is the players head of Maltby's Tour Series, the forged hollow line Ralph Maltby's GolfWorks launched to prove a tour-profile iron could measure high on his own MPF forgiveness scale — the TS-1 rates 711, inside the Super Game Improvement band, despite a compact shape. On sale by early 2019 alongside the larger TS-2, it is a true billet-forged three-piece design: a 2 mm 1025C carbon steel face and 1025C body with an internal tungsten weight low and toward the toe, precision-welded and injection-filled with polymer behind the face to lift ball speed and soften feel, with a CNC-milled multi-groove face. Lofts are modern (32-degree 7-iron, 45-degree pitching wedge) across a 3-GW chart in satin polished chrome, right hand only, on a universal hosel that accepts .370 parallel or .355 taper shafts. GolfWorks sells the TS-1 as bare heads or built-to-spec custom sets rather than off the rack, so the lengths below are The GolfWorks' published standard men's steel assembly lengths. Still sold as of 2026, alongside the TS1-IM that joined the line in 2023.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Maltby TS-1 7-iron?

The Maltby TS-1 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees and a lie of 62.5 degrees, with 0.115 inches of offset and a 37-inch length on The GolfWorks' standard steel build.

What is the MPF rating of the Maltby TS-1?

711 at the 6-iron — inside Maltby's Super Game Improvement playability band, which was the point: a compact players profile that still measures highly forgiving on the MPF scale.

Is the Maltby TS-1 forged?

Yes — a true billet-forged three-piece design: 1025C carbon steel body and 2 mm face with an internal tungsten toe weight, welded and injection-filled with polymer behind the face.

How do you buy Maltby TS-1 irons?

From The GolfWorks as bare heads or as custom-built sets to your specs; there is no off-the-rack retail version, and heads accept both .370 parallel and .355 taper shafts.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2019, the Maltby TS-1 is a current players set in the Maltby range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
