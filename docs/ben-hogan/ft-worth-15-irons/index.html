About the Ben Hogan Ft. Worth 15

The Ft. Worth 15 was the first iron released by the relaunched Ben Hogan Golf Equipment Company and is a compact, progressively weighted forged blade. Its defining quirk is that it is not numbered: every head is stamped with its actual loft, and Ben Hogan built heads in every loft from 20 to 47 degrees. A stock set is seven clubs in 4-degree loft gaps, chosen from one of four launch profiles - low 20/24/28/32/36/40/44, mid 21/25/29/33/37/41/45, mid-high 22/26/30/34/38/42/46, or high 23/27/31/35/39/43/47. The rows below use the low-launch profile; length, lie and swing weight are set by the loft band, so the 24-27 degree band is always 38.25 inches and 61 degrees regardless of which profile you order. The set was replaced in the line-up by the Ft. Worth Black and Ft. Worth White irons.

Frequently asked questions

Why are Ben Hogan Ft. Worth 15 irons stamped with a number like 32 instead of a 7-iron?

The relaunched Ben Hogan Golf Equipment Company marked each Ft. Worth 15 head with its actual loft in degrees instead of a club number. Heads were made in every loft from 20 to 47 degrees, and a set is seven of them in 4-degree gaps. A 32-degree Ft. Worth 15 is the club that sits where a 7-iron would in a conventional set.

What is the loft of the middle iron in a Ben Hogan Ft. Worth 15 set?

In the low-launch profile the fourth club of the seven is stamped 32, so its loft is 32 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37.00-inch steel length. The equivalent club is 33 degrees in the mid profile, 34 degrees in the mid-high profile and 35 degrees in the high profile.

What clubs are in a stock Ben Hogan Ft. Worth 15 set?

Seven clubs spanning 20 to 47 degrees in 4-degree gaps, ordered as one of four launch profiles. That is roughly the equivalent of a 4-iron through pitching wedge, though no head carries a club number.

Finding a used set

The Ben Hogan Ft. Worth 15 ran from 2015 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
