About the Ben Hogan Fort Worth MB

The Fort Worth MB is the muscle-back blade half of Ben Hogan Golf's 2025 Fort Worth relaunch, announced 14 January 2025 at the PGA Show alongside the Fort Worth CB, Legend and M50-01. It is forged from 1025C carbon steel with Hogan's Ceramic Matrix Composite (CMC) technology - a fibre-infused ceramic core, lighter than steel, co-forged into each head and precisely positioned to manipulate the center of gravity club by club. It keeps the V-SOLE, with a high-bounce leading edge and a softer, lower-bounce trailing edge. Reviewers (Golf Monthly, GolfWRX) describe it as a traditional, compact muscle-back blade for better players, with no distance-chasing loft jacking: the 7-iron sits at a traditional 34 degrees. It ships 4-PW or 5-PW, hand-built to order in the USA, and can be paired with the Fort Worth CB in a combo set. Ben Hogan's own spec table lists identical length, lie, head weight, blade length and bounce figures for the Fort Worth MB and CB, so the two share the same external head dimensions and differ in their internal cavity/CMC construction rather than their outward geometry.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ben Hogan Fort Worth MB 7-iron?

The Ben Hogan Fort Worth MB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.00 inches, with 0.100 inches of offset.

Is the Ben Hogan Fort Worth MB a blade or a cavity back?

It is a muscle-back blade - the workability-focused half of Hogan's 2025 Fort Worth relaunch, forged from 1025C carbon steel with a co-forged Ceramic Matrix Composite core. Its cavity-back sibling, the Fort Worth CB, launched the same day.

What clubs come in a stock Ben Hogan Fort Worth MB set?

It ships as a 4-PW (7-piece) or 5-PW (6-piece) set, hand-built to order in the USA, priced from about $899.99-$1,049.99 depending on set size and shaft.

When was the Ben Hogan Fort Worth MB released?

It was announced 14 January 2025 at the PGA Show in Orlando, alongside the Fort Worth CB, Legend and M50-01.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Ben Hogan Fort Worth MB is a current blade set in the Ben Hogan range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
