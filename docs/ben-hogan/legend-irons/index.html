About the Ben Hogan Legend

The Legend is Ben Hogan Golf's first hollow-body, foam-filled iron, announced 14 January 2025 alongside the Fort Worth MB, Fort Worth CB and M50-01 at the PGA Show (Golf Brands booth #2301) and engineered with the help of AI-assisted design. A forged face sits on a hollow head injected with a high-performance foam, tuned in mass and density club by club, which Hogan says redistributes discretionary mass for an enlarged "Effective Hitting Area" and better acoustics/feel on off-center hits. It carries the V-SOLE with its high-bounce leading edge and lower-bounce trailing edge. Reviewers (Plugged In Golf, GolfMagic) class it as a players-distance/ game-improvement iron aimed at mid-to-low handicappers, comparing it directly to distance-forgiveness irons like the TaylorMade P790 at roughly two-thirds the price. It is offered as a 4-PW or 5-PW stock set, hand-built to order in Hogan's USA workshop (10-15 working days), with full custom loft/lie/length adjustment available. Neither bounce nor swing weight is published on Hogan's spec chart for this model.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ben Hogan Legend 7-iron?

The Ben Hogan Legend 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37.00 inches, with 0.118 inches of offset.

Is the Ben Hogan Legend a hollow-body iron?

Yes. Ben Hogan describes it as the company's first hollow-body, foam-filled iron head - a forged face over a hollow body injected with a tuned foam fill, intended to redistribute mass for a larger effective hitting area and better feel on mis-hits.

What clubs come in a stock Ben Hogan Legend set?

It is offered as a 4-PW (7-piece) or 5-PW (6-piece) stock set, hand-built to order in Hogan's USA workshop, at $949.99 for the standard build (custom builds from $849.99).

When was the Ben Hogan Legend iron released?

It was announced 14 January 2025 at the PGA Show in Orlando, alongside the Fort Worth MB, Fort Worth CB and M50-01 - the same launch that introduced Ben Hogan's whole 2025 iron lineup.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Ben Hogan Legend is a current players distance set in the Ben Hogan range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
