About the Ben Hogan Radial

The Radial was Hogan's attempt to reach mid-to-high handicap golfers with a forged iron, built around a wide, radiused sole shaped to resist digging and to keep the center of gravity low and back for an easy, forgiving ball flight — "an extremely easy to hit forging" that vintage-club reviewers still rate among the most playable Hogan ever made. This forged 1987 generation followed the original 1983 Radial, which used the same radial-sole concept in a cast head; Hogan collectors catalog the two as distinct, consecutive spec sheets ("1983 Radial Cast" and "1987 Radial Forged"). At roughly $1,000 a set in 1983 dollars, the Radial concept was expensive for the category, and it never dislodged Hogan's reputation as a players-only brand in the mid-handicap market it targeted. Lofts are moderate for a forgiveness-oriented iron of the era (37-degree 7-iron, 50-degree Equalizer), sold as a 2-iron through Equalizer set. Hogan's letter wedge is recorded here as PW (Equalizer, 50°).

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ben Hogan Radial 7-iron?

The Ben Hogan Radial 7-iron has a loft of 37 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch length.

What made the Ben Hogan Radial different from other Hogan irons of its era?

A wide, radiused sole designed to resist digging and keep the center of gravity low and back, aimed at making the club easier to hit for mid-to-high handicap players — a clear departure from Hogan's usual players-first forged blades.

Was the Ben Hogan Radial forged or cast?

Both existed: the original 1983 Radial used a cast head, and this 1987 generation carried the same wide radial-sole concept into a forged head.

What clubs came in a stock Ben Hogan Radial set?

It was sold as a 2-iron through Equalizer (pitching wedge) set on a stock Ben Hogan Apex steel shaft.

Finding a used set

The Ben Hogan Radial ran from 1987 to 1990 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
