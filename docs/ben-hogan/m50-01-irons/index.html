About the Ben Hogan M50-01

The M50-01 is the forged players-distance entry in Ben Hogan Golf's 2025 lineup, announced 14 January 2025 at the PGA Show alongside the Fort Worth MB, Fort Worth CB and Legend. It is a one-piece 1020C carbon steel forging with a CNC-milled face, a compact head and a thin topline, carrying the V-SOLE sole design. Lofts run about two degrees stronger than a traditional set (21-degree 4-iron through 44-degree pitching wedge), and Hogan extends the stock set down to a 48-degree gap wedge. GolfReviewsGuide positions it explicitly as Hogan's player's-distance model - aimed at elite and low-handicap golfers wanting forged feel and workability with a bit more forgiveness and carry than the Fort Worth MB/CB blades or cavity backs, comparable to the earlier PTx Tour. It is offered in 5-PW (6 clubs), 4-PW (7 clubs), 5-GW (7 clubs) or 4-GW (8 clubs), hand-built to order in the USA. Ben Hogan's own spec chart for this model does not publish a length column (unlike the Legend, Fort Worth MB and Fort Worth CB charts released the same day); the lengths below are carried over from that identical 39.00in-35.75in ladder used across Hogan's current lineup, with the gap wedge extrapolated a further quarter inch short of the pitching wedge, so confidence on the length column specifically is lower than on the loft, lie and offset figures Hogan does publish.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ben Hogan M50-01 7-iron?

The Ben Hogan M50-01 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees and a lie of 62 degrees. Ben Hogan does not publish a length figure for the M50-01; the 37.00-inch length recorded here is carried over from the identical length ladder used on the Legend, Fort Worth MB and Fort Worth CB launched alongside it, so treat it as slightly less certain than the loft and lie.

Is the Ben Hogan M50-01 a game-improvement iron?

No - Ben Hogan and reviewers place it in the player's-distance category, aimed at elite and low-handicap golfers who want forged feel and workability with some extra forgiveness and carry, not maximum forgiveness.

What clubs come in a stock Ben Hogan M50-01 set?

It is offered as 5-PW (6 clubs), 4-PW (7 clubs), 5-GW (7 clubs) or 4-GW (8 clubs), hand-built to order in the USA, priced from $799.99 for the 6-piece set up to $899.99 (7-piece 4-PW) and beyond depending on set size and shaft.

When was the Ben Hogan M50-01 released?

It was announced 14 January 2025 at the PGA Show in Orlando, alongside the Legend, Fort Worth MB and Fort Worth CB.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Ben Hogan M50-01 is a current players distance set in the Ben Hogan range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
