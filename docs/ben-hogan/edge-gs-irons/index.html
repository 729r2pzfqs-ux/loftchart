About the Ben Hogan Edge GS

The Edge GS refined Hogan's original 1990 Edge Forged cavity-back with a custom sole grind attributed to Hogan master model-maker Gene Sheely — the "GS" initials — reportedly developed first for Hogan's tour players and its own management group because the standard Edge "did not look too good" at address. It kept the Edge Forged's cavity-back, perimeter-weighted construction while refining the grind and profile, and was sold in configurations spanning 2-iron through Equalizer and sand wedge. Vintage listings confirm a 36-degree 7-iron loft; the remaining loft, lie and length ladder below is not from a located Edge GS-specific factory sheet but from Hogan's contemporaneous "1999 Apex" factory table, which shares the identical 36-degree 7-iron and the same lie/length progression Hogan used across its forged iron lines of this era — recorded here as the closest documented match rather than an Edge GS-specific figure, hence the low confidence.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ben Hogan Edge GS 7-iron?

The Ben Hogan Edge GS 7-iron has a loft of 36 degrees, confirmed from vintage club listings; the surrounding lie and length figures are drawn from Hogan's closest documented same-era table rather than an Edge GS-specific sheet.

What does GS stand for on the Ben Hogan Edge GS?

Gene Sheely, the Hogan master model-maker credited with the custom sole grind that refined the original 1990 Edge Forged into the Edge GS in 1991.

Why was the Ben Hogan Edge GS created?

According to MyGolfSpy's history of the line, it began as a custom grind Gene Sheely built for Hogan's own tour players and management group because the standard Edge Forged did not look as good at address; it was then adopted into the regular line-up.

What replaced the Ben Hogan Edge GS?

The Edge GCD, which capitalized on the oversized-iron trend when it arrived in 1994-95.

Finding a used set

The Ben Hogan Edge GS ran from 1991 to 1995 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
