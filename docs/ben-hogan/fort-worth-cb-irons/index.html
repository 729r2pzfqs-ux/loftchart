About the Ben Hogan Fort Worth CB

The Fort Worth CB is the cavity-back half of Ben Hogan Golf's 2025 Fort Worth relaunch, announced 14 January 2025 at the PGA Show alongside the Fort Worth MB, Legend and M50-01. Like the MB it is forged from 1025C carbon steel around a co-forged Ceramic Matrix Composite (CMC) core that lets Hogan manipulate each club's center of gravity, but the CB's open cavity pushes more of that mass to the perimeter for extra forgiveness on top of the MB's workability. It carries the same V-SOLE, with a high-bounce leading edge and softer, lower-bounce trailing edge. GolfMagic and GolfWRX describe it as a players/better-player cavity back that blends player and game-improvement characteristics for single-digit and low mid-handicap ball-strikers, and note it can be paired with the Fort Worth MB in a combo set. It ships 4-PW or 5-PW, hand-built to order in the USA. Ben Hogan's own spec table lists identical length, lie, offset, head weight, blade length and bounce figures for the Fort Worth CB and MB, so the two share the same external head dimensions and differ in their internal cavity/CMC construction rather than their outward geometry.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ben Hogan Fort Worth CB 7-iron?

The Ben Hogan Fort Worth CB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.00 inches, with 0.100 inches of offset.

What is the difference between the Ben Hogan Fort Worth CB and Fort Worth MB?

Internal construction, not external shape. Both are forged from 1025C carbon steel with a co-forged Ceramic Matrix Composite core and share identical length, lie, offset and bounce figures on Hogan's own spec chart. The CB opens an internal cavity to push weight to the perimeter for more forgiveness, while the MB is a solid muscle-back build aimed at workability.

What clubs come in a stock Ben Hogan Fort Worth CB set?

It ships as a 4-PW (7-piece) or 5-PW (6-piece) set, hand-built to order in the USA, priced from roughly $900 (5-PW) up to $1,049.99 depending on set size and shaft.

When was the Ben Hogan Fort Worth CB released?

It was announced 14 January 2025 at the PGA Show in Orlando, alongside the Fort Worth MB, Legend and M50-01.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Ben Hogan Fort Worth CB is a current players set in the Ben Hogan range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
