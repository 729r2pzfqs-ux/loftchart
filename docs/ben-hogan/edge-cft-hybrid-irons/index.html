About the Ben Hogan Edge CFT Hybrid

The Edge C•F•T Hybrid "h" Iron Set — the gap-analysis brief's "Apex Edge CFT" refers to this 2004 line, which built on the 2003 Edge CFT by adding true hybrid heads — pairs Hogan's Compression Forged Titanium face technology (a titanium face compression-forged onto a 17-4 stainless steel body for perimeter and sole weighting) with 3h and 4h hybrid heads that replace the standard 3- and 4-irons for easier long-iron shotmaking. A low, deep center of gravity from the two-piece, undercut-cavity construction is pitched for a higher launch angle and higher moment of inertia — "higher, longer and more accurate ball flight." Every club from 1 through 5 was also offered as a conventional iron head alongside its "h" hybrid counterpart, so the loft/lie/length ladder below uses the standard iron heads for 1-9 plus the wedges. Hogan's letter wedges are recorded here as PW (Equalizer, 46°), GW (F wedge, 50°), SW (56°) and LW (60°). It was sold right hand 1-E/F/S/L and left hand 2-E/S, at $825 (steel) or $900 (graphite).

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ben Hogan Edge CFT Hybrid 7-iron?

The Ben Hogan Edge CFT Hybrid 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch length.

What is Compression Forged Titanium on the Edge CFT Hybrid?

A titanium face compression-forged onto a 17-4 stainless steel body, with an undercut cavity that shifts weight to the perimeter and sole for a lower, deeper center of gravity and higher launch.

Which clubs in the Edge CFT Hybrid set are hybrids instead of irons?

The 3-iron and 4-iron were replaced by 3h and 4h hybrid heads in the set Hogan positioned as its improved long-iron option, though conventional 3- and 4-iron heads (and 1h, 2h and 5h hybrids) were also available individually.

What clubs came in a stock Ben Hogan Edge CFT Hybrid set?

It was offered right hand from 1-iron through Equalizer, F wedge, SW and LW, and left hand from 2-iron through Equalizer and SW, at $825 in steel or $900 in graphite.

Finding a used set

The Ben Hogan Edge CFT Hybrid ran from 2004 to 2006 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
