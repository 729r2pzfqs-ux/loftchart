About the Ping G740

The G740 is Ping's super-game-improvement iron, announced on 10 March 2026 as the replacement for the G730. It is a perimeter-weighted cavity-back head in heat-treated 17-4 stainless steel with a wider dual-camber sole, a longer blade, a thicker topline and a PurFlex cavity badge with three flex zones. The standard lofts are almost unchanged from the G730 — 28 degrees at the 7-iron and 40 at the pitching wedge, with the 5-iron half a degree stronger at 21 — while the published standard lies are about one degree more upright through the set. The set runs 5-iron to pitching wedge with a 45-degree utility wedge and 50- and 56-degree wedges. Ping also publishes Power Spec and Retro Spec loft options; the figures below are the standard lofts only, with lies for the black (standard) colour code.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping G740 7-iron?

The Ping G740 7-iron has a standard loft of 28 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 63.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.0 inches. Ping's optional Power Spec and Retro Spec builds are 26.5 and 30.0 degrees.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Ping G740?

40 degrees in the standard loft, followed by a 45-degree utility wedge and 50- and 56-degree wedges.

How do the Ping G740 lofts compare with the G730?

They are nearly identical. The 6-iron through 56-degree wedge have the same standard lofts as the G730, and the 5-iron is half a degree stronger at 21 degrees. The published standard lies are about one degree more upright, 63.0 degrees at the 7-iron against 62.0.

When were Ping G740 irons released?

Ping announced the G740 on 10 March 2026, with custom fittings and pre-orders opening the same day.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Ping G740 is a current super game improvement set in the Ping range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Ping G730. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
