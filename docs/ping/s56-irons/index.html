About the Ping S56

The S56 carried over the S57's lofts, lies, lengths and offset unchanged, with the 7-iron at 34.25 degrees and 0.09 inches of offset. It is a cast players iron with a tungsten toe weight, running 2-iron through pitching wedge and holding a D1 swing weight through the irons. Ping's colour-code (dot) system sets lie angle relative to standard: gold 4 flat, brown 3 flat, orange 2 flat, red 1 flat, BLACK standard, blue 1 upright, green 2 upright, white 3 upright, silver 4 upright, maroon 5 upright. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping S56 7-iron?

The Ping S56 7-iron has a loft of 34.25 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 62.25 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

When were Ping S56 irons made?

The S56 was introduced in 2010, following the S57, and was later replaced by the S55.

What is the difference between the Ping S57 and S56?

Lofts, lies, lengths and offset are identical through the set. The published charts differ in which columns they record — bounce for the S57, swing weight for the S56.

Finding a used set

The Ping S56 ran from 2010 to 2013 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
