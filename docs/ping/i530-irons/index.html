About the Ping i530

The i530 is Ping's players-distance iron in a blade-style package: a hollow body with a forged maraging steel face, internal tungsten toe and shaft-tip weights and a shorter blade than the i525 it replaced. Lofts are strong for the shape — the 7-iron is 29 degrees, four stronger than the i230 — but the set carries far more bounce than a players iron, up to 13 degrees in the wedges. Ping's colour-code (dot) system sets lie angle relative to standard: gold 4 flat, brown 3 flat, orange 2 flat, red 1 flat, BLACK standard, blue 1 upright, green 2 upright, white 3 upright, silver 4 upright, maroon 5 upright. The lies below are the BLACK (standard) lies.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping i530 7-iron?

The Ping i530 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.0 inches.

When were Ping i530 irons released?

Ping introduced them in March 2024, replacing the i525.

How much stronger is the Ping i530 than the i230?

Four degrees at the 7-iron — 29 against 33 — and three degrees at the pitching wedge, 42 against 45.

Finding a used set

The Ping i530 ran from 2024 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
