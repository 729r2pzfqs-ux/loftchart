About the Ping i10

The i10 replaced the i5 as Ping's cast players iron, cutting offset again and narrowing the sole relative to the G10 of the same generation. It keeps the i5's loft chart exactly — 21 degrees at the 3-iron, 34 at the 7-iron — but moves to the flatter, shorter-hosel geometry Ping then carried through the i15 and i20, so the lies are a straight half-degree progression from 59.25. Lies below are the black (standard) colour code.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping i10 7-iron?

The Ping i10 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 62.25 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

When were Ping i10 irons made?

The i10 was introduced in 2007 and was replaced by the i15 in 2009.

Are Ping i10 and i5 lofts the same?

Yes. Both run 21, 24, 27, 30.5, 34, 38, 42 and 46 degrees from 3-iron to pitching wedge. The i10 differs in offset — about 0.05 inches less at the 3-iron — and in its lie progression.

How much offset does a Ping i10 have?

Offset runs from 0.19 inches at the 3-iron down to 0.05 inches at the pitching wedge, roughly 0.05 inches less than the i5 through the long irons.

Finding a used set

The Ping i10 ran from 2007 to 2009 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
