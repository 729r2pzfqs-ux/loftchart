About the Ping G Le4

The G Le4 is the fourth generation of Ping's club line engineered specifically for women, announced on 10 March 2026 as the replacement for the G Le3. The irons are lightweight, perimeter-weighted stainless steel heads with a thin face, high-density tip and toe weights and a PurFlex cavity badge, sold as an iron/hybrid combination: the irons run 6-iron through sand wedge, with G Le4 hybrids of 22, 26.5, 31 and 36 degrees (5H to 8H) available above and alongside them. Lofts, lies and lengths are close to the G Le3 — 29.5 degrees at the 7-iron — with the pitching wedge half a degree weaker at 46.5 and the utility wedge half a degree stronger at 51.5. The specs below are the women's standard graphite build with Ping's ALTA Le i shaft; no steel shaft is offered. Ping also publishes Power Spec and Retro Spec loft options; the figures below are the standard lofts only, with lies for the black (standard) colour code.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping G Le4 7-iron?

The Ping G Le4 7-iron has a standard loft of 29.5 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 62.0 degrees and a graphite-shaft length of 36.5 inches.

What clubs are in the Ping G Le4 iron set?

The irons run from a 25-degree 6-iron to a 41-degree 9-iron, with a 46.5-degree pitching wedge, a 51.5-degree utility wedge and a 56-degree sand wedge. Matching G Le4 hybrids of 22, 26.5, 31 and 36 degrees (5H to 8H) fill the longer slots.

How do the Ping G Le4 lofts differ from the G Le3?

The 6-iron to 9-iron and the sand wedge keep the same standard lofts. The pitching wedge is half a degree weaker at 46.5 degrees and the utility wedge half a degree stronger at 51.5, which evens the gaps between the 9-iron and the sand wedge.

When were Ping G Le4 irons released?

Ping announced the G Le4 family on 10 March 2026, with custom fittings and pre-orders opening the same day.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Ping G Le4 is a current super game improvement set in the Ping range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Ping G Le3. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
