About the Ping i540

The i540 is Ping's players-distance iron, launched on 10 March 2026 as the replacement for the i530. It keeps the hollow-body, blade-style formula but the forged variable-thickness maraging steel face is about 9 percent thinner than the i530's, and tungsten weights sit along the sole of the 4- through 7-irons to lower the centre of gravity. Two additions are new to the model: Ping's patent-pending inR-Air technology, an internal air chamber that tunes frequency for better sound and feel in a hollow head, and an i-Beam brace across the cavity for structural support. Lofts, lies, bounce and swing weights carry over from the i530 unchanged — a 29-degree 7-iron and 42-degree pitching wedge — though the published lengths of the 4- through 6-irons run about a quarter to three-eighths of an inch longer, and Power Spec and Retro Spec loft options are offered. Ping's colour-code (dot) system sets lie angle relative to standard; the lies below are the BLACK (standard) lies quoted at standard length.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping i540 7-iron?

The Ping i540 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.0 inches — the same as the i530 it replaced.

When were Ping i540 irons released?

Ping launched the i540 on 10 March 2026 as the successor to the i530.

What is the difference between the Ping i540 and the i530?

The lofts, lies, bounce and swing weights are unchanged. The i540's forged maraging steel face is about 9 percent thinner, tungsten weights move along the sole of the 4-7 irons, and the head adds Ping's inR-Air air-chamber technology and an i-Beam cavity brace for sound and feel.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Ping i540 is a current players distance set in the Ping range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Ping i530. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
