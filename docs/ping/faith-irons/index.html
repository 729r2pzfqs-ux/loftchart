About the Ping Faith

The Faith was Ping's women's line of the early 2010s, replacing the 2007 Rhapsody. The irons carry extra toe weighting and a Custom Tuning Port to drive the centre of gravity low for a high launch at slower swing speeds, and the stock set replaces the long and mid irons entirely: Faith hybrids at 26 and 30 degrees cover the 5H and 6H slots, and the irons run only from the 32 degree 7-iron to a 54 degree sand wedge. Lengths are women's standard, about an inch under men's, with the whole family shafted in Ping's ULT 200 ladies graphite. The Serene replaced it in 2013.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping Faith 7-iron?

The Ping Faith 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a 62.25 degree lie and a 36.25 inch women's standard graphite length.

Why does the Ping Faith set start at the 7-iron?

The Faith was sold as a hybrid-iron combo for moderate swing speeds: Faith hybrids at 26 and 30 degrees take the 5H and 6H slots, and the irons run from the 7-iron down to a 54 degree sand wedge.

When were Ping Faith irons made?

The Faith line launched in 2011 as the successor to the 2007 Rhapsody and was replaced by the Serene in 2013.

Finding a used set

The Ping Faith ran from 2011 to 2013 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
