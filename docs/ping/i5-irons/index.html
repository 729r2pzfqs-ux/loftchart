About the Ping i5

The i5 was Ping's mid-size cast players iron of the mid-2000s, sold as the smaller, lower-offset counterpart to the G5. The head is a shorter blade with a thinner top line and narrower sole than the G-series equivalent, and offset runs about 0.03 inches tighter club for club. The set was catalogued all the way from a 2-iron to a lob wedge. Ping's colour-code (dot) system sets lie angle relative to standard, and the lies below are the black (standard) lies.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping i5 7-iron?

The Ping i5 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 62.28 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

When were Ping i5 irons made?

The i5 was introduced in 2005 and was replaced by the i10 in 2007.

What is the difference between the Ping i5 and the G5?

The i5 is the smaller head of the pair — shorter blade, thinner top line, narrower sole and roughly 0.03 inches less offset per club than the G5, which is the game-improvement model of the same generation.

How many clubs were in a Ping i5 set?

The i5 was catalogued from a 2-iron through to a lob wedge — 2 to 9, pitching wedge, utility wedge, sand wedge and lob wedge — though most retail sets were built 3-PW or 4-PW.

Finding a used set

The Ping i5 ran from 2005 to 2007 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
