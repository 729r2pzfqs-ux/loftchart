About the Ping G Le2

The G Le2 is the second generation of Ping's line engineered specifically for women, launched on 2 July 2019. The irons use Ping's COR-Eye face technology with a deep top-rail undercut for more face flexing, a tungsten toe weight that lifts MOI about ten percent over the original G Le, and a lightweight overall build with C-range swing weights. The iron set runs 6-iron through sand wedge, with matching G Le2 hybrids covering the 4H to 7H slots above it. Specs below are the women's standard graphite build with the ULT 240 i shaft; Ping's colour-code system sets lie relative to the standard lies listed. The G Le3 replaced it in 2023.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping G Le2 7-iron?

The Ping G Le2 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 36.5 inches in the women's standard graphite build.

What clubs came in the Ping G Le2 iron set?

The iron set ran 6-iron through sand wedge — 6-9, PW (45 degrees), UW (52 degrees) and SW (58 degrees) — with G Le2 hybrids covering the 4H to 7H slots above it.

When were the Ping G Le2 irons made?

They launched on 2 July 2019 as the second generation of Ping's women's line and were replaced by the G Le3 in July 2023.

Finding a used set

The Ping G Le2 ran from 2019 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
