About the Ping Rhapsody

The 2007 Rhapsody launched Ping's modern line of dedicated women's clubs, a complete driver-through-putter family whose irons are deep cavity backs built light and upright for slower swing speeds. The set blends hybrids into the top end, with matching Rhapsody hybrids replacing the long irons above the 5-iron, and the irons run generous offset - .28 inches at the 5-iron - with a 34 degree 7-iron and 46 degree pitching wedge on women's standard lengths about an inch under men's. Specs below are the women's standard build with the stock ULT 129i graphite. Ping revived the Rhapsody name for an unrelated second-generation set in 2015; this entry is the original. The Faith replaced it in 2011.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping Rhapsody 7-iron?

The 2007 Ping Rhapsody 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a 62.25 degree lie and a 36.25 inch women's standard graphite length.

Are Ping Rhapsody irons for women?

Yes. The Rhapsody was Ping's dedicated women's line, launched in 2007 as a complete set - driver, fairways, hybrids, irons and putter - built light and upright for slower swing speeds.

Are there two generations of Ping Rhapsody irons?

Yes. The original Rhapsody launched in 2007; Ping reused the name in 2015 for a different second-generation women's set with 24 to 58 degree lofts and ULT 220 shafts. This page covers the 2007 original.

Finding a used set

The Ping Rhapsody ran from 2007 to 2011 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
