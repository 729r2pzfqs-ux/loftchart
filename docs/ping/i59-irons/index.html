About the Ping i59

The i59 is Ping's compact forged players iron, built from a 1025 carbon steel body with a laser-cut 17-4 stainless face and a light aluminium insert filling the cavity, which frees mass for tungsten toe weighting without growing the head. Lofts are traditional — the 7-iron is 34 degrees, a degree weaker than the i230 of the same period — and offset runs tighter than the cast i-series at every club. Ping's colour-code (dot) system sets lie angle relative to standard: gold 4 flat, brown 3 flat, orange 2 flat, red 1 flat, BLACK standard, blue 1 upright, green 2 upright, white 3 upright, silver 4 upright, maroon 5 upright. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping i59 7-iron?

The Ping i59 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.0 inches.

When were Ping i59 irons made?

Ping announced the i59 on 24 August 2021 and it reached retail on 2 September 2021, replacing the iBlade.

How do Ping i59 lofts compare with the i230?

The i59 is a degree weaker at the 7-iron, 34 against 33, and carries about a hundredth of an inch less offset at each club.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2021, the Ping i59 is a current players set in the Ping range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
