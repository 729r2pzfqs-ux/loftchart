About the Ping G710

The G710 is the second generation of Ping's hollow-body distance iron and carries the G700's loft, lie, length and offset chart over unchanged — a 29.5-degree 7-iron at 37.00 inches. What changed is the mass: tungsten toe and hosel weights push the moment of inertia about 5 per cent above the G700, the maraging face is machined rather than only plasma-welded, and the body gets a hydropearl chrome finish. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Ping G710 7-iron?

The Ping G710 7-iron has a loft of 29.5 degrees, a standard (black dot) lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When were Ping G710 irons made?

The G710 arrived in 2020, following the G700, and was replaced by the G730 in 2024.

Are Ping G700 and G710 lofts the same?

Yes. Both run 20, 23, 26, 29.5, 34, 39, 44, 49 and 54 degrees from 4-iron to sand wedge, at the same lies, lengths and offsets. The G710's gains come from tungsten toe and hosel weighting and a machined face, not from stronger lofts.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Ping G710?

44 degrees, with a 49-degree utility wedge and a 54-degree sand wedge completing the set.

Finding a used set

The Ping G710 ran from 2020 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
