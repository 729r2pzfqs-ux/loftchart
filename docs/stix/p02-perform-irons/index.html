About the Stix P02 Perform

The P02 Perform is the second generation of the iron in Stix Golf's direct-to-consumer Perform sets, launched in January 2026. The deep cavity back head moves from 431 to 17-4 stainless steel, with a larger cavity, a 2.5 mm face and a centre of gravity Stix says is lower and deeper than before. Lofts are weaker than the first Perform through most of the set - a 30-degree 7-iron against 28 - while the pitching wedge moves from 45 to 46 degrees. Sold 5-iron through pitching wedge with 120-gram steel shafts in regular and stiff flex. Stix does not publish offset or bounce for this model, so those keys are omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Stix P02 Perform 7-iron?

The Stix P02 Perform 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch length.

How do the P02 Perform lofts compare with the original Stix Perform?

They are weaker through most of the set. The 5-iron goes from 22 to 23 degrees, the 6-iron from 25 to 26, the 7-iron from 28 to 30, the 8-iron from 33 to 34, the 9-iron from 39 to 40 and the pitching wedge from 45 to 46. Lies and lengths are unchanged.

What are Stix P02 Perform irons made of?

They are 17-4 stainless steel deep cavity backs. The first-generation Perform used 431 stainless steel.

What clubs come in a Stix P02 Perform iron set?

The set is six clubs, 5-iron through a 46-degree pitching wedge, with 120-gram steel shafts in regular or stiff flex.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Stix P02 Perform is a current game improvement set in the Stix range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Stix Perform. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
