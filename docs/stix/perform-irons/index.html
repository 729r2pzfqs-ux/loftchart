About the Stix Perform

The Perform (retroactively badged P01 when the P02 arrived) is the iron at the heart of Stix Golf's direct-to-consumer sets - an all-black, minimalist-looking 431 stainless deep cavity back sold 5-iron through pitching wedge. The heads behind the Perform branding were on sale in Stix's 2021 sets with the same lofts and lies, and Stix itself dates the model to 2021. Lofts sit in classic game-improvement territory with a 28-degree 7-iron but a 45-degree pitching wedge, leaving a wide 39-to-45 gap-friendly wedge flow, and offset is generous throughout. Stock builds use 120-gram black steel shafts (graphite optional) at a D1 swing weight across the set; offsets below are converted from Stix's published millimetre figures.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Stix Perform 7-iron?

The Stix Perform 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch length.

Are Stix Perform irons forged?

No. They are cast 431 stainless steel deep cavity backs - the design puts forgiveness and price ahead of forged feel.

What clubs come in a Stix Perform iron set?

The stock set is six clubs, 5-iron through 45-degree pitching wedge, sold with 120-gram black steel shafts or optional graphite at a D1 swing weight.

Finding a used set

The Stix Perform ran from 2021 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
