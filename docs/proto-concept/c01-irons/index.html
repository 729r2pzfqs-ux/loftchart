About the Proto-Concept C01

The C01 was the muscle back in PROTOCONCEPT's original C-series - the premium Japanese brand established in 2019 as a collaboration between the Endo forging house and the Golf Partner retail group, with its first irons reaching the market in 2020. Its signature technology is a roughly 25-gram pure titanium round rod impregnated into the S20C billet before forging in the 5- through 7-irons; the titanium is about half the weight of the steel it displaces, deepening the centre of gravity to give the blade profile something closer to cavity-back launch, while the 8-iron to pitching wedge are conventional S25C forgings. Heads are sold component-style, 5-PW, and assembled to order in Japan or the USA. Lengths below are the manufacturer's recommended steel assembly lengths; PROTOCONCEPT publishes face progression rather than offset, so that key is omitted. The C01 TBic, which replaced the titanium rod with an inner ceramic, superseded it in July 2023.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Proto-Concept C01 7-iron?

The Proto-Concept C01 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 37-inch recommended steel length.

What is special about the Proto-Concept C01?

The 5-, 6- and 7-irons are forged with a pure titanium round rod inside the S20C billet - roughly 25 grams of titanium that deepens the centre of gravity. At the time it was the only iron on the market with titanium forged inside the head.

Who makes Proto-Concept clubs?

PROTOCONCEPT is a Tokyo-based premium brand created in 2019 by the Endo forging house together with the Golf Partner retail group; heads are forged to Endo's spec and clubs are assembled to order in Japan or the USA.

Finding a used set

The Proto-Concept C01 ran from 2020 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
