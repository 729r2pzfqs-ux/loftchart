About the Proto-Concept C03 TC

The C03 TC ("Tour Cavity") is a players iron in PROTOCONCEPT's C-series, sitting between the muscle-back C01 and the pocket-cavity C05 - a soft-iron forging with a CNC-milled undercut back cavity on the #4 through #7 irons only, so the long irons get extra forgiveness while the short irons keep a higher, more compact centre of gravity for a controlled, flatter flight with more spin. It was announced 19 May 2023, not 2022 as sometimes listed - PROTOCONCEPT's own first-look coverage and MyGolfSpy's review both date the launch to spring 2023. Heads are sold component-style, 4-PW, and assembled to order in Japan or the USA. Lengths below are the manufacturer's recommended steel assembly lengths; PROTOCONCEPT publishes face progression rather than offset, so that key is omitted, consistent with the rest of the C-series in this dataset.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Proto-Concept C03 TC 7-iron?

The Proto-Concept C03 TC 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees, and a recommended steel length of 37 inches.

When was the Proto-Concept C03 TC released?

It was announced on 19 May 2023 - not 2022, which some listings imply by association with PROTOCONCEPT's earlier Golf Digest Hot List recognition.

What does 'TC' mean on the Proto-Concept C03 TC?

Tour Cavity. A CNC-milled undercut is machined into the back of the #4 through #7 heads only, giving the long irons extra forgiveness while the shorter irons keep a higher, more controlled centre of gravity.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Proto-Concept C03 TC is a current players set in the Proto-Concept range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
