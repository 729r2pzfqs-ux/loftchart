About the Mizuno MP-67

The MP-67 is a Grain Flow Forged split muscle-back blade sold in a 3-iron through pitching wedge range. It reverted to the traditional loft ladder of the MP-33 rather than the stronger MP-37 numbers, with a 36-degree 7-iron, a 48-degree pitching wedge and a consistent 4-degree gap from the 6-iron down. Lie angles run from 59.5 degrees in the 3-iron to 63 degrees at the wedge. Lengths and lofts listed are the standard men's steel-shaft specification.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno MP-67 7-iron?

The Mizuno MP-67 7-iron has a loft of 36 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length.

Why are the Mizuno MP-67 lofts weaker than the MP-68?

The MP-67 uses the traditional MP-33 loft ladder, one degree weaker than the MP-68 through the set: 36 degrees at the 7-iron and 48 at the pitching wedge, against 35 and 47.

What clubs came in the Mizuno MP-67 set?

The published stock range is a 3-iron through pitching wedge. A 2-iron was catalogued at 19 degrees but its standard length was not published.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno MP-67 ran from 2007 to 2009 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
