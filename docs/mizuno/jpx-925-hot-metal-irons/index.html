About the Mizuno JPX-925 Hot Metal

JPX 925 Hot Metal is Mizuno's mainstream game-improvement iron, redesigned for the generation with tungsten weighting through the 4 to 7 irons to raise launch where the loft is lowest. Lofts are strong — 28 degrees at the 7-iron, six stronger than the JPX 923 Tour — with a long 4-iron at 19 degrees, and offset is the widest in Mizuno's range at 0.224 inches at the top of the set. The set runs to a 54-degree sand wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX 925 Hot Metal 7-iron?

The Mizuno JPX 925 Hot Metal 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

When were Mizuno JPX 925 irons released?

Mizuno launched the JPX 925 Hot Metal line in September 2024; most retailers catalogue it as the 2025 range.

How strong are JPX 925 Hot Metal lofts?

Strong. The 28-degree 7-iron is five degrees stronger than the JPX 925 Forged and six stronger than the JPX 923 Tour.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Mizuno JPX-925 Hot Metal is a current game improvement set in the Mizuno range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the JPX-923 Hot Metal. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
