About the Mizuno Pro 245

The Mizuno Pro 245 is the players-distance model of the 2024 Mizuno Pro series, replacing the Pro 225. The 2-iron through 8-iron are hollow bodied with a Grain Flow Forged 4135 Chromoly face and neck, a laser-welded 431 stainless back piece and a 46-gram internally suspended tungsten weight in the 2-7 irons, while the 9-iron through gap wedge use a partial hollow 1025E Pure Select body with a 17-4 back piece. The 7-iron is 30 degrees, unchanged from the Pro 225, but the 9-iron, pitching wedge and gap wedge were each strengthened by a degree. The stock set is 2-GW in right hand and 4-GW in left. Lofts, lies and lengths listed are the men's standard steel-shaft build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno Pro 245 7-iron?

The Mizuno Pro 245 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Mizuno Pro 245?

The Mizuno Pro 245 pitching wedge is 43 degrees and the matching gap wedge is 48 degrees, each a degree stronger than the Pro 225.

Are the Mizuno Pro 245 irons hollow?

The 2-iron through 8-iron are hollow bodied with a forged 4135 Chromoly face and neck and an internal tungsten weight in the 2-7 irons. The 9-iron through gap wedge use a partial hollow 1025E mild carbon steel body.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno Pro 245 ran from 2023 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
