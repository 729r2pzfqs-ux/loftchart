About the Mizuno ST-MAX 230

The ST-MAX 230 hybrids launched in early 2024 with the ST-MAX 230 woods — one high-MOI head in four lofts from a 19° 3-hybrid to a 28° 6-hybrid, each carrying Mizuno's Quick Switch adjustable hosel with a 4° window. Head sizes run 122 cc down to 109 cc. The JPX One hybrids replaced the line in 2026.

Head variants

The heads this family is sold in, with each one’s stock loft options.

ST-MAX 23019°, 22°, 25°, 28°
The single hybrid head in four lofts; the 19° 3HB and 28° 6HB are right-hand only, the 22° and 25° heads come in both hands.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Mizuno ST-MAX 230 hybrid come in?

Four lofts: 19, 22, 25 and 28 degrees. The 19 and 28 degree heads are right-hand only.

Is the Mizuno ST-MAX 230 hybrid adjustable?

Yes — every loft uses Mizuno's Quick Switch hosel with a 4 degree window, so the 19 degree head plays from 17 to 21 degrees. Mizuno publishes the ranges rather than a position-by-position chart.

What replaced the Mizuno ST-MAX 230 hybrid?

The Mizuno JPX One hybrids, launched for 2026.

Buying one

Mizuno has since introduced the Mizuno JPX One above it in the range, so the ST-MAX 230 is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and hybrids one generation back are the sweet spot of the used market. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
