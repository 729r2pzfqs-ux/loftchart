About the Mizuno JPX-921 Tour

The JPX 921 Tour is the compact players model of the JPX 921 family, Grain Flow Forged HD from 1025E Pure Select mild carbon steel in Hiroshima. It keeps the Stability Frame with toe-bias weighting introduced in the JPX Tour line, adds a thicker cavity pad behind impact for feel, narrows the soles of the short irons and wears an anti-glare pearl brush finish. Lofts are traditional — 34 degrees at the 7-iron, 46 at the pitching wedge — with a tight offset progression from 0.126 inches at the 4-iron to 0.098 at the gap wedge. The stock set is 4-GW: Mizuno's 2019 tour audit found not a single JPX 919 3-iron in play, so the 3-iron was dropped, and left-handers were served 6-GW through the JPX 921 SEL programme. Mizuno announced the JPX 921 line on 10 August 2020 with retail from 17 September 2020, and the successor JPX 923 family was unveiled in September 2022.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX 921 Tour 7-iron?

The Mizuno JPX 921 Tour 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length.

Why does the Mizuno JPX 921 Tour set start at the 4-iron?

Mizuno's 2019 tour audit found not a single JPX 919 Tour 3-iron in play, so the JPX 921 Tour was offered 4-iron through gap wedge, with the MP-20 HMB suggested to fill the 3-iron slot.

Are the Mizuno JPX 921 Tour irons forged?

Yes. They are Grain Flow Forged HD from 1025E Pure Select mild carbon steel at Mizuno's Hiroshima facility.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno JPX-921 Tour ran from 2020 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
