About the Mizuno Pro M-13

The Mizuno Pro M-13 is the players cavity-back of Mizuno's 2026 Pro line, the successor to the Pro 243 and one of two "Modern Series" models launched alongside the more forgiving M-15. The set splits into three constructions: the 4 and 5 irons are pocket-cavity Grain Flow Forged with a 4120 Chromoly face and a 431 stainless back piece, the 6 through 8 irons are micro-slot Grain Flow Forged with a face 0.3 mm thinner than the prior generation, and the 9-iron through gap wedge are one-piece Grain Flow Forged 1025E. The Contour Ellipse face is 1.37 mm at its thinnest, which Mizuno says is about 35 percent thinner than the Pro 243. Lofts carry over from the Pro 243 unchanged — 32 degrees at the 7-iron, 44 at the pitching wedge — but the published lie angles sit half a degree to a degree more upright and the lengths a quarter inch longer than the 243 table, matching Mizuno's JPX-style build specs. Mizuno unveiled the Modern Series on 5 January 2026, with presale from 19 January and in-store availability on 5 February 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno Pro M-13 7-iron?

The Mizuno Pro M-13 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

When were the Mizuno Pro M-13 irons released?

Mizuno announced the Pro Modern Series, including the M-13, on 5 January 2026, with presale from 19 January and in-store availability on 5 February 2026.

Are the Mizuno Pro M-13 lofts different from the Pro 243?

No. The lofts carry over unchanged from the Pro 243 — 22 degrees at the 4-iron, 32 at the 7-iron, 44 at the pitching wedge and 48 at the gap wedge — though the published lies are slightly more upright and lengths a quarter inch longer.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Mizuno Pro M-13 is a current players set in the Mizuno range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Pro 243. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
