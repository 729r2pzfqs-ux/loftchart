About the Mizuno Pro T-3

The Pro T-3 is the cavity-back half of Mizuno's 2025 wedge pair, the successor to the S23, launched alongside the Pro T-1 in February 2025. It shares the T-1's Grain Flow Forged HD 1025E construction, copper underlay and loft-specific Quad Cut+ grooves, but adds a shallow cavity back and a slightly longer blade for stability and forgiveness on off-centre strikes. The matrix runs 11 loft/bounce/grind combinations from 46 to 60 degrees across three grinds. Finishes are Soft White Satin and Black Ion. Club lengths are omitted here because US and UK retailer charts disagree on the measuring basis.

Grind options

The sole grinds this model is sold in, with the lofts each one is available at.

S-Type Grind46°, 48°, 50°, 52°, 54°
High bounce with limited relief — heel relief and a bevelled trailing edge — for full shots. The grind at the strong lofts from 46 to 54 degrees.
M-Type Grind54°, 56°, 58°
Mid bounce with moderate heel and toe relief for gentle face manipulation, replacing the old D grind.
C-Type Grind56°, 58°, 60°
Generous heel, toe and trailing-edge relief for a wide range of players and conditions — the T-3's highest-loft grind.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Mizuno Pro T-3 come in?

The Pro T-3 is sold from 46 to 60 degrees — 11 loft, bounce and grind combinations across the S, M and C grinds.

What is the difference between the Mizuno Pro T-1 and Pro T-3?

Both are Grain Flow Forged HD from 1025E steel, but the T-3 adds a shallow cavity back and a slightly longer blade for forgiveness, and trims the grind matrix to three grinds against the T-1's six.

What grinds does the Mizuno Pro T-3 offer?

Three: S-Type at the strong lofts (46-54 degrees), M-Type mid bounce (54-58) and C-Type with generous relief (56-60).

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Mizuno Pro T-3 is a current tour wedge in the Mizuno range as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Mizuno S23. New wedges are built to the loft, bounce and grind options in the chart above; a fitting can bend loft and lie from standard, so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Check the loft and bounce against the chart above before buying used — wedges are routinely bent from standard, and worn grooves cost spin long before a wedge looks worn.
