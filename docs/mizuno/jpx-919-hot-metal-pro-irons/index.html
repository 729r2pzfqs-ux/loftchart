About the Mizuno JPX-919 Hot Metal Pro

The JPX-919 Hot Metal Pro was the first Hot Metal Pro model, added to the JPX-919 family in early 2019, several months after the Tour, Forged and standard Hot Metal launched in September 2018. It packs the standard Hot Metal's cast Chromoly 4140M head and identical lofts, lies and lengths — a 30-degree 7-iron and 45-degree pitching wedge — into a smaller profile with a slimmer topline, narrower sole and offset reduced to roughly the JPX-919 Tour's numbers, 0.112 inches at the 7-iron against the standard model's 0.179. The US stock set ran 4-iron through gap wedge. Lofts, lies and lengths listed are the men's standard steel-shaft build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX-919 Hot Metal Pro 7-iron?

The Mizuno JPX-919 Hot Metal Pro 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length — the same as the standard JPX-919 Hot Metal.

What is the difference between the JPX-919 Hot Metal Pro and the standard Hot Metal?

Lofts, lies and lengths are identical. The Pro has a slimmer topline, a narrower sole and much less offset — 0.112 inches at the 7-iron against 0.179 — putting it close to the JPX-919 Tour's offset numbers.

When did the JPX-919 Hot Metal Pro come out?

It was added to the JPX-919 family in early 2019, reaching retail in February 2019, several months after the Tour, Forged and standard Hot Metal models launched in September 2018.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno JPX-919 Hot Metal Pro ran from 2019 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
