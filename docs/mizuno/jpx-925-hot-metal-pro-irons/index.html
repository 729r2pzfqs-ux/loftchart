About the Mizuno JPX-925 Hot Metal Pro

The JPX 925 Hot Metal Pro is the compact model of Mizuno's JPX 925 Hot Metal family, which Mizuno labels a "Players Speed Cavity" and aims at low to mid handicaps. It shares the standard Hot Metal's Nickel Chromoly 4335 construction, tungsten weighting low in the 4 to 7 irons and identical strong lofts, lies and lengths — 28 degrees at the 7-iron — but in a shorter head (80.9 mm blade length against 85.4 mm) with much less offset: 0.150 inches at the 4-iron against the standard model's 0.224. The set runs 4-iron through gap wedge in right and left hand, without the standard model's 54-degree sand wedge. Mizuno launched the JPX 925 line in September 2024 and retailers catalogue it as the 2025 range, so year_introduced records the announcement year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX 925 Hot Metal Pro 7-iron?

The Mizuno JPX 925 Hot Metal Pro 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches — the same as the standard JPX 925 Hot Metal.

What is the difference between the JPX 925 Hot Metal Pro and the standard Hot Metal?

Lofts, lies and lengths are identical. The Pro has a shorter head (80.9 mm blade length against 85.4 mm) and much less offset — 0.150 inches at the 4-iron against 0.224 — and its stock set is 4-GW without the standard model's 54-degree sand wedge.

Who are the JPX 925 Hot Metal Pro irons for?

Mizuno positions the Hot Metal Pro as a 'Players Speed Cavity' for low to mid handicaps who want the Hot Metal's strong-lofted distance in a more compact, lower-offset head.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Mizuno JPX-925 Hot Metal Pro is a current players distance set in the Mizuno range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the JPX-923 Hot Metal Pro. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
