About the Mizuno JPX-923 Tour

The Tour is the compact end of the JPX 923 family, a Grain Flow Forged cavity back for single-figure players rather than the game-improvement Hot Metal that carries the same number. Lofts are traditional — 34 degrees at the 7-iron, six weaker than the JPX 925 Hot Metal — and offset is uniform and tight, moving only 0.025 inches across the whole set. Mizuno announced the JPX 923 family in 2022 and the Tour and Forged models reached retail in February 2023.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX 923 Tour 7-iron?

The Mizuno JPX 923 Tour 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

When were Mizuno JPX 923 Tour irons made?

Mizuno announced the JPX 923 family in 2022; the Tour and Forged models reached retail in February 2023.

How do JPX 923 Tour lofts compare with the Hot Metal?

Much weaker. The Tour's 34-degree 7-iron is a players loft; the Hot Metal side of the same family runs six degrees stronger.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Mizuno JPX-923 Tour is a current players set in the Mizuno range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the JPX-921 Tour. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
