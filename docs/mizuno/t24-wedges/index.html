About the Mizuno T24

The T24 is the 2023 generation of Mizuno's T-Series tour wedge, Grain Flow Forged HD in Hiroshima and replaced by the Mizuno Pro T-1 in 2025. It tightened the groove pattern with QuadCut+ milling (15 grooves on the 46-54 heads for full shots, 17 on the 56-60 for partials), kept the laser-etched HydroFlow micro grooves for wet spin, and added a fifth sole — the tour-inspired V with high bounce near the leading edge and extreme trailing-edge relief. The 17-combination matrix runs 46 to 60 degrees, with head shapes that flow from a straight-edged profile at the strong lofts to a rounder teardrop at 58 and up. Finishes were Soft White Satin, Denim Copper and Raw, with a copper underlay beneath the chrome for feel; a Blue Ion limited run followed. Lie and length are omitted because Mizuno's published US and UK charts disagree.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Mizuno T24 come in?

46 to 60 degrees in 2-degree steps — 17 loft, bounce and grind combinations. The 46-52 gap lofts come only in the S-Type sole.

What grinds does the Mizuno T24 offer?

Five sole types: S (46-56, high bounce, limited relief), D (54-58, mid bounce), C (56-60, generous relief), the new V (56-60, leading-edge bounce with aggressive trailing-edge relief) and X (58-60, the low-bounce option).

Is the Mizuno T24 forged?

Yes — one-piece Grain Flow Forged HD from 1025E Pure Select mild carbon steel with boron, at Mizuno's Hiroshima plant.

What replaced the Mizuno T24?

The Mizuno Pro T-1 took over the tour-wedge slot in 2025, alongside the wider-soled Mizuno Pro T-3.

Finding one used

The Mizuno T24 ran from 2023 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings. Check the loft and bounce against the chart above before buying used — wedges are routinely bent from standard, and worn grooves cost spin long before a wedge looks worn.
