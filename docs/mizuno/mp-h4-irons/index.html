About the Mizuno MP-H4

The MP-H4 was Mizuno's first full hollow-construction MP iron, launched in September 2012 alongside the MP-64 as part of the 2013 MP line. A Grain Flow Forged face, neck and sole are welded to a hollow body so weight sits low and deep for high launch, while the address view keeps the low-offset MP look. It was catalogued as a full 2-PW set but is best known as a long-iron replacement blended into MP sets. Lofts are traditional, with a 34-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno MP-H4 7-iron?

The Mizuno MP-H4 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length.

Is the Mizuno MP-H4 a full set or just long irons?

Mizuno published specs from a 2-iron through pitching wedge, so it could be played as a full set, but most golfers used the 2-5 irons as forgiving long-iron replacements blended into MP-64 or MP-4 sets.

How is the MP-H4 constructed?

The face, neck and sole are Grain Flow Forged from 1025E steel and welded to a hollow body, placing weight low and deep for high launch while keeping a forged feel and a compact, low-offset address view.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno MP-H4 ran from 2012 to 2014 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
