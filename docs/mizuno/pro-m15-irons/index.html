About the Mizuno Pro M-15

The Mizuno Pro M-15 is the players-distance model of Mizuno's 2026 Pro "Modern Series", the successor to the Pro 245 and the more forgiving companion to the M-13. The 4 through 8 irons are hollow-body: a Grain Flow Forged 4135+ Chromoly face and neck welded to a stainless steel back, with suspended tungsten inside the head — 51 g in the long irons and 50.3 g in the mid irons — while the 9-iron through gap wedge use a partial-hollow Grain Flow Forged 1025E HD body. The Contoured Ellipse CORTECH face runs through the 8-iron. Lofts are about a degree stronger than the Pro 245 in the middle of the set — 29 degrees at the 7-iron against the 245's 30 — with the same 43-degree pitching wedge and 48-degree gap wedge, and the set is right-hand only in 4-GW. Mizuno unveiled the Modern Series on 5 January 2026, with presale from 19 January and in-store availability on 5 February 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno Pro M-15 7-iron?

The Mizuno Pro M-15 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

What is the difference between the Mizuno Pro M-15 and M-13?

The M-15 is the players-distance model, with hollow-body 4-8 irons, tungsten weighting and lofts around three degrees stronger (29-degree 7-iron against the M-13's 32). The M-13 is the players cavity-back with traditional lofts and a three-way split forged construction.

Are the Mizuno Pro M-15 irons available left-handed?

No. Mizuno's specification page lists the M-15 as right hand only in 4-iron through gap wedge, while the sibling M-13 is offered in both right and left hand.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Mizuno Pro M-15 is a current players distance set in the Mizuno range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Pro 245. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
