About the Mizuno JPX-925 Forged

JPX 925 Forged is the players-distance model in the JPX 925 family, sitting between the cast Hot Metal and Mizuno's Pro line. Lofts run five degrees weaker than the Hot Metal at the 7-iron — 30 against 28 — with a much tighter offset progression, 0.150 inches at the 4-iron against the Hot Metal's 0.224, and the bounce climbs a degree per club through the set.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX 925 Forged 7-iron?

The Mizuno JPX 925 Forged 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What is the difference between JPX 925 Forged and JPX 925 Hot Metal?

The Forged is two to three degrees weaker through the set, carries roughly a third less offset in the long irons, and is a forging rather than a cast Chromoly head.

When were Mizuno JPX 925 Forged irons released?

They reached the USGA conforming list on 19 August 2024 and launched later that year, sold as part of the 2025 range.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Mizuno JPX-925 Forged is a current players distance set in the Mizuno range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
