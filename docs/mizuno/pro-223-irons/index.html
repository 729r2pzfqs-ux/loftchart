About the Mizuno Pro 223

The Mizuno Pro 223 is the players cavity-back of the 2022 Mizuno Pro series and the replacement for the MP-20 MMC. The 4-7 irons use a forged Chromoly face with a flow micro slot for ball speed, while the 8-iron through gap wedge are one-piece 1025E Pure Select mild carbon steel built the same way as the Pro 221. Lofts sit between the Pro 221 and Pro 225, with a 32-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge, and offset matches the Pro 225 at 0.122 inch in the 7-iron. The stock set is 4-GW in both right and left hand. Lofts, lies and lengths listed are the men's standard steel-shaft build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno Pro 223 7-iron?

The Mizuno Pro 223 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Mizuno Pro 223?

The Mizuno Pro 223 pitching wedge is 46 degrees and the matching gap wedge is 51 degrees, which is a 5-degree gap.

Are the Mizuno Pro 223 irons forged?

Yes. The 8-iron through gap wedge are one-piece Grain Flow Forged HD 1025E mild carbon steel, and the 4-7 irons pair a forged Chromoly face and micro slot with a forged body.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno Pro 223 ran from 2021 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
