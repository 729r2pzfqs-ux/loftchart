About the Mizuno JPX EZ Forged

The JPX EZ Forged is the Grain Flow Forged model of the JPX EZ family Mizuno launched in the autumn of 2013, built to bridge the gap between game-improvement distance and forged feel. It is forged from a single billet of 1025E Pure Select mild carbon steel in a dark black-nickel finish, with Mizuno's Max Undercut pocket in the 4- through 7-irons enlarging the sweet spot and a full cavity through the scoring clubs. The loft chart matches the cast JPX EZ — a strong 32-degree 7-iron and 45-degree pitching wedge — but with roughly half the offset and a more compact head, which is why it sits in the players-distance category. The stock set ran 4-iron through gap wedge. Mizuno announced the boron-steel 2016-model JPX EZ Forged in August 2015 with this same chart, which closes this record. All figures are the men's standard steel-shaft specification.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX EZ Forged 7-iron?

The 2013 Mizuno JPX EZ Forged 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

Is the Mizuno JPX EZ Forged really forged?

Yes — it is Grain Flow Forged from a single billet of 1025E Pure Select mild carbon steel, the same process as Mizuno's MP irons, with a pocket-cavity design for distance and forgiveness.

How does the JPX EZ Forged differ from the JPX-825 Pro?

The EZ Forged is one degree stronger at the 7-iron (32 against 33), carries more offset and a larger head with a deeper undercut, trading some of the 825 Pro's compactness for distance and forgiveness.

Did the 2016 JPX EZ Forged change the lofts?

No. The 2016 model switched to stronger 1025 Boron steel for a thinner face but kept the 2013 loft, lie, length and offset chart unchanged.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno JPX EZ Forged ran from 2013 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
