About the Mizuno JPX-925 Hot Metal HL

The JPX-925 Hot Metal HL is the high-launch model of the 2024 JPX family, built on the same Nickel Chromoly chassis and Contour Ellipse face as the standard JPX-925 Hot Metal but with lofts three degrees weaker — a 31-degree 7-iron against 28 — and the most offset in the range at 0.260 inches on the 4-iron. Unlike the previous HL generation it is catalogued from a 4-iron, running through a 55-degree sand wedge, in right hand only. Lofts, lies and lengths listed are the men's standard steel-shaft build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX-925 Hot Metal HL 7-iron?

The Mizuno JPX-925 Hot Metal HL 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

How does the JPX-925 Hot Metal HL differ from the standard Hot Metal?

Lofts are three degrees weaker throughout — a 31-degree 7-iron against 28 — with more offset (0.260 against 0.224 inches at the 4-iron), which raises launch for moderate swing speeds.

What clubs are in the JPX-925 Hot Metal HL set?

It is catalogued 4-iron through sand wedge: a 45-degree pitching wedge, 50-degree gap wedge and 55-degree sand wedge, available in right hand only.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Mizuno JPX-925 Hot Metal HL is a current super game improvement set in the Mizuno range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the JPX-923 Hot Metal HL. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
