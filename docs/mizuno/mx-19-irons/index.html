About the Mizuno MX-19

The MX-19 took over the cast, forgiving end of Mizuno's MX range from the MX-17 in 2008, running alongside the forged MX-25. It is an investment-cast pocket cavity with heavy offset — 0.230 inches at the 4-iron — and very low bounce at the top of the set, including a slightly negative 4-iron. Lofts are conventional for the late 2000s, 34 degrees at the 7-iron and 46 at the pitching wedge, and the catalogued set runs 4-iron through a 55-degree sand wedge. Figures are the men's standard steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno MX-19 7-iron?

The Mizuno MX-19 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length.

Are the Mizuno MX-19 irons forged or cast?

The MX-19 is investment cast from stainless steel; within the same MX generation, the forged alternative was the MX-25.

What clubs came in the Mizuno MX-19 set?

The catalogued set ran 4-iron through sand wedge, with a 46-degree pitching wedge, 50-degree gap wedge and 55-degree sand wedge.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno MX-19 ran from 2008 to 2009 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
