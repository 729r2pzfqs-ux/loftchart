About the Mizuno JPX-921 Hot Metal Pro

The JPX-921 Hot Metal Pro is the compact model of the 2020 Hot Metal pair, cast from the same Nickel Chromoly 4335 as the standard JPX-921 Hot Metal with the same seamless cup face and identical lofts, lies and lengths — a 29-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge. The difference is the package: a thinner topline, narrower sole and much less offset, 0.126 inches at the 7-iron against the standard model's 0.189. The stock set ran 4-iron through gap wedge, without the standard model's sand and lob wedges. Mizuno announced the JPX-921 line in August 2020. Lofts, lies and lengths listed are the men's standard steel-shaft build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Mizuno JPX-921 Hot Metal Pro 7-iron?

The Mizuno JPX-921 Hot Metal Pro 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length — the same as the standard JPX-921 Hot Metal.

What is the difference between the JPX-921 Hot Metal Pro and the standard Hot Metal?

Lofts, lies and lengths are identical. The Pro has a thinner topline, narrower sole and much less offset — 0.126 inches at the 7-iron against 0.189 — and its stock set is 4-GW, without the standard model's sand and lob wedges.

What clubs came in a JPX-921 Hot Metal Pro set?

The stock range was 4-iron through gap wedge, finishing with a 44-degree pitching wedge and 49-degree gap wedge.

Finding a used set

The Mizuno JPX-921 Hot Metal Pro ran from 2020 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
