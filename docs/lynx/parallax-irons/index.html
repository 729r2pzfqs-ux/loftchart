About the Lynx Parallax

The Parallax was Lynx's flagship game-improvement iron of the 1990s, a one-piece investment-cast stainless cavity back designed by David Boone and inspired by the Tommy Armour 845s and Ping Eye 2. The first set went to Fred Couples at the end of 1991 — he won that December's Johnnie Walker World Championship with it and the 1992 Masters on Lynx staff — and Ernie Els won the 1994 US Open playing Parallax irons. Lofts are classic early-90s spec with a 36-degree 7-iron and 50-degree pitching wedge over a 1-iron-to-LW range. Like Ping, Lynx marked the lie on each head: a coloured lynx-head stamp in the cavity, white for standard with red two degrees flat and black two degrees upright; the lies below are the white-stamp standards. The larger Black Cat joined the line in 1995, and original-era production ended with the US company's 1998 bankruptcy, though today's UK-owned Lynx has reused the Parallax name on new designs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Lynx Parallax 7-iron?

The Lynx Parallax 7-iron has a loft of 36 degrees, a standard lie of 62 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.5 inches.

What does the coloured lynx-head stamp on Parallax irons mean?

It marks the lie angle, like Ping's colour-code dots: white is standard, red is two degrees flat and black is two degrees upright. The lies in the chart are the white-stamp standards.

Did any majors get won with Lynx Parallax irons?

Yes. Fred Couples won the 1992 Masters on Lynx staff after receiving the first Parallax set in late 1991, and Ernie Els won the 1994 US Open playing Parallax irons.

Are Lynx Parallax lofts weak by modern standards?

Yes — the 36-degree 7-iron is roughly four to six degrees weaker than a modern game-improvement 7-iron, about a club and a half of difference.

Finding a used set

The Lynx Parallax ran from 1991 to 1998 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search the used listings on eBay, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
