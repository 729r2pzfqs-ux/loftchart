About the TaylorMade Tour Preferred CB (2011)

The Tour Preferred CB was the most forgiving model in the Tour Preferred trio TaylorMade announced in autumn 2010 for its 2011 irons line, below the MB blade and MC muscle-cavity. Its two-piece multi-material construction melds a thin forged face with a cast, undercut cavity, giving it the highest MOI of TaylorMade's player's irons of that season along with a hotter face than the MB and MC. The stock set runs 3-iron through a 50-degree approach wedge with a 33-degree 7-iron. Specs below are the men's standard steel-shaft build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Tour Preferred CB (2011) 7-iron?

The 2011 TaylorMade Tour Preferred CB 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length.

Are the 2011 Tour Preferred CB irons forged or cast?

Both. The construction melds a thin forged face with a cast, undercut stainless cavity body, so it delivers forged feel at impact with the stability and higher MOI of a deep cavityback.

How do the 2011 Tour Preferred CB lofts compare to the MC and MB?

The CB is the strongest-lofted of the trio, with a 33-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge against 34 and 47 degrees for the MC and 35 and 47 degrees for the MB. It is also the only one of the three sold with a stock 50-degree approach wedge instead of a 2-iron.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade Tour Preferred CB (2011) ran from 2010 to 2014 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
