About the TaylorMade SpeedBlade

SpeedBlade carried over the RocketBladez idea — a handlebar-shaped slot cut through the sole of the 3 to 7 irons so a larger area of the face can flex at impact. Lofts are strong even by the standards of the period, with a 30.5-degree 7-iron and a 17-degree 3-iron, and the set extends to a 60-degree lob wedge. Offset is large in the long irons, 6.3 mm at the 3-iron, and falls to under a millimetre in the wedges.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade SpeedBlade 7-iron?

The TaylorMade SpeedBlade 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, a lie of 63.0 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

When were TaylorMade SpeedBlade irons made?

They were introduced in 2013 and reached retail on 4 October 2013. TaylorMade ran several overlapping game-improvement models around it, so there is no single clean replacement.

Which SpeedBlade irons have the Speed Pocket?

The 3 through 7-irons. The slot is cut all the way through the sole in those heads and omitted in the short irons and wedges.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade SpeedBlade ran from 2013 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
