About the TaylorMade P760

The P760 is a progressive set: the 3 to 7 irons are hollow-bodied with a thin 4140 face and internal SpeedFoam, while the 8-iron through wedges are solid forged cavity backs, so the long irons launch like a distance iron and the scoring clubs behave like a players iron. Tungsten sits low in the toe of the long irons. Lofts are traditional for the players category, 33 degrees at the 7-iron and 46 at the pitching wedge, which puts it three degrees weaker than the P790 of the same period. Figures are the men's standard steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade P760 7-iron?

The TaylorMade P760 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When were TaylorMade P760 irons made?

The P760 was introduced in 2018 and stayed in the range until the P770 took over that slot in 2020.

Is the TaylorMade P760 hollow?

Only in part. The 3 to 7 irons are hollow with a thin 4140 face and SpeedFoam inside; the 8-iron through wedges are solid forged cavity backs.

How do P760 lofts compare with the P790?

The P760 is about three degrees weaker through the set — 33 degrees at the 7-iron against the P790's 30.5, and 46 at the pitching wedge against 45 — at the same lengths.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade P760 ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
