About the TaylorMade Stealth 2

The Stealth 2 fairway woods launched in January 2023 with the Stealth 2 drivers, carrying the carbon-crown "Fargiveness" story of that generation. The core Stealth 2 is a bonded five-loft line including a 16.5° 3HL; the Stealth 2 HD is the draw-biased high-launch head in three weaker lofts; and the Stealth 2 Plus is the compact titanium head with a 50 g sliding sole weight, the family's only loft sleeve, and a 13.5° "Rocket 3" at the strong end. The Qi10 fairways replaced the line in January 2024.

Head variants

The heads this family is sold in, with each one’s stock loft options.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the TaylorMade Stealth 2 fairway woods come in?

The core Stealth 2 comes in 15, 16.5, 18, 21 and 24 degrees; the Stealth 2 Plus in 13.5, 15 and 18; and the Stealth 2 HD in 16, 19 and 22.

Is the TaylorMade Stealth 2 fairway wood adjustable?

Only the Stealth 2 Plus, which carries TaylorMade's 4 degree loft sleeve plus a 50 gram sliding sole weight. The core Stealth 2 and the HD are bonded and play at their stamped loft.

What is the Stealth 2 Rocket 3 fairway wood?

The 13.5 degree Stealth 2 Plus head — a strong-lofted 3-wood built for distance off the tee and firm turf, playable because the sleeve can add loft back when needed.

Finding one used

The TaylorMade Stealth 2 ran from 2023 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search the used listings on eBay. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
