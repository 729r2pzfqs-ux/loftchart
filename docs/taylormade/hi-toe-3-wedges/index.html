About the TaylorMade Hi-Toe 3

The Hi-Toe 3 is the 2022 generation of TaylorMade's high-toe specialty wedge, at retail from 9 August 2022 until the Hi-Toe 4 replaced it in August 2024. The tall toe and full-face scoring lines (extended to the 54-degree head this generation) are built for open-face shots, with raised micro-ribs between the grooves and the most centered CG of any TaylorMade wedge of its day for a lower, spinnier flight. A four-way camber sole keeps the leading edge low; the previous generation's single 10-degree bounce at 58 and 60 degrees grew into low- (7), standard- (10) and high-bounce (13) options. The retail finish is aged copper; the MyHi-Toe 3 program added chrome, black and raw.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the TaylorMade Hi-Toe 3 come in?

50 to 60 degrees in 2-degree steps. Standard bounce covers every loft; 58 and 60 degrees also come in low-bounce (7 degrees) and high-bounce (13 degrees) versions, for 10 combinations in all.

Does the Hi-Toe 3 have full-face grooves?

Yes, on the 54- through 60-degree heads — this generation extended the full-face scoring lines down to the 54. The 50 and 52 use standard grooves.

When did the Hi-Toe 3 come out and when was it replaced?

It went on sale on 9 August 2022 and was replaced by the Hi-Toe 4 in August 2024.

Finding one used

The TaylorMade Hi-Toe 3 ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings. Check the loft and bounce against the chart above before buying used — wedges are routinely bent from standard, and worn grooves cost spin long before a wedge looks worn.
