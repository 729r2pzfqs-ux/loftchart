About the TaylorMade MG5

The MG5 (Milled Grind 5) is the 2025 generation of TaylorMade's tour wedge line and the first fully forged Milled Grind, announced on 7 August 2025 and at retail from 4 September. Designed with Greg Cesario, it pairs aggressive saw-milled grooves with a raw face carrying Spin Tread water-channeling texture, and precision-milled soles. The matrix runs 23 loft/bounce/grind combinations from 46 to 60 degrees across six grinds, including the new wide-sole SX and the Tiger Woods grind sold as its own model at 56 and 60 degrees. Finishes are Satin Chrome and, on a subset of combinations, Charcoal.

Grind options

The sole grinds this model is sold in, with the lofts each one is available at.

SB Grind (Standard Bounce)46°, 48°, 50°, 52°, 54°, 56°, 58°, 60°
Four-way camber design that suits an array of swing styles and turf conditions for all-around performance — the grind offered at every loft.
SC Grind54°, 56°, 58°, 60°
Built for greenside versatility, with heel and toe relief for manipulating the face in a variety of turf conditions.
SX Grind58°, 60°
New for MG5 — a unique combination of sole width and mid-bounce versatility, with a reverse-C trailing edge that keeps the bounce consistent as you manipulate the face.
LB Grind (Low Bounce)56°, 58°, 60°
For players with shallow swing styles and tight, firm conditions. Built for greenside versatility.
HB Grind (High Bounce)54°, 56°, 58°, 60°
High-bounce sole for steeper swings or soft conditions, with built-in heel relief for versatility.
TW Grind (Tiger Woods)56°, 60°
Tiger Woods's dual-sole grind, updated with a new leading-edge blend and increased effective bounce in the heel. A narrow leading edge with heavy heel relief, sold as its own model at 56 and 60 degrees.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the TaylorMade MG5 come in?

The MG5 is sold from 46 to 60 degrees in 2-degree steps — 23 loft, bounce and grind combinations including the two Tiger Woods grind models.

What grinds does the TaylorMade MG5 offer?

Six: SB standard bounce (every loft 46-60), SC (54-60), the new wide-sole SX (58-60), LB low bounce (56-60), HB high bounce (54-60) and the Tiger Woods TW grind (56 and 60 degrees).

Is the TaylorMade MG5 forged?

Yes — it is the first fully forged Milled Grind wedge, forged from ultrasoft carbon steel with precision-milled soles, saw-milled grooves and a raw face finish.

What is the TW grind on the MG5?

Tiger Woods's dual-sole design: a narrow leading edge with heavy heel relief and increased effective bounce in the heel, sold as its own model at 56 degrees (12 bounce) and 60 degrees (11 bounce).

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the TaylorMade MG5 is a current tour wedge in the TaylorMade range as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the TaylorMade MG4. New wedges are built to the loft, bounce and grind options in the chart above; a fitting can bend loft and lie from standard, so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Check the loft and bounce against the chart above before buying used — wedges are routinely bent from standard, and worn grooves cost spin long before a wedge looks worn.
