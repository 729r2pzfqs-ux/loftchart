About the TaylorMade Qi4D

The Qi4D Rescue family launched with the Qi4D woods — pre-orders from 8 January 2026, retail from 29 January — at $299.99 per head across all three models. The core Qi4D Rescue is the adjustable head with a 3° loft sleeve and an 8 g Trajectory Adjustment System (TAS) sole weight, in 17-25°. The Qi4D Max Rescue is the bonded high-MOI head, numbered like a wood set from a 20° 3-Rescue to a 34° 7-Rescue, and the Qi4D Max Lite Rescue repeats the Max shape in a lighter build without the 20° head.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the TaylorMade Qi4D Rescues come in?

The core Qi4D Rescue comes in 17, 19, 22 and 25 degrees; the Qi4D Max Rescue in 20, 23, 26, 30 and 34; and the Qi4D Max Lite Rescue in 23, 26, 30 and 34.

Is the TaylorMade Qi4D Rescue adjustable?

The core Qi4D Rescue is — it carries a 3 degree loft sleeve (1.5 degrees each way) plus an 8 gram TAS sole weight. The Max and Max Lite Rescues are bonded and adjust only through the TAS weight.

What is the difference between the Qi4D Rescue and the Qi4D Max Rescue?

The core Rescue is the more compact, adjustable head in 17-25 degrees. The Max is the bonded high-MOI head sold about one club number weaker at each loft and extending to a 34 degree 7-Rescue, with the Max Lite repeating that shape in a lighter build.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the TaylorMade Qi4D is the current TaylorMade hybrid line as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the TaylorMade Qi35. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above; the hosel settings extend each head beyond its stamped loft, so a loft between two stock options is usually reachable from either. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
