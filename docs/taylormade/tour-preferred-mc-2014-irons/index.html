About the TaylorMade Tour Preferred MC (2014)

The 2014 Tour Preferred MC is the middle model of TaylorMade's second Tour Preferred generation, between the TP MB muscleback and the more forgiving TP CB. The 3- through 7-irons carry a Speed Pocket milled into the sole and filled with a 3M polyurethane, while the 8-iron through pitching wedge are forged for feel in the scoring clubs, all under a hand-polished satin nickel-chrome finish. Lofts are traditional for the class — a 34-degree 7-iron and a 47-degree pitching wedge — one degree stronger than the TP MB through the middle of the set. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Tour Preferred MC (2014) 7-iron?

The 2014 Tour Preferred MC 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

Are the 2014 Tour Preferred MC irons forged?

Partly. The 8-iron, 9-iron and pitching wedge are forged, while the 3- through 7-irons are cast so a Speed Pocket could be milled into the sole and filled with a 3M polyurethane.

How do the 2014 TP MC lofts compare with the TP MB and TP CB?

The MC sits between them: its 34-degree 7-iron is one degree stronger than the MB's 35 but 1.5 degrees weaker than the CB's 32.5, and its 47-degree pitching wedge matches the MB against the CB's 46.

What shaft did the 2014 Tour Preferred MC come with?

The stock shaft was the KBS Tour in steel, offered in regular, stiff and X flexes, with a Golf Pride Tour Velvet grip.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade Tour Preferred MC (2014) ran from 2014 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
