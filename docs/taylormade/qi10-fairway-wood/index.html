About the TaylorMade Qi10

The Qi10 fairway woods launched on 9 January 2024 with the Qi10 drivers. The core Qi10 is a bonded five-loft line including a 16.5° 3HL, with the wraparound Infinity Carbon Crown of the drivers; the Qi10 Max is the bonded high-MOI head in weaker lofts on a 200 cc 3-wood footprint; and the Qi10 Tour is the compact titanium head with a 50 g sliding sole weight and the only loft sleeve in the family. The Qi35 fairways replaced the line in January 2025, which then put a sleeve on every 3- and 5-wood.

Head variants

The heads this family is sold in, with each one’s stock loft options.

Qi1015°, 16.5°, 18°, 21°, 24°
The core bonded head for most players, sold in five lofts including the high-launch 16.5° 3HL (195 cc, matching the 3-wood). Mid-high launch with mid-low spin and a neutral bias.
Qi10 Tour15°, 18°
The compact titanium-bodied tour head — 170 cc 3-wood, 150 cc 5-wood — with a 50 g sliding sole weight for spin and launch tuning and the family's only 4° loft sleeve.
Qi10 Max16°, 19°, 22°
The bonded high-MOI forgiveness head, a degree or so weaker at each slot — 200 cc 3-wood, 180 cc 5-wood, 165 cc 7-wood — with a shallow face and low CG for easy launch. Swing weight D2.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the TaylorMade Qi10 fairway woods come in?

The core Qi10 comes in 15, 16.5, 18, 21 and 24 degrees; the Qi10 Tour in 15 and 18; and the Qi10 Max in 16, 19 and 22.

Is the TaylorMade Qi10 fairway wood adjustable?

Only the Qi10 Tour, which carries TaylorMade's 4 degree loft sleeve and a 50 gram sliding sole weight. The core Qi10 and the Qi10 Max have bonded hosels and play at their stamped loft.

What is the difference between the Qi10 and Qi10 Max fairway woods?

The Max is the forgiveness head: a larger 200 cc 3-wood footprint, a shallower face, a lower and deeper centre of gravity, and lofts about a degree weaker at each slot (16/19/22 against 15/18/21).

Buying one

TaylorMade has since introduced the TaylorMade Qi35 above it in the range, so the Qi10 is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and fairway woods one generation back are the sweet spot of the used market. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
