About the TaylorMade Stealth 2

The Stealth 2 Rescue family launched in January 2023 alongside the Stealth 2 woods. The core Stealth 2 Rescue is a bonded five-loft line from 19° to 31°; the Stealth 2 HD Rescue is the draw-biased version in four lofts for players fighting a right miss; and the compact Stealth 2 Plus Rescue is the adjustable player's head in 17°, 19.5° and 22°, built to flight down like a long iron. The Qi10 Rescues replaced the family in January 2024.

Head variants

The heads this family is sold in, with each one’s stock loft options.

Stealth 2 Rescue19°, 22°, 25°, 28°, 31°
The core bonded Rescue in five lofts from 19° to 31°; the 28° and 31° heads are right-hand only.
Stealth 2 Plus Rescue17°, 19.5°, 22°
The compact tour head with the family's only 3° loft sleeve, in 17°, 19.5° and 22° — lower flight, more workability, iron-like address look.
Stealth 2 HD Rescue19°, 22°, 25°, 28°
The draw-biased forgiveness head in four lofts, with heel weighting and a higher flight to straighten a fade. Swing weight D3; the 28° head is right-hand only.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the TaylorMade Stealth 2 Rescues come in?

The core Stealth 2 Rescue comes in 19, 22, 25, 28 and 31 degrees (28 and 31 right-hand only); the Stealth 2 Plus Rescue in 17, 19.5 and 22; and the Stealth 2 HD Rescue in 19, 22, 25 and 28.

Is the TaylorMade Stealth 2 Rescue adjustable?

Only the Stealth 2 Plus Rescue, which carries a 3 degree loft sleeve (1.5 degrees each way). The core and HD Rescues are bonded and play at their stamped loft.

What is the difference between the Stealth 2 Rescue and the HD Rescue?

The HD is the draw-biased model: heel weighting and a higher flight to fight a right miss, in four lofts. The core Rescue is neutral and adds a fifth loft at 31 degrees.

Finding one used

The TaylorMade Stealth 2 ran from 2023 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search the used listings on eBay. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
