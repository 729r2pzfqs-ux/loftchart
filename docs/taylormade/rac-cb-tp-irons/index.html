About the TaylorMade rac CB TP

Sold by TaylorMade as the rac Forged TP, this 2005 iron is widely known as the rac CB TP: a coin-forged S25C muscle-cavity developed with tour input, with milled face, cavity and grooves, larger rac Feel Pockets for vibration control and a thin, tour-bevelled sole. It combines keen workability with modest forgiveness and carries fully traditional lofts, with a 36-degree 7-iron and 48-degree pitching wedge across the 2-iron to pitching wedge, right-hand-only range. Specs below are the standard Dynamic Gold steel build; TaylorMade published no offset figures for this model.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade rac CB TP 7-iron?

The TaylorMade rac CB TP (rac Forged TP) 7-iron has a loft of 36 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length.

Is the rac CB TP the same club as the rac Forged TP?

Yes. TaylorMade's own sell sheets call the 2005 model the rac Forged TP, while retailers and the second-hand market commonly list it as the rac CB TP to distinguish it from the rac MB TP blade that followed in 2006.

Were the rac CB TP irons available left-handed?

No, TaylorMade produced the model in right-hand only, in a 2-iron through pitching wedge range with Dynamic Gold steel shafts.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade rac CB TP ran from 2005 to 2006 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
