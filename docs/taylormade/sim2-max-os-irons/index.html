About the TaylorMade SIM2 Max OS

The SIM2 Max OS is the oversized companion to the 2021 SIM2 Max, with a wider chamfered sole, a larger head, more offset and lofts at least a degree stronger through most of the set — a 27-degree 7-iron and a 42-degree pitching wedge against the standard model's 28.5 and 43.5. It shares the Cap Back construction and ECHO Damping System of the SIM2 Max, and its long irons use a shorter face height to push the centre of gravity even lower. For 2022 TaylorMade consolidated the Max and Max OS into the single Stealth iron. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade SIM2 Max OS 7-iron?

The SIM2 Max OS 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

What is the difference between the SIM2 Max and SIM2 Max OS irons?

The OS is the oversized model: a bigger head, a wider chamfered sole, noticeably more offset and lofts at least a degree stronger through most of the set — 27 degrees at the 7-iron against 28.5, and a 42-degree pitching wedge against 43.5.

What replaced the SIM2 Max OS irons?

The 2022 TaylorMade Stealth iron. TaylorMade consolidated the SIM2 Max and SIM2 Max OS into a single Stealth model rather than continuing a separate oversized version.

What wedges were available for the SIM2 Max OS?

Beyond the 42-degree pitching wedge, the set extended to a 48-degree approach wedge, a 54-degree sand wedge and a 59-degree lob wedge.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade SIM2 Max OS ran from 2021 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
