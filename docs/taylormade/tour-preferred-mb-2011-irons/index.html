About the TaylorMade Tour Preferred MB (2011)

The Tour Preferred MB was the muscleback blade in the Tour Preferred trio TaylorMade announced in autumn 2010 and sold as its 2011 irons line, above the MC muscle-cavity and CB cavityback. It is a compact, minimal-offset forged blade produced with TaylorMade's multi-step net forging process, with a precision-weighting port hidden in the head to set swingweight without disturbing the centre of gravity. Lofts are fully traditional, with a 35-degree 7-iron and a 47-degree pitching wedge across the stock 2-iron through pitching wedge range. Specs below are the men's standard steel-shaft build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Tour Preferred MB (2011) 7-iron?

The 2011 TaylorMade Tour Preferred MB 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length.

Are the 2011 Tour Preferred MB irons forged?

Yes. The Tour Preferred MB is a one-piece forged muscleback finished with TaylorMade's net forging process, with a precision-weighting port used to set exact swingweights without moving the centre of gravity.

How do the 2011 Tour Preferred MB lofts compare to the MC and CB?

The MB runs 18 to 47 degrees from 2-iron to pitching wedge with a 35-degree 7-iron. The MC is one degree stronger through most of the set at 34 degrees in the 7-iron, and the CB is two degrees stronger again at 33 degrees, though all three share a 46-47-degree pitching wedge.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade Tour Preferred MB (2011) ran from 2010 to 2014 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
