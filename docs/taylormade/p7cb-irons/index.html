About the TaylorMade P7CB

The P7CB is a compact tour cavity back, forged from 1025 carbon steel under what TaylorMade calls Compact Grain Forging at 2,000 tons, with a milled face and grooves. Co-forged tungsten in the perimeter holds the centre of gravity constant through the set rather than moving it, which is the opposite of the progressive-CG approach in the P790 and P770. Lofts are traditional for the category — 33 degrees at the 7-iron and 46 at the pitching wedge. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade P7CB 7-iron?

The TaylorMade P7CB 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When did the TaylorMade P7CB come out?

2024, as the forged cavity back in the P7 tour line alongside the P7MB muscleback.

What is the difference between the P7CB and the P7MC?

Both are forged tour cavity backs. The P7CB uses co-forged tungsten in the perimeter to hold the centre of gravity constant through the set and is forged under 2,000 tons of pressure with a milled face and grooves.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the TaylorMade P7CB?

46 degrees, with a 51-degree gap wedge available below it.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the TaylorMade P7CB is a current players set in the TaylorMade range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
