About the TaylorMade P790 (2025)

The fifth-generation P790 is the first in the line to change the loft chart: after four generations of 30.5 degrees at the 7-iron and 45 at the pitching wedge, the 2025 model moves to 30 and 44, drops the 3-iron from the published range, and starts the set at a 20-degree 4-iron. The face is a new 4340M forged material TaylorMade says is 20 percent stronger than before, allowing a thinner face and a sweet spot up to 24 percent larger on the 7-iron, with SpeedFoam Air behind it and the FLTD CG tungsten progression keeping the centre of gravity lowest in the long irons and rising through the set. The release timing is also new: TaylorMade announced the irons on 18 February 2025 with retail on 13 March, moving the P790's two-year cycle from its traditional late-summer window to spring, so a launch that would previously have arrived in August 2025 came six months early. The stock US set is 4-PW with a 49-degree approach wedge available. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade P790 (2025) 7-iron?

The 2025 P790 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches — half a degree stronger than the 30.5-degree 7-iron used by every previous P790 generation.

Did the P790 lofts change for 2025?

Yes, for the first time in the line's history. The 2017 through 2023 generations all used the same chart; the 2025 model strengthens most of the set — the 4-iron goes from 21 to 20 degrees, the 7-iron from 30.5 to 30, the 9-iron from 40 to 39 and the pitching wedge from 45 to 44 — and drops the 3-iron from the published range.

What is the difference between the 2023 and 2025 P790?

The 2025 model introduces a stronger 4340M forged face that allows a sweet spot up to 24 percent larger, revises the sole geometry, and changes the lofts for the first time in the line. It also moved the launch from the traditional late-summer window to spring, reaching retail in March 2025.

When was the 2025 TaylorMade P790 released?

TaylorMade announced it on 18 February 2025, with preorders the same day and retail on 13 March 2025 — about six months earlier in the cycle than the August-September windows of previous generations.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the TaylorMade P790 (2025) is a current players distance set in the TaylorMade range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the P790 (2023). New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
