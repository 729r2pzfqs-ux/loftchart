About the TaylorMade RocketBladez Tour

The RocketBladez Tour is the compact better-player version of the 2013 RocketBladez, announced in late 2012 alongside the standard model and played on tour before release. It keeps the Speed Pocket slot in the sole of the 3- to 7-irons but uses a smaller head, a thinner topline, less offset and markedly weaker lofts — a 33.75-degree 7-iron and a 47-degree pitching wedge against 30.5 and 45 on the standard RocketBladez. The stock set is 3-iron through pitching wedge with a 51.5-degree approach wedge available. Specs below are the men's standard steel-shaft build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade RocketBladez Tour 7-iron?

The TaylorMade RocketBladez Tour 7-iron has a loft of 33.75 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length.

How do RocketBladez Tour lofts compare to the standard RocketBladez?

The Tour model is considerably weaker lofted: 33.75 degrees at the 7-iron against 30.5, and a 47-degree pitching wedge against 45. It also starts the set at a 19-degree 3-iron rather than 17.5 degrees.

Does the RocketBladez Tour have the Speed Pocket?

Yes. Like the standard model, the 3- through 7-irons carry the Speed Pocket slot in the sole; the short irons use a conventional solid sole.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade RocketBladez Tour ran from 2013 to 2014 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
