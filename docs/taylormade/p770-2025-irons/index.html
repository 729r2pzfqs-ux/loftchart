About the TaylorMade P770 (2025)

The third-generation P770 keeps the compact forged hollow-body formula — a forged 4140 face over a Thru-Slot Speed Pocket, an 8620 body filled with SpeedFoam Air, and the FLTD CG progression backed by up to 41 grams of tungsten per head — in a sleeker shape with a thinner topline and a sharper leading edge on the long irons. The scoring lofts move a degree stronger than the 2023 model, a 45-degree pitching wedge and 50-degree approach wedge against 46 and 51, and the 3- and 4-irons half a degree, while the 5-iron through 9-iron chart is unchanged, 33 degrees at the 7-iron. TaylorMade announced it alongside the P7CB on 31 July 2024 with retail from 6 September 2024; it is marketed for the 2024/25 season and commonly catalogued as the 2025 P770, so year_introduced records the 2024 announcement per this database's convention. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade P770 (2025) 7-iron?

The 2025-generation P770 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches — the same 7-iron chart every P770 generation has used.

Did the P770 lofts change for the 2025 generation?

Only at the ends of the set. The pitching wedge strengthens from 46 to 45 degrees and the approach wedge from 51 to 50, while the 3-iron goes from 19.5 to 19 and the 4-iron from 22.5 to 22. The 5-iron through 9-iron, including the 33-degree 7-iron, carry over from the 2023 model.

When was the 2025 TaylorMade P770 released?

TaylorMade announced it with the P7CB on 31 July 2024, with preorders the same day at $1,399.99 for a seven-piece set and retail from 6 September 2024. It is marketed as the 2024/25 model and widely catalogued as the 2025 P770.

How do the 2025 P770 lofts compare with the 2025 P790?

The P770 is about three degrees weaker through the middle of the set — a 33-degree 7-iron against the 2025 P790's 30 — and one degree weaker at the pitching wedge, 45 degrees against 44.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the TaylorMade P770 (2025) is a current players set in the TaylorMade range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the P770 (2023). New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
