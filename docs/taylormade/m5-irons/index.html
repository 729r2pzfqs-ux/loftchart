About the TaylorMade M5

The M5 iron is the compact model of TaylorMade's 2019 M pair, sold beside the larger M6 and sharing its Speed Bridge — a stiffening rib across the back of the cavity that lets the face and Speed Pocket flex without the harsh sound. Lofts are broadly the M3's, with a 30-degree 7-iron, but the whole set is built noticeably longer: 37.50 inches at the 7-iron against the M3's 36.75. Lengths use TaylorMade's ground-plane measurement, adopted in November 2018.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade M5 7-iron?

The TaylorMade M5 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.50 inches.

When were TaylorMade M5 irons made?

The M5 was the 2019 model, sold alongside the M6, and was replaced by the SIM family in 2020.

Why are TaylorMade M5 irons longer than the M3?

Partly build, partly measurement. TaylorMade switched from pin-length to ground-plane measurement in November 2018, and the M5 set is genuinely built longer as well — 37.50 inches at the 7-iron against 36.75 on the M3.

What is the Speed Bridge on the TaylorMade M5?

A rib that spans the back cavity and ties the top line to the sole. It stiffens the body so the thin face and the Speed Pocket sole slot can flex for ball speed without producing the hollow sound that unsupported slots create.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade M5 ran from 2019 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
