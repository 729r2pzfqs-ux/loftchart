About the TaylorMade SpeedBlade HL

The SpeedBlade HL is the high-launch companion to the 2013 SpeedBlade, sold from 2014 mainly as boxed 4-PW and 4-PW-plus-AW sets for players who need more height. Each head carries one degree more loft than the standard SpeedBlade — 31.5 degrees at the 7-iron against 30.5 — while lie, length and offset stay on the standard SpeedBlade ladder. The Speed Pocket sole slot and deep undercut cavity are unchanged from the standard model.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade SpeedBlade HL 7-iron?

The SpeedBlade HL 7-iron has a loft of 31.5 degrees — one degree weaker than the standard SpeedBlade — with a 63-degree lie and 37-inch length.

What is the difference between the SpeedBlade and SpeedBlade HL irons?

The HL (high launch) version adds one degree of loft to every head to make the set easier to flight, while lie angles, lengths and offsets are identical to the standard SpeedBlade.

What clubs came in the TaylorMade SpeedBlade HL set?

The HL was sold as boxed 4-PW and 4-PW plus 51-degree AW sets from 2014, in steel and graphite builds.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade SpeedBlade HL ran from 2014 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
