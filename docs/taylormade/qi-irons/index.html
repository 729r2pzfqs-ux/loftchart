About the TaylorMade Qi

The Qi iron replaced the Stealth as TaylorMade's mainstream game-improvement model for 2024, and is the iron counterpart to the Qi10 metalwoods rather than a set called Qi10. It carries over the Cap Back design and ECHO Damping System and adds flighted centres of gravity, positioned low in the long irons and progressively higher through the short irons, along with a 450 stainless steel face TaylorMade says enlarges the effective hitting area by about 14 per cent over the Stealth. Lofts and lengths are identical to the Stealth, with a 28-degree 7-iron and a 43-degree pitching wedge. TaylorMade did not publish offset or bounce figures for the Qi set, so those are omitted here. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Qi 7-iron?

The TaylorMade Qi 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

Is the 2024 TaylorMade iron called Qi or Qi10?

The iron is called Qi. Qi10 is the name TaylorMade used for the 2024 drivers, fairway woods and rescues; the matching irons dropped the number and were sold simply as Qi, with Qi HL as the higher-launching companion set.

Do the Qi irons have the same lofts as the Stealth irons?

Yes. The Qi set matches the Stealth through the whole bag, from an 18.5-degree 4-iron and a 28-degree 7-iron to a 43-degree pitching wedge and a 59-degree lob wedge, and the lengths are identical too.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Qi irons?

The Qi pitching wedge is 43 degrees. The set also offers a 49-degree approach wedge, a 54-degree sand wedge and a 59-degree lob wedge.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade Qi ran from 2024 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
