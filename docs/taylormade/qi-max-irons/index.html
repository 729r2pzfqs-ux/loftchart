About the TaylorMade Qi Max

The Qi Max replaced the Qi as TaylorMade's mainstream game-improvement iron for 2026, after the company let the Qi run for two seasons rather than replacing it in 2025. Its headline change is a face engineered to flex uniformly at impact: TaylorMade varied the face sections to counteract the extra toe flexing typical of large game-improvement faces, which it says reduces cut spin and keeps off-centre strikes straighter. A new sound stabilization bar connects the back bar to the top line, which allowed the ECHO Damping System material to be extended higher behind the face, and the FLTD CG progression sits the centre of gravity lowest in the long irons and progressively higher through the short irons. Lies and lengths are identical to the Qi and the 28-degree 7-iron carries over, but the short irons are tweaked slightly — the pitching wedge strengthens from 43 to 42.5 degrees, the approach wedge from 49 to 48, and the lob wedge from 59 to 58. Presale opened 8 January 2026 with retail on 29 January 2026; the US stock set is 4-PW with the AW, SW and LW sold separately. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Qi Max 7-iron?

The TaylorMade Qi Max 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length — the same 7-iron chart as the Qi it replaced.

Do the Qi Max irons have the same lofts as the Qi irons?

Mostly. The 4-iron through 7-iron and the sand wedge carry over unchanged, but the 8-iron strengthens half a degree to 32.5, the pitching wedge goes from 43 to 42.5 degrees, the approach wedge from 49 to 48, and the lob wedge from 59 to 58. Lies and lengths are identical throughout.

What clubs come in a stock TaylorMade Qi Max set?

The US stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge, with the 48-degree approach wedge, 54-degree sand wedge and 58-degree lob wedge sold separately. UK sets were sold as 5-PW.

When were the TaylorMade Qi Max irons released?

TaylorMade opened presale on 8 January 2026 and the irons reached retail on 29 January 2026, at $1,099 in steel and $1,199 in graphite for the 7-piece set.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the TaylorMade Qi Max is a current game improvement set in the TaylorMade range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the TaylorMade Qi. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
