About the TaylorMade M3

The M3 is the compact half of TaylorMade's 2018 M pairing, sitting under the oversized M4 with a shorter blade, thinner top line and less offset. It uses the RIBCOR bars that lock the perimeter of the face to channel more energy into the centre, over a Speed Pocket sole slot, with tungsten low in the head. Lofts are strong for a better-player iron — 30.5 degrees at the 7-iron — and the set is built at 36.75 inches there, a quarter inch shorter than the P790 of the same period. Figures are the men's standard steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade M3 7-iron?

The TaylorMade M3 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

When were TaylorMade M3 irons made?

The M3 was the 2018 model, launched alongside the M4 and replaced by the M5 and M6 in 2019.

What is the difference between the TaylorMade M3 and M4 irons?

The M3 is the smaller, lower-offset head aimed at better players; the M4 is the oversized game-improvement model. The M3's 7-iron is 30.5 degrees against the M4's 30.5-degree equivalent at a longer build, and the M3 carries a shorter blade and thinner top line.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the TaylorMade M3?

45 degrees, with a 50-degree approach wedge and a 55-degree sand wedge available to complete the set.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade M3 ran from 2018 to 2019 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
