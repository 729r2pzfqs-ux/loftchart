About the TaylorMade RSi TP

The RSi TP sat at the top of TaylorMade's 2015 RSi family, pairing the range's Face Slot Technology with a forged players head. In the 3- through 7-irons a forged carbon steel face and hosel is plasma-welded to a stainless steel back, while the 8-iron to pitching wedge are conventional forged heads with a slight muscle cavity. Lofts are traditional rather than jacked — a 34-degree 7-iron and 47-degree pitching wedge — and the published range runs from a 2-iron to the pitching wedge, with 3-PW the normal set. It was replaced by the PSi Tour, announced in late 2015.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade RSi TP 7-iron?

The RSi TP 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch length — traditional players-iron lofts rather than the strong lofts of the RSi 1.

Are the TaylorMade RSi TP irons forged?

Partly. The 3- through 7-irons plasma-weld a forged carbon steel face and hosel to a stainless steel back, while the 8-iron through pitching wedge are fully forged heads with a slight muscle cavity.

Do the RSi TP irons have Face Slots?

Yes — the 3- through 7-irons carry the same Face Slot Technology as the rest of the RSi family to protect ball speed on heel and toe strikes.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade RSi TP ran from 2015 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
