About the TaylorMade Qi HL

Qi HL is the high-launch model in TaylorMade's 2024 Qi iron family, built for moderate swing speeds. Against the standard Qi it weakens every loft by about two degrees — 28 degrees at the 7-iron rather than 26 — and pairs that with lighter stock shafts and a 5-PW stock make-up, so the ball gets airborne without the golfer having to generate the speed. The set extends to a 59-degree lob wedge. Figures are the men's standard right-handed build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Qi HL 7-iron?

The TaylorMade Qi HL 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 63.0 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What is the difference between the TaylorMade Qi and Qi HL?

HL stands for high launch. Its lofts are about two degrees weaker than the standard Qi through the set, and it is built with lighter stock shafts, so slower swings launch the ball higher.

Is the Qi HL the same as the Qi10 irons?

TaylorMade catalogues the 2024 irons as Qi and Qi HL. Because they launched alongside the Qi10 driver, fairway woods and hybrids, much of the coverage calls them Qi10 and Qi10 HL — they are the same clubs.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the TaylorMade Qi HL?

43 degrees, with 49-, 54- and 59-degree wedges available to complete the set.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the TaylorMade Qi HL is a current super game improvement set in the TaylorMade range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the TaylorMade Stealth HD. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
