About the TaylorMade Qi HL

Qi HL is the high-launch model of TaylorMade's 2024 Qi iron family, aimed at moderate swing speeds. It uses the same lies and lengths as the standard Qi but weakens every loft by two degrees — 30 degrees at the 7-iron against 28 — and pairs that with a head about 6 grams lighter and lighter, higher-launching stock shafts, so total club weight drops by more than 20 grams. The stock set runs 5-PW. Figures below are the men's standard right-handed build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Qi HL 7-iron?

The TaylorMade Qi HL 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 63.0 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What is the difference between the TaylorMade Qi and Qi HL?

HL stands for higher and lighter. The lies and lengths are the same, but every Qi HL loft is two degrees weaker — 30 degrees at the 7-iron against 28 — and the head is about 6 grams lighter with lighter stock shafts, so the whole club is more than 20 grams lighter.

Is the Qi HL the same as the Qi10 irons?

TaylorMade catalogues the 2024 irons as Qi and Qi HL. They launched with the Qi10 driver, fairway woods and hybrids, so much of the coverage calls them Qi10 and Qi10 HL — they are the same clubs.

Does the Qi HL come with a 4-iron?

No. The published set starts at a 23.5-degree 5-iron and runs to a 54-degree sand wedge, with 5-PW the stock build.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the TaylorMade Qi HL is a current super game improvement set in the TaylorMade range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the TaylorMade Stealth HD. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
