About the TaylorMade P790 (2019)

The second-generation P790 keeps the original's loft chart exactly — 30.5 degrees at the 7-iron, 45 at the pitching wedge — and changes the build around it. The forged face is thinner, the internal SpeedFoam is reformulated and the tungsten weight is repositioned, while the set is a quarter inch longer than the 2017 model through the mid irons at 37.00 inches for the 7-iron. Offset also drops slightly in the long irons. Figures below are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade P790 (2019) 7-iron?

The 2019 P790 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

Are 2017 and 2019 P790 lofts different?

No. Both use 19, 21, 23.5, 26.5, 30.5, 35, 40, 45 and 50 degrees from 3-iron to approach wedge. The 2019 set is built a quarter inch longer through the mid irons and carries slightly less long-iron offset.

When were the 2019 P790 irons replaced?

In 2021, by the third-generation P790, which kept the same loft chart again and moved to the lighter SpeedFoam Air.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the 2019 P790?

45 degrees, with a 50-degree approach wedge below it.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade P790 (2019) ran from 2019 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
