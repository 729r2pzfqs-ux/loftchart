About the TaylorMade Kalea

Kalea was TaylorMade's dedicated women's line, launched in 2016 as a coordinated set in which every club — driver through sand wedge — was individually designed for the swing speeds and launch conditions of women golfers rather than being a lighter version of a men's model. The irons run 6-iron through pitching wedge plus a matching sand wedge, with the 3-, 4- and 5-irons replaced by fairway woods and hybrids in the full set. They combine a low, deep centre of gravity, Speed Pocket soles and ultralight low-kick Slim-Tech graphite shafts. Specs below are the women's stock graphite build, the only configuration offered.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Kalea 7-iron?

The TaylorMade Kalea 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 61.75-degree lie and a 36.75-inch graphite-shaft length.

What clubs come in the TaylorMade Kalea iron set?

The Kalea irons run 6-iron through pitching wedge plus a 54-degree sand wedge. In the full Kalea set the long irons are replaced by 4- and 5-hybrids and fairway woods, which TaylorMade considered easier for the target player to launch.

Are the Kalea irons available with steel shafts?

No. Kalea was designed as an ultralight women's set and was sold only with TaylorMade's low-kick Slim-Tech graphite shafts in ladies flex.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade Kalea ran from 2016 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
