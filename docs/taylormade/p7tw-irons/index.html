About the TaylorMade P7TW

The P7TW is the production version of the muscleback blade TaylorMade co-designed with Tiger Woods, announced in April 2019 in the wake of his Masters win and at retail from 1 May 2019 at $1,999.99 for the set. Each head is forged from 1025 carbon steel with a milled sole — the Milled Grind process removing head-to-head variance in sole geometry — internal tungsten weighting, a long blade length, thin top line and progressive face height shaped to Woods's specification. It is sold 3-iron through pitching wedge, right hand only, through TaylorMade's custom program, with the True Temper Dynamic Gold Tour Issue S400 the stock shaft. One point of confusion: the retail set uses standard lofts, which are not Tiger's personal specs — his own set is bent weaker (for example 36 degrees at the 7-iron against the retail 35, and 22.5 at the 3-iron against 21). The model has remained in TaylorMade's custom line ever since, outliving the P7MB and P7MC generations launched after it. Figures below are the standard right-handed steel build; the pitching wedge offset is omitted because the two retailer spec tables consulted disagreed on it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade P7TW 7-iron?

The retail P7TW 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length. Tiger Woods's personal 7-iron is bent to 36 degrees.

Are the retail P7TW irons the same specs Tiger Woods plays?

The heads are the same, but the retail set ships at standard lofts while Tiger's personal set is bent weaker — 22.5, 25.5, 29, 32.5 and 36 degrees from 3-iron to 7-iron against the retail 21, 24, 27, 31 and 35. Buyers can order Tiger's lofts through the custom program.

Is the TaylorMade P7TW still available?

Yes. It launched on 1 May 2019 at $1,999.99 and has stayed in TaylorMade's custom-order line ever since, sold 3-iron through pitching wedge in right hand only.

What shaft comes standard in the P7TW?

The True Temper Dynamic Gold Tour Issue S400 in stiff flex, with a Golf Pride Tour Velvet Full Cord grip — the same shaft and grip Tiger Woods uses.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2019, the TaylorMade P7TW is a current blade set in the TaylorMade range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
