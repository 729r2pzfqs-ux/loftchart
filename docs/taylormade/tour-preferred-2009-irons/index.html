About the TaylorMade Tour Preferred (2009)

The 2009 Tour Preferred was TaylorMade's single player's iron of the Tour Burner era, announced in 2008 and sold as the '09 model. It pairs a classic blade shape and thin topline with a shallow undercut cavity that moves weight to heel and toe pads, and an Inverted Cone face for more consistent speed on off-centre hits. Lofts are traditional, with a 35-degree 7-iron and 47-degree pitching wedge; the stock range runs 3-iron to sand wedge, with the 2-iron a custom-order club. Specs below are the men's standard steel-shaft build; a TaylorMade TP 110 graphite shaft was custom only.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade Tour Preferred (2009) 7-iron?

The 2009 TaylorMade Tour Preferred 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, with a 61.5-degree lie and a 36.75-inch steel-shaft length.

Do the 2009 Tour Preferred irons have flat lie angles?

Yes, noticeably so for the era. The set runs from a 58-degree lie in the 2-iron to 63 degrees in the wedges, roughly a degree flatter through most of the set than TaylorMade's 2011 Tour Preferred models.

What wedges came with the 2009 Tour Preferred set?

The stock set offered a 47-degree pitching wedge, a 51-degree approach wedge and a 55-degree sand wedge. The 2-iron at the other end of the range was available through custom order only.

Finding a used set

The TaylorMade Tour Preferred (2009) ran from 2008 to 2010 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
