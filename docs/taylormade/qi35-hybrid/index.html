About the TaylorMade Qi35

TaylorMade calls its hybrids Rescues, and the Qi35 Rescue family launched on 7 January 2025 with the Qi35 fairway woods. The core Qi35 Rescue is the adjustable, slightly compact head with a mild draw bias and a 3° loft sleeve; the Qi35 Max Rescue is the bonded high-MOI head, sold one degree weaker at each club number and all the way out to a 35° 7-Rescue; and the Qi35 Max Lite Rescue is the same Max shape in a lighter head, shaft and grip build without the 20° head. The Qi4D Rescues replaced the family in January 2026.

Head variants

The heads this family is sold in, with each one’s stock loft options.

Qi35 Rescue17°, 19°, 22°, 25°
The core adjustable Rescue: a 106 cc head with the 3° loft sleeve and a touch of draw bias, in 17-25°. The 17° head is right-hand only.
Qi35 Max Rescue20°, 23°, 27°, 31°, 35°
The bonded forgiveness head with higher MOI and a slight draw bias, lofted one degree weaker than the core Rescue at each club number and sold in five lofts from 20° to 35°.
Qi35 Max Lite Rescue23°, 27°, 31°, 35°
The Max geometry in a lighter build — lighter head, Vanquish 5 shaft and lighter grip — for moderate swing speeds, in four lofts from 23°.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the TaylorMade Qi35 Rescues come in?

The core Qi35 Rescue comes in 17, 19, 22 and 25 degrees; the Qi35 Max Rescue in 20, 23, 27, 31 and 35; and the Qi35 Max Lite Rescue in 23, 27, 31 and 35.

Are the Qi35 Rescues adjustable?

Only the core Qi35 Rescue, which carries a 3 degree loft sleeve (1.5 degrees each way). The Qi35 Max and Max Lite Rescues are bonded and play at their stamped loft.

What is the difference between the Qi35 Rescue and the Qi35 Max Rescue?

The core Rescue is the slightly more compact, adjustable head with a mild draw bias. The Max is the bonded high-MOI forgiveness head, lofted one degree weaker at each club number and sold in more lofts — out to a 35 degree 7-Rescue.

Buying one

TaylorMade has since introduced the TaylorMade Qi4D above it in the range, so the Qi35 is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and hybrids one generation back are the sweet spot of the used market. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
