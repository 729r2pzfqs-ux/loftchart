About the TaylorMade P770 (2023)

The 2023 P770 is the compact hollow body that sits between the P7MC blade-ish cavity and the P790 distance iron. Against the previous P770 it cuts offset in the long irons to suit a better player's eye, keeps the thin forged 4140 face over a Speed Pocket, and uses the FLTD CG progression so the centre of gravity is lowest in the long irons and rises into the scoring clubs. Lofts are two to three degrees weaker than the P790 through the set — 33 degrees at the 7-iron against 30.5. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a TaylorMade P770 (2023) 7-iron?

The 2023 P770 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

How do 2023 P770 lofts compare with the P790?

The P770 is two to three degrees weaker through the set — 33 degrees at the 7-iron against the P790's 30.5, and a 46-degree pitching wedge against 45 — at the same lengths.

What changed between the 2020 and 2023 P770?

The 2023 model cuts offset in the long irons for a cleaner look at address and adopts the FLTD CG progression, while keeping the forged hollow-body construction with a thin forged 4140 face.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the 2023 P770?

46 degrees, with a 51-degree approach wedge below it.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the TaylorMade P770 (2023) is a current players set in the TaylorMade range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the P770. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
