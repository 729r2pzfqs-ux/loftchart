Hosel adjustment

The XXIO 14 has a glued, non-adjustable hosel — the loft printed on the head is the loft it plays. Loft and lie can only be altered by a clubmaker, so pick the stock loft to match your launch rather than planning to tune it later.

About the XXIO 14

The XXIO 14 hybrids launched in early 2025 with the XXIO 14 woods — one lightweight head in five lofts from an 18° 3-hybrid to a 30° 7-hybrid, with head sizes from 131 cc down to 123 cc, every build at a D1 swing weight with XXIO's counterbalanced Weight Plus grip. The hosels are glued. As of October 2026 the line is current, with the next generation expected in 2027.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the XXIO 14 hybrid come in?

Five lofts: 18, 20, 23, 26 and 30 degrees — the 3-hybrid through 7-hybrid.

Is the XXIO 14 hybrid adjustable?

No — the hosel is glued, as across the XXIO line. The loft printed on the head is the loft it plays.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the XXIO 14 is a current lightweight line in the XXIO range as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the XXIO 13. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above, and with no adjustable hosel the stock loft is the one to get right. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
