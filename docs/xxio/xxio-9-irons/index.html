About the XXIO 9

XXIO 9 is the ninth generation of Dunlop's lightweight flagship, announced in late 2015 and sold in the US through 2016 and 2017 as the 2016 line. The 4- through 7-irons use a five-piece head — Super-TIX PLUS titanium milled face insert, SUS630 stainless body and tungsten-nickel weights in the sole, toe, heel and neck — which XXIO claimed gave the lowest centre of gravity in the line's history, while the short irons simplify to fewer pieces. The matching Dual Speed Technology build pairs the heavier head with the MP900 graphite shaft, whose balance point sits 15mm closer to the grip than the MP800 it replaced. Lofts are traditional for the category — a 30-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge — and XXIO publishes face progression (1mm at the 4-iron to 5.5mm at the wedges) rather than offset. The stock set was 6-PW with 4, 5, AW and SW singles.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO 9 7-iron?

The XXIO 9 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

What shafts did the XXIO 9 irons come with?

The stock graphite shaft was the XXIO MP900 in R, SR and S flexes, with the Nippon N.S. Pro 890GH DST for XXIO as the steel option in R and S.

What is the XXIO 9 head made of?

The 4- through 7-irons use a five-piece head with a milled Super-TIX PLUS titanium face insert on a SUS630 stainless steel body, plus tungsten-nickel weights in the sole, toe, heel and neck. The short irons use a simpler construction.

When did the XXIO 9 irons come out?

The XXIO 9 series was announced in late 2015, reached Japanese retail in December 2015 and US retail in January 2016, and was replaced by the XXIO 10 announced in late 2017.

Finding a used set

The XXIO 9 ran from 2015 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
