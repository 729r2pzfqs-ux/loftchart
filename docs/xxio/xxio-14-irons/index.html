Loft progression

About the XXIO 14

XXIO 14 keeps the lofts and lies of the XXIO 13 exactly and lengthens the whole set by a quarter of an inch, which lifts the stock swing weight from C8 to C9 while the static club weights actually drop by five grams a club. It is also the first XXIO numbered set to split into two models: the standard 14 for moderate swing speeds and a stronger-lofted 14+ for slightly faster ones. XXIO announced both in November 2025 for a December on-sale date, so retailers list them as the 2026 line.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO 14 7-iron?

The XXIO 14 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

What is the difference between the XXIO 14 and XXIO 14+?

The 14+ is stronger through the set — 28.5 degrees at the 7-iron against 28, and 23 at the 5-iron against 22 — and is built a quarter inch shorter, ending at the approach wedge rather than a sand wedge.

Is the XXIO 14 a 2025 or 2026 model?

Both, depending on how you count. XXIO unveiled it in November 2025 and put it on sale on 8 December 2025; most retailers catalogue it as the 2026 line.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the XXIO 14 is a current game improvement set in the XXIO range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the XXIO 13. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
