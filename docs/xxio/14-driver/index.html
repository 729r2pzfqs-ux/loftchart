About the XXIO 14

The XXIO 14 is the 2026 generation of XXIO's ultralight flagship, at US retail from 8 December 2025 — and a first for the brand: after thirteen generations of glued hosels, the 14 and 14+ carry an adjustable "Lightweight Quick Tune" loft sleeve. The standard 14 is a 283-gram total build at 46 inches for swing speeds of roughly 75-90 mph; the 14+ is the slightly heavier (299 g), lower-launching build for 90-100 mph swings, right-hand only. A separate XXIO 14 Ladies line runs rounded ultralight heads at 10.5° to 13.5° with the MP-1400L shaft. Both men's models use the silicon-enhanced VR-Titanium face with XXIO's ULTiFLEX flex design.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the XXIO 14 come in?

The standard XXIO 14 comes in 9.5, 10.5 and 11.5 degrees (9.5 right-hand only); the XXIO 14+ in 9 and 10.5, right-hand only. The Ladies line runs 10.5 to 13.5 degrees.

Is the XXIO 14 adjustable?

Yes — a first for the brand. The 14 and 14+ carry XXIO's Lightweight Quick Tune sleeve with about 1.5 degrees of loft adjustment either way; XXIO publishes the range but not a position-by-position chart. The Ladies models appear to keep a fixed hosel.

What is the difference between the XXIO 14 and XXIO 14+?

Weight and flight. The standard 14 is about 283 grams total at 46 inches for roughly 75-90 mph swings; the 14+ is 299 grams at 45.75 inches with a lower launch for 90-100 mph swings, and comes right-hand only.

Who is the XXIO 14 for?

Golfers with moderate swing speeds who gain more from an ultralight, draw-biased, high-launch build than from tour-style adjustability — the classic XXIO buyer. Faster swingers will find the heads too light and too spinny.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the XXIO 14 is a current lightweight line in the XXIO range as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the XXIO 13. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above; the hosel settings extend each head beyond its stamped loft, so a loft between two stock options is usually reachable from either. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable driver may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
