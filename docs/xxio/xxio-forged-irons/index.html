About the XXIO XXIO Forged

XXIO Forged is Dunlop's attempt to put the XXIO name into a better player's hands: a forged S25C soft carbon steel body with an HT1770 steel face insert, a compact shape, a straight leading edge and a V-shaped sole. Lofts are moderate — 30 degrees at the 7-iron, 44 at the pitching wedge — and offset is described as moderate rather than minimal, running 3.0mm in the long irons and widening to 5.5mm in the wedges. The stock set is a six-club 5-PW, with the 4-iron, approach wedge and sand wedge sold individually.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO Forged 7-iron?

The XXIO Forged 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.00 inches.

What is the XXIO Forged made from?

A forged S25C soft carbon steel body with a high-strength HT1770 steel face insert.

How many clubs come in an XXIO Forged set?

The stock set is six clubs, 5-iron to pitching wedge. The 4-iron, approach wedge and sand wedge are sold individually.

Is the XXIO Forged as light as the rest of the XXIO range?

No. It is built to conventional players-iron swing weights of D2 and D3, where the numbered XXIO line runs C8 and C9.

Finding a used set

The XXIO XXIO Forged ran from 2015 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
