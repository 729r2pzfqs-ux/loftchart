About the XXIO 10

XXIO 10 — sold in the US as "XXIO X", the Roman numeral, which invites confusion with the later letter-X players line — is the tenth generation of Dunlop's lightweight flagship, announced in late 2017 and at US retail through 2018 and 2019. The Hi-Energy Impact Head pairs a thin titanium face with tungsten-nickel sole weights for a larger high-COR area, and the matching MP1000 Smart Impact graphite shaft is built around Dual Speed Technology: a heavier head on an extremely light, counterbalanced shaft. Lofts run two to three degrees stronger than the XXIO 9 it replaced, with a 29-degree 7-iron. The chart below is the graphite build; the steel build plays a quarter inch shorter in every club.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO 10 7-iron?

The XXIO 10 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a graphite-build length of 37.25 inches.

Are the XXIO 10 and the XXIO X the same club?

The tenth-generation XXIO (2017-2019) was marketed in the US as 'XXIO X' using the Roman numeral ten — that is this set. The XXIO X players-distance line introduced in 2020 is a different, unrelated model that happens to share the name.

What shafts did the XXIO 10 irons come with?

The stock graphite shaft was the ultralight XXIO MP1000 in R, SR and S flexes, and the stock steel option was the Nippon N.S. Pro 870GH DST made for XXIO, with steel builds a quarter inch shorter per club.

Do XXIO 10 irons have a titanium face?

Yes. The Hi-Energy Impact Head combines a thin titanium face with tungsten-nickel sole weights to enlarge the high-COR area of the face.

Finding a used set

The XXIO 10 ran from 2017 to 2019 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
