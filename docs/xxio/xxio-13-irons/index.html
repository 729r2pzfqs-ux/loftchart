About the XXIO 13

XXIO 13 keeps the loft, lie and length chart of the XXIO 12 unchanged and reworks the four-piece construction behind it, with 31 grams of tungsten used through the 5 to 7 irons to drive the centre of gravity low and back. The stock shafts are extremely light for the category — 47 grams in regular, 50 in stiff. Like the rest of the XXIO line the reference build is graphite, so the set runs at C8 rather than the D-range swing weights of a steel-shafted set.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO 13 7-iron?

The XXIO 13 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What is the difference between the XXIO 13 and XXIO 14?

The lofts and lies match club for club, but the XXIO 14 is built a quarter of an inch longer at every club and runs one swing-weight point heavier at C9.

What shafts came stock in the XXIO 13 irons?

The XXIO MP-1300 graphite shaft, with the Nippon N.S.Pro 850GH DST as the steel alternative, both in R2, R, SR and S.

Finding a used set

The XXIO 13 ran from 2024 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
