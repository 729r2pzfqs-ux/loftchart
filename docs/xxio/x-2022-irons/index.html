About the XXIO X 2022

XXIO X is the brand's line for better players who still want the ultralight build, sitting alongside the numbered sets rather than replacing them. This 2022 edition runs one degree stronger than the XXIO 12 of the same year at the 7-iron, 29 against 28, and is built a quarter inch shorter through the set. The stock build is the Miyazaki AX-II graphite shaft; a steel option was offered and runs one swing-weight point heavier.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO X (2022) 7-iron?

The 2022 XXIO X 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

How does the XXIO X differ from the numbered XXIO sets?

It is the better-player version of the same ultralight idea: stronger lofts, a shorter build and heavier swing weights than the XXIO 12 sold in the same year.

Did the XXIO X come with steel shafts?

Graphite was the stock build, with the Miyazaki AX-II. A steel option was offered and measures one swing-weight point heavier through the set.

Finding a used set

The XXIO X 2022 ran from 2022 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
