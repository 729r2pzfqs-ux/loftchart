Hosel adjustment

The XXIO 14 has a glued, non-adjustable hosel — the loft printed on the head is the loft it plays. Loft and lie can only be altered by a clubmaker, so pick the stock loft to match your launch rather than planning to tune it later.

About the XXIO 14

The XXIO 14 fairway woods launched in early 2025 with the XXIO 14 driver, continuing XXIO's ultra-lightweight formula for moderate swing speeds: total club weights well under 300 g, counterbalanced Weight Plus grips, and high-launch builds at longer-than-standard lengths. Five lofts run from a 15° 3-wood to a 23° 9-wood on glued hosels. As of October 2026 the line is current — XXIO refreshes its core line every two years, so a 15th generation is expected in 2027.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the XXIO 14 fairway wood come in?

Five lofts: 15, 16.5, 18, 20 and 23 degrees — a 3-wood through 9-wood ladder.

Is the XXIO 14 fairway wood adjustable?

No — XXIO builds glued hosels throughout, keeping weight out of the hosel for its ultra-light builds. The loft printed on the head is the loft it plays.

Who is the XXIO 14 fairway wood for?

Golfers with moderate swing speeds — the whole XXIO formula is sub-300-gram club weights, counterbalanced grips and slightly longer builds that add clubhead speed without extra effort.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the XXIO 14 is a current lightweight line in the XXIO range as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the XXIO 13. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above, and with no adjustable hosel the stock loft is the one to get right. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
