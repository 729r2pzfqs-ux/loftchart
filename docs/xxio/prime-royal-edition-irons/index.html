About the XXIO Prime Royal Edition

Royal Edition is the dress version of XXIO Prime, built on the same four-piece titanium-face platform with an expanded Rebound Frame and its own SP-1300K shaft. It shares the Prime's lofts, lies and lengths from the 6-iron down and adds a 5-iron at 22 degrees on special order. Swing weights are the same C7 through the irons, so the two sets feel alike; the differences are cosmetic and in the shaft.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO Prime Royal Edition 7-iron?

The XXIO Prime Royal Edition 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

What is the difference between XXIO Prime and Prime Royal Edition?

The loft, lie, length and swing weight charts are the same from the 6-iron down. Royal Edition uses the SP-1300K shaft rather than the SP-1300, adds a 22-degree 5-iron on special order and carries the dressier finish.

When was the XXIO Prime Royal Edition released?

This generation launched in 2025, alongside the March 2025 Prime line.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the XXIO Prime Royal Edition is a current super game improvement set in the XXIO range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
