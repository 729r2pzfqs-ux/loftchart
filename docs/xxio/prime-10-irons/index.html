About the XXIO Prime 10

Prime 10 is the second XXIO Prime iron generation, following the original Prime 9 into the US market in 2019. It carried over the same loft, lie, bounce and length table as the Prime 9 while adding a thin, forged Super-TIX PLUS titanium face insert with a CNC-milled speed groove in place of the first generation's stainless-only face, for more ball speed from the same very light head weight. The offset is pronounced in the long irons and tapers sharply toward the wedges for control. The stock shaft is the XXIO Prime SP-1000, built with Toray T1100G carbon fibre and Nanoalloy resin. The stock set was 7-9-PW, sold individually as 5, 6, AW and SW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO Prime 10 7-iron?

The XXIO Prime 10 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches, the same table used by the Prime 9 and Prime 11 generations either side of it.

What changed between the XXIO Prime 9 and Prime 10?

The head geometry held steady, but the Prime 10 added a thin, forged Super-TIX PLUS titanium face insert with a CNC-milled speed groove in place of the Prime 9's stainless-only face, plus a new SP-1000 stock shaft, for more ball speed at the same very light swing weight.

What shaft came stock in the XXIO Prime 10?

The XXIO Prime SP-1000, a graphite shaft built with Toray T1100G carbon fibre and Nanoalloy resin, in R and SR flex.

Finding a used set

The XXIO Prime 10 ran from 2019 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
