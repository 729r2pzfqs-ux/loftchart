About the XXIO Prime 9

Prime 9 was the first XXIO Prime iron, launched 17 December 2016 alongside the XXIO Forged irons as a new ultra-light tier above the numbered XXIO line. XXIO built it for the lowest and deepest centre of gravity in an XXIO iron to that point, using a 630 stainless-steel body with a tungsten-nickel sole weight (28 grams in the 7-iron) and an additional high-density tungsten-nickel inner weight in the long irons. The stock XXIO Prime SP-900 graphite shaft is extremely light, built with Toray T1100G material and a shock-absorbing tip section to add draw bias. Total club weight is just 339 grams for the 5- and 6-irons and 349 grams for the 7-9 irons. The stock set was 7-9-PW, sold individually as 5, 6, AW and SW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an XXIO Prime 9 7-iron?

The XXIO Prime 9 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What is the Prime 9 iron head made of?

A cast 630 stainless-steel body with a tungsten-nickel sole weight and, in the long irons, an additional high-density tungsten-nickel inner weight for a low, deep centre of gravity. Unlike later Prime generations, XXIO's own launch materials do not describe a separate titanium face insert for this generation.

How light is the XXIO Prime 9 iron?

Very light: total club weight is 339 grams for the 5- and 6-irons and 349 grams for the 7-9 irons, built around the extremely light XXIO Prime SP-900 graphite shaft.

Finding a used set

The XXIO Prime 9 ran from 2016 to 2018 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
