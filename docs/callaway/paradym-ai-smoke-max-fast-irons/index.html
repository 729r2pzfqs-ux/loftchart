About the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast

The Max Fast is the ultra-lightweight model of Callaway's 2024 Paradym Ai Smoke iron family, announced in January 2024 alongside the standard Ai Smoke, the wide-body Max and the high-launch HL. It brought the Japan-market "Fast" designation to the global line: a deep cavity back with the family's Ai Smart Face and tungsten weighting, sold graphite-only with a 40 gram Tensei stock shaft, sub-D0 swingweights and each club built long — a 39 inch 5-iron against the standard model's 38 — so slower swingers can generate speed. The chart runs 5-iron through sand wedge with no 4-iron. Specs below are the standard men's graphite build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast 7-iron?

The Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37.5 inch graphite length.

Why are the Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast irons longer than the standard model?

The Max Fast is built for slower swing speeds: each club is roughly half an inch longer than the standard Ai Smoke — a 39 inch 5-iron — and paired with a 40 gram graphite shaft and C9 swingweight so the club is easier to move fast.

Is there a 4-iron in the Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast set?

No. Callaway publishes the Max Fast from the 5-iron to a 56 degree sand wedge, with 5-PW the stock configuration.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast ran from 2024 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
