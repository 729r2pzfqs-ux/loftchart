About the Callaway Epic Forged Star

Epic Forged Star is the ultra-lightweight build of the 2019 Epic Forged, announced with the Epic Star family on 16 July 2019 for golfers with slower and moderate swing speeds. A lighter head, 40 and 50 gram class UST ATTAS Speed shafts and a 43 gram grip cut the total weight, lofts are two to three degrees weaker than the standard Epic Forged for launch (a 26 degree 7-iron against 27, but a 38 degree pitching wedge against 41), and the set was sold as a custom-order 5-iron through sand wedge with four wedges closing the gaps.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Epic Forged Star 7-iron?

The Callaway Epic Forged Star 7-iron has a loft of 26 degrees, a lie of 60.5 degrees and a length of 37.625 inches.

What is the difference between Epic Forged and Epic Forged Star irons?

Star is the ultra-lightweight build for slower and moderate swing speeds: lighter head, 40-50 gram graphite shafts and a 43 gram grip, with lofts two to three degrees weaker through the scoring clubs than the standard Epic Forged.

Who are Callaway Epic Forged Star irons for?

Callaway built them for golfers with slower or moderate swing speeds who want maximum distance and launch from an ultra-light package. They were sold as a custom-order set.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Epic Forged Star ran from 2019 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
