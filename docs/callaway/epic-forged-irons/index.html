About the Callaway Epic Forged

Epic Forged was Callaway's most expensive iron of 2019, a forged hollow body with a cup face, tungsten weighting and urethane microspheres behind the face. The lofts are among the strongest ever put on a forged iron — 27 degrees at the 7-iron and 18 at the 4 — and the set runs long, at 39.5 inches for the 4-iron, so the carry gaps at the top of the bag are unusually wide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Epic Forged 7-iron?

The Callaway Epic Forged 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 61.0 degrees and a length of 37.625 inches.

When were Callaway Epic Forged irons released?

Callaway announced them on 16 July 2019 and they reached retail on 2 August 2019.

How strong are Epic Forged lofts?

Very. The 27-degree 7-iron is six degrees stronger than the Apex Pro 21 and matches what most makers were calling a 6-iron at the time.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2019, the Callaway Epic Forged is a current players distance set in the Callaway range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
