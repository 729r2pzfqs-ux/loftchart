About the Callaway Apex MB 21

The Apex MB 21 is the muscleback of Callaway's 2021 line, announced on 13 October 2020 alongside the X Forged CB and X Forged UT and at retail from 29 October 2020 — a 2020 announcement sold and catalogued as the 2021 model. It is forged from 1025 carbon steel in a brushed finish, with 20V grooves and a removable tungsten screw in the back of the head that sets swing weight without moving the centre of gravity. Lofts are traditional — a 34-degree 7-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge — and heads run 3-iron through a 50-degree approach wedge, with 4-PW the stock set. In the US it was sold through custom order only.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex MB 21 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex MB 21 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

When were Callaway Apex MB 21 irons released?

Callaway announced them on 13 October 2020 and they reached retail on 29 October 2020, sold as part of the 2021 line — hence the Apex MB 21 name.

What is the difference between the Apex MB 21 and the Apex Pro 21?

The MB is a one-piece 1025 forging with a tungsten swing-weight screw and no cavity, and its lofts run one degree weaker through the scoring clubs — a 34-degree 7-iron against the Apex Pro 21's 33. The Apex Pro 21 adds hollow long irons, tungsten cores and urethane microspheres.

Did the Apex MB 21 come in a stock set?

The stock set is 4-PW, with heads available from 3-iron through a 50-degree approach wedge. In the US the model was sold through custom order only.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Apex MB 21 ran from 2020 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
