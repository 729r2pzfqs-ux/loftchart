About the Callaway Big Bertha B21

The Big Bertha B21 revived the Big Bertha iron name for golfers who want maximum help, announced on 18 August 2020 with the matching B21 driver, fairway woods and hybrids and at retail from 10 September 2020 at 899.99 dollars for a seven-piece steel set. It was the first Big Bertha iron designed with artificial intelligence: each head carries its own A.I.-designed Flash Face Cup, backed by a Visible Tungsten Energy Core for launch and urethane microspheres for sound. The shaping is extreme even by game-improvement standards — soles up to 50 percent wider than the Mavrik Max in parts of the set, one of the longest blade lengths Callaway had produced, and around 8 mm of offset in the 4-iron. Lofts are strong for the era of its launch, a 29-degree 7-iron and a 43-degree pitching wedge, and the chart runs from the 4-iron to a 60-degree lob wedge, the lob wedge right hand only. The Big Bertha irons announced in 2023 replaced it, dialling much of the offset back out. Figures are the men's standard steel build; swing weight is published only as a combined D0/D1 range and is omitted per club.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Big Bertha B21 7-iron?

The Callaway Big Bertha B21 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When did the Callaway Big Bertha B21 irons come out?

Callaway announced the B21 family on 18 August 2020, and the irons reached retail on 10 September 2020.

Are the Callaway Big Bertha B21 irons forgiving?

They are Callaway's maximum-forgiveness design of that generation: soles up to 50 percent wider than the Mavrik Max in parts of the set, one of Callaway's longest blade lengths, about 8 mm of offset in the 4-iron, and tungsten weighting for easy launch.

What clubs come in a Big Bertha B21 set?

The chart runs from the 4-iron to a 60-degree lob wedge — 4-PW plus approach, sand and lob wedges — with the lob wedge right hand only. The standard retail set was seven pieces.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Big Bertha B21 ran from 2020 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
