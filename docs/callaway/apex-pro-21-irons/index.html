About the Callaway Apex Pro 21

Apex Pro 21 is the tour end of Callaway's Apex family: a 1025 carbon steel forging with tungsten energy cores, urethane microspheres filling the cavity to damp vibration, and a hollow construction through the long irons. Lofts are traditional for a players iron — the 7-iron is 33 degrees, four weaker than the Rogue ST Pro of the following year — and offset is tight, from 3.56 mm at the 3-iron to 1.65 mm at the approach wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Pro 21 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex Pro 21 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

When were Callaway Apex Pro 21 irons released?

Callaway announced them on 12 January 2021 and they reached retail on 11 February 2021.

Are Apex Pro 21 lofts strong?

No. At 33 degrees the 7-iron is traditional for a players iron, and four degrees weaker than Callaway's own Rogue ST Pro of the following year.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Apex Pro 21 ran from 2021 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
