About the Callaway X-22 Tour

The X-22 Tour is the compact, better-player version of the X-22, unveiled at the Golf Europe show in October 2008 as a 2009 model alongside the 2009 X-Forged. It keeps the notched perimeter weighting of the standard head on a smaller profile with a thinner top line, less offset and a sleeker hosel that lowers the centre of gravity. Lofts run one degree weaker than the standard X-22 through the scoring clubs, with a 34 degree 7-iron and a 46 degree pitching wedge, and the lie ladder tops out at a flat 63.5 degrees rather than the standard model's 65.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway X-22 Tour 7-iron?

The Callaway X-22 Tour 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a 37 inch standard length.

What is the difference between the X-22 and the X-22 Tour?

The X-22 Tour has a smaller head, thinner top line, less offset and lofts one degree weaker through the scoring clubs — a 34 degree 7-iron against 33 — with much flatter short-iron lies: 63.5 degrees at the pitching wedge against 65 on the standard X-22.

When was the Callaway X-22 Tour released?

It was unveiled at the Golf Europe show in October 2008 and sold as a 2009 model, a season after the standard X-22 reached retail.

Finding a used set

The Callaway X-22 Tour ran from 2009 to 2011 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
