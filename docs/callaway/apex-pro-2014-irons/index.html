About the Callaway Apex Pro (2014)

The original Apex Pro is the compact players iron Callaway sold alongside the 2014 Apex, announced in November 2013 and at retail in early 2014. It is a one-piece forging from soft 1020 carbon steel with high-density tungsten weights in the soles of the 2 through 5 irons for easier launch, a soft polymer insert behind the lower face, and a face 17 percent thinner than the preceding X Forged. Lofts are traditional — a 33 degree 7-iron and a 46 degree pitching wedge — and the chart ran from an 18 degree 2-iron to a 51 degree A-wedge around the standard 3-PW set. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Pro (2014) 7-iron?

The original Callaway Apex Pro 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Pro (2014) pitching wedge?

The 2014 Apex Pro pitching wedge is 46 degrees, with a 51 degree A-wedge below it.

How do the 2014 Apex Pro lofts compare with the 2014 Apex?

The Pro is weaker in the scoring clubs — a 33 degree 7-iron against 31, and a 46 degree pitching wedge against 45 — with the gap coming from its traditional players loft chart.

Was the 2014 Callaway Apex Pro available in a 2-iron?

Yes. The published chart ran from an 18 degree 2-iron to a 51 degree A-wedge, although the standard set make-up was 3-PW.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Apex Pro (2014) ran from 2014 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
