About the Callaway Apex MB (2024)

The Apex MB is the blade of the Apex range Callaway announced in 2023 and sold as its 2024 line, forged in one piece from 1025 carbon steel. It shares the Apex CB's loft, lie and length chart exactly — a 34-degree 7-iron at 62.0 lie and 37 inches — and separates itself on offset alone, carrying about a third less at every club: 1.5 mm at the 7-iron against the CB's 2.3. The stock set is 4-PW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex MB 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex MB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

What is the difference between the Callaway Apex MB and Apex CB?

Loft, lie and length are identical club for club. The MB is a one-piece muscle back with roughly a third less offset — 1.5 mm at the 7-iron against the CB's 2.3 — and no cavity behind the face.

Is the Apex MB a 2023 or a 2024 model?

Both, depending on how you count. Callaway announced the Apex range in 2023 and sold it as the 2024 line, so retailers list it as the Apex MB 24.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Callaway Apex MB (2024) is a current blade set in the Callaway range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Callaway Apex MB 21. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
