About the Callaway Elyte Max Fast

The Max Fast is the ultra-lightweight model of Callaway's 2025 Elyte iron family, announced in January 2025 alongside the standard Elyte, the Elyte X and the high-launch Elyte HL. It pairs the family's Ai10x Face with Speed Frame construction — a frame connecting the body to the topline that stiffens the body while freeing the face to flex — and is sold graphite-only with a 40 gram Mitsubishi Vanquish stock shaft, C9 swingweights and long builds, including a 39 inch 5-iron, for players who need help generating speed. Lofts are one to two degrees stronger than the Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast it replaced — 30 degrees at the 7-iron against 31 — and the chart runs 5-iron through sand wedge with no 4-iron. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Elyte Max Fast 7-iron?

The Callaway Elyte Max Fast 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 62.25 degree lie and a 37.5 inch graphite length.

What is the difference between the Elyte Max Fast and the Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast?

The Elyte Max Fast runs about a degree to two degrees stronger through the set — 30 degrees at the 7-iron against 31 — swaps the Ai Smart Face for the Ai10x Face with Speed Frame construction, and moves to a Mitsubishi Vanquish PL 40 stock shaft. Both are graphite-only, C9-swingweight builds with a 39 inch 5-iron.

Who are the Callaway Elyte Max Fast irons for?

They are built for players with slower swing speeds: 40 gram graphite shafts, C9 swingweights and clubs built roughly half an inch longer than standard help generate clubhead speed and launch.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Elyte Max Fast ran from 2025 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
