About the Callaway JAWS Raw Full Toe

The JAWS Raw Full Toe is Callaway's 2023 high-toe specialty wedge, at retail from 7 April 2023. It moves the JAWS Full Toe concept onto the JAWS Raw platform: sharp full-face grooves on an unplated face that rusts into play, with a taller toe for open-face shots. The whole line uses the new J Grind — moderate heel-to-toe camber, a slightly raised heel, a pre-worn leading edge and a sole that widens toward the toe — at a single 10-degree bounce across four lofts. It left the range when the Opus family replaced Callaway's wedge lineup from late 2024; the full-toe slot continued with the Opus-generation full-toe models.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Callaway JAWS Raw Full Toe come in?

Four: 54, 56, 58 and 60 degrees, all with 10 degrees of bounce on the J Grind sole.

What is the J Grind on the JAWS Raw Full Toe?

A sole designed around the full-toe shape: moderate heel-to-toe camber, a slightly raised heel so the face can open, a pre-worn leading edge and extra width toward the toe. It suits medium divots and softer conditions.

How is this different from the standard JAWS Raw?

Same raw-face JAWS groove platform, but with a taller toe, grooves across the whole face, a single grind and bounce instead of the standard model's 24-option matrix, and only the four scoring lofts.

Finding one used

The Callaway JAWS Raw Full Toe ran from 2023 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings. Check the loft and bounce against the chart above before buying used — wedges are routinely bent from standard, and worn grooves cost spin long before a wedge looks worn.
