About the Callaway RAZR X Forged

RAZR X Forged was the elite model of the RAZR X line announced on 20 January 2011, and the only forged head in it: 1020 carbon steel shaped with Callaway's Triple Net Forging process, a shallow cavity back and tightly spaced Tour CC grooves. It took over from the 2009 X-Forged and carried its loft, lie and length ladder forward unchanged — a 35 degree 7-iron and a 47 degree pitching wedge from the 2-iron down — adding a 51 degree approach wedge. The 2013 X Forged replaced it when the X name returned.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway RAZR X Forged 7-iron?

The Callaway RAZR X Forged 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a 37 inch standard length.

Are the Callaway RAZR X Forged irons really forged?

Yes. Unlike the cast RAZR X and RAZR X Tour, the RAZR X Forged is 1020 carbon steel shaped with Callaway's Triple Net Forging process.

How do RAZR X Forged lofts compare to the X-Forged irons?

They are identical to the 2009 X-Forged from the 2-iron through pitching wedge — a 35 degree 7-iron and 47 degree pitching wedge — with a 51 degree approach wedge added to the set.

Finding a used set

The Callaway RAZR X Forged ran from 2011 to 2012 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
