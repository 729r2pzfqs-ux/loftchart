Hosel adjustment chart

OptiFit hosel (Ai Smoke and HL) · loft −1° to +2° · 8 settings.

The lie cog has two positions: N (neutral, the stated lie) and D (draw, a more upright lie — Callaway does not publish the degree change). Changing loft re-orients the head on the shaft, so lower settings sit slightly open and higher settings slightly closed at address.

About the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke

The Paradym Ai Smoke hybrids launched in January 2024 with the Ai Smoke woods. The core Ai Smoke is the adjustable iron-replacement head in 18° to 27°; the Ai Smoke HL is the higher-launching, slightly longer-built adjustable head that extends the ladder to 30° and 33°; and the MAX Fast is the lightweight bonded model for slower swings, in 21° to 33°. The Elyte hybrids replaced the line in early 2025.

Head variants

The heads this family is sold in, with each one’s stock loft options.

Paradym Ai Smoke18°, 21°, 24°, 27°
The core adjustable hybrid in four lofts at a D2 swing weight, with the tungsten speed cartridge keeping launch and spin down; the 27° head is right-hand only.
Paradym Ai Smoke HL18°, 21°, 24°, 27°, 30°, 33°
The high-launch adjustable head in six lofts from 18° to 33°, built slightly longer (41.25 inches at 3H) at a D1 swing weight with flatter lies — a half-fairway, half-hybrid for average swing speeds.

The lightweight model for slower-to-moderate swings: bonded hosel, lighter components and the highest launch in the family, in five lofts from 21° to 33°.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke hybrids come in?

The core Ai Smoke comes in 18, 21, 24 and 27 degrees; the Ai Smoke HL in 18, 21, 24, 27, 30 and 33; and the MAX Fast in 21, 24, 27, 30 and 33.

Are the Callaway Ai Smoke hybrids adjustable?

The core Ai Smoke and the HL are adjustable at every loft through the OptiFit hosel (minus 1 to plus 2 degrees with a neutral or draw lie cog). The MAX Fast uses a bonded hosel to save weight.

What is the difference between the Ai Smoke and Ai Smoke HL hybrids?

The HL is built a half inch or so longer at each loft with flatter lies and a shallower, higher-launching head — closer to a small fairway wood — and extends the ladder to 30 and 33 degrees. The core head is the more compact iron replacement.

Buying one

Callaway has since introduced the Callaway Elyte above it in the range, so the Paradym Ai Smoke is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and hybrids one generation back are the sweet spot of the used market. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
