About the Callaway Apex Pro 16

Announced in September 2015 for the 2016 model year alongside the Apex CF16, the Apex Pro 16 is the compact players iron of the second Apex generation. The head is forged from 1025 mild carbon steel, with tungsten inserts in the 3 through 5 irons to raise launch while the short irons keep a higher centre of gravity for control — Callaway's progressive CG design. Lofts are a degree weaker than the original Apex Pro through most of the set, with a 34 degree 7-iron and a 46 degree pitching wedge. Specs below are the standard men's steel-shaft numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Pro 16 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex Pro 16 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 62 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Pro 16 pitching wedge?

The Apex Pro 16 pitching wedge is 46 degrees, with a 51 degree A-wedge below it.

How do Apex Pro 16 lofts compare with the Apex CF16?

The Pro is three degrees weaker at the 7-iron — 34 degrees against 31 — and one degree weaker at the pitching wedge, 46 degrees against 45.

Are the Apex Pro 16 irons forged?

Yes. The head is forged from 1025 mild carbon steel, with tungsten inserts in the 3 through 5 irons to raise launch in the long irons.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Apex Pro 16 ran from 2016 to 2019 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
