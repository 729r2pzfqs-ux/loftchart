Hosel adjustment chart

OptiFit hosel (3-wood and 3HL heads of the MAX and Triple Diamond) · loft −1° to +2° · 8 settings.

The lie cog has two positions: N (neutral, the stated lie) and D (draw, a more upright lie — Callaway does not publish the degree change). Changing loft re-orients the head on the shaft, so lower settings sit slightly open and higher settings slightly closed at address.

About the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke

The Paradym Ai Smoke fairway woods launched in January 2024 with the Ai Smoke drivers, led by the machine-learned "Ai Smart Face". The MAX is the core head, carrying the full seven-loft ladder from the 15° 3-wood to the 27° 11-wood including the 20° Heavenwood; the Triple Diamond is the compact low-spin tour head; and the MAX Fast is the lightweight, draw-biased bonded build for slower swings. Only the 15° and 16.5° heads of the MAX and Triple Diamond are adjustable. The Elyte fairways replaced the line in early 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke fairway woods come in?

The Ai Smoke MAX comes in 15, 16.5, 18, 20 (Heavenwood), 21, 24 and 27 degrees; the Triple Diamond in 15, 18 and 21; and the MAX Fast in 16, 19, 22 and 25.

Is the Callaway Ai Smoke fairway wood adjustable?

Only the 15 degree 3-woods and the 16.5 degree 3HL carry the OptiFit hosel (minus 1 to plus 2 degrees with a neutral or draw lie cog). Every other loft, and the whole MAX Fast line, is bonded.

What is the difference between the Ai Smoke MAX and MAX Fast fairway woods?

The MAX is the standard-weight core head in seven lofts. The MAX Fast is the lightweight package — bonded hosel, lighter head, shaft and grip, draw bias and more upright lies — in four lofts a degree weaker, built for slower swing speeds.

Buying one

Callaway has since introduced the Callaway Elyte above it in the range, so the Paradym Ai Smoke is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and fairway woods one generation back are the sweet spot of the used market. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
