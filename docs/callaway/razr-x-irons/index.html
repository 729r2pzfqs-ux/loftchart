About the Callaway RAZR X

RAZR X was the mainstream game-improvement iron in the range Callaway announced in January 2011 alongside the RAZR X Tour and the forged RAZR X. It pairs an integrated cup face and undercut cavity with a low, deep centre of gravity, and the bounce climbs steeply through the set — from 2 degrees at the 3-iron to 13.5 at the sand wedge. Lengths below are the steel build; the graphite build is a quarter inch longer at every club.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway RAZR X 7-iron?

The Callaway RAZR X 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 36.75 inches.

When were Callaway RAZR X irons released?

Callaway announced the RAZR X line on 20 January 2011 and it reached retail on 18 February 2011.

What is the difference between RAZR X and RAZR X Forged?

RAZR X is the cast game-improvement model recorded on this page. RAZR X Forged, announced at the same time, is a 1020 carbon steel forging with a tour-shaped head — a different club with its own spec chart.

Finding a used set

The Callaway RAZR X ran from 2011 to 2012 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
