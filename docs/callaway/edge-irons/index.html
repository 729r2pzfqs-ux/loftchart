About the Callaway Edge (Costco)

The Edge is a Costco-exclusive Callaway line sold only inside a boxed 10-piece set — driver, 3-wood, 5-hybrid, 6-iron through pitching wedge, sand wedge and an Odyssey putter — on sale by early 2018 at $499.99 and famous for repeatedly selling out once coverage of it went viral through 2019. The irons are large cavity backs with a stabilizing arch and perimeter weighting, on uniflex steel shafts, and their loft ladder mirrors the Steelhead XR generation exactly, from a 26 degree 6-iron and 30 degree 7-iron to a 44 degree pitching wedge and 54 degree sand wedge. The set has been refreshed since — later versions run 12 pieces with a revised iron ladder — and the specs below are the original uniflex-steel configuration from Costco's official spec chart.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Edge 7-iron?

The Costco Callaway Edge 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37 inch length on the original uniflex steel shaft.

What is the loft of the Callaway Edge pitching wedge and sand wedge?

The pitching wedge is 44 degrees and the sand wedge is 54 degrees; the boxed set includes no gap wedge between them.

Are the Costco Callaway Edge irons the same as a regular Callaway model?

They are a Costco-exclusive design, but the original set's iron lofts, lies and loft ladder match Callaway's 2016 Steelhead XR generation exactly, in a simpler cavity-back head.

When did the Callaway Edge set come out at Costco?

Costco's online listing is archived from April 2018 at $499.99 for the 10-piece set, and it became a recurring, frequently sold-out item as coverage spread through 2019; the set has since been refreshed with new configurations.

Production status

Introduced in 2018, the Callaway Edge (Costco) has no confirmed end date on file. LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the Callaway catalogue — check with Callaway or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
