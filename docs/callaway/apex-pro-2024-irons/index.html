About the Callaway Apex Pro (2024)

Announced in 2023 and sold as the '24 model, the Apex Pro combines a distance-iron long-iron construction — hollow body, cup face, tungsten low in the head — with a compact players shape through the scoring irons. Lofts are one degree stronger than the Apex CB in the mid irons and a degree stronger again at the pitching wedge: 33 degrees at the 7-iron and 45 at the pitching wedge. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Pro (2024) 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex Pro '24 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When was the Callaway Apex Pro '24 announced?

In 2023, alongside the Apex CB and Apex MB, and sold as the 2024 range.

Are Apex Pro 21 and Apex Pro '24 lofts the same?

In the mid and short irons, yes — both are 33 degrees at the 7-iron and 45 at the pitching wedge. The '24 model strengthens the long irons: 19 degrees at the 3-iron against 20.5.

Is the Callaway Apex Pro hollow?

In the long irons, yes — a hollow body with a high-strength cup face and tungsten weighting. The scoring irons move to a more conventional forged players build.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Apex Pro (2024) ran from 2023 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
