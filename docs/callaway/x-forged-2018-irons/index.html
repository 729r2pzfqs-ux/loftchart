About the Callaway X Forged (2018)

The 2018 X Forged is Callaway's tour cavity back of that era: a streamlined cavity, a compact profile, a thin top line and minimal offset, forged in Callaway's triple-net process from 1025 carbon steel. The sole is tri-level, with a bevelled leading and trailing edge, and the face carries 20V grooves. Lofts are traditional — 33 degrees at the 7-iron, 46 at the pitching wedge. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway X Forged (2018) 7-iron?

The 2018 Callaway X Forged 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When was the 2018 Callaway X Forged made?

It was introduced in 2018 and was replaced in October 2020, when Callaway announced the X Forged CB of the 2021 line alongside the Apex MB 21.

What is triple-net forging?

Callaway's term for forging the head in three successive strikes, each closer to the finished shape, which lets the final form be produced with less material removal and a more consistent grain.

Does the Callaway X Forged (2018) come in a 3-iron?

Sets were sold as 3-PW and 4-PW, but the published specification chart runs from the 4-iron down, which is what is recorded here.

Finding a used set

The Callaway X Forged (2018) ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
