About the Callaway RAZR X Black

RAZR X Black extended the RAZR X iron family for 2012, announced at the PGA Merchandise Show on 25 January 2012 and at retail from 16 March 2012 at 799 dollars for steel sets. It is an advanced game-improvement head in a black finish, one degree weaker than the 2011 RAZR X at the 7-iron (31 against 32 degrees) but a quarter inch longer at the 3-iron, on the same upright lie ladder topping out at 65 degrees through the wedges.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway RAZR X Black 7-iron?

The Callaway RAZR X Black 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a standard length of 37 inches.

When were Callaway RAZR X Black irons released?

Callaway announced them at the PGA Merchandise Show on 25 January 2012 and they reached retail on 16 March 2012.

What is the difference between the RAZR X and the RAZR X Black irons?

The Black is the 2012 refresh in a black finish with lofts a degree weaker through most of the set — a 31 degree 7-iron against 32 — and a 39 inch 3-iron against 38.75 on the 2011 RAZR X steel build.

Finding a used set

The Callaway RAZR X Black ran from 2012 to 2012 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
