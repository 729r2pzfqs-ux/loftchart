About the Callaway Paradym Star

Paradym Star is the ultra-lightweight edition of the 2023 Paradym iron, built for golfers with moderate swing speeds. The set is roughly 33 grams lighter than the standard Paradym through a lighter head, 50 gram class UST ATTAS Speed shafts and a light grip, the shafts run a quarter inch longer per club, and the lofts are weaker for launch — a 30 degree 7-iron against 29 on the standard model, opening to a 45 degree pitching wedge. The stock set ran 5-iron through sand wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Paradym Star 7-iron?

The Callaway Paradym Star 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What is the difference between Paradym and Paradym Star irons?

Star is about 33 grams lighter per club with 50 gram class graphite shafts, runs a quarter inch longer, and its lofts are around a degree weaker — a 30 degree 7-iron against 29 — to help moderate swing speeds launch the ball.

Who are Callaway Paradym Star irons for?

Golfers with moderate swing speeds who want more distance and height from an ultra-light build without moving to a ladies flex set.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Paradym Star ran from 2023 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
