About the Callaway Big Bertha Gold

The Big Bertha Gold irons were the 1996 update of Callaway's original 1994 Big Bertha, keeping the S2H2 short, straight, hollow hosel and the same loft ladder — 33 degree 7-iron, 46.5 degree pitching wedge, 50.8 degree approach wedge — while reworking the sole and weight distribution. The catalogued range ran from a 16 degree 1-iron to a 60 degree lob wedge with Callaway's typically upright 65 degree wedge lies. It gave way to the Big Bertha X-12 in 1998.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Big Bertha Gold 7-iron?

The Big Bertha Gold 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37 inch standard length.

What is the difference between the 1994 Big Bertha and the Big Bertha Gold irons?

The Gold is the 1996 refresh of the same design: the loft, lie and length ladder is unchanged, with the changes limited to the sole shape and head weighting.

What wedges came with the Big Bertha Gold irons?

The pitching wedge is 46.5 degrees, followed by a 50.8 degree approach wedge, a 55 degree sand wedge and a 60 degree lob wedge, all at a 65 degree lie.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Big Bertha Gold ran from 1996 to 1998 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
