About the Callaway Rogue ST Max OS Lite

The Rogue ST Max OS Lite is the lightweight member of Callaway's 2022 Rogue ST iron family, built for players with moderate swing speeds: it pairs the oversized Max OS head shape with lighter graphite shafts and a D0 swing weight. The head keeps the family's high-strength 450 stainless steel body, A.I.-designed Flash Face Cup, high-density tungsten weighting and urethane microspheres, and lofts are three degrees weaker than the standard Max OS through most of the set — a 31.5 degree 7-iron against 28.5 — to help flight the ball. The published range runs from a 4-iron to a 56 degree sand wedge, with 4-PW the stock set. Specs below are the standard men's graphite-shaft numbers, as the model was sold as a graphite build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Rogue ST Max OS Lite 7-iron?

The Callaway Rogue ST Max OS Lite 7-iron has a loft of 31.5 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37 inch standard length.

What is the difference between the Rogue ST Max OS and the Max OS Lite?

The Lite uses the same oversized head shape but in a lighter overall build for moderate swing speeds: light Project X Cypher graphite shafts, a D0 swing weight and lofts three degrees weaker through most of the set — a 31.5 degree 7-iron against the Max OS's 28.5.

Who are the Callaway Rogue ST Max OS Lite irons for?

Callaway aimed them at players with moderate swing speeds who want maximum forgiveness and help getting the ball airborne, which is why the set combines the oversized Max OS head with lighter shafts and weaker lofts.

When were the Callaway Rogue ST Max OS Lite irons released?

Callaway unveiled the Rogue ST line in January 2022, with pre-orders from 22 January and retail from 18 February 2022.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Rogue ST Max OS Lite ran from 2022 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
