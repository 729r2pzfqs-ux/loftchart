About the Callaway Diablo Forged

The Diablo Forged is Callaway's 2009 forged distance iron, an unusual pairing for its day: a forged carbon steel head configured with a lower, deeper centre of gravity and a penetrating trajectory for more distance than a conventional forged players iron. The lofts foreshadow the modern players-distance formula — a 31 degree 7-iron and 45 degree pitching wedge, a full two clubs stronger than the X-Forged of the same season — with half-degree steps at the top of the set and a 40 degree 9-iron. Steel lengths are recorded here; the graphite build is a quarter inch longer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Diablo Forged 7-iron?

The Callaway Diablo Forged 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a 37 inch steel-shaft length.

Are the Callaway Diablo Forged irons blades?

No. They are forged from carbon steel but configured for distance, with a low, deep centre of gravity and lofts about two clubs stronger than Callaway's X-Forged of the same year.

When were Callaway Diablo Forged irons made?

They were introduced in 2009 as part of the Diablo family and left the line in 2011, when Callaway's RAZR X range arrived.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Diablo Forged ran from 2009 to 2011 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
