About the Callaway Big Bertha OS

Announced on 7 September 2016 and at retail from 30 September, the Big Bertha OS was Callaway's oversized distance iron of the Steelhead XR generation, at $1,099 in steel and $1,299 in graphite. Its Exo-Cage construction stiffens the body so the 360 Face Cup can flex more, and a tungsten-loaded internal standing wave positions the centre of gravity progressively through the set. The head is large, with wide soles and generous offset, and the loft ladder matches the Steelhead XR at 30 degrees in the 7-iron and 44 in the pitching wedge, though the build is roughly a quarter to three-eighths of an inch longer per club. The published range runs 4-iron through sand wedge. Specs below are the standard men's steel-shaft numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Big Bertha OS 7-iron?

The Callaway Big Bertha OS 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37.25 inch standard steel length.

What is the loft of a Callaway Big Bertha OS pitching wedge?

The Big Bertha OS pitching wedge is 44 degrees, followed by a 49 degree A-wedge and a 54 degree sand wedge.

How do the Big Bertha OS and Steelhead XR irons differ?

They share the same loft ladder, but the Big Bertha OS head is larger with more offset and a wider sole, and each club is built roughly a quarter to three-eighths of an inch longer with lighter stock shafts.

When did the Callaway Big Bertha OS irons come out?

Callaway announced them on 7 September 2016 and they reached retail on 30 September 2016 at $1,099 for a steel set.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Big Bertha OS ran from 2016 to 2019 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
