About the Callaway RAZR X Muscleback

The RAZR X Muscleback is the tour blade of the RAZR era, designed by Roger Cleveland with tour input and forged from 1020 carbon steel with a white chrome finish, a thin topline, narrow sole and minimal offset. It reached golfers in mid-2011 through specialist shops, though it is commonly catalogued as a 2012 model because the wide release came later. Lofts are traditional — a 35 degree 7-iron and 47 degree pitching wedge — on lies several degrees flatter than the cast RAZR X, and the catalogued range ran 2-iron to pitching wedge with 3-PW the usual set.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway RAZR X Muscleback 7-iron?

The Callaway RAZR X Muscleback 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a standard length of 37 inches.

Are Callaway RAZR X Muscleback irons forged?

Yes. They are forged from 1020 carbon steel with a white chrome finish, designed by Roger Cleveland for highly skilled players.

When were Callaway RAZR X Muscleback irons released?

They first reached golfers in mid-2011, though many retailers catalogue them as a 2012 model because the wide release came later.

Finding a used set

The Callaway RAZR X Muscleback ran from 2011 to 2014 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
