About the Callaway X-Tour

The X-Tour was Callaway's first forged iron, a Roger Cleveland design that began shipping to pro shops on 15 April 2005 at 1,280 dollars for a 3-PW set. It marked the entry of a company built on cast game improvement heads into the traditional forged players market, and its loft ladder is correspondingly classical: a 35 degree 7-iron and a 47 degree pitching wedge, three degrees per club through the middle of the set. The X-Forged of 2007 replaced it and kept the same lofts, lies and lengths.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway X-Tour 7-iron?

The Callaway X-Tour 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a 37 inch standard length.

When were Callaway X-Tour irons released?

The X-Tour began shipping on 15 April 2005. It was Callaway's first forged iron set.

Who designed the Callaway X-Tour irons?

Roger Cleveland, Callaway's chief club designer, shaped the X-Tour. It carried traditional lofts with a 35 degree 7-iron and 47 degree pitching wedge.

Finding a used set

The Callaway X-Tour ran from 2005 to 2007 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
