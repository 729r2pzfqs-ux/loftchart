About the Callaway Apex Pro 19

The Apex Pro 19 is the compact players companion to the Apex 19, sold from the 2019 season as the third generation of Callaway's forged Apex Pro. The body is forged from 1025 mild carbon steel with roughly 50 grams of tungsten placed club by club to set the centre of gravity, and urethane microspheres behind the face to damp vibration. Lofts are traditional for a players iron — a 33 degree 7-iron and a 45 degree pitching wedge, four and a half degrees weaker than the Apex 19's short irons — and the loft, lie and length chart carried over unchanged to the Apex Pro 21 that replaced it. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Pro 19 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex Pro 19 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Pro 19 pitching wedge?

The Apex Pro 19 pitching wedge is 45 degrees, with a 50 degree A-wedge below it.

How do Apex Pro 19 lofts compare with the standard Apex 19?

The Pro is noticeably weaker through the set. Its 7-iron is 33 degrees against the Apex 19's 30.5, and its pitching wedge 45 degrees against 43, reflecting its traditional players positioning.

Did the lofts change from the Apex Pro 19 to the Apex Pro 21?

No. The Apex Pro 21 kept the same loft, lie and length chart, from the 20.5 degree 3-iron to the 50 degree A-wedge.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Apex Pro 19 ran from 2019 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
