About the Callaway Elyte HL

HL is the high-launch head in Callaway's 2025 Elyte iron family, announced on 2 January 2025 alongside the standard Elyte, Elyte X and Elyte Max Fast, and follows the Paradym Ai Smoke HL. It is a degree weaker than the standard Elyte at the 7-iron and pitching wedge, 30 and 43 degrees against 29 and 42, and a quarter of an inch longer at 37.5 inches in the 7-iron. The set runs from the 4-iron to a 56-degree sand wedge. Callaway replaced the Elyte family with the Quantum irons in January 2026; there is no separate HL model in that line, where HL became an optional loft specification on the Quantum Max and Quantum Max OS. Figures are the men's standard build, with the steel swing weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Elyte HL 7-iron?

The Callaway Elyte HL 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.25 degrees and a length of 37.50 inches.

What is the difference between the Callaway Elyte and Elyte HL?

The HL is built to launch higher. It is a degree weaker at the 7-iron and pitching wedge, 30 and 43 degrees against 29 and 42, and a quarter of an inch longer at the 7-iron, 37.5 inches against 37.25.

Are Elyte HL lofts the same as the Paradym Ai Smoke HL?

From the 7-iron to the pitching wedge, yes: 30, 34, 38 and 43 degrees. The Elyte HL is a degree stronger in the 4-, 5- and 6-irons, at 20, 23 and 26 degrees, and a degree weaker in the three wedges below the pitching wedge, at 48, 52 and 56 degrees.

Is there a Callaway Quantum HL iron?

Not as a separate model. The Quantum irons that replaced the Elyte family in January 2026 come as Max, Max OS and Max Fast, and HL is an optional loft specification on the Max and Max OS.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Elyte HL ran from 2025 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
