About the Callaway Elyte

Elyte is Callaway's 2025 iron and replaces the Paradym Ai Smoke. It pairs the Ai 10x face — Callaway's name for a face model built from ten times as many control points as the Ai Smart Face it follows — with a Speed Frame body and urethane microspheres for sound. Lofts move a degree weaker than the Ai Smoke in the mid irons, 29 degrees at the 7-iron against 28, while the pitching wedge stays at 42. The set is sold 4-SW. Figures are the men's standard build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Elyte 7-iron?

The Callaway Elyte 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

When did the Callaway Elyte irons come out?

2025, replacing the Paradym Ai Smoke.

Are Elyte lofts weaker than the Paradym Ai Smoke?

Slightly, in the mid irons — 29 degrees at the 7-iron against 28, and 33 at the 8-iron against 32. The long irons and the 42-degree pitching wedge are unchanged.

How many clubs are in a Callaway Elyte set?

Callaway publishes the chart from the 4-iron to a 55-degree sand wedge, ten heads in total, with the 4-PW the usual retail build.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Elyte ran from 2025 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
