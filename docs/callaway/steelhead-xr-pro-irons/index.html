About the Callaway Steelhead XR Pro

The Steelhead XR Pro is the compact, better-player addition to the Steelhead XR family, announced in January 2017 — a few months after the standard 2016 model — and at retail from March 2017. It keeps the family's next-generation 360 Face Cup, steel-infused polyurethane layer and hollow bore-through hosel in a smaller head with a thinner top line and a narrower sole, finished in non-glare black PVD. Lofts are around a degree weaker than the standard Steelhead XR, with a 31 degree 7-iron and a 45 degree pitching wedge, and heads ran from a 3-iron to a 50 degree A-wedge. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Steelhead XR Pro 7-iron?

The Callaway Steelhead XR Pro 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37 inches.

What is the difference between the Callaway Steelhead XR and the Steelhead XR Pro?

The Pro is a more compact head with a thinner top line, a narrower sole and less offset, finished in black PVD, and its lofts are around a degree weaker — a 31 degree 7-iron against 30, and a 45 degree pitching wedge against 44.

When were the Callaway Steelhead XR Pro irons released?

Callaway announced them in January 2017, a few months after the standard 2016 Steelhead XR, and they reached retail on 17 March 2017.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Steelhead XR Pro ran from 2017 to 2018 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
