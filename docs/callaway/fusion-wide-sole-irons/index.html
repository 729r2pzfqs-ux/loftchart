About the Callaway Fusion Wide Sole

The Fusion Wide Sole took the multi-material Big Bertha Fusion concept and added a much wider sole for maximum forgiveness from turf and sand, aimed squarely at mid- and high-handicap golfers. It was announced in late 2006 and sold as Callaway's 2007-season super-game-improvement iron. The loft ladder is identical to the original Fusion with a 33 degree 7-iron and a 46 degree pitching wedge. Specs below are the standard men's steel build with the NS Pro 990 shaft; graphite was built a quarter inch longer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Fusion Wide Sole 7-iron?

The Fusion Wide Sole 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 36.75 inch steel-shaft length.

What year did the Callaway Fusion Wide Sole irons come out?

Callaway announced the Fusion Wide Sole in late 2006 and sold it as its 2007-season super-game-improvement iron, replacing the original Big Bertha Fusion.

Who are the Fusion Wide Sole irons for?

They are super-game-improvement irons: the wide sole, multi-material head and upright 65 degree wedge lies are aimed at mid- and high-handicap golfers who want maximum forgiveness.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Fusion Wide Sole ran from 2006 to 2008 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
