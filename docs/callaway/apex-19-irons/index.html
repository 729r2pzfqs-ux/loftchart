About the Callaway Apex 19

The Apex 19 arrived for the 2019 season as the third generation of Callaway's forged players-distance iron. It keeps the forged 1025 mild carbon steel body of the CF16 but adds a 360 Face Cup across the set, internal tungsten weighting to place the centre of gravity by club, and urethane microspheres behind the face to damp vibration without slowing it. Lofts are noticeably stronger than the CF16 it replaced, moving the 7-iron from 31 to 30.5 degrees and the pitching wedge from 45 to 43 degrees. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex 19 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex 19 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, with a 62 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex 19 pitching wedge?

The Apex 19 pitching wedge is 43 degrees, with a 48 degree A-wedge and a 54 degree sand wedge.

Are the Apex 19 irons stronger lofted than the Apex CF16?

Yes. The Apex 19 is half a degree stronger at the 7-iron (30.5 versus 31 degrees) and two degrees stronger at the pitching wedge (43 versus 45 degrees), with the gap widening through the short irons.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Apex 19 ran from 2019 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
