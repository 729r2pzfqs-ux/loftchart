About the Callaway Rogue ST Max OS

The Rogue ST Max OS is the oversized model of Callaway's 2022 Rogue ST iron family, sitting between the core Max and the ultra-light Max OS Lite. It uses the same high-strength 450 stainless steel body and A.I.-designed Flash Face Cup as the Max, with up to 49 grams of high-density tungsten, but adds a wider sole, more offset and slight draw bias for maximum forgiveness. Lofts are a degree weaker than the Max at the 7-iron — 28.5 against 27.5 degrees — but the two share a 41 degree pitching wedge. Specs below are the standard men's steel-shaft numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Rogue ST Max OS 7-iron?

The Callaway Rogue ST Max OS 7-iron has a loft of 28.5 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

What is the loft of a Callaway Rogue ST Max OS pitching wedge?

The Rogue ST Max OS pitching wedge is 41 degrees, with a 46 degree A-wedge, a 51 degree gap wedge and a 56 degree sand wedge.

What is the difference between the Rogue ST Max and the Max OS?

The Max OS has a larger head with a wider sole, more offset and slight draw bias, and its lofts are a degree weaker through the middle of the set — 28.5 degrees at the 7-iron against 27.5 for the Max.

When were the Callaway Rogue ST Max OS irons released?

Callaway unveiled the Rogue ST line in January 2022, with retail from February 2022.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Rogue ST Max OS ran from 2022 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
