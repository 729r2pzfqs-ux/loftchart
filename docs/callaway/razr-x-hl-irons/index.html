About the Callaway RAZR X HL

RAZR X HL is the high-launch companion to the 2011 RAZR X, a cast game-improvement iron whose centre of gravity sits lower and deeper so shots struck low on the face still climb to full height. Lofts are identical to the standard RAZR X — a 32 degree 7-iron and 44 degree pitching wedge — but every lie is half a degree more upright, the bounce ladder runs higher, and the sand wedge carries a heavy 17 degrees of bounce. It was aimed at slower-swinging players who want the RAZR X look with more help getting the ball airborne.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway RAZR X HL 7-iron?

The Callaway RAZR X HL 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a 37 inch standard length.

What is the difference between the RAZR X and the RAZR X HL?

Lofts are the same, but the HL's centre of gravity is lower and deeper for a higher launch, every lie is half a degree more upright, and the bounce ladder runs higher — the sand wedge carries 17 degrees.

Who are the Callaway RAZR X HL irons for?

Golfers with moderate swing speeds who struggle to flight the ball. The HL launches shots higher than the standard RAZR X, even on impacts low on the face.

Finding a used set

The Callaway RAZR X HL ran from 2011 to 2012 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
