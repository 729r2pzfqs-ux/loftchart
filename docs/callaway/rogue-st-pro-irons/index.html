About the Callaway Rogue ST Pro

Rogue ST Pro is the compact head in Callaway's 2022 Rogue ST iron family, sitting under the Max, Max OS and Max OS Lite. It keeps the hollow body and tungsten weighting of the larger models in a shorter blade with less offset — 4.2 mm at the 3-iron against the Apex Pro's 3.56 at the same club, but on a much stronger loft chart, with a 30.5-degree 7-iron.

Stock shafts

  • Project X Rifle Tour Flighted 105 — steel · flexes: R, S
  • Mitsubishi Tensei AV White — graphite · 70 g (R), 80 g (S)

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Rogue ST Pro 7-iron?

The Callaway Rogue ST Pro 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

When were Callaway Rogue ST Pro irons released?

Callaway unveiled the Rogue ST line in January 2022, with pre-orders from 22 January and retail from 18 February 2022.

How does the Rogue ST Pro compare with the Apex Pro 21?

It is two and a half degrees stronger at the 7-iron — 30.5 against 33 — in a hollow-body distance head rather than a forged players head.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Rogue ST Pro ran from 2022 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
