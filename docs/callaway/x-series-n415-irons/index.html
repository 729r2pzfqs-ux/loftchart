About the Callaway X Series N415

The X Series N415 is a value-priced game-improvement set Callaway sold from 2015 through large golf retailers rather than as part of its premium line. A deep undercut cavity spreads ball speed across the face, and the build is long and upright for the price class — a 39.125 inch 4-iron, a 30 degree 7-iron and lies reaching 64 degrees at the wedges. The catalogued men's set ran 4-iron to approach wedge on Callaway's own uniflex steel shaft, with a graphite build swinging lighter at D0 against D1 for steel.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway X Series N415 7-iron?

The Callaway X Series N415 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a standard length of 37 inches.

Are Callaway X Series N415 irons any good for beginners?

They were built as a value game-improvement set with a deep undercut cavity for forgiveness, long shafts and strong lofts, sold through large retailers — a typical improving-golfer package rather than a premium line.

What clubs came in the Callaway X Series N415 set?

The catalogued men's range ran 4-iron through approach wedge, with 5-PW and larger make-ups sold at retail.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2015, the Callaway X Series N415 is a current game improvement set in the Callaway range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
