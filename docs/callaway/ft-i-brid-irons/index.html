About the Callaway FT i-brid

The FT i-brid was the last iron of Callaway's Fusion era, a super-game-improvement set in which the 3-, 4- and 5-irons are full i-brid hybrid-bodied clubs and the rest of the set carries hybrid-like weighting in an iron profile. It used a modified Tru-Bore design with a polycarbonate tip plug at the end of the shaft to damp vibration. Lofts are weak by modern standards with a 32 degree 7-iron and 44 degree pitching wedge, and the i-brid long clubs run noticeably longer than conventional irons.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway FT i-brid 7-iron?

The FT i-brid 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37 inch standard length.

What are the i-brid clubs in the Callaway FT i-brid set?

The 3-, 4- and 5-irons (19, 22 and 25 degrees) are full hybrid-bodied i-brids built at 39.75, 39 and 38.25 inches; from the 6-iron down the set transitions to iron-profile heads.

Are the Callaway FT i-brid irons good for high handicappers?

Yes — the FT i-brid is a super-game-improvement design: hybrid long clubs, weak lofts, upright 65 degree wedge lies and heavy perimeter weighting make it one of the easiest-launching sets Callaway made.

Finding a used set

The Callaway FT i-brid ran from 2008 to 2010 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
