About the Callaway X Forged CB (2021)

The 2021 X Forged CB is Callaway's tour cavity back, announced on 13 October 2020 alongside the Apex MB and X Forged UT and at retail from 29 October 2020, which is why it is catalogued to 2020 here despite the 2021 model name. Unlike the typical one-piece forging in this category, it pairs a forged 1025 mild carbon steel body with custom internal and external MIM'd tungsten weights that centre the CG behind the face, and a Tour Tuned face plate. Lofts are traditional — a 33 degree 7-iron and a 45 degree pitching wedge with a 49 degree A-wedge. Specs below are the standard men's steel-shaft numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway X Forged CB (2021) 7-iron?

The 2021 X Forged CB 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

What is the loft of a Callaway X Forged CB (2021) pitching wedge?

The X Forged CB pitching wedge is 45 degrees, with a 49 degree A-wedge below it.

When were the 2021 Callaway X Forged CB irons released?

Callaway announced them on 13 October 2020 alongside the Apex MB and X Forged UT, with retail from 29 October 2020, marketed as the 2021 line.

Is the 2021 X Forged CB a one-piece forging?

No. Unusually for a tour cavity back, it combines a forged 1025 mild carbon steel body with internal and external MIM'd tungsten weights and a Tour Tuned face plate.

Finding a used set

The Callaway X Forged CB (2021) ran from 2020 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
