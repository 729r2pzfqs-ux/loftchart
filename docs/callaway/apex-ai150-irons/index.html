About the Callaway Apex Ai150

The Apex Ai150 is the compact players-distance iron in Callaway's Apex Ai range, launched in July 2025 as the replacement for the Apex Pro, with pre-orders from 10 July and retail from 25 July 2025. It is a forged 1025 hollow body with a 455 face cup in the 3- to 7-irons and a 17-4 face from the 8-iron down, and uses the Ai10x face. Lofts are two degrees stronger than the Apex Pro through the middle of the set, 31 degrees at the 7-iron against 33, and one degree weaker than the Apex Ai200, with a 44-degree pitching wedge. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build; the 3-iron is made right-handed only, and swing weight and bounce are not published.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex Ai150 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex Ai150 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When did the Callaway Apex Ai150 irons come out?

Callaway launched them in July 2025, with pre-orders from 10 July and retail from 25 July 2025, as the replacement for the Apex Pro.

Are Apex Ai150 lofts stronger than the Apex Pro?

Yes. The Ai150 is two degrees stronger through the middle of the set, 31 degrees at the 7-iron against 33 on the Apex Pro '24, and one degree stronger at the pitching wedge, 44 degrees against 45.

What is the difference between the Apex Ai150 and the Apex Ai200?

The Ai150 is the more compact of the two and its lofts are a degree weaker: 31 degrees at the 7-iron and 44 at the pitching wedge, against 30 and 43 on the Ai200. It also carries less offset, 0.093 inches at the 7-iron against 0.122.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Callaway Apex Ai150 is a current players distance set in the Callaway range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Callaway Apex Pro (2024). New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
