About the Callaway X-20

The X-20 was Callaway's 2007 game-improvement iron, replacing the X-18 in the long-running X-series. It combined Variable Face Thickness technology with "Extreme Notch Weighting" perimeter weighting and the S2H2 hosel to push mass low and to the edges of the head. Callaway published a lower centre of gravity than the X-18. The set ran 3-PW with a 2-iron, A-wedge, sand wedge and lob wedge available. A separate X-20 Tour model with a smaller head and different lofts was sold alongside it. Specs below are the standard men's steel-shaft numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway X-20 7-iron?

The Callaway X-20 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees.

What is the loft of a Callaway X-20 pitching wedge and A-wedge?

The Callaway X-20 pitching wedge is 45 degrees and the A-wedge (gap wedge) is 50 degrees.

How long is a standard Callaway X-20 7-iron?

The standard men's steel-shaft Callaway X-20 7-iron is 36.75 inches with a 62.5 degree lie angle.

Finding a used set

The Callaway X-20 ran from 2007 to 2008 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
