About the Callaway Mavrik Pro

The Mavrik Pro is the compact model of Callaway's 2020 Mavrik family, announced in January 2020 with retail from 6 February. It carries the same A.I.-designed Flash Face Cup, Tungsten Energy Core and urethane microspheres as the standard Mavrik, but in a shorter blade with a thinner sole and less offset, and on much weaker lofts — a 30.5 degree 7-iron and a 43 degree pitching wedge against the standard model's 27 and 41. Heads ran from a 3-iron to a 48 degree A-wedge, with 4-PW the stock set. Specs below are the standard men's steel-shaft numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Mavrik Pro 7-iron?

The Callaway Mavrik Pro 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37 inches.

Why are the Mavrik Pro lofts weaker than the standard Mavrik?

Because the standard Mavrik was the distance set in the family, at 27 degrees in the 7-iron. The Pro targets players who generate their own launch and spin, so it uses more traditional lofts — a 30.5 degree 7-iron and a 43 degree pitching wedge — in a shorter, thinner-soled head with less offset.

When were the Callaway Mavrik Pro irons released?

Callaway announced the Mavrik family in January 2020, with pre-orders from 14 January and retail from 6 February 2020.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Mavrik Pro ran from 2020 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
