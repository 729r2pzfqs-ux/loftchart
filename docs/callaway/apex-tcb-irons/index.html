About the Callaway Apex TCB

The Apex TCB — Tour Cavity Back — is the tour-inspired players cavity back Callaway built for its staff professionals, slotting between the Apex MB blade and the Apex Pro. Prototypes were on tour from late 2020, and Callaway unveiled it publicly on 12 January 2021 with the Apex 21 range, selling it custom-fit only from 11 February 2021 in 3-iron through pitching wedge with an approach wedge available. The head is a forged 1025 mild carbon steel body with metal-injection-moulded tungsten weighting, and lofts are traditional: a 34-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge, with very little offset in the scoring clubs. Jon Rahm won the 2021 U.S. Open with the TCB and Xander Schauffele played it for years afterwards. It left the line when the Apex CB was announced in 2023 as the cavity back of the '24 Apex tour range. Figures are the men's standard steel build; bounce and swing weight were not published and are omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Apex TCB 7-iron?

The Callaway Apex TCB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When did the Callaway Apex TCB come out?

Callaway unveiled it on 12 January 2021 with the Apex 21 range, after prototypes appeared on tour in late 2020. It reached retail on 11 February 2021, sold custom-fit only.

Which tour players used the Callaway Apex TCB?

Jon Rahm won the 2021 U.S. Open with it, and Xander Schauffele played the TCB for years — Callaway later released a limited run of his raw-finish version.

What replaced the Callaway Apex TCB?

The Apex CB, announced in 2023 as the forged cavity back of Callaway's '24 tour range alongside the Apex Pro and Apex MB.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Apex TCB ran from 2021 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
