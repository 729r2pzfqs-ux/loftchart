About the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke

Paradym Ai Smoke is Callaway's 2024 game-improvement iron and the first to use the Ai Smart Face, a variable-thickness face whose geometry was generated from swing data — club delivery and face orientation at impact — rather than from a single optimisation point. Urethane microspheres behind the face control sound and feel. Lofts are strong: 28 degrees at the 7-iron and 42 at the pitching wedge, with a four-wedge ladder underneath. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke 7-iron?

The Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.25 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

Is there a Callaway iron called just Ai Smoke?

No. The 2024 family is Paradym Ai Smoke, with HL and MAX Fast variants. Ai Smoke on its own is the technology name, not a separate iron model.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Paradym Ai Smoke?

42 degrees, with 46-, 50- and 54-degree wedges available underneath to keep the gapping usable.

What is the Ai Smart Face?

A variable-thickness face whose thickness map was generated from recorded golfer data — swing speed, club delivery and face orientation at impact — rather than optimised for a single centred strike.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke ran from 2024 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
