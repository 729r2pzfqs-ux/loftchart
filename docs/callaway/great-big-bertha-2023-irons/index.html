About the Callaway Great Big Bertha (2023)

The Great Big Bertha irons are the flagship of Callaway's Great Big Bertha family, announced in late October 2022 with retail from 11 November 2022 and catalogued as the 2023 line, sitting above the standard Big Bertha at a super-premium price. The head is a hollow two-piece titanium construction — a forged titanium face on a commercially pure titanium body — with a tungsten speed cartridge of up to 145 grams low in the trailing edge, reviving the titanium-iron idea Callaway last sold in the 1990s Great Big Bertha era. Lofts are moderate for a distance iron, with a 30 degree 7-iron and a 43 degree pitching wedge, and the chart runs from the 4-iron to a 54 degree sand wedge with no gap wedge. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Great Big Bertha 7-iron?

The 2023 Great Big Bertha 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37 inch standard length.

What is the difference between the 2023 Big Bertha and Great Big Bertha irons?

The Great Big Bertha is the titanium flagship: a forged titanium face on a commercially pure titanium body with up to 145 grams of tungsten, sold at a super-premium price. Its lofts are also weaker — a 30 degree 7-iron and 43 degree pitching wedge against 27 and 42 degrees for the steel-bodied Big Bertha.

Are the 2023 Great Big Bertha irons really made of titanium?

Yes. The head is a hollow two-piece titanium construction — a forged titanium face on a commercially pure titanium body — with tungsten used to restore mass low in the head. Titanium's light weight is what allows up to 145 grams of tungsten placement.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Callaway Great Big Bertha (2023) is a current game improvement set in the Callaway range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
