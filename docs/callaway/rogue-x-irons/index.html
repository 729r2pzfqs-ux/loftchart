About the Callaway Rogue X

Rogue X is the distance model of Callaway's 2018 Rogue iron family, sold alongside the standard Rogue and the Rogue Pro from February 2018. It shares the 360 Face Cup with Variable Face Thickness, the tungsten weighting and the urethane microspheres of the standard model, but in a larger head with a deeper centre of gravity, a lighter overall build, longer shafts in the long irons and much stronger lofts — the 27 degree 7-iron is three degrees stronger than the standard Rogue's. The set was sold 4-SW, without the 3-iron and lob wedge of the standard model. Specs below are the standard men's steel-shaft numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway Rogue X 7-iron?

The Callaway Rogue X 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37.125 inch standard steel length.

What is the loft of a Callaway Rogue X pitching wedge?

The Rogue X pitching wedge is 41 degrees, with a 46 degree A-wedge, a 51 degree gap wedge and a 56 degree sand wedge.

How do the Rogue X lofts compare with the standard Callaway Rogue?

They are much stronger. The Rogue X 7-iron is 27 degrees against 30 for the standard Rogue, and the pitching wedge is 41 degrees against 44, with the long irons also built to slightly longer lengths.

Does the Callaway Rogue X come in a 3-iron?

No. The Rogue X was sold 4-SW. Its 18.5 degree 4-iron carries roughly the loft of a traditional 2-iron, which is why the set starts there.

Finding a used set

The Callaway Rogue X ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
