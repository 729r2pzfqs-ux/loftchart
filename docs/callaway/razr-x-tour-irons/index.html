About the Callaway RAZR X Tour

RAZR X Tour was the better-player model in the RAZR X line Callaway announced on 20 January 2011, sitting between the game-improvement RAZR X and the forged RAZR X Forged. It is a cast iron for low-to-mid handicap players, with a more compact head than the RAZR X and lofts a degree weaker through most of the set — a 33 degree 7-iron and 45 degree pitching wedge — on the same 63.5 degree short-iron lie plane as Callaway's earlier Tour models rather than the upright 65 degrees of the standard head. A 50 degree approach wedge extended the set.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Callaway RAZR X Tour 7-iron?

The Callaway RAZR X Tour 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a 37 inch standard length.

What is the difference between the RAZR X and the RAZR X Tour?

The RAZR X Tour has a more compact head with lofts a degree weaker through most of the set — a 33 degree 7-iron against 32 — and flatter short-iron lies of 63.5 degrees against 65 on the standard RAZR X.

Is the Callaway RAZR X Tour forged?

No, it is a cast stainless steel head. The forged iron in the 2011 RAZR line was the RAZR X Forged, a separate model with its own specifications.

Finding a used set

The Callaway RAZR X Tour ran from 2011 to 2012 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
