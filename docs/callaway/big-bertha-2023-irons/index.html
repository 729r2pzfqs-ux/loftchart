About the Callaway Big Bertha (2023)

The 2023 Big Bertha irons continue Callaway's premium game-improvement line above the core Paradym family, replacing the Big Bertha B21. Callaway announced them with the women's Big Bertha REVA on 18 April 2023, with retail from 26 May 2023 at 999.99 dollars for a six-piece steel set. The package was refined over the B21 — lofts a degree stronger through most of the set, a gap wedge added to the chart and the lob wedge dropped — while keeping an A.I.-designed Flash Face Cup, tungsten weighting, urethane microspheres and the B21's generous offset, still around 8 mm at the 4-iron. Lofts are strong for the category — a 27 degree 7-iron and a 42 degree pitching wedge — and the chart runs from an 18 degree 4-iron to a 56 degree sand wedge. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a 2023 Callaway Big Bertha 7-iron?

The 2023 Big Bertha 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, with a 62.5 degree lie and a 37 inch standard length.

What is the loft of the 2023 Big Bertha pitching wedge?

The pitching wedge is 42 degrees, followed by a 47 degree A-wedge, a 52 degree gap wedge and a 56 degree sand wedge.

What changed from the Big Bertha B21 to the 2023 Big Bertha?

The 2023 model kept the A.I.-designed Flash Face Cup, the tungsten weighting and essentially the B21's offset ladder — both run about 8 mm at the 4-iron — but strengthened the lofts by roughly a degree per club, added a 52 degree gap wedge to the chart and dropped the B21's lob wedge.

Production status

Introduced in 2023, the Callaway Big Bertha (2023) has no confirmed end date on file. It replaced the Callaway Big Bertha B21. LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the Callaway catalogue — check with Callaway or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
