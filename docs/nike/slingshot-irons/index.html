About the Nike Slingshot

The Slingshot is the original model in Nike's longest-running iron family, designed by Tom Stites and built around the feature that named it: a Slingback bridge bar that stretches across the back cavity, moving weight low, deep and toward the heel in the long irons and progressively higher and toward the toe as the set shortens. The face is cold-rolled Custom 455 stainless steel, CNC-milled flat, on a wide-soled cavity body. Lofts are moderate for a game-improvement set of its era, with a 34-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge. Nike's own spec chart runs 2-iron through sand wedge: the men's stock set is 3-PW, with the 2-iron, 50-degree A-wedge and 55-degree sand wedge sold individually, and a women's 4-SW set built one inch shorter. Stock shafts were Nike's Speed Step lightweight steel and a constant-weight Slingshot graphite. Nike's Slingshot mini-site was live by October 2003 and the set made Golf Digest's inaugural 2004 Hot List, so it is commonly catalogued as a 2004 model; year_introduced records the 2003 public debut per the repository convention.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Nike Slingshot 7-iron?

The Nike Slingshot 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

What clubs came in a stock Nike Slingshot set?

The men's stock set was 3-iron through pitching wedge, in steel or graphite. The 2-iron, the 50-degree A-wedge and the 55-degree sand wedge were sold individually, and a women's 4-iron-through-sand-wedge set was offered in graphite, built one inch shorter per club.

When did the original Nike Slingshot irons come out?

Nike's Slingshot mini-site was live by October 2003 and the set was named to Golf Digest's inaugural 2004 Hot List, so it is usually catalogued as a 2004 model. It was replaced by the Slingshot OSS in 2006.

What made the Nike Slingshot design distinctive?

The Slingback bridge — a bar spanning the back cavity that let Nike move weight low, deep and toward the heel in the long irons for easier launch, shifting progressively higher and toward the toe in the short irons for control. It also gave the irons their unconventional look at address.

Finding a used set

The Nike Slingshot ran from 2003 to 2006 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
