About the Nike Slingshot 4D

The Slingshot 4D was the final generation of Nike's Slingshot family, named for four design elements that change progressively through the set: the position of the rear Slingback bridge, the sole width, the degree of offset and the overall head profile. Long irons get a wider sole and a lower, deeper Slingback for launch, while the short irons tighten up for control. Lofts carry over from the 2007 Slingshot, with a 34-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge. The stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge with approach and 55-degree sand wedges available; figures below are the standard men's steel specification (women's builds run one inch shorter).

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Nike Slingshot 4D 7-iron?

The Nike Slingshot 4D 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a lie around 63.5 degrees and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

What does 4D mean on the Nike Slingshot 4D irons?

It refers to four design elements that change progressively through the set: the position of the rear Slingback bridge, the sole width, the amount of offset and the overall clubhead profile.

What clubs come in a stock Nike Slingshot 4D set?

The stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge, with an approach wedge and a 55-degree sand wedge available to complete the set.

Finding a used set

The Nike Slingshot 4D ran from 2009 to 2010 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
