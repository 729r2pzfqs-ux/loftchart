About the Nike VR Full Cavity

The Victory Red Full Cavity - retailers and reviewers shortened it to "VR Full Cavity" well before Nike itself adopted the VR abbreviation for the 2010 VR Pro generation - was the third and most forgiving tier of Nike's three-way Victory Red iron launch, announced alongside the muscleback Victory Red Forged TW Blade and the mid-tier Victory Red Forged Split Cavity. It is cast from 431 stainless steel, about 25% softer than the 17-4 stainless commonly used elsewhere, with a deep undercut cavity, wide sole, thick topline, more offset than its forged siblings, and progressive face thickness for a lower, deeper centre of gravity. Lofts start level with the Split Cavity and TW Blade at the 4-iron (24 degrees) but run about two degrees stronger by the pitching wedge (45 versus 47), consistent with its game-improvement, more distance-oriented role. The stock set is 4-iron through approach wedge in steel or graphite, with a women's 5-iron through sand wedge set offered in graphite only.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Nike VR Full Cavity 7-iron?

The Nike VR Full Cavity 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length (36.5 inches in the graphite version).

How does the VR Full Cavity compare to the Victory Red Split Cavity and TW Blade?

The Full Cavity shares an identical 24-degree 4-iron with its forged siblings but runs progressively stronger from there - a 33-degree 7-iron against 35 degrees for both the Split Cavity and TW Blade, and a 45-degree pitching wedge against their 47 degrees - reflecting its game-improvement, more distance-oriented design and added offset.

What clubs came in a stock Nike Victory Red Full Cavity set?

The standard set was 4-iron through a 50-degree approach wedge (8 clubs), offered in steel or graphite. A women's 5-iron through sand wedge set was offered in graphite only. No 3-iron appears on the retailer specification tables found for this model.

Was the Nike VR Full Cavity introduced in 2010?

No. It was announced in 2008 (26 August 2008 per Nike's press release, with a 1 November 2008 retail date) as part of the same three-way Victory Red launch as the Victory Red Forged TW Blade and Forged Split Cavity. It remained in retail and resale channels well into 2010-2011, which is likely why it is sometimes associated with the 2010 VR Pro generation, but no evidence of a separate 2010 launch was found.

Finding a used set

The Nike VR Full Cavity ran from 2008 to 2010 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
