About the Nike Vapor Pro Combo

The Vapor Pro Combo is the middle model of the Vapor iron family Nike unveiled in August 2014 and sold as its 2015 line, sitting between the Vapor Pro blade and the game-improvement Vapor Speed. It is a forged 1025 carbon steel combo set: the 2- to 7-irons have a cavity filled with Nike's RZN resin behind a spring steel face, and the 8-iron through A-wedge are one-piece muscle-cavity heads. Lofts are one degree stronger than the Vapor Pro throughout, with a 34-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge. Nike's chart runs from a right-hand-only 2-iron to a 51-degree A-wedge; the boxed retail set was eight clubs, 4-iron through A-wedge, with True Temper DG Pro steel shafts.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Nike Vapor Pro Combo 7-iron?

The Nike Vapor Pro Combo 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Nike Vapor Pro Combo?

The Vapor Pro Combo pitching wedge is 46 degrees, and the matching A-wedge is 51 degrees, a five-degree gap.

How do the Nike Vapor Pro Combo lofts compare to the Vapor Pro?

The Vapor Pro Combo is one degree stronger in every club: its 7-iron is 34 degrees against 35 for the Vapor Pro, and its pitching wedge 46 degrees against 47. Lengths and lies are the same, while the Pro Combo has more offset, 0.125 inches at the 7-iron against 0.080.

Which Nike Vapor Pro Combo irons have the RZN cavity?

The 2- through 7-irons have the RZN-filled cavity with a spring steel face. The 8-iron, 9-iron, pitching wedge and A-wedge are muscle-cavity heads.

Finding a used set

The Nike Vapor Pro Combo ran from 2014 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
