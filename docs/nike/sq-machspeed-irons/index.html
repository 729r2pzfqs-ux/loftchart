About the Nike SQ MachSpeed

The SQ MachSpeed is a super-game-improvement cavity-back iron Nike Golf introduced for retail on February 25, 2010, carrying the "SQ" (Sasquatch) branding it had used since the mid-2000s on its square-headed, forgiveness-focused drivers and fairway woods over to an iron aimed at mid-to-high handicappers. A two-piece, off-the-face-weld construction pairs a stainless-steel body with a hot AM355 alloy face insert, and a deep undercut behind the face relocates roughly 50 grams of weight into Nike's PowerBow perimeter-weighting system for a low, deep center of gravity, higher launch and a softer landing. The stock men's steel set ran 4-iron through pitching wedge plus an approach/gap wedge (8 clubs), with a 3-iron and sand wedge available individually and a women's set substituting 5- and 6-hybrids for the longest irons. Lofts run from 20 degrees in the 3-iron up to 50 degrees in the approach wedge, with the 7-iron at 33 degrees.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Nike SQ MachSpeed 7-iron?

The Nike SQ MachSpeed 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 64-degree lie and a 37.25-inch steel-shaft length.

What clubs came in a stock Nike SQ MachSpeed iron set?

The men's stock steel set ran 4-iron through pitching wedge plus an approach/gap wedge (8 clubs), with a 3-iron and sand wedge sold individually. The women's set substituted 5- and 6-hybrids for the longest irons ahead of a 7-iron-through-sand-wedge graphite run.

What does the 'SQ' in Nike SQ MachSpeed mean?

'SQ' is short for Sasquatch, the branding Nike had already used for years on its square-headed forgiveness-focused drivers and fairway woods. Applied to the MachSpeed iron it signals the same game-improvement, forgiveness-first positioning rather than describing a square clubhead shape.

What replaced the Nike SQ MachSpeed irons?

Nike's SQ MachSpeed and Slingshot lines both remained in the 2011 super-game-improvement lineup before Nike replaced them with the VR_S irons for 2012.

Finding a used set

The Nike SQ MachSpeed ran from 2010 to 2011 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
