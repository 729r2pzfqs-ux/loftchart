About the Nike Vapor Speed

Vapor Speed is the game-improvement model of the Vapor family Nike unveiled with Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy in August 2014 — the forgiving counterpart to the Vapor Pro and Vapor Pro Combo. Weight sits low and forward behind the face throughout the set for a higher, more forgiving launch, and the set is built long, at 39.75 inches for the 3-iron. The sand wedge is much heavier than the rest of the set, at D5.5.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Nike Vapor Speed 7-iron?

The Nike Vapor Speed 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.75 inches.

When were Nike Vapor Speed irons made?

Nike unveiled the Vapor family in August 2014 and the Vapor Speed irons reached retail on 31 January 2015.

Why is the Nike Vapor Speed sand wedge so much heavier?

It is built to D5.5 against D2.5 for the rest of the set — three full swing-weight points heavier — which is normal for a wedge sold as part of an iron set but unusually large a jump here.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2015, the Nike Vapor Speed is a current game improvement set in the Nike range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
