About the Nike VR_S Forged

The VR_S Forged paired Nike's NexCOR variable-thickness face with a forged players-sized cavity back, aimed at better players who wanted the 2012 VR_S distance story in a compact head with modest offset. Lofts are strong for a players iron of the era - a 31-degree 7-iron and a 45-degree pitching wedge - with wider gaps through the scoring clubs down to the 51-degree approach wedge. The stock set is 4-iron through approach wedge on the Nippon N.S. Pro 950GH HT steel shaft, a high-launch profile chosen to offset the strong lofts; a 3-iron and sand wedge were available outside the stock set. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Nike VR_S Forged 7-iron?

The Nike VR_S Forged 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length.

Are the Nike VR_S Forged irons strong lofted?

Yes, for their class. The 7-iron is 31 degrees and the pitching wedge 45 degrees - roughly two to four degrees stronger than Nike's traditional players irons like the VR Pro Combo - which is why Nike paired them with the high-launch N.S. Pro 950GH HT shaft.

What clubs come in a stock Nike VR_S Forged set?

The stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge plus a 51-degree approach wedge, with a 3-iron and sand wedge available outside the stock set.

Finding a used set

The Nike VR_S Forged ran from 2012 to 2013 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
