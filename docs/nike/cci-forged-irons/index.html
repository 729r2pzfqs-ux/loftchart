About the Nike CCi Forged

The CCi Forged was announced in 2006 as the better-player half of a two-iron split that took over from the Forged Pro Combo/Pro Combo Tour, arriving alongside the more forgiving cast CCi (successor to the Pro Combo OS). "CCi" is Nike's name for a Composite Cavity Insert - a tungsten-and-composite bar running heel to toe low on the back of the head, intended to lower the center of gravity without the thick topline or heavy offset of a full cavity back. The heads are forged from soft 1025 carbon steel with a CNC-milled face, and reviewers described the result as closer to a muscleback than a cavity back, with a thin topline and minimal offset. Lofts are traditional for the mid-2000s, from an individually sold 18-degree 2-iron down to a 47-degree pitching wedge, and Nike replaced the model in 2008 with the Victory Red Forged Split Cavity.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Nike CCi Forged 7-iron?

The Nike CCi Forged 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, with 6 degrees of bounce and 0.095 inches of offset - a traditional, low-offset spec aimed at better players.

What does CCi stand for on Nike's CCi Forged irons?

CCi stands for Composite Cavity Insert - a tungsten-and-composite bar Nike ran low across the back of the head, from heel to toe, to lower the center of gravity while keeping a thin topline and minimal offset more typical of a muscleback blade.

What is the Nike CCi Forged made of?

The heads are forged from soft 1025 carbon steel with a CNC-milled face, paired stock with True Temper Dynamic Gold steel shafts and Golf Pride Tour Velvet ribbed grips.

What replaced the Nike CCi Forged irons?

Nike announced the Victory Red line in August 2008, and the Victory Red Forged Split Cavity took over the CCi Forged's role as Nike's better-player forged cavity iron at retail that November.

Finding a used set

The Nike CCi Forged ran from 2006 to 2008 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
