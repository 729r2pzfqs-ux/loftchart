About the Sub 70 699 V2

Second generation of the hollow-body distance iron from Sub 70, the direct-to-consumer maker in Sycamore, Illinois, announced in early January 2023 after what the company described as three years of development on the original 699 of 2019. The two-piece head keeps the TPE resin-injected hollow body in 431 stainless steel and adds a 1.7 mm variable-face-thickness (VFT) face in S450 aerospace steel, aimed at more consistent ball speed across the face. It is the wider-bodied, more offset sibling of the 699 Pro V2 — the two share an identical loft, lie, length and bounce chart (30 degrees at the 7-iron, 43 at the pitching wedge), but the standard 699 V2 carries roughly twice the offset and an 8 mm topline on a longer 75 mm blade. Heads run 4-iron through sand wedge with set-matching gap and sand wedges; a single-length version built to 7-iron length and lie is sold separately. Sub 70 builds every set to order with a wide menu of no-upcharge shafts and grips, in Brushed Satin and black finishes. The 2025 discontinuation date is the announcement of the 699 V3; Sub 70 continued to sell the V2 alongside it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Sub 70 699 V2 7-iron?

The Sub 70 699 V2 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a standard steel length of 37 inches.

What is the difference between the Sub 70 699 V2 and the 699 Pro V2?

They share the same lofts, lies, lengths and bounces, but the standard 699 V2 is the game-improvement head: a longer 75 mm blade with roughly twice the offset (0.16 inches at the 7-iron against the Pro's 0.10) and a slightly heavier head at each club. The Pro V2 also adds a 17-degree 3-iron that the standard set does not offer.

Is the Sub 70 699 V2 forged?

No. It is a two-piece hollow-body iron with a 431 stainless steel body, a TPE resin-injected cavity and a 1.7 mm S450 aerospace steel face.

Did the Sub 70 699 V2 come in a single-length version?

Yes. Sub 70 sold a 699 v2 SL variant with every club built to 7-iron length and lie, alongside the conventional progressive-length set listed here.

Finding a used set

The Sub 70 699 V2 ran from 2023 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
