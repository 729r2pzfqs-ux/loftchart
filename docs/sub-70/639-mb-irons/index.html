About the Sub 70 639 MB

The 639 MB was the muscle-back half of Sub 70's first forged iron family, released in July 2019 alongside the 639 CB from the direct-to-consumer maker in Sycamore, Illinois. Each head is precision forged from a solid billet of what Sub 70's copy calls DT-4 soft stainless steel and finished with a custom milling process; the blade keeps a thin top line — 4.6 mm at the 6-iron on a 74 mm blade length — but a moderate face size intended to add confidence over a traditional musclerback. The chart runs 3-PW with a 32-degree 7-iron and, unlike the 639 CB's 44-degree pitching wedge, a 45-degree PW. The 659 MB of 2022 was designed as its successor, but Sub 70 kept 639 heads in the range, and as of a May 2025 site capture the 639 MB was still sold in factory MB/CB combo sets, so no discontinuation year is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Sub 70 639 MB 7-iron?

The Sub 70 639 MB 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a standard steel length of 37 inches.

Do the Sub 70 639 MB and 639 CB share the same lofts?

Almost. The two share lies, lengths and lofts from the 3-iron through 9-iron, but the MB's pitching wedge is 45 degrees against the CB's 44.

Is the Sub 70 639 MB a true blade?

Yes — it is a forged muscle-back with a 4.6 mm top line at the 6-iron, though Sub 70 kept the face at a moderate size to make it more approachable than a traditional tour blade.

Is the Sub 70 639 MB still available?

Sub 70 launched the 659 MB as its successor in 2022 but kept the 639 MB around; as of May 2025 it was still sold in factory 639 MB/CB combo sets.

Finding a used set

The Sub 70 639 MB ran from 2019 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
