About the Sub 70 649 MB Tour

The 649 MB Tour is the tour-level blade from Sub 70, the direct-to-consumer maker in Sycamore, Illinois, released on 26 January 2021 after what the company described as two years of input from tour-level players. It starts from the compact 639 MB but widens the sole by a millimetre, trims offset to a uniform 0.5 mm through the whole set — effectively a neutral face — and ports 6 grams from the rear centre of the head, replacing it with a 6-gram tungsten weight in the toe to keep the face from rotating through impact and, in Sub 70's words, eliminate the left miss. Lofts are traditional (34-degree 7-iron, 46-degree pitching wedge) across a 3-PW chart with a 4 mm topline and a 76 mm blade at the 6-iron. Sold in satin and raw finishes — the raw heads ship unplated and rust into a patina — and still in the line alongside the 2025 649 MB TR. Every set is built to order from Sub 70's no-upcharge shaft and grip menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Sub 70 649 MB Tour 7-iron?

The Sub 70 649 MB Tour 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a standard steel length of 37 inches.

How much offset does the Sub 70 649 MB Tour have?

A uniform 0.5 millimetres (0.02 inches) on every club from 3-iron to pitching wedge — Sub 70 designed it as an effectively neutral face for players who fight a left miss.

What does the tungsten weight in the Sub 70 649 MB Tour do?

Six grams are ported out of the rear centre of the head and replaced with a 6-gram tungsten plug in the toe, which resists face rotation through impact to guard against the hook.

Is the Sub 70 649 MB Tour still made?

Yes. As of 2026 it is still sold in satin, raw and taper-tip builds, with the 649 MB TR added alongside it in 2025 rather than replacing it.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2021, the Sub 70 649 MB Tour is a current blade set in the Sub 70 range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Sub 70 639 MB. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
