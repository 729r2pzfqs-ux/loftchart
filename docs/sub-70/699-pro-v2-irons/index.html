About the Sub 70 699 Pro V2

The compact players-distance head of Sub 70's January 2023 hollow-body pair, built by the direct-to-consumer maker in Sycamore, Illinois as the successor to the original 699 Pro of 2019. It keeps the two-piece, TPE resin-injected hollow body in 431 stainless steel and adds the generation's 1.7 mm variable-face-thickness (VFT) face in S450 aerospace steel, with an expanded channel-back, two internal reinforcing ribs, a squared leading-edge grind and a 4-way cambered sole. Against the standard 699 V2 it runs a shorter 72 mm blade with a narrower sole and roughly half the offset — 0.10 inches at the 7-iron, tapering to zero at the gap wedge and slightly onset at the sand wedge — on an identical loft chart: 30 degrees at the 7-iron, 43 at the pitching wedge. The head range is unusually wide, from a 17-degree 3-iron through a CNC-milled-face 54-degree sand wedge, and Sub 70 also sold a matching 699 Pro v2 utility iron. Every set is built to order with no-upcharge shaft and grip options, in Satin and black DLC finishes. The 2025 discontinuation date is the announcement of the 699 Pro V3; Sub 70 continued to sell the V2 alongside it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Sub 70 699 Pro V2 7-iron?

The Sub 70 699 Pro V2 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a standard steel length of 37 inches.

How does the Sub 70 699 Pro V2 differ from the standard 699 V2?

The loft, lie, length and bounce charts are identical, but the Pro V2 has a shorter 72 mm blade, a narrower sole and about half the offset — 0.10 inches at the 7-iron against the standard head's 0.16 — plus a 17-degree 3-iron the standard set does not offer.

Is the Sub 70 699 Pro V2 forged?

No. Like the standard 699 V2 it is a two-piece hollow-body iron: a 431 stainless steel body with a TPE resin-injected cavity and a 1.7 mm S450 aerospace steel variable-thickness face.

What wedges matched the Sub 70 699 Pro V2 set?

Sub 70 offered set-matching gap (48 degree) and sand (54 degree) wedges; the sand wedge face is CNC milled for extra spin, and a matching 699 Pro v2 utility iron was sold separately.

Finding a used set

The Sub 70 699 Pro V2 ran from 2023 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
