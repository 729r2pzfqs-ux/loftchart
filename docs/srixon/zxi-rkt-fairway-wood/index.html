About the Srixon ZXi RKT

The ZXi RKT fairway woods reached retail on 16 October 2026 with the ZXi RKT drivers and hybrids, replacing the ZXi woods after less than two seasons. The headline change is the face: a new VR-Titanium alloy with the i-FLEX milled pattern, which Srixon pairs with revised acoustics and — new to its fairways — an adjustable sole weight (10 g stock, swappable from 2 g to 16 g) alongside the 12-position QTS 1.5 hosel sleeve. Four lofts run from a 13.5° 3+ wood to a 21° 7-wood, with slightly smaller heads than the outgoing ZXi and the Kai'li Dark Waves Blue as the stock shaft.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Srixon ZXi RKT fairway woods come in?

Four lofts: a 13.5 degree 3+ wood, a 15 degree 3-wood, an 18 degree 5-wood and a 21 degree 7-wood. The 3+ is right-hand only; the 15 and 18 come in both hands.

Are the Srixon ZXi RKT fairway woods adjustable?

Doubly so — every head carries the 12-position QTS 1.5 sleeve (plus or minus 1.5 degrees of loft with flat-lie options to 3 degrees) and, new for this generation, a screw-in sole weight swappable from 2 to 16 grams around the stock 10 gram weight.

What is new on the ZXi RKT fairway woods versus the ZXi?

A new VR-Titanium face alloy with the i-FLEX milled pattern, revised sound, slightly smaller heads and the adjustable sole weight. They replaced the ZXi fairways at retail on 16 October 2026.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Srixon ZXi RKT is the current Srixon fairway wood line as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Srixon ZXi. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above; the hosel settings extend each head beyond its stamped loft, so a loft between two stock options is usually reachable from either. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
