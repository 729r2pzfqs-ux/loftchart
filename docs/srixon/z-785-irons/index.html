About the Srixon Z 785

The Z 785 is the players model in Srixon's 2018 two-model Z Series iron line, sitting above the distance-oriented Z 585. It is forged from a single billet of soft 1020 carbon steel and uses a tour cavity that concentrates mass behind the impact area for shot shaping. Lofts are traditional for a modern players iron, with a 32-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge. The stock range is 3-iron through pitching wedge in right and left hand, with a 51-degree approach wedge in right hand only. Srixon published this model's offset figures directly in inches, so no unit conversion was applied. The sole is Srixon's Tour V.T. design, for which Srixon lists separate leading- and trailing-edge bounce figures rather than a single bounce number, so bounce is omitted here.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Srixon Z 785 7-iron?

The Srixon Z 785 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length.

What clubs come in a stock Srixon Z 785 set?

The stock range is 3-iron through pitching wedge, available in both right and left hand, plus a 51-degree approach wedge offered in right hand only.

Are the Srixon Z 785 irons forged?

Yes. The Z 785 head is forged from a single billet of soft 1020 carbon steel, with a tour cavity behind the impact area.

Finding a used set

The Srixon Z 785 ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
