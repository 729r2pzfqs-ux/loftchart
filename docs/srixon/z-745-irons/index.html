About the Srixon Z 745

The Z 745 is the players iron of Srixon's 2014 Z line, a compact forged cavity back in 1020 carbon steel sitting between the Z 545 distance model and the Z 945 muscleback. Lofts are a degree or two weaker than the Z 545 through the middle of the set — 32 degrees at the 7-iron against 31 — and offset is cut by about a third, 0.14 inches at the 3-iron against 0.159. It uses the same Tour V.T. Sole and double-laser-milled face as the rest of the line.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Srixon Z 745 7-iron?

The Srixon Z 745 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

When were Srixon Z 745 irons made?

The Z 745 launched alongside the Z 545 in 2014 and was replaced by the Z 765 in 2016.

What is the difference between the Srixon Z 745 and Z 545?

The Z 745 is the smaller, more workable head: weaker by a degree or two through the middle of the set, about a third less offset, and forged in one piece rather than carrying the Z 545's SUP10 face insert.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Srixon Z 745?

46 degrees, against 44 on the Z 545.

Finding a used set

The Srixon Z 745 ran from 2014 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
