About the Srixon Z 355

The Z 355 is the forgiving model in Srixon's 2015 Z line and uses a split construction: the 4 to 7 irons are two-piece, with an HT1770 maraging steel face on a 17-4 stainless body, while the 8-iron through approach wedge are one-piece soft 431 steel for feel. Srixon pairs the heavier heads with a high-balance-point shaft — its Action Mass Tech — and shapes the sole with the Tour V.T. profile, more bounce at the leading edge and less at the trailing edge. Lofts are moderate for a game-improvement iron at 31 degrees for the 7-iron.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Srixon Z 355 7-iron?

The Srixon Z 355 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.00 inches.

When were Srixon Z 355 irons made?

The Z 355 launched in North America in September 2015 and stayed in the range until Srixon moved to the Z 565 and Z 765 in 2016 and 2017.

Why is the Srixon Z 355 built from two different steels?

The 4 to 7 irons use a hard HT1770 maraging face on a 17-4 body to get ball speed where distance matters. The 8-iron down is one-piece soft 431 steel, because those clubs are about feel and control rather than speed.

What is the Tour V.T. Sole?

Srixon's sole profile: more bounce at the leading edge and less at the trailing edge, creating a V shape that lets the head enter the turf cleanly and exit without dragging.

Finding a used set

The Srixon Z 355 ran from 2015 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
