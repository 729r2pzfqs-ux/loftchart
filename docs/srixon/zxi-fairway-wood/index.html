Hosel adjustment chart

QTS 1.5 sleeve · loft ±1.5° · 12 settings.

Every position other than standard also flattens lie, from 0.5° to 3° flat; there is no upright setting. Face angle moves up to 3° closed on the loft-up side and 3° open on the loft-down side.

Lofts at a glance

About the Srixon ZXi

The ZXi fairway woods launched in January 2025 with the ZXi drivers, on a single head design in four lofts — including a 13.5° 3+ wood — rather than a multi-model lineup. Srixon's Rebound Frame alternates stiff and flexible zones behind the HT1770M steel face for ball speed, and every loft carries the 12-position QTS 1.5 sleeve. The ZXi RKT woods replaced the line in October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Srixon ZXi fairway wood come in?

Four lofts: a 13.5 degree 3+ wood, 15 degree 3-wood, 18 degree 5-wood and 21 degree 7-wood. The 3+ and 7-wood are right-hand only.

Is the Srixon ZXi fairway wood adjustable?

Yes — every loft carries Srixon's 12-position QTS 1.5 sleeve: loft moves ±1.5 degrees, every non-standard position also flattens lie by up to 3 degrees, and face angle moves up to 3 degrees either way.

Is the Srixon ZXi fairway wood still current?

No — the ZXi RKT woods replaced the line in October 2026, making the ZXi the value buy on closeout while stock lasts.

Buying one

Srixon has since introduced the Srixon ZXi RKT above it in the range, so the ZXi is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and fairway woods one generation back are the sweet spot of the used market. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
