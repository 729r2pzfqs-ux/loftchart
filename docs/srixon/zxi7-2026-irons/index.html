About the Srixon ZXi7 (2026)

The second-generation ZXi7 is the players model in Srixon's second ZXi iron line, announced on 8 September 2026 with pre-sale from the same day and an official launch date of 16 October 2026. It remains a single-piece S15C forging made by Endo in Japan, now with PureFrame iQ, a wider and thicker forged ridge behind the sweet spot, and slightly thinner toplines than the first generation. The lofts, lies, offsets and swing weights in Srixon's table are identical to the first-generation ZXi7, with a 32-degree 7-iron and a 46-degree pitching wedge; the only published differences are the pitching wedge, listed at 36.00 inches rather than 35.75, and the sand wedge, listed at 35.5 inches rather than 35.75. Heads run 3-iron through a 57-degree sand wedge, with 4-PW and 5-PW the stock sets and the 3-iron and sand wedge custom order only. Offsets are converted from Srixon's published millimetre figures.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Srixon ZXi7 (2026) 7-iron?

The second-generation Srixon ZXi7 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a published steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

Did the lofts change between the first and second generation Srixon ZXi7?

No. Srixon's table for the second generation has the same lofts, lies, offsets and swing weights as the first, from a 20-degree 3-iron to a 46-degree pitching wedge. Only two published lengths differ: the pitching wedge is listed at 36.00 inches instead of 35.75 and the sand wedge at 35.5 instead of 35.75.

When were the second-generation Srixon ZXi7 irons released?

Srixon announced them on 8 September 2026, opening pre-sale the same day, with an official launch date of 16 October 2026.

What clubs come in a stock Srixon ZXi7 (2026) set?

The stock sets are 4-iron through pitching wedge and 5-iron through pitching wedge, with the 51-degree approach wedge sold individually. The 3-iron and the 57-degree sand wedge are custom order only and right hand only.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Srixon ZXi7 (2026) is a current players set in the Srixon range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Srixon ZXi7. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
