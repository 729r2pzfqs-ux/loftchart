About the Srixon ZXi RKT

The ZXi RKT hybrids reached retail on 16 October 2026 with the ZXi RKT woods, replacing the ZXi hybrids. The five-loft ladder from a 17° 2-hybrid to a 28° 6-hybrid carries over, but each head is slightly larger than its ZXi counterpart, picks up the i-FLEX milled face pattern, and adds the screw-in adjustable sole weight (10 g stock, 2-16 g options) alongside the 12-position QTS 1.5 sleeve. The stock shaft moves to the heavier Mitsubishi MMT HY 80 at a D2 swing weight.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Srixon ZXi RKT hybrid come in?

Five lofts: 17, 19, 22, 25 and 28 degrees — the 2-hybrid through 6-hybrid. The 19 and 22 degree heads come in both hands.

Is the Srixon ZXi RKT hybrid adjustable?

Yes, twice over — every loft carries the 12-position QTS 1.5 sleeve (plus or minus 1.5 degrees with flat-lie options to 3 degrees) plus a screw-in sole weight swappable from 2 to 16 grams around the stock 10 gram weight.

What is different about the ZXi RKT hybrid versus the ZXi hybrid?

Slightly larger heads, the i-FLEX milled face pattern, the new adjustable sole weight and a heavier Mitsubishi MMT HY 80 stock shaft. It replaced the ZXi hybrid at retail on 16 October 2026.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Srixon ZXi RKT is the current Srixon hybrid line as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Srixon ZXi. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above; the hosel settings extend each head beyond its stamped loft, so a loft between two stock options is usually reachable from either. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
