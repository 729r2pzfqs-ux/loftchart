About the Srixon Z 945

The Z 945 is the muscleback at the top of Srixon's first Z-Series, forged from soft 1020 carbon steel with a slim top line, a compact profile and the least offset of the range — 0.124 inches at the 3-iron falling to 0.053 at the pitching wedge. It carries the Tour V.T. Sole, Srixon's cambered grind with a high leading-edge bounce and a relieved trailing edge, and a double-laser-milled face for spin consistency. Lofts are traditional: 33 degrees at the 7-iron and 46 at the pitching wedge. Srixon catalogued the set 3-iron through pitching wedge and sold it stock as 5-PW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Srixon Z 945 7-iron?

The Srixon Z 945 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.0 inches.

When were Srixon Z 945 irons made?

The Z 945 arrived with Srixon's first Z Series in 2014 and was replaced by the Z 965 in 2016.

Is the Srixon Z 945 a blade?

Yes — a forged 1020 carbon steel muscleback with a slim top line and 0.080 inches of offset at the 7-iron, the least of the Z Series.

What is the Tour V.T. Sole on the Srixon Z 945?

Srixon's cambered sole grind, with a high leading-edge bounce to stop the club digging and a relieved trailing edge so it exits the turf cleanly. Because the two figures differ, Srixon publishes them separately rather than quoting a single bounce angle.

Finding a used set

The Srixon Z 945 ran from 2014 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
