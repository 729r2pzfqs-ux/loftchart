About the Haywood Signature

The Signature Series was Haywood Golf's first iron - the hollow-body model that launched the Vancouver direct-to-consumer brand's club line. A 431 stainless body is welded to a heat-treated 17-4 stainless face, giving a high-launching, distance-first head with generous progressive offset behind a fairly clean topline. It was on sale by mid-2020 and stayed in the line until the SV.2 replaced it in 2025. Lofts are strong, with a 30-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge. The specs below are Haywood's revised 2022 chart; at launch the published lengths ran a quarter-inch longer in 4-iron through 9-iron (38.75 to 36.25 inches) with the same lofts, lies and offsets, and launch-era swing weights were listed one point heavier at D2/D3.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Haywood Signature 7-iron?

The Haywood Signature 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie, 37-inch length and 0.13 inches of offset.

Is the Haywood Signature forged?

No. It is a two-piece hollow-body iron - a cast 431 stainless steel body welded to a heat-treated 17-4 stainless steel face. Haywood's forged models are the CB and MB.

When was the Haywood Signature sold?

It launched Haywood's iron line in 2020 and remained the brand's game-improvement-leaning distance iron until the SV.2 replaced it in 2025.

Finding a used set

The Haywood Signature ran from 2020 to 2025 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
