Hosel adjustment chart

PXG adjustable shaft adapter · loft ±1.5° · 8 settings.

The Standard "Dot" is the most upright position; every other setting flattens lie by 0.5° to 3° relative to it. There is no upright-beyond-Dot setting. PXG markets the upright Dot setting for draw bias and the flattest "PXG Standard" setting for fade bias.

About the PXG Black Ops

The 0311 Black Ops fairway woods launched in 2024 with the Black Ops drivers — a single five-loft head rather than a multi-model lineup, running from a 15° 3-wood to a 24° 9-wood with a 17° 4-wood in between. Each head carries PXG's adjustable shaft adapter and three interchangeable sole weights for flight and bias tuning. The Lightning woods replaced the line for 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the PXG Black Ops fairway wood come in?

Five lofts: 15 (3-wood), 17 (4-wood), 18 (5-wood), 21 (7-wood) and 24 degrees (9-wood).

Is the PXG Black Ops fairway wood adjustable?

Yes, twice over: the eight-position PXG shaft adapter moves loft ±1.5 degrees (with flat-lie options to 3 degrees), and three interchangeable sole weights tune swing weight and shot bias.

What replaced the PXG Black Ops fairway woods?

The PXG Lightning fairway woods and hybrids, announced in December 2025 as the first release of PXG's 2026 line.

Buying one

PXG has since introduced the PXG Lightning above it in the range, so the Black Ops is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and fairway woods one generation back are the sweet spot of the used market. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
