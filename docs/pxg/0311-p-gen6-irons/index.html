About the PXG 0311 P GEN6

The GEN6 0311 P is a mid-size head with a moderate amount of offset, aimed at low to mid handicap players, and it publishes exactly the same loft, lie, length, offset and bounce chart as the GEN5 model it replaces — 30 degrees at the 7-iron, 44 at the pitching wedge. The changes are in the internals and the finish options, which run to Chrome, Xtreme Dark, Double Chrome and Double Black. PXG builds every set to order, so the lengths below are its published standard.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 P GEN6 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 P GEN6 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a standard lie of 62.5 degrees and a standard length of 37.00 inches.

Did PXG change the 0311 P specs from GEN5 to GEN6?

No. PXG publishes identical lofts, lies, lengths, offsets and bounce angles for both generations; the differences are internal and cosmetic.

What finishes does the PXG 0311 P GEN6 come in?

Chrome, Xtreme Dark, Double Chrome and Double Black.

How much bounce does a PXG 0311 P GEN6 have?

Progressive, from 6 degrees at the 4-iron to 12 degrees at the pitching and gap wedges.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 P GEN6 ran from 2023 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
