About the PXG 0211

The original 0211, launched on 21 May 2019, was PXG's first attempt at a more affordable iron — $195 per club in steel and $210 in graphite, against $349 or more for the flagship 0311 line. It kept the core GEN2 0311 technology package: a hollow body filled with COR2 polymer, a 0.058-inch (about 1.5 mm) HT1770 maraging steel face that PXG billed as the thinnest in golf, and a 360-degree undercut channel around the face that PXG said enlarged the functional face area by 15 per cent. The savings came from the body, which is investment cast rather than forged and milled, and from dropping the adjustable tungsten perimeter weights of the 0311s. Lofts are moderately strong, with a 31-degree 7-iron and a 45-degree pitching wedge, and PXG published specs from the 4-iron all the way through 55-degree sand and 60-degree lob wedges, stamping the scoring clubs W, G, S and L — listed here as PW, GW, SW and LW. The second-generation 0211, announced on 7 January 2021 as part of the 2021 0211 Collection, replaced it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0211 7-iron?

The original 2019 PXG 0211 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

What is the difference between the PXG 0211 and 0311 irons?

The 0211 kept the 0311 GEN2's hollow body, COR2 polymer core and 0.058-inch maraging steel face, but its body is investment cast rather than forged and milled and it has no adjustable tungsten perimeter weights — which is how PXG sold it at $195 per club instead of $349 and up.

When were the original PXG 0211 irons released?

They went on sale on 21 May 2019. The second-generation 0211, announced on 7 January 2021, replaced them.

What clubs were offered in the PXG 0211?

PXG published specs for the 4-iron through a 60-degree lob wedge, including a 45-degree pitching wedge, 50-degree gap wedge and 55-degree sand wedge, stamped W, G, S and L. Sets were built to order rather than sold in fixed compositions.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0211 ran from 2019 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
