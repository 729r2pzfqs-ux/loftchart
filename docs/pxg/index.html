PXG Golf Club Specifications

Parsons Xtreme Golf was founded by Bob Parsons in 2014 and sells direct, built to order. The 0311 line runs a T/P/XP family off one generation number, and PXG publishes a full loft, lie, length, offset and bounce chart for every head.
