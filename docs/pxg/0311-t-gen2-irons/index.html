About the PXG 0311 T GEN2

The 0311 T is the tour head of PXG's GEN2 range, announced on 15 March 2018 and on sale from 19 April 2018 at $400 per club in chrome or $500 in Xtreme Dark — the most compact of the four GEN2 heads, with the least offset and lofts a degree weaker than the P throughout, 32 degrees at the 7-iron and 46 at the pitching wedge. Like its siblings it is a forged hollow body filled with COR2, the polymer core PXG claimed was 20 percent faster than the original TPE filling, behind a thin maraging steel face with adjustable tungsten and titanium perimeter weights. PXG published the chart for 3-iron through pitching wedge, stamped W and listed here as PW; this exact loft ladder then survived unchanged through the T GEN3, GEN4 and GEN5. Every set was built to order from PXG's shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 T GEN2 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 T GEN2 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

What is the difference between the PXG 0311 T GEN2 and the P GEN2?

The T is the compact tour head: lofts run a degree weaker (32 against 31 at the 7-iron), offset is roughly half the P's, and bounce is lower through the long irons — 2 degrees at the 3-iron against the P's 5.

Did PXG 0311 T lofts change between generations?

No. The 20-degree 3-iron through 46-degree pitching wedge ladder introduced with the T GEN2 carried over unchanged to the T GEN3, GEN4 and GEN5.

When did the PXG 0311 T GEN2 come out?

PXG announced the GEN2 range on 15 March 2018 and put it on sale on 19 April 2018. The GEN3 range replaced it in February 2020.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 T GEN2 ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
