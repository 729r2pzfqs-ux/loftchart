About the PXG 0311 P GEN4

The 0311 P is the middle head of PXG's GEN4 line, unveiled in February 2021 with pre-sales from 9 March and retail from 30 March at $349 per club. GEN4 introduced XCOR, the polymer core that replaced the GEN3's COR2 inside the hollow, five-times-forged 8620 carbon steel body, behind a 0.058-inch HT1770 maraging steel face, with a milled back surface and a Precision Weighting Technology plug near the centre of gravity that fitters could swap in 2-gram increments. The loft, lie, length, offset and bounce chart — a 30-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge — is the same one PXG went on to publish unchanged for the GEN5, GEN6 and GEN7 P heads, though the GEN4 chart also lists an 18-degree 3-iron that the GEN5 page dropped. PXG stamps the two scoring clubs W and G; they are listed here as PW and GW. Every set was built to order from PXG's shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 P GEN4 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 P GEN4 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

Did PXG 0311 P specs change from GEN4 to GEN5?

No. The GEN5 published the same loft, lie, length, offset and bounce figures for the 4-iron through gap wedge; the only chart difference is that the GEN4 page also listed an 18-degree 3-iron.

What is XCOR?

The polymer core PXG introduced with GEN4, replacing COR2. It fills the hollow forged head, supporting the 0.058-inch maraging steel face so it can flex for ball speed while damping sound and feel.

When were the PXG GEN4 irons released?

PXG announced the GEN4 line in February 2021, opened pre-sales on 9 March and put it on sale on 30 March 2021 at $349 per club. The GEN5 range replaced it in 2022.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 P GEN4 ran from 2021 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
