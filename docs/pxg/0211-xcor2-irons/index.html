About the PXG 0211 XCOR2

The third and final 0211 iron, unveiled on 21 July 2022, brought the XCOR2 polymer core from PXG's flagship GEN5 line down to the budget 0211 — the lighter core material freed mass for the perimeter of the hollow cast head, behind a cold-rolled HT1770 maraging steel face. The loft, lie, length and offset chart carried over unchanged from the 0211 DC (a 28-degree 7-iron and 42-degree pitching wedge), with one degree more bounce at every club, and introductory pricing was $129 per club in Chrome and $149 in Xtreme Dark. It had an unusually long run with no successor: PXG kept it in the range at $99.99 per club through the GEN6 era and was still selling it "while stocks last" into early 2026, before it disappeared from pxg.com's catalog. PXG stamps the scoring clubs W, G, S and L; they are listed here as PW, GW, SW and LW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0211 XCOR2 7-iron?

The PXG 0211 XCOR2 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

What is the difference between the PXG 0211 XCOR2 and the 0211 DC?

The XCOR2 polymer core replaced the DC's DualCOR system, freeing mass for the perimeter of the head. The loft, lie, length and offset chart is identical; the only spec change is one degree more bounce at every club.

Was the PXG 0211 XCOR2 ever replaced?

No direct successor was announced. PXG kept selling it at falling prices — $99.99 per club by late 2023, then 'while stocks last' — until it quietly left the pxg.com catalog in 2026.

Are PXG 0211 XCOR2 lofts strong?

Moderately strong for a players-distance iron — a 28-degree 7-iron and 42-degree pitching wedge, with published gapping down through 48-degree gap, 54-degree sand and 60-degree lob wedges.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0211 XCOR2 ran from 2022 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
