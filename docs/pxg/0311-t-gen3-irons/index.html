About the PXG 0311 T GEN3

The 0311 T is the tour head of PXG's GEN3 line, announced in February 2020 at $349 per club alongside the P and XP — the most compact of the three, with the least offset, the lowest bounce and traditional lofts of 32 degrees at the 7-iron and 46 at the pitching wedge. GEN3 introduced Impact Reactor technology powered by the DualCOR system, a soft high-COR inner polymer core wrapped in a stiffer outer core inside the five-times-forged 8620 carbon steel hollow body, behind a 0.058-inch HT1770 maraging steel face attached by robotic laser welding, with a CNC-milled back surface. The loft ladder carries over from the T GEN2 and continued unchanged into the T GEN4 and GEN5; GEN3 added a 51-degree gap wedge to the T chart. PXG stamps the two scoring clubs W and G; they are listed here as PW and GW. Every set was built to order from PXG's shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 T GEN3 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 T GEN3 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

What is the DualCOR system in the PXG 0311 T GEN3?

A two-polymer core filling the hollow forged head: a soft, high-COR inner core that increases face loading at impact, supported by a stiffer high-strength outer core behind the 0.058-inch maraging steel face.

What changed from the PXG 0311 T GEN2 to the T GEN3?

The loft, lie and length ladder carried over; GEN3 swapped the COR2 core for DualCOR, added the 360-degree face perimeter channel, trimmed short-iron offset slightly and added a 51-degree gap wedge to the published chart.

How does the PXG 0311 T GEN3 differ from the P GEN3?

The T is the compact tour head with lofts one degree weaker (32 against 31 at the 7-iron), roughly half the offset, and much lower bounce in the long irons — 2 degrees at the 3-iron against the P's 5.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 T GEN3 ran from 2020 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
