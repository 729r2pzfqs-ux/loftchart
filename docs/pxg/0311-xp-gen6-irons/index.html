About the PXG 0311 XP GEN6

The larger of the two GEN6 iron heads — PXG launched the generation on 23 March 2023 with only P and XP models, dropping the T — the 0311 XP keeps the XP line's strong lofts (a 27-degree 7-iron and 41-degree pitching wedge) and adds slightly more offset than the GEN5 XP, plus published specs for 54-degree sand and 60-degree lob wedges that the GEN5 chart lacked. GEN6 thinned the HT1770 maraging steel face 15 per cent to 0.050 inches (about 1.27 mm) over the XCOR2 polymer core, added Power Channel Technology — a U-shaped corridor milled into the internal face surface to increase deflection — and kept the five-times-forged 8620 carbon steel body. PXG stamps the scoring clubs W, G, S and L; they are listed here as PW, GW, SW and LW. Sets were built to order in Chrome, Xtreme Dark, Double Chrome and Double Black finishes, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 XP GEN6 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 XP GEN6 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

Did PXG 0311 XP specs change from GEN5 to GEN6?

The lofts, lies and lengths are identical, but the GEN6 chart adds about a hundredth of an inch of offset at every club and publishes 54-degree sand and 60-degree lob wedges that the GEN5 chart did not.

Was there a PXG 0311 T GEN6?

No. PXG launched GEN6 on 23 March 2023 with only the P and XP iron heads; the tour-iron slot was filled by the separate 0317 line.

What changed inside the PXG 0311 XP GEN6?

The HT1770 maraging steel face was thinned 15 per cent to 0.050 inches and Power Channel Technology, a U-shaped internal channel behind the face, was added — both on top of the XCOR2 polymer core carried over from GEN5.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 XP GEN6 ran from 2023 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
