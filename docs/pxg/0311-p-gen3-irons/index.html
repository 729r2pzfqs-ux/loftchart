About the PXG 0311 P GEN3

The middle head of PXG's GEN3 line, announced in February 2020 at $349 per club in a three-model range — T for tour, P for players, XP for Xtreme Performance — the 0311 P was pitched as the balance of playability and forgiveness for the widest range of golfers. GEN3 introduced Impact Reactor technology powered by the DualCOR system, a soft high-COR inner polymer core wrapped in a stiffer outer core inside the five-times-forged 8620 carbon steel hollow body, behind a 0.058-inch HT1770 maraging steel face attached by robotic laser welding, with a CNC-milled back surface. Its chart runs from a 19-degree 3-iron to a 50-degree gap wedge with a 31-degree 7-iron; the GEN4 P that replaced it in 2021 strengthened every loft by one degree. PXG stamps the two scoring clubs W and G; they are listed here as PW and GW. Every set was built to order from PXG's shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 P GEN3 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 P GEN3 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

What changed from the PXG 0311 P GEN3 to the P GEN4?

The GEN4 strengthened every loft by one degree — 30 at the 7-iron against 31 — swapped the DualCOR core for XCOR, and added the Precision Weighting plug. Lies, lengths and bounce angles carried over.

What was the PXG GEN3 iron line-up?

Three heads announced in February 2020: the 0311 T for highly skilled players, the 0311 P as the all-round players model, and the larger 0311 XP for distance and forgiveness. All sold at $349 per club.

What is the DualCOR system in the PXG 0311 P GEN3?

A two-polymer core filling the hollow forged head: a soft, high-COR inner core that increases face loading at impact, supported by a stiffer high-strength outer core behind the 0.058-inch maraging steel face.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 P GEN3 ran from 2020 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
