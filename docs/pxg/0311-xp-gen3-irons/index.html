About the PXG 0311 XP GEN3

XP stands for Xtreme Performance, the name PXG adopted with the GEN3 line of February 2020 for its largest head, replacing the GEN2's XF — a bigger profile with the most offset in the range, longer club lengths and flatter lies than the T and P, sold at $349 per club. GEN3 introduced Impact Reactor technology powered by the DualCOR system, a soft high-COR inner polymer core wrapped in a stiffer outer core inside the five-times-forged 8620 carbon steel hollow body, behind a 0.058-inch HT1770 maraging steel face. Its chart runs from a 17-degree 3-iron to a 48-degree gap wedge with a 28-degree 7-iron; the XP GEN4 that replaced it in 2021 strengthened every loft by one degree. PXG stamps the two scoring clubs W and G; they are listed here as PW and GW. Every set was built to order from PXG's shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 XP GEN3 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 XP GEN3 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

How do PXG 0311 XP GEN3 lofts compare to the XP GEN4?

One degree weaker throughout — a 28-degree 7-iron and 42-degree pitching wedge against the GEN4's 27 and 41 — on the same lies, lengths, offsets and bounces.

What happened to the PXG 0311 XF?

The GEN3 line of 2020 renamed the forgiveness slot XP, for Xtreme Performance. The XP GEN3 is a half-inch longer per club than the XF GEN2 was, with flatter lies and lofts about two degrees stronger.

Are the PXG 0311 XP GEN3 clubs longer than the other GEN3 irons?

Yes. Every XP club is half an inch longer than the corresponding T and P — a 37.5-inch 7-iron against 37 — with lies half a degree flatter.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 XP GEN3 ran from 2020 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
