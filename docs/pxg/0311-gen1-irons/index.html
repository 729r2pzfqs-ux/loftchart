About the PXG 0311 GEN1

The 0311 is the iron that launched Parsons Xtreme Golf in 2015 at an unheard-of $300 per club — a hollow, CNC-milled blade-style head injected with TPE (thermoplastic elastomer) behind what PXG billed as the world's thinnest face, 1.5 mm of HT1770 high-strength maraging steel, with high-density tungsten alloy weights around the perimeter to raise MOI. The chart is traditional for the era — a 31-degree 7-iron and 45-degree pitching wedge — and carried over unchanged to the 0311 P GEN2 that replaced it in 2018. A tour version, the 0311T, followed later in 2015 and an Xtreme Forgiveness 0311XF completed the original line. PXG stamps its scoring clubs W, G, S and L; they are listed here as PW, GW, SW and LW. Every set was built to order from PXG's shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an original PXG 0311 7-iron?

The original PXG 0311 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

When did the original PXG 0311 irons come out?

In 2015, when Bob Parsons launched Parsons Xtreme Golf; GolfWRX dates the 0311 retail launch to July 2015. The GEN2 range replaced it in March 2018.

Why were the original PXG 0311 irons so expensive?

They sold at $300 per club, roughly triple the going rate in 2015. PXG's pitch was a cost-no-object construction: a CNC-milled hollow body injected with TPE, a 1.5 mm maraging steel face and tungsten perimeter weights, every set built to order.

What replaced the original PXG 0311?

The 0311 P GEN2 of 2018 was its direct replacement, with identical lofts. The GEN2 line also split into T, XF and SGI heads.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 GEN1 ran from 2015 to 2018 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
