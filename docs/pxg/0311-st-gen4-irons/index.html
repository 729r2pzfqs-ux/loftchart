About the PXG 0311 ST GEN4

The 0311 ST GEN4 Blades reached retail around 6 August 2021 (Chrome $349, Xtreme Dark $399) after appearing in James Hahn's and Wyndham Clark's bags on tour, succeeding the earlier 0311 ST as PXG's tour blade. Each head is triple-forged from 8620 carbon steel and finished with PXG's proprietary Precision Weighting Technology, which lets fitters dial in swing weight without shifting the center of gravity. The set is progressive: the 3-, 4- and 5-iron get a slightly larger profile and a small internal cavity for extra MOI and forgiveness, the 6- and 7-iron step down to a thinner top line and moderate blade size, and the 8-iron through gap wedge finish as thin-topline, shorter-blade pure blades for complete control. Minimal, progressive offset runs throughout. PXG publishes the chart from 3-iron through gap wedge, stamping the scoring clubs W and G — listed here as PW and GW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 ST GEN4 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 ST GEN4 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a standard lie of 62.5 degrees and a standard length of 37.0 inches.

Are PXG 0311 ST GEN4 irons true blades all the way through the set?

Not quite. The 3-, 4- and 5-iron carry a slightly larger profile with a small cavity for extra forgiveness, the 6- and 7-iron are a moderate blade, and only the 8-iron through gap wedge are pure, thin-topline blades.

What is Precision Weighting Technology on the PXG 0311 ST GEN4?

A proprietary system that lets PXG fitters adjust swing weight without moving the club's center of gravity, so players can dial in feel independent of the head's launch and forgiveness characteristics.

When did the PXG 0311 ST GEN4 come out?

It reached retail around 6 August 2021 at $349 per club in Chrome (or $399 in Xtreme Dark), after James Hahn and Wyndham Clark had already put it in play on the PGA Tour.

Production status

Introduced in 2021, the PXG 0311 ST GEN4 has no confirmed end date on file. It replaced the PXG 0311 ST. LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the PXG catalogue — check with PXG or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
