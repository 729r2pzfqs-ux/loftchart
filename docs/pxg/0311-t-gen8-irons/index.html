About the PXG 0311 T GEN8

The 0311 T is the tour head of PXG's GEN8 line, announced on 15 January 2026, and its return is notable because the GEN7 range of 2024 shipped no T at all — the triple-forged 0317 T covered the tour slot in the interim. Like its siblings it is five-times forged from 8620 carbon steel around a QuantumCOR polymer core with an ultra-thin HT1770 maraging steel face, and it carries the generation's Dual Perimeter Weighting System: interchangeable heel and toe weights that let a fitter tune swing weight and fade or draw bias, moving roughly 11 grams to the perimeter for a claimed 17 per cent MOI gain over the 0317 T. It is the most compact GEN8 head, with the thinnest topline and the least offset — 0.075 inches at the 7-iron, well under half the P's chart. Lofts are traditional for the family at 32 degrees for the 7-iron and 46 for the pitching wedge, two degrees weaker than the P throughout, and the chart matches the 0317 T's published lofts, lies, lengths and offsets. Heads run 3-iron through a 51-degree gap wedge; PXG stamps the scoring clubs W and G, listed here as PW and GW, and builds every set to order in Chrome and Xtreme Dark finishes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 T GEN8 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 T GEN8 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

Was there a PXG 0311 T GEN7?

No. The GEN7 range of 2024 shipped only P and XP heads, and the triple-forged 0317 T covered PXG's tour-iron slot until the T returned with GEN8 in January 2026.

How do PXG 0311 T GEN8 lofts compare with the P?

Two degrees weaker at every club — 32 degrees at the 7-iron against the P's 30, and a 46-degree pitching wedge against the P's 44 — with well under half the P's offset.

What is the Dual Perimeter Weighting System?

Two weight ports in the heel and toe of each GEN8 head that take interchangeable weights, letting a fitter tune swing weight and bias the head toward a fade or draw. PXG says it moves about 11 grams to the perimeter, raising the T's MOI a claimed 17 per cent over the 0317 T.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the PXG 0311 T GEN8 is a current players set in the PXG range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the PXG 0311 T GEN6. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
