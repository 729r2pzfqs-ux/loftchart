About the PXG 0311 P GEN8

The 0311 P is the mid-size players-distance head of PXG's GEN8 line, announced on 15 January 2026. The body is five-times forged from 8620 carbon steel around a QuantumCOR polymer core — what PXG calls Deep Core Recoil Technology — behind an ultra-thin HT1770 maraging steel face, with internal tungsten toe weighting. The generation's headline change is the Dual Perimeter Weighting System: two weight ports in the heel and toe take interchangeable weights so a fitter can tune swing weight and bias the head toward a fade or draw, and PXG says the shift of mass to the perimeter raises the P's MOI about 4 per cent over GEN7. The loft, lie and length chart is the same one PXG has published for the 0311 P since GEN5 — a 30-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge — but the published offsets are trimmed by five thousandths of an inch at every club (the 7-iron reads 0.125 inches against the GEN7's 0.130) and PXG now publishes bounce. Heads run 4-iron through a 49-degree gap wedge; PXG stamps the two scoring clubs W and G, and they are listed here as PW and GW. PXG builds every set to order from its shaft menu, in Chrome and Xtreme Dark finishes, so no single stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 P GEN8 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 P GEN8 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

What changed between the PXG 0311 P GEN7 and GEN8?

The Dual Perimeter Weighting System — interchangeable heel and toe weights that let a fitter tune swing weight and shot bias — plus Deep Core Recoil Technology built on the QuantumCOR core, for a claimed 4 per cent MOI gain. Lofts, lies and lengths are unchanged; the published offsets are trimmed by 0.005 inches at every club.

When were the PXG GEN8 irons released and what do they cost?

PXG announced the GEN8 T, P and XP irons on 15 January 2026, at $229 per club in Chrome and $249 in Xtreme Dark.

What clubs are available in the PXG 0311 P GEN8?

4-iron through a 49-degree gap wedge. PXG stamps the two scoring clubs W (44 degrees) and G (49 degrees), and builds any composition to order rather than selling fixed boxed sets.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the PXG 0311 P GEN8 is a current players distance set in the PXG range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the PXG 0311 P GEN7. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
