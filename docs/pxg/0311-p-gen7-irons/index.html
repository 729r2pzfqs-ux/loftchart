About the PXG 0311 P GEN7

GEN7 replaced the XCOR2 core with QuantumCOR and thinned the maraging face to 0.050 inches, a claimed 15 per cent reduction, with a 20-gram internal tungsten toe weight pulling the centre of gravity back to the middle of the face. The loft, lie, length and offset chart is unchanged from the GEN5 and GEN6 P heads — PXG has run the same P numbers for three generations. PXG stamps the two scoring clubs W and G; they are listed here as PW and GW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 P GEN7 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 P GEN7 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

When were the PXG 0311 GEN7 irons released?

PXG released GEN7 on 7 August 2024, alongside the Black Ops irons. The GEN8 range replaced it in January 2026.

What changed between the PXG GEN6 and GEN7 irons?

The core material — QuantumCOR replaced XCOR2 — the face was thinned to 0.050 inches, and a 20-gram internal tungsten toe weight was added. The loft, lie, length and offset chart did not change.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 P GEN7 ran from 2024 to 2026 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
