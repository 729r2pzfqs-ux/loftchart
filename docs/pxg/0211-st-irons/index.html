About the PXG 0211 ST

The 2021 0211 ST Irons, announced 11 February 2021 at $149 per club (list $195, promoted at $145-149), brought PXG's tour-blade concept down to the budget 0211 line: a clean, traditional blade design that is triple-forged from 8620 soft carbon steel rather than the hollow, polymer-filled bodies of the rest of the 0211 and 0311 ranges. PXG calls it "surprisingly forgiving for a solid-bodied blade" — the forging process's tight grain structure and a strategic repositioning of mass through the head lift its MOI above what a classic blade would deliver, while a shorter blade length and lower MOI above the shaft axis keep it workable for skilled ball strikers. PXG published the chart from 4-iron through gap wedge, stamping the scoring clubs W and G — listed here as PW and GW; the configurator also offered a 3-iron, S and L, but PXG published no specs for them. Every set was built to order from PXG's shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0211 ST 7-iron?

The PXG 0211 ST 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a standard lie of 62.5 degrees and a standard length of 37.0 inches.

Is the PXG 0211 ST a hollow iron like the rest of the 0211 line?

No. Unlike the polymer-filled hollow bodies of the 0211 and 0211 DC, the 0211 ST is a solid-body blade, triple-forged from 8620 soft carbon steel.

How can a solid-body blade like the PXG 0211 ST be forgiving?

PXG repositions mass through the clubhead to raise the overall moment of inertia well above what a classic blade delivers, while a shorter blade length and lower MOI above the shaft axis keep it workable for better players.

When did the PXG 0211 ST come out?

PXG announced it on 11 February 2021 as part of that year's 0211 refresh, pricing it from $145-149 per club against a $195 list — well below its 0311 ST tour-blade sibling.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2021, the PXG 0211 ST is a current players set in the PXG range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
