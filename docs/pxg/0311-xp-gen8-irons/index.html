About the PXG 0311 XP GEN8

XP stands for Xtreme Performance, and the 0311 XP is the largest head of PXG's GEN8 line, announced on 15 January 2026 — the widest sole, the most offset and the strongest lofts, at 27 degrees for the 7-iron and a 41-degree pitching wedge, built a half-inch longer than the P through most of the set. Like its siblings it is five-times forged from 8620 carbon steel around a QuantumCOR polymer core with an ultra-thin HT1770 maraging steel face, plus the generation's Dual Perimeter Weighting System of interchangeable heel and toe weights, which PXG says lifts the XP's MOI about 6 per cent over GEN7. Because the set lofts are so strong, the scoring end runs deep: PXG publishes specs from the 4-iron down through 47-degree gap, 54-degree sand and 60-degree lob wedges to keep the gapping usable under the 41-degree pitching wedge. PXG stamps those clubs W, G, S and L; they are listed here as PW, GW, SW and LW. Every set is built to order in Chrome and Xtreme Dark finishes, so no single stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311 XP GEN8 7-iron?

The PXG 0311 XP GEN8 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

How strong are PXG 0311 XP GEN8 lofts?

Very strong — 27 degrees at the 7-iron and a 41-degree pitching wedge, three degrees stronger than the 0311 P GEN8 and five stronger than the T. The 4-iron is 18 degrees, the loft of many hybrids.

What wedges does the PXG 0311 XP GEN8 offer?

A 41-degree pitching wedge, 47-degree gap wedge, 54-degree sand wedge and 60-degree lob wedge — PXG stamps them W, G, S and L — so the strong set lofts can be gapped all the way down.

What changed from the PXG 0311 XP GEN7 to GEN8?

The Dual Perimeter Weighting System of interchangeable heel and toe weights and Deep Core Recoil Technology on the QuantumCOR core, for a claimed 6 per cent MOI gain. The published lofts stay the same as earlier XP generations, with a 27-degree 7-iron.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the PXG 0311 XP GEN8 is a current game improvement set in the PXG range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the PXG 0311 XP GEN7. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
