About the PXG 0311 T GEN1

The 0311T — "T" for Tour — was PXG's first tour-compact iron, launched on 21 October 2015 a few months after the original 0311. Founder Bob Parsons was explicit about its target: "PXG 0311T irons are not built to compete with game improvement or player's irons... we designed them to compete with blades." The head is more compact than the original 0311, with a thinner top line, a narrower sole and less offset, but keeps the same forged S25C carbon steel hollow body, TPE injection between face and back cavity, a 1.5 mm HT1770 maraging steel face and tungsten screws spreading weight to the perimeter for forgiveness inside a traditional blade shape. It sold at the same $350-per-club price as the original 0311. PXG stamps the pitching wedge W; it is listed here as PW. Every set was built to order from PXG's shaft menu, so no stock shaft is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a PXG 0311T GEN1 7-iron?

The PXG 0311T GEN1 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

What was PXG's goal with the 0311T?

Founder Bob Parsons said the 0311T was not built to compete with game-improvement or players irons but with blades, pairing a compact tour shape and narrow sole with the same TPE-filled, tungsten-weighted forged construction as the original 0311.

When did the PXG 0311T come out?

PXG launched it on 21 October 2015, a few months after its original 0311, at the same $350-per-club price. The T GEN2 replaced it in 2018.

Did the PXG 0311T loft ladder change in later generations?

No. The 20-degree 3-iron through 46-degree pitching wedge ladder introduced with the GEN1 T carried over unchanged through GEN2 and beyond.

Finding a used set

The PXG 0311 T GEN1 ran from 2015 to 2018 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
