About the Tommy Armour 845+

The 2024 845+ brought the Tommy Armour 845 name back to Golf Galaxy and Dick's Sporting Goods after a two-year gap, as a $399.99 maximum-forgiveness set. A rail system on the sole improves turf interaction, and a new head shape with a shorter hosel and longer blade lowers the centre of gravity and raises the moment of inertia. It swept MyGolfSpy's 2024 Most Wanted test in the super-game-improvement class — best overall, best for forgiveness and best value — so while it is filed here as a game-improvement iron, testers treat it as the most forgiving end of that spectrum. The stock set runs 5-iron through pitching wedge plus a 50-degree approach wedge with SL85 steel or UST Mamiya Recoil graphite shafts.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tommy Armour 845+ 7-iron?

The 2024 Tommy Armour 845+ 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

How good are the Tommy Armour 845+ irons?

In MyGolfSpy's 2024 Most Wanted test they took first place in the super-game-improvement class — best for forgiveness, second best for accuracy and the best value in the test at $399.99 for the seven-piece set.

What shafts come in the Tommy Armour 845+ irons?

Stock options are the lightweight SL85 steel shaft or UST Mamiya Recoil graphite, sold exclusively through Dick's Sporting Goods and Golf Galaxy.

Production status

Introduced in 2024, the Tommy Armour 845+ has no confirmed end date on file. It replaced the Tommy Armour 845-MAX (2021). LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the Tommy Armour catalogue — check with Tommy Armour or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
