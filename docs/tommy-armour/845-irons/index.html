About the Tommy Armour 845

The 845 irons revived Tommy Armour's most famous model number for Dick's Sporting Goods' relaunched house brand, announced in autumn 2019 as the game-improvement iron of the MM20 line (a companion 845 FORGED players-distance model was sold alongside). The two-piece hollow-body stainless construction carries an L-Cup face with variable face thickness, high-density tungsten toe weighting and 3M vibration-dampening tape, and the set won a 2020 Golf Digest Hot List award. The stock seven-piece set ran 5-iron through pitching wedge plus a 49-degree approach wedge, sold exclusively through Dick's Sporting Goods and Golf Galaxy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tommy Armour 845 (2019) 7-iron?

The 2019 Tommy Armour 845 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37 inches.

Are the 2019 Tommy Armour 845 irons related to the classic 845s Silver Scot?

Only by name. Dick's Sporting Goods bought the Tommy Armour brand and relaunched it in 2018; the 2019 845 is a modern two-piece hollow-body game-improvement iron sold exclusively at Dick's and Golf Galaxy, trading on the 845 name of the 1987 classic.

What is the set makeup of the Tommy Armour 845 irons?

The stock set is seven pieces: 5-iron through pitching wedge (43.5 degrees) plus a 49-degree approach wedge, with True Temper XP 85 steel shafts.

Finding a used set

The Tommy Armour 845 ran from 2019 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
