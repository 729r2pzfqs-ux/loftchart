About the Tommy Armour 845s Oversize

The 845s Oversize enlarged Tommy Armour's cavity-balanced Silver Scot head for extra forgiveness, carrying the full 1-iron-through-lob-wedge range the original 845s catalog offered rather than the shorter sets typical of later Tommy Armour lines. The GolfWorks' own Maltby MPF ratings chart — the same database this site uses for the 845s and 855s Silver Scot entries — dates the model to 1998 and lists the larger-headed 845s Oversize Plus as its 2002 successor, described on GolfWRX as "the same design as the Oversize but a little bigger." Owners on GolfWRX report both stock and aftermarket shaft builds, including Royal Precision stepless rifle steel. Lofts are identical to the original cast 845s catalog through the wedges, and the set was cataloged in both steel and graphite.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tommy Armour 845s Oversize 7-iron?

The 845s Oversize 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 36.75 inches — the same loft as the original 1987 845s Silver Scot catalog, in a larger cavity-balanced head.

When was the Tommy Armour 845s Oversize made?

The GolfWorks' own Maltby MPF ratings chart dates it to 1998, with the larger-headed 845s Oversize Plus following in 2002.

What is the difference between the 845s Oversize and 845s Oversize Plus?

Per GolfWRX forum consensus, the Oversize Plus (2002) is 'the same design as the Oversize but a little bigger' — a further-enlarged version of the same cavity-balanced cast head.

What wedges came with the Tommy Armour 845s Oversize?

The full factory chart runs to a 51-degree gap wedge, 55-degree sand wedge and 60-degree lob wedge, though most retail sets were sold as a shorter 3-PW or 4-PW-plus-SW selection.

Finding a used set

The Tommy Armour 845s Oversize ran from 1998 to 2002 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
