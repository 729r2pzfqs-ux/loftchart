About the Tommy Armour 845 Max

The 845 Max joined Dick's Sporting Goods' Tommy Armour line in 2020 (model code MM20) as the super-game-improvement companion to the standard 845, replacing the Atomic as the brand's maximum-forgiveness iron. It shares the 845's L-Cup variable-thickness face, tungsten toe weighting and 3M vibration-dampening tape but swaps the hollow body for an ultra-wide sole that pushes the centre of gravity lower and deeper. Lofts run one degree weaker than the standard 845 and the stock build used lightweight UST Mamiya Recoil ESX graphite shafts at a light D1 swing weight. A refreshed 2021 845-MAX with different lofts replaced it after one season.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tommy Armour 845 Max (2020) 7-iron?

The 2020 Tommy Armour 845 Max 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a graphite-build length of 37.125 inches.

What is the difference between the Tommy Armour 845 and 845 Max?

The 845 Max is the super-game-improvement version: it has an ultra-wide sole instead of the 845's hollow body, a slightly larger head with more offset, lofts one degree weaker across the board, and a lighter graphite-first build at a D1 swing weight.

Is the 2020 845 Max the same as the 2021 845-MAX?

No. The 2021 845-MAX is a refreshed model with different lofts (a 31-degree 7-iron versus 30 degrees here) and steel-shaft builds at D2; the 2020 original recorded here lasted a single season.

Finding a used set

The Tommy Armour 845 Max ran from 2020 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
