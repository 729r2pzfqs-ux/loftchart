About the Miura CB-1008

The CB-1008 is a one-piece forged S25C cavity-back from Miura's CB-1000 series, released in Japan on 22 February 2017 and brought to Western markets that autumn as part of the transitional MG Collection (with the CB-2008 and MB-5005) at $279 per club. A weight bar across the bottom of the cavity concentrates mass low and behind the sweet spot, and the 19-millimetre sole at the 7-iron was the widest of any CB-1000-series head, making it the most forgiving and lowest-launching-friendly of Miura's compact players cavity-backs while keeping the thin topline, minimal offset and high, pointed toe of the marque. MG-series heads carry a W.D.D. stamp for Miura's accurate-forging process covering weight, dimension and density. Miura sold it through fitters as heads (4-PW and 3-PW make-ups, right hand only, satin chrome), so the lengths below are Miura's standard assembly lengths of the era rather than a model-specific factory sheet. It left the range around 2019, when the wide-sole CB-301 took over the forgiving-cavity-back slot.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Miura CB-1008 7-iron?

The Miura CB-1008 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, with a 61-degree lie and a 37-inch length in a standard build.

Is the Miura CB-1008 forgiving?

By Miura standards, yes — its 19-millimetre 7-iron sole was the widest of any CB-1000-series head, and the weight bar at the bottom of the cavity keeps the centre of gravity low for easier launch, while the head stays compact with a thin topline and minimal offset.

When did the Miura CB-1008 come out?

It was released in Japan on 22 February 2017 and reached Western markets that October as part of the MG Collection at $279 per head. It left the line-up around 2019, when the CB-301 took over the wide-sole cavity-back role.

What does the W.D.D. stamp on the Miura CB-1008 mean?

Weight, dimension and density — Miura's 'accurate forging' designation applied to the MG Collection heads, denoting the tolerances held through the Himeji forging process.

Finding a used set

The Miura CB-1008 ran from 2017 to 2019 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
