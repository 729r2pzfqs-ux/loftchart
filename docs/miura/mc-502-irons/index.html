About the Miura MC-502

The MC-502 is Miura's muscle-cavity players iron, announced on 15 November 2022 as the successor to the MC-501 with retail orders opening in 2023 at roughly 350 US dollars per head. Compared with the MC-501 it carries less offset, a softer and more rounded top line and toe profile, and improved turf interaction, keeping a near-blade address look while a shallow back relief adds a small measure of forgiveness. Heads are hand-forged from S20C soft carbon steel in Himeji, Japan and offered 3-iron through pitching wedge in a satin chrome finish, with a copper-finish variant added later. Miura sells heads component-style through authorized dealers and fitters, so there is no stock shaft; the lengths below are the standard steel lengths from Miura's own spec table. Miura publishes face progression rather than offset, and no bounce or swing weight, so those keys are omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Miura MC-502 7-iron?

The Miura MC-502 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch standard steel length.

What did the Miura MC-502 replace?

The MC-502 succeeded the MC-501 when it was announced on 15 November 2022, keeping the muscle-cavity concept but with less offset, a softer rounded top line and toe, and improved turf interaction.

Is the Miura MC-502 a blade?

Not quite. It is a muscle-cavity design: it looks close to a blade at address, but a shallow relief in the back adds slightly more forgiveness than Miura's pure MB muscle-backs.

What clubs are available in the Miura MC-502?

Heads are offered 3-iron through pitching wedge (46 degrees), sold component-style through Miura's authorized dealers and fitters in satin chrome or copper finishes.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Miura MC-502 is a current players set in the Miura range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Miura MC-501. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
