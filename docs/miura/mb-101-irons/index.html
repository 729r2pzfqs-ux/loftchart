About the Miura MB-101

The MB-101 is Miura's flagship muscle-back blade, unveiled on 3 October 2019 as the company's first new blade iron since the MB-001 of 2013, with the sole width and face thickness reworked from that predecessor and a smoother head-to-hosel transition. Each head is hand-forged from S20C soft carbon steel at the Miura family factory in Himeji, Japan, with the muscle pad redistributed relative to earlier Miura blades to sit more directly behind the impact area. Lofts are traditional — a 34-degree 7-iron and a 47-degree pitching wedge — across a 3-iron through PW range, and heads are offered in Satin Chrome and Black QPQ finishes. Miura sells heads through authorized dealers and fitters and builds sets to order from a shaft menu, so there is no single stock shaft; specs below are Miura's published men's steel standard. A limited Retro edition wearing Miura's original logo was released in August 2022, and the standard MB-101 remains in the line at 310 dollars per head as of mid-2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Miura MB-101 7-iron?

The Miura MB-101 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

When was the Miura MB-101 released?

Miura unveiled the MB-101 on 3 October 2019. It was the company's first new blade iron since 2013 and remains in the line.

What clubs come in a Miura MB-101 set?

Heads are offered from 3-iron through pitching wedge (47 degrees). Miura sells them through authorized dealers and fitters, who build any composition to order — 4-PW is the most common set.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2019, the Miura MB-101 is a current blade set in the Miura range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Miura MB-001. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
