About the Miura CB-302

The CB-302 is Miura's mid-size cavity-back, announced on 14 March 2023 as the successor to the CB-301 and billed by Miura as the longest forged cavity-back iron it has ever produced, with the widest sole in the company's history at 19 mm on the 7-iron. Heads are hand-forged from S20C soft carbon steel at the family factory in Himeji, Japan. Despite the larger profile the loft ladder stays traditional, with a 32-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge, and heads run 4-iron through gap wedge. Miura sells heads component-style through authorized dealers and fitters, so there is no stock shaft; the lengths below are the standard steel lengths from Miura's own spec table. Miura publishes face progression rather than offset, and no bounce or swing weight, so those keys are omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Miura CB-302 7-iron?

The Miura CB-302 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch standard steel length.

What did the Miura CB-302 replace?

The CB-302 succeeded the CB-301, Miura's previous mid-size cavity-back, when it was announced in March 2023.

Is the Miura CB-302 forgiving?

By Miura standards, yes. Miura calls it the longest forged cavity-back it has ever made, with the widest sole in company history at 19 mm on the 7-iron, though the lofts remain traditional with a 32-degree 7-iron.

What clubs are available in the Miura CB-302?

Heads are offered 4-iron through gap wedge (48 degrees), sold component-style through Miura's authorized dealers and fitters.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Miura CB-302 is a current players set in the Miura range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Miura CB-301. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
