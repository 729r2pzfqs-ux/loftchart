Miura Golf Club Specifications

Miura Giken has forged irons in Himeji since 1957, and the heads are still hand-forged from S20C by the Miura family workshop. Heads are sold component-style through authorised fitters rather than as stock sets, so published charts cover loft, lie and length but rarely a stock shaft.
