About the Tour Edge Hot Launch Max

The Hot Launch Max is the cavity-back iron in the Hot Launch Max series that replaced Tour Edge's 525 line in February 2026, sold alongside the hollow-bodied Max D. It keeps the deep undercut cavity and VIBRCOR insert of the E525 it follows, with the same lies and lengths, but the lofts are weaker through most of the set: 29 degrees at the 7-iron against 27.5, and 43 at the pitching wedge against 42. The set runs 4-iron to AW, in right and left hand, with a graphite or a steel stock shaft.

Stock shafts

  • Aldila Ascent — graphite · 55-59 g · flexes: A, R, S
  • KBS Max MT 85 — steel · 85 g · flexes: R, S

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tour Edge Hot Launch Max 7-iron?

The Hot Launch Max 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

Are the Hot Launch Max lofts stronger than the E525?

No, they are weaker through most of the set. The 4-iron is 20 degrees against 19 on the E525, the 7-iron 29 against 27.5, the pitching wedge 43 against 42 and the AW 48 against 46. Lies and lengths are the same.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Hot Launch Max?

43 degrees, with a 48-degree AW below it.

What shafts do the Hot Launch Max irons come with?

The stock graphite shaft is the Aldila Ascent in A, R and S flex, and the stock steel shaft is the KBS Max MT 85 in R and S flex.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Tour Edge Hot Launch Max is a current game improvement set in the Tour Edge range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Tour Edge Hot Launch E525. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
