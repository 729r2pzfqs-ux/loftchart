About the Tour Edge Hot Launch E523

The Hot Launch E523 Iron-Wood is the third Extreme Spec model in Tour Edge's value line, announced on 11 October 2022 and at retail from 1 November 2022 as the 2023-season line at $99.99 per club. The numbers carry over from the E522 unchanged — a 20-degree #3 through 49-degree AW around a 32-degree 7-iron — but the head moved from hybrid-like towards a sleeker hollow-body iron: new concave body shaping slims the top line and hides the back end at address, while the patented Houdini Sole, shallow face, slice-fighting offset and extreme perimeter weighting stay. Stock shafts are the UST Mamiya HL-SST graphite (49-65 grams) and True Temper XP-85 steel, and the stock combo set paired 4 and 5 hybrids with 6-PW iron-woods in both right and left hand.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tour Edge Hot Launch E523 7-iron?

The Hot Launch E523 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What changed between the Hot Launch E522 and E523?

The spec chart is identical; the change is the head. The E523 adopted concave body shaping that slims the top line and makes the bulbous back end less visible at address, moving the look from hybrid-like towards a hollow-body iron.

Why does the E523 have weaker lofts than the C523?

As a super-game-improvement iron-wood, the E523's distance comes from its metalwood-style hollow body and very low CG rather than strong lofts — the extra loft (32 versus 29 degrees at the 7-iron) helps its slower-swinging target player flight the ball.

What did the Hot Launch E523 stock combo set include?

The standard set combined 4 and 5 Hot Launch hybrids with 6-iron through pitching wedge iron-woods, for both men and women in right or left hand.

Finding a used set

The Tour Edge Hot Launch E523 ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
