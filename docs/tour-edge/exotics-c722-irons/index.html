About the Tour Edge Exotics C722

The Exotics C722 was the players-distance iron in Tour Edge's Exotics 722 Series, announced 15 February 2022 and at retail worldwide from 5 March 2022 at $899.99 for a 7-piece steel set. It combines a military-grade maraging steel L-Cup face with the Diamond Face VFT variable-thickness pattern on a hollow, dual-VIBRCOR TPU-filled body, in a player-preferred shape with a thinner topline than the companion E722. Its loft ladder — 30 degrees at the 7-iron, 44 at the pitching wedge — was carried over unchanged by the C723 that replaced it in February 2023. Stock 7-piece sets were 4-PW or 5-PW plus AW, with heads offered from the 3-iron through the 49-degree AW; Tour Edge does not publish offset, bounce or swing weight for this model, so those keys are omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tour Edge Exotics C722 7-iron?

The Exotics C722 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37.25-inch standard steel length.

When did the Tour Edge Exotics C722 irons come out?

Tour Edge announced the Exotics 722 Series irons on 15 February 2022, with worldwide retail availability from 5 March 2022. The C722 was replaced by the Exotics C723, announced in February 2023 with the same loft, lie and length specs.

What is the difference between the Tour Edge Exotics C722 and E722?

The C722 is the smaller players-distance model with a hollow maraging-steel-faced body, a thinner topline and more traditional lofts. The E722 is the larger game-improvement model in the same series, with a one-piece body, wider sole and stronger lofts.

Finding a used set

The Tour Edge Exotics C722 ran from 2022 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
