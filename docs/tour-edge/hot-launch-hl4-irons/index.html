About the Tour Edge Hot Launch HL4

The HL4 iron is the standard cavity-back model in Tour Edge's fourth Hot Launch generation, announced in July 2019 alongside an HL4 Iron-Wood and an HL4 Triple Combo set as the brand's value-priced, custom-fit line. Cast from soft 431 stainless steel, it pairs an expanded undercut cavity with toe weighting and variable face thickness, with modest game-improvement lofts — a 31-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge. Stock shafts were the UST Mamiya-for-Tour Edge graphite or the KBS Tour 90 steel, both built to the same lengths, and a seven-piece set sold for $420 in steel. The Hot Launch line split into the C (Competition) and E (Extreme) ranges when the 521 series arrived in late 2020, making the C521 the HL4 iron's direct successor.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tour Edge HL4 7-iron?

The Hot Launch HL4 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What shafts did the Tour Edge HL4 irons come with?

Stock options were an UST Mamiya graphite shaft made for Tour Edge in L, A, R and S flexes, or the KBS Tour 90 steel shaft in regular and stiff. Unusually, both builds used the same lengths.

What replaced the Tour Edge HL4 irons?

The Hot Launch 521 series, announced in October 2020, split the line into the C521 Competition Spec cavity back — the HL4 iron's direct successor, with the same loft ladder — and the E521 Extreme Spec iron-wood.

Are the HL4 wedges the same length as the short irons?

Yes — the pitching wedge through lob wedge are all built at 35.75 inches with a 64-degree lie, an unusually uniform wedge setup.

Finding a used set

The Tour Edge Hot Launch HL4 ran from 2019 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
