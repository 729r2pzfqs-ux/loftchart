About the Tour Edge Exotics EXS

The Exotics EXS was Tour Edge's first "unapologetically strong"-lofted distance iron, announced 10 October 2018 for 1 November 2018 retail (the set is nonetheless widely sold and reviewed as a 2019-model-year club). Founder Dave Glod built it around a hollow lower cavity and a forged cup face with a web-shaped variable-thickness pattern to boost ball speed and limit falloff on off-center hits, a 19-gram tungsten weight in the mid-toe of the 4- through 7-irons for stability, and a thermoplastic-elastomer insert (with a vibration-damping gel coating) fitted into the top of the hollow cavity to control feel. A distinctive ramped sole raises the middle section to open up heel and toe relief for turf interaction. The 21.5-degree 5-iron was among the strongest in the game-improvement category at launch. The seven-piece set (4-PW) retailed for $700 in steel, $800 in graphite, with AW, GW and SW available; stock shafts were the KBS Max 80 steel and Mitsubishi Tensei CK Blue graphite.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tour Edge Exotics EXS 7-iron?

The Exotics EXS 7-iron has a loft of 27.5 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a standard length of 37.25 inches.

How strong is the Tour Edge Exotics EXS 5-iron loft?

At 21.5 degrees, it was among the strongest 5-iron lofts in the game-improvement category at its October 2018 launch — only a couple of niche competitor models were stronger at the time.

What clubs came in the Tour Edge Exotics EXS iron set?

A seven-piece stock set ran 4-iron through pitching wedge, with AW, GW and SW available as additional pieces, for a full 4-through-SW range.

Finding a used set

The Tour Edge Exotics EXS ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
