About the Tour Edge Exotics EXS Pro

The Exotics EXS Pro Forged was a limited-edition, tour-driven players iron released 15 June 2020 in a run of just 250 sets, built from higher-grade Japanese S25C carbon steel and CNC-milled throughout. It pairs a compact, short heel-to-toe forged cavity-back shape with a 10-gram tungsten toe weight in the 3- through 6-irons for extra forgiveness, and an acoustic engineering cavity insert combining steel, ABS polymer and a damping gel for feel. It launched alongside a companion true-blade model, the Exotics EXS Blade, which uses weaker lofts (27-degree 5-iron versus this model's 25) and a thinner top line; the Pro Forged has a slightly thicker top line and a stronger, more distance-oriented loft set for a players club (a 25-degree 5-iron and 46-degree pitching wedge, developed with PGA Tour player feedback and robotic testing).

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Tour Edge Exotics EXS Pro 7-iron?

The Exotics EXS Pro Forged 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a standard length of 36.75 inches.

How limited was the Tour Edge Exotics EXS Pro release?

Very: Tour Edge built only 250 sets of the EXS Pro Forged, released 15 June 2020 at $149.99 per club, developed with feedback from PGA Tour players and extensive robotic testing.

What is the difference between the Exotics EXS Pro Forged and Exotics EXS Blade?

Both launched the same day from the same S25C carbon steel, but the Pro Forged is a compact cavity back with a slightly thicker top line and stronger lofts (25-degree 5-iron), while the Blade is a true muscle-back with a thinner top line and weaker lofts (27-degree 5-iron).

Finding a used set

The Tour Edge Exotics EXS Pro ran from 2020 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
