About the Vega VDC

The VDC (Dual Cavity) sits in the middle of Vega's Japan-forged Classic Line, alongside a muscle-back VMB blade and a shallow-cavity VSC - a family the brand builds to one shared loft and lie chart so the three can be blended into a single set. GolfWRX's Club Junkie review described "fantastic milling work on the back side with dual cavities that allow Vega to adjust the CG higher on the irons for the perfect players cavity set," with a slightly squarer toe and edgier look than the Mizar range and a narrow, pre-worn leading edge for turf interaction; all three Classic Line faces are micro-milled for spin consistency. Lofts run strong for a players iron - a 31-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge across a 4-PW factory chart - aimed at accomplished ball-strikers wanting extra distance without losing feel. Reviews clustered in late 2022 as Western coverage caught up with the Japan-forged range; stock build uses the Shimada Steel Taper Tour shaft, with a 4-PW steel set priced around £1,645 in the UK.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Vega VDC 7-iron?

The Vega VDC 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees and a lie of 61.5 degrees - strong for a players cavity iron, aimed at accomplished ball-strikers wanting extra distance.

How does the Vega VDC differ from the Mizar range?

The VDC belongs to Vega's separate Classic Line rather than the Star Range Mizar irons - a squarer toe, edgier look and dual-cavity milling that raises the centre of gravity, alongside Classic Line siblings VMB (blade) and VSC (shallow cavity) that share the same loft and lie chart.

What shaft comes stock in the Vega VDC?

The Shimada Steel Taper Tour, as tested by Golf Monthly in its 4-PW review set.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2021, the Vega VDC is a current players set in the Vega range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
