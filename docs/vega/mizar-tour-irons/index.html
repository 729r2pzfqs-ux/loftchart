About the Vega Mizar Tour

The Mizar Tour is the compact multi-material iron in Vega's Star Range - a one-piece body forged from S25C carbon steel with a softer S20C face insert held at a constant 3.5 mm thickness. Weight is removed internally near the sole to keep the centre of gravity high for a flighted, penetrating ball, and moves across the head through the set. GolfWRX spotlighted the model in October 2019 and Vega's own site has carried the same loft, lie, face progression and bounce chart from at least January 2022 through the present, so the specs below describe the club as sold across its whole run; Vega later marketed it under the "next generation, multi-material" banner alongside the VDC, and reviews in late 2022 renewed attention on it. Vega builds to order through fitters and publishes no standard lengths or offsets, so those keys are omitted. The set runs 4-iron to attack wedge with a 30-degree 7-iron and 42-degree pitching wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Vega Mizar Tour 7-iron?

The Vega Mizar Tour 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a 61.5-degree lie and 2.5 degrees of bounce.

Is the Vega Mizar Tour forged?

Yes. The one-piece body is forged from S25C carbon steel and carries a softer S20C carbon steel face insert at a constant 3.5 mm thickness.

Is the Mizar Tour a blade?

No. It is a compact multi-material players-distance design: the constant-thickness face and internal weight removal near the sole give it distance and a penetrating flight in a players profile.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2019, the Vega Mizar Tour is a current players distance set in the Vega range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
