About the Vega Mizar Max

The Mizar Max is Vega's driving iron rather than a full iron set - at launch MyGolfSpy called it "the only driving iron on the market" with a titanium face, adding that Vega had plans to extend it into a full set that had not materialised as of Vega's 2025 range announcement (which instead introduced a separate game-improvement model, the Lyra). A soft steel body with an internal cavity carries a thin, 2.8 mm floating titanium face for extra ball speed, in a thin-topline, squat heel-to-toe profile with sole relief aimed at accomplished players wanting a penetrating, high-launching long-iron replacement rather than higher handicappers needing forgiveness through a full set. Vega sells it only as the #2, #3 and #4 heads, with fully CNC-milled grooves and surface texturing and the same interchangeable weight-bar system as the rest of the Mizar range.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Vega Mizar Max a full iron set?

No. Vega sells it only as a driving iron in the #2, #3 and #4 lofts - a long-iron/hybrid replacement - not as a complete 4-PW or similar set, despite early coverage noting Vega's plans to expand it.

What is the loft of a Vega Mizar Max 4-iron?

20 degrees, with a 60-degree lie and a 38.5-inch steel length - the strongest (shortest-headed) club in the three-piece Mizar Max range.

What makes the Vega Mizar Max different from the Mizar Pro?

The Mizar Max pairs a soft steel body with a thin 2.8 mm floating titanium face for extra ball speed in a driving-iron head, while the Mizar Pro is a full forged S20C iron set (Blade or Cavity) covering 4-iron through pitching wedge.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Vega Mizar Max is a current players distance set in the Vega range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
