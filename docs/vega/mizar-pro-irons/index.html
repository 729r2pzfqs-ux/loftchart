About the Vega Mizar Pro

The Mizar Pro replaced the original Mizar as the flagship of Vega's Star Range when Golf Monthly reviewed it in October 2023, after MyGolfSpy noted the original had run six years in the catalogue. Vega sells the head in two shapes off one identical spec chart - a traditional muscle-back Mizar Pro Blade and a thin-topline Mizar Pro Cavity - both forged from a single piece of S20C soft steel with a brushed chrome finish and a through-the-head weight port that runs from heel to toe. The weight bar itself changes material by loft: tungsten in the long irons, titanium through the middle of the set and aluminium in the short irons, shifting the centre of gravity to help launch without changing head shape. Lofts are strong for a players iron, about three degrees stronger than a Titleist 620 MB club for club, with a 32-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge across a 4-PW factory chart. Stock build uses the Shimada Nine9 steel shaft and VEGA Tour Density grip; UK retail for a 4-PW set started at £1,743.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Vega Mizar Pro 7-iron?

The Vega Mizar Pro 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees and a lie of 61.5 degrees, with a 37-inch steel length - about 3 degrees stronger than a comparable Titleist 620 MB.

Is the Vega Mizar Pro a blade or a cavity back?

Both - Vega sells the Mizar Pro as a traditional muscle-back Blade and a thinner-topline Cavity model off one identical loft, lie and length chart, differentiated by head shape rather than numbers.

What is the Mizar Pro's weight bar made of?

It varies by club: tungsten in the long irons, titanium through the middle irons and aluminium in the short irons, shifting the centre of gravity to help launch without altering the forged S20C head shape.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Vega Mizar Pro is a current players set in the Vega range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Vega Mizar. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
