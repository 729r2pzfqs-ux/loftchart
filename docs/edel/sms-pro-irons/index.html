About the Edel SMS Pro

The SMS Pro is the compact players iron in Edel Golf's Swing Match System line, announced on 23 January 2023 and at fitters from 24 February 2023 at $250 per club. Where the 2022 SMS is a hollow-body distance head, the Pro is forged from 1025 carbon steel in a multi-step Precision Grain Forging process, with a thin topline, minimal offset, a CNC-milled tri-step sole — leading-edge bounce, a middle bounce tier and trailing relief — and the same three weight ports in the muscle, stocked with two 2-gram titanium weights and one 8-gram tungsten weight that a fitter repositions to match the swing. Lofts are traditional (32-degree 7-iron, 45-degree pitching wedge) across a right-hand-only 3-PW range, sold fitter-built with KBS Tour steel as the stock shaft. Still current as of mid-2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of an Edel SMS Pro 7-iron?

The Edel SMS Pro 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 62-degree lie and a 37-inch standard length.

When did the Edel SMS Pro irons come out?

Edel announced the SMS Pro on 23 January 2023 and it reached select fitters and retailers on 24 February 2023 at $250 per club.

Is the Edel SMS Pro a blade?

No — it is a compact one-piece-forged players cavity with a thin topline and minimal offset, distinguished by three movable weight ports in the muscle and a CNC-milled tri-step sole.

How does the SMS Pro differ from the Edel SMS?

The SMS Pro is a compact one-piece 1025 forging for better players, while the SMS is a larger hollow-body players-distance head; both share the Swing Match movable-weight system and a 45-degree pitching wedge, but the Pro runs 3-PW where the SMS runs 4-iron to gap wedge.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Edel SMS Pro is a current players set in the Edel range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
