About the Honma TW737 Vs

The Vs sits between the traditional V and the pocket-cavity P in Honma's 2016 Tour World 737 range: a forged mild-steel cavity back with a slightly larger face and lofts about two degrees stronger than the V (30-degree 7-iron), aimed at players who want a compact look with a little more speed and height. The set runs 3 through 11 — Honma numbers the scoring clubs, so the 43-degree 10 is the pitching wedge and the 49-degree 11 the gap wedge. Lengths below are the N.S. Pro Modus3 Tour 105 steel build; Vizard graphite builds ran 0.375 inch longer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW737 Vs 7-iron?

The Honma TW737 Vs 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a Modus3 Tour 105 steel length of 36.75 inches.

What is the difference between the Honma TW737 V and Vs?

The Vs has a slightly larger forged cavity-back head with lofts about two degrees stronger than the V (30 versus 32 degrees in the 7-iron), plus a nine-club set that adds the 49-degree 11 gap wedge. It sits between the V players iron and the P pocket cavity.

What do the 10 and 11 irons in a Honma TW737 Vs set mean?

Honma numbers its scoring clubs: the 10 at 43 degrees is the pitching wedge and the 11 at 49 degrees is the gap wedge.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW737 Vs ran from 2016 to 2018 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
