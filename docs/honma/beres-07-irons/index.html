About the Honma Beres 07

The seventh-generation Beres is Honma's ultra-premium lightweight line: a forged mild-steel body carries a cast maraging-steel L-cup face with inside and outside sole slots in the 5- through 9-irons, and a rolled maraging-steel flat face in the scoring clubs, all built for golfers with moderate swing speeds who want hand-finished Sakata craftsmanship. Lofts are strong for the category (27-degree 7-iron) because the deep, low centre of gravity is tuned to launch high regardless. Stock shafts are Honma's ARMRQ 47 / ARMRQ 42 graphite in ascending 2-Star to 5-Star grades at very light C9-D1 swing weights, with matching grades of shaft technology and price. Honma numbers the scoring clubs: the 40-degree 10 is the pitching wedge and the 45-degree 11 the gap wedge, ahead of 50-degree AW and 55-degree SW. The loft, lie and offset table carried over unchanged into the Beres 08 that replaced it in 2021; only the lengths grew, by a quarter inch through the mid-irons.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma Beres 07 7-iron?

The Honma Beres 07 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37 inches — a strong loft that the deep, low centre of gravity is designed to launch high despite the number.

How does the Honma Beres 07 compare to the Beres 08 that replaced it?

The loft, lie and offset table is identical between the two generations. The Beres 08 grew the lengths by a quarter inch through the mid-irons (5-10) and updated the face alloy naming (AM355P cast face, ES235 rolled face) and shaft lineup (ARMRQ MX).

Who are Honma Beres 07 irons for?

Golfers with moderate swing speeds who want an easy-to-accelerate club: the ARMRQ 47/42 graphite builds run very light C9-D1 swing weights, wrapped in Honma's hand-finished Sakata craftsmanship and star-graded shaft technology.

Finding a used set

The Honma Beres 07 ran from 2019 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
