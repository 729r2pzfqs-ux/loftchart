About the Honma T//WORLD Px (2026)

The 2026 Px is the distance-oriented model in Honma's T//WORLD iron line, announced on 26 May 2026 and released in Japan on 19 June 2026 in place of the TW767 Px; Honma's US store sells it as the "T//WORLD IRON Px 26". Honma describes it as a two-piece construction, a forged S25C soft iron body with a high-strength L-cup face, now chromium molybdenum steel in the 5 to 8 irons, and a vibration-damping emblem in the cavity. Lofts, lies, lengths and offsets are unchanged from the TW767 Px through the 9-iron, with a 30-degree 7-iron, but the pitching wedge, which Honma now numbers as the 10-iron, is 42 degrees rather than 43, and the published table no longer includes an A-wedge. Figures are the N.S.PRO 950GH neo steel build in S flex, with lengths measured by the Japanese 60-degree method; the VIZARD IRON Lite graphite build is a quarter inch longer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma T//WORLD Px (2026) 7-iron?

The 2026 Honma T//WORLD Px 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches in the steel-shaft build.

What is the difference between the Honma TW767 Px and the 2026 Px?

Lofts, lies, lengths and offsets match from the 5-iron through the 9-iron. The 2026 pitching wedge, numbered as the 10-iron, is 42 degrees instead of 43, the published set no longer includes the 49-degree A-wedge, and the face of the 5 to 8 irons is chromium molybdenum steel.

What is the 10-iron in the Honma T//WORLD Px (2026) set?

Honma numbers the pitching wedge as the 10-iron. It has 42 degrees of loft, a 63-degree lie and a length of 35.5 inches in the steel-shaft build.

When were the 2026 Honma T//WORLD Px irons released?

Honma announced the T//WORLD Tour V, Vx and Px irons on 26 May 2026 and released them in Japan on 19 June 2026.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Honma T//WORLD Px (2026) is a current players distance set in the Honma range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Honma TW767 Px. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
