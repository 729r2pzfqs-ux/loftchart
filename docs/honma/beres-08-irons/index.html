About the Honma Beres 08

The eighth-generation Beres is Honma's ultra-premium lightweight line, built in Sakata for golfers with moderate swing speeds who pay for hand-finished craftsmanship: a forged mild-steel body carries a cast AM355P face in a 3D L-Cup structure with inside and outside sole slots in the 5- through 9-irons, and an ES235 rolled face in the scoring clubs. Lofts are strong for the category (27-degree 7-iron) because the deep, low centre of gravity is tuned to launch high anyway. Stock shafts are Honma's hand-rolled ARMRQ MX graphite in ascending 2-Star to 5-Star grades at very light C9-D0 swing weights, with matching grades of finish and price. Honma numbers the scoring clubs: the 40-degree 10 is the pitching wedge and the 45-degree 11 the gap wedge, ahead of 50-degree AW and 55-degree SW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma Beres 08 7-iron?

The Honma Beres 08 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches — strong lofts that the deep, low centre of gravity is designed to launch high despite the number.

What do the star ratings on Honma Beres 08 clubs mean?

The 2-Star through 5-Star grades refer to the ARMRQ MX shaft and finish level: higher star counts get more advanced shaft layups (M40X and T1100G plies, lower torque) and more elaborate plating and gold detailing, at sharply higher prices. The head specifications are the same.

Who are Honma Beres 08 irons for?

Golfers with moderate swing speeds who struggle to accelerate a standard-weight club: the ARMRQ MX graphite builds run very light C9-D0 swing weights so the club is easy to swing fast, wrapped in hand-finished Sakata craftsmanship.

Finding a used set

The Honma Beres 08 ran from 2021 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
