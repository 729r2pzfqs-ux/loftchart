About the Honma TR20 V

The V is the cavity-back players iron of Honma's Tour Release TR20 family, a one-piece S20C forging with a modest topline, a tour-length blade and weight pushed low in the head. Its 7-iron is 32 degrees, traditional for the category. Honma numbers the scoring clubs, so the 10 at 44 degrees is the pitching wedge and the 11 at 49 the approach wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TR20 V 7-iron?

The Honma TR20 V 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.125 inches.

What is the 11-iron in a Honma TR20 V set?

It is the approach or gap wedge at 49 degrees. Honma numbers the scoring clubs, so 10 is the pitching wedge and 11 is the approach wedge.

When were the Honma TR20 irons released?

Honma announced the Tour Release TR20 line on 20 January 2020.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2020, the Honma TR20 V is a current players set in the Honma range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
