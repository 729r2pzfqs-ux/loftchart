About the Honma TW727 V

The V is the cavity-back players iron of Honma's January 2015 Tour World 727 range, forged from S25C mild steel and marketed as combining distance with workability. Lofts and lie angles carried over essentially unchanged into the TW737 V released the following year (32-degree 7-iron in both). The set runs 3 through 10 — Honma numbers the pitching wedge as the 10-iron at 46 degrees. Lengths below are the N.S. Pro Modus3 Tour 120 steel build; Vizard IB95 graphite builds ran 0.375 inch longer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW727 V 7-iron?

The Honma TW727 V 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and an N.S. Pro Modus3 Tour 120 steel length of 36.75 inches.

What is the difference between the Honma TW727 V and TW727 Vn?

The V has a wider sole and deeper cavity with stronger lofts (32-degree 7-iron) for more forgiveness and distance, while the Vn is a more compact, muscle-back-feeling half-cavity with weaker lofts (34-degree 7-iron) aimed at better players who want a blade-like look.

How does the Honma TW727 V compare to the TW737 V that replaced it?

The two share essentially the same specification: identical 20-to-46-degree loft range, the same 59.5-to-63-degree lie progression and the same forged S25C mild-steel construction. The TW737 V that followed in 2016 is a close continuation of the same head rather than a redesign.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW727 V ran from 2015 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
