About the Honma TW757 Vx

The Vx is the players iron of Honma's 2022 Tour World 757 range, a one-piece S20C forging with a seven-gram tungsten plug in the toe of the 4 to 8 irons to pull the centre of gravity back towards the middle of the face. It carries the TW747 Vx loft chart over unchanged from the 4-iron down — 30 degrees at the 7 — and builds the set an eighth of an inch longer at every club. Honma stamps the scoring clubs P and A on this generation rather than numbering them 10 and 11 as it did on the TW747.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW757 Vx 7-iron?

The Honma TW757 Vx 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 37.125 inches.

What is the difference between the Honma TW747 Vx and TW757 Vx?

The lofts and lies are identical from the 4-iron down. The TW757 Vx is an eighth of an inch longer at every club and adds a seven-gram tungsten toe weight through the 4 to 8 irons.

When were the Honma TW757 Vx irons released?

Honma announced the T//World TW757 collection on 29 March 2022.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW757 Vx ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
