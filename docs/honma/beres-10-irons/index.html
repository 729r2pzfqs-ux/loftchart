About the Honma Beres 10

The tenth-generation Beres, sold in Japan as the BERES 2026 Model, continues Honma's premium lightweight line with a head chart carried over unchanged from the Beres 09: the same lofts, lies, lengths and offsets, with a 27-degree 7-iron. It went on sale in Japan on 30 January 2026 and was announced for the US on 3 February 2026. Honma describes a revised "Speed Core Face" with a three-dimensional L-cup face and variable thickness, and the styling is drawn from Edo Kiriko cut glass. The set is graphite only, on new ARMRQ FLIGHT shafts in 3-Star to 5-Star grades at very light C8 to D0 swing weights, which vary by flex and are not tabled here. Honma numbers the scoring clubs: the 40-degree 10 is the pitching wedge and the 45-degree 11 the gap wedge, ahead of a 50-degree AW and 55-degree SW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma Beres 10 7-iron?

The Honma Beres 10 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What changed between the Honma Beres 09 and Beres 10?

The head chart is identical: the same lofts, lies, lengths and offsets. The Beres 10 introduces the ARMRQ FLIGHT shaft family in place of the 09's ARMRQ FX, a revised Speed Core Face with a three-dimensional L-cup face, and new styling based on Edo Kiriko cut glass.

What are the 10 and 11 irons in a Honma Beres 10 set?

Honma numbers its scoring clubs. The 10 is the pitching wedge at 40 degrees and the 11 is the gap wedge at 45 degrees, followed by a 50-degree AW and a 55-degree SW.

When were Honma Beres 10 irons released?

The line went on sale in Japan, as the BERES 2026 Model, on 30 January 2026, and Honma announced it for the US on 3 February 2026.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Honma Beres 10 is a current super game improvement set in the Honma range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Honma Beres 09. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
