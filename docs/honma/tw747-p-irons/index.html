About the Honma TW747 P

The TW747 P is the forgiving model in Honma's Tour World 747 line, but it is built around a compact head rather than an oversized one: the hosel is shortened to free mass, and roughly 20 grams of tungsten sits in the sole behind a pocket cavity to drop the centre of gravity. Offset is minimal and the top line is narrow. Honma numbers the wedges as 10 and 11 rather than PW and GW; those are recorded below as PW and GW. Two stock lengths are published, one for the VIZARD graphite build and one a quarter inch shorter for the N.S. Pro 950GH steel build — the steel figures are used here.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW747 P 7-iron?

The Honma TW747 P 7-iron has a loft of 28.5 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

Why are Honma irons numbered up to 11?

Honma labels its wedges by continuing the iron numbering: the 10-iron is the pitching wedge at 43.5 degrees and the 11-iron the gap wedge at 48 degrees.

Do Honma TW747 P lengths depend on the shaft?

Yes. Honma publishes two length columns — the VIZARD graphite build runs a quarter inch longer than the N.S. Pro 950GH steel build at every club. The steel figures are the ones recorded here.

When were Honma TW747 P irons made?

Honma announced the Tour World 747 range in 2018 for the 2019 season, and replaced the P model with the TR20 P in 2020.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW747 P ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
