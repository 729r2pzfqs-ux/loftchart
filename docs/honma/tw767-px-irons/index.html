About the Honma TW767 Px

The Px is the players-distance iron of Honma's T//World 767 range and Honma's first "Cap-Back" construction and first three-piece iron in the TW series, combining a forged S25C body with a variable-thickness L-cup face on the 5 to 8 irons for extra ball speed on off-centre strikes. It replaces the TW757 P in Honma's lineup: lofts run about a degree stronger through the middle of the set than the TW757 P (30 degrees at the 7-iron versus 28.5) on a similar lie progression, and the set now extends to a 49-degree A-wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW767 Px 7-iron?

The Honma TW767 Px 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches in the steel-shaft build.

What is the difference between the Honma TW757 P and TW767 Px?

The TW767 Px runs about a degree stronger through the middle irons (30 degrees at the 7-iron versus 28.5 on the TW757 P) and switches to Honma's first Cap-Back, three-piece forged construction with an L-Cup face on the 5 to 8 irons, where the TW757 P was a cast hollow body.

When were the Honma TW767 Px irons released?

Honma announced the T//World TW767 collection on 22 October 2024, with the Px going on sale in the US market around October 2024.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Honma TW767 Px is a current players distance set in the Honma range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Honma TW757 P. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
