About the Honma TW747 Vx

The Vx is the stronger-lofted sibling of the TW747 V, running about two degrees stronger through the middle of the set — its 7-iron is 30 degrees against the V's 32 — while keeping the same lie progression. Honma numbers the scoring clubs, so the 10 is the pitching wedge. Lengths below are the N.S.Pro Modus3 for T//World steel build; the VIZARD IB-WF 85 graphite build is an eighth of an inch longer in the 3-iron.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW747 Vx 7-iron?

The Honma TW747 Vx 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches in the steel build.

What is the difference between the Honma TW747 V and Vx?

The Vx is stronger through the set — 30 degrees at the 7-iron against the V's 32, and 19 at the 3-iron against 20 — on the same lie progression.

When were the Honma TW747 Vx irons made?

They were announced with the rest of the TW747 range on 5 October 2018 and were succeeded by the TW757 range announced in March 2022.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW747 Vx ran from 2018 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
