About the Honma TW767 Vx

The Vx is the players cavity-back iron of Honma's T//World 767 range, forged from S20C carbon steel with a thickened cavity for a softer, deeper sound at impact than the TW757 Vx it replaces. Lofts and lie angles are unchanged club for club from the TW757 Vx — a 30-degree 7-iron — while the set is now offered from the 4-iron down to an A-wedge stamped at 49 degrees. The 4 to 8 irons carry a tungsten toe weight; the 9-iron through A-wedge are single-piece forgings without added tungsten.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW767 Vx 7-iron?

The Honma TW767 Vx 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches in the steel-shaft build.

What is the difference between the Honma TW757 Vx and TW767 Vx?

The lofts, lies and lengths are unchanged club for club between the two generations. The TW767 Vx thickens the cavity for a deeper impact sound and extends the stock set down to a 49-degree A-wedge.

When were the Honma TW767 Vx irons released?

Honma announced the T//World TW767 collection on 22 October 2024.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Honma TW767 Vx is a current players set in the Honma range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Honma TW757 Vx. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
