About the Honma TW727 Vn

The Vn is Honma's "half-cavity" iron in the January 2015 Tour World 727 range: a compact, muscle-back-feeling head forged from S25C mild steel, one step more forgiving than the true-blade TW727 M but sharing that model's lofts, and marketed as a blade alternative for tour staff. Lofts are one step weaker than the cavity-back V (34-degree 7-iron versus 32) with a correspondingly smaller, more compact profile. The set runs 3 through 10 — Honma numbers the pitching wedge as the 10-iron at 46 degrees. Lengths below are the Dynamic Gold S200 steel build; Vizard IB95 graphite builds ran 0.375 inch longer. Lofts, lies and lengths carried over unchanged into the TW737 Vn released the following year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW727 Vn 7-iron?

The Honma TW727 Vn 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a Dynamic Gold steel length of 36.75 inches.

What does the 'n' mean in Honma TW727 Vn?

It marks the near-blade, half-cavity head of the TW727 range: Honma built it to share its lofts with the true-blade TW727 M while adding a touch of cavity-back forgiveness, and marketed it as a blade alternative for its tour staff.

How does the Honma TW727 Vn compare to the TW727 V?

Both are forged from S25C mild steel with the same 3-10 set makeup, but the Vn has weaker lofts (34-degree 7-iron versus the V's 32) and a smaller, more compact head aimed at better players who want a blade-like look and feel.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW727 Vn ran from 2015 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
