About the Honma TR20 B

The B is the muscle back of Honma's Tour Release TR20 family, an S20C forging with a satin finish made in Japan. It runs the weakest lofts of the three TR20 heads at the top of the set — the 3-iron is 20 degrees — but the strongest through the middle, with a 34-degree 7-iron, and its offset falls away sharply in the short irons, from 3.1 mm at the 3-iron to 1.0 mm at the 10.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TR20 B 7-iron?

The Honma TR20 B 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

Is the Honma TR20 B a blade?

Yes. It is a one-piece S20C muscle back with a satin finish, made in Japan, and the least forgiving of the three TR20 iron heads.

When were the Honma TR20 B irons released?

The TR20 family was announced on 20 January 2020 and the B iron reached retail on 17 July 2020.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2020, the Honma TR20 B is a current blade set in the Honma range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
