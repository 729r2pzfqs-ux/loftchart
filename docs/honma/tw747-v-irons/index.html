About the Honma TW747 V

The V is the cavity-back players iron of Honma's 2018 Tour World 747 range, a thin-topline shape with progressive offset and a progressive centre of gravity through the set. Lofts sit a touch stronger than a traditional players iron — the 7-iron is 32 degrees. Honma numbers the scoring clubs rather than lettering them, so the club shown as 10 is the pitching wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW747 V 7-iron?

The Honma TW747 V 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 36.75 inches.

What does the 10-iron in a Honma TW747 V set mean?

It is the pitching wedge. Honma numbers its scoring clubs rather than stamping PW and AW, so the 10 at 46 degrees is the set's pitching wedge.

When were the Honma TW747 irons released?

Honma announced the Tour World 747 range on 5 October 2018.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW747 V ran from 2018 to 2020 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
