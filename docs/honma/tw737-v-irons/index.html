About the Honma TW737 V

The V is the classic cavity-back players iron of Honma's 2016 Tour World 737 range (2017 model year), forged from mild steel in Sakata with a thin top line and a traditional 32-degree 7-iron. The set runs 3 through 10 — Honma numbers the pitching wedge as the 10-iron at 46 degrees. Lofts, lies and the general head concept carried over almost unchanged into the TW747 V two years later. Lengths below are the Dynamic Gold AMT steel build; N.S. Pro 950GH builds ran a quarter inch longer and Vizard graphite builds 0.375 inch longer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW737 V 7-iron?

The Honma TW737 V 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a Dynamic Gold AMT steel length of 36.75 inches.

What does the 10-iron in a Honma TW737 V set mean?

It is the pitching wedge. Honma numbers its scoring clubs rather than stamping PW, so the 10 at 46 degrees is the set's pitching wedge.

When were the Honma TW737 irons released?

Honma announced the Tour World 737 range in autumn 2016 for the 2017 season; it was replaced by the TW747 range announced in October 2018.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW737 V ran from 2016 to 2018 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
