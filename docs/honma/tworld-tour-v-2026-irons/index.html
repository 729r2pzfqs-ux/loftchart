About the Honma T//WORLD Tour V (2026)

The 2026 Tour V is the compact semi-cavity players iron in Honma's T//WORLD iron line, announced on 26 May 2026 and released in Japan on 19 June 2026 in place of the TW767 Tour V; Honma's US store sells it as the "T//WORLD IRON TOUR V 26". The head is a one-piece forging that moves from the S20C steel of the TW767 to softer S15C, and Honma has raised the bounce, to 8 degrees at the 7-iron. Lofts, lies and offsets are unchanged from the TW767 Tour V, with a 32-degree 7-iron, while the published lengths are a quarter inch longer at every club. Honma numbers the pitching wedge as the 10-iron, at 45 degrees. Figures are the stock N.S.PRO MODUS3 for T//WORLD BLACK steel build in S flex, with lengths measured by the Japanese 60-degree method.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma T//WORLD Tour V (2026) 7-iron?

The 2026 Honma T//WORLD Tour V 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.0 inches.

What is the difference between the Honma TW767 Tour V and the 2026 Tour V?

Lofts, lies and offsets are unchanged club for club. The 2026 head is forged from S15C rather than S20C steel, has more bounce (8 degrees at the 7-iron), and is listed a quarter inch longer at every club, for example 37.0 inches against 36.75 at the 7-iron.

What is the 10-iron in the Honma T//WORLD Tour V (2026) set?

Honma numbers the pitching wedge as the 10-iron. It has 45 degrees of loft, a 63-degree lie and a length of 35.5 inches.

When were the 2026 Honma T//WORLD Tour V irons released?

Honma announced the T//WORLD Tour V, Vx and Px irons on 26 May 2026 and released them in Japan on 19 June 2026.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Honma T//WORLD Tour V (2026) is a current players set in the Honma range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Honma TW767 Tour V. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
