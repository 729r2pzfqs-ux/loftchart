About the Honma TW727 P

The P is the distance model of Honma's January 2015 Tour World 727 range, marketed as pursuing distance and easy launch. Unlike the later TW737 P (a cast, single-material pocket cavity), the TW727 P is a forged two-piece head with a maraging stainless-steel face welded to a mild-steel body. Lofts and lie angles carried over essentially unchanged into the TW737 P released the following year. The set runs 3 through 11 plus a 56-degree sand wedge — Honma numbers the scoring clubs, so the 43-degree 10 is the pitching wedge and the 49-degree 11 the gap wedge. Lengths below are the N.S. Pro 950GH steel build; Vizard IB85 and Vizard I55 graphite builds ran roughly a quarter inch longer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW727 P 7-iron?

The Honma TW727 P 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and an N.S. Pro 950GH steel length of 37 inches.

Is the Honma TW727 P forged or cast?

It is forged, and built as a two-piece head with a maraging stainless-steel face welded to a mild-steel body. This differs from the TW737 P that replaced it, which is a single-material cast head.

What is the set makeup of the Honma TW727 P?

3-iron through the numbered wedges plus a sand wedge: the 43-degree 10 is the pitching wedge, the 49-degree 11 the gap wedge, and the SW is 56 degrees.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW727 P ran from 2015 to 2016 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
