About the Honma TW777 PCB Max

The PCB Max is the sole genuinely new iron head Honma introduced with the TW777 ("T//World 2026") collection — marketed alongside carried-over Tour V, Vx and Px heads from the TW767 range rather than replacing them outright. It pairs a soft forged S25C body with a full-cup AM355P face on the 5 to 8 irons and a flat ES235 face on the 9-iron through sand wedge, plus a milled "Honma CUT" top-line slope for a cleaner look at address. It is the most forgiving, highest-launching iron in the current T//World range and the only one sold with a stock sand wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW777 PCB Max 7-iron?

The Honma TW777 PCB Max 7-iron has a loft of 28 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

Does the Honma TW777 PCB Max come with a sand wedge?

Yes. Unlike the other T//World irons, the PCB Max ships with a 56-degree sand wedge as part of its 5-SW set, in addition to Honma's usual numbered 10 and 11 scoring clubs and an AW.

When was the Honma TW777 PCB Max released?

Honma announced the TW777 collection, including the PCB Max irons, on 11 December 2025, marketed as the T//World 2026 line.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Honma TW777 PCB Max is a current game improvement set in the Honma range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
