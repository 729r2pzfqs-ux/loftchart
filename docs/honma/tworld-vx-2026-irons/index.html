About the Honma T//WORLD Vx (2026)

The 2026 Vx is the players cavity-back in Honma's T//WORLD iron line, announced on 26 May 2026 and released in Japan on 19 June 2026 in place of the TW767 Vx; Honma's US store sells it as the "T//WORLD IRON Vx 26". The head moves from the S20C steel of the TW767 to softer S15C, keeps a tungsten toe weight in the 4 to 8 irons, and carries less offset than before, 2.5 mm at the 7-iron against 3.0 mm. Lofts, lies and lengths are unchanged from the TW767 Vx through the 9-iron, with a 30-degree 7-iron, but the pitching wedge, which Honma now numbers as the 10-iron, is 42 degrees rather than 43. Figures are the stock N.S.PRO MODUS3 for T//WORLD RED steel build in S flex, with lengths measured by the Japanese 60-degree method.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma T//WORLD Vx (2026) 7-iron?

The 2026 Honma T//WORLD Vx 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.0 inches.

What is the difference between the Honma TW767 Vx and the 2026 Vx?

Lofts, lies and lengths match from the 4-iron through the 9-iron. The 2026 head is forged from S15C rather than S20C steel, has 0.5 mm less offset at every club, and its pitching wedge, numbered as the 10-iron, is 42 degrees instead of 43.

What is the 10-iron in the Honma T//WORLD Vx (2026) set?

Honma numbers the pitching wedge as the 10-iron. It has 42 degrees of loft, a 63-degree lie and a length of 35.5 inches.

When were the 2026 Honma T//WORLD Vx irons released?

Honma announced the T//WORLD Tour V, Vx and Px irons on 26 May 2026 and released them in Japan on 19 June 2026.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Honma T//WORLD Vx (2026) is a current players set in the Honma range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Honma TW767 Vx. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
