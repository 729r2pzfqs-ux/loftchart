About the Honma Beres Aizu

Beres Aizu is a 2022 limited-edition dress-up of the Beres 08 head, built on the identical forged mild-steel body/AM355P+ cast-face/ES235 rolled-face construction and the exact same loft, lie, offset and length table as the standard Beres 08. What sets it apart is traditional Aizu-nuri lacquerwork from the Aizu region of Fukushima applied to the clubhead, a craft Honma has used on limited runs since its earliest Beres sets. Stock shafts are the ARMRQ MX 2-Star through 5-Star graphite range at very light C9-D1 swing weights. Honma numbers the scoring clubs: the 40-degree 10 is the pitching wedge and the 45-degree 11 the gap wedge, ahead of 50-degree AW and 55-degree SW.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma Beres Aizu 7-iron?

The Honma Beres Aizu 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches — identical to the standard Beres 08 7-iron.

What is different about the Honma Beres Aizu compared to the standard Beres 08?

Nothing in the head geometry or loft/lie/length table — it is club for club identical. The Aizu edition adds traditional Aizu-nuri lacquer artwork from Fukushima's Aizu region to the clubhead as a 2022 limited-edition dress-up.

Is the Honma Beres Aizu part of the numbered Beres generations?

No — it is a limited-edition finish variant built on the Beres 08 platform (launched 2022, a year before the Beres 09 generation), not a separate numbered step in Honma's mainline Beres lineage.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Honma Beres Aizu is a current super game improvement set in the Honma range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
