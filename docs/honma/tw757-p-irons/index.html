About the Honma TW757 P

The TW757 P is the compact players-distance iron of Honma's 2022 Tour World 757 range, built in 17-4 stainless with an undercut pocket cavity, roughly 12 grams of tungsten in the sole, a six-gram toe weight and a 2.2 mm L-cup face. Lofts match the TW747 P it replaced club for club — the 7-iron is 28.5 degrees — while the lengths run a quarter inch longer. Honma numbers the scoring clubs, so the P shown here is the pitching wedge at 43.5 degrees.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW757 P 7-iron?

The Honma TW757 P 7-iron has a loft of 28.5 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What is the difference between the Honma TW747 P and TW757 P?

The lofts and lies are identical club for club. The TW757 P is a quarter inch longer at every club and switches to a 17-4 stainless hollow body with an L-cup face, where the TW747 P was a forging with tungsten sole weighting.

When were the Honma TW757 irons released?

Honma announced the T//World TW757 collection on 29 March 2022.

Finding a used set

The Honma TW757 P ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
