About the Honma TW767 Tour V

The Tour V is the compact, one-piece forged iron of Honma's T//World 767 range, built for accomplished ball-strikers who want a thin topline and low offset over outright forgiveness. It carries the lowest offset of the four TW767 iron models at every club and the strongest lofts, running a 32-degree 7-iron and a 45-degree pitching wedge. Honma revived the plain "V" nomenclature for this release; the previous TW757 generation did not include a corresponding one-piece blade-style model, so this line has no direct predecessor in that generation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Honma TW767 Tour V 7-iron?

The Honma TW767 Tour V 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 61.5 degrees and a length of 36.75 inches.

What set makeup does the Honma TW767 Tour V ship in?

The Tour V is sold 4-P (4-iron through pitching wedge); Honma's spec chart also carries the 4-iron as an added-order option in some markets.

When was the Honma TW767 Tour V released?

Honma announced the T//World TW767 collection on 22 October 2024, with the Tour V available to order by early 2025.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Honma TW767 Tour V is a current players set in the Honma range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
