About the Cobra KING TEC (2024)

The KING TEC replaced the 2022 KING Forged Tec in October 2024 as Cobra's compact players-distance iron, with a body and face that Cobra describes as five-step forged. The loft, lie and length chart is unchanged from the model it replaced — 29.5 degrees at the 7-iron and 44 at the pitching wedge, built to 37.25 inches at the 7-iron — and a 19-degree 3-iron is listed in right hand only. The stock set is 4-iron to pitching wedge in steel. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING TEC (2024) 7-iron?

The 2024 Cobra KING TEC 7-iron has a loft of 29.5 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

Did the lofts change from the 2022 KING Forged Tec?

No. The 4-iron through gap wedge run 21, 23, 26, 29.5, 34, 39, 44 and 49 degrees on both, with the same lies and lengths.

What is the difference between the KING TEC and the KING TEC X?

The KING TEC X is the larger, stronger-lofted model. Its 7-iron is 27 degrees against 29.5 and its pitching wedge 42 against 44, and its lies are one degree more upright. Lengths are the same club for club.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the KING TEC?

44 degrees, with a 49-degree gap wedge below it.

Production status

Introduced in 2024, the Cobra KING TEC (2024) has no confirmed end date on file. It replaced the Cobra KING Forged Tec (2022). LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the Cobra catalogue — check with Cobra or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
