About the Cobra KING (2026)

The KING is Cobra's standard game-improvement iron for 2026, announced in January 2026 alongside the larger KING MAX as the replacement for the DS-ADAPT, and at retail from 6 February 2026 at $999 in steel. It has the more traditional shape and the stronger lofts of the two — a 27.5-degree 7-iron and a 42.5-degree pitching wedge, about 2 degrees stronger than the KING MAX — and Cobra aims it at faster swingers in the 10 to 20 handicap range. A 3D-printed lattice medallion frees mass to lower the centre of gravity, an 80g catapult weight is suspended in polymer behind the 360 SPEEDSHELL face, and the SKID SOLE adds leading-edge bounce. Stock steel sets are 4-iron through pitching wedge or 5-iron through gap wedge; graphite is sold as 5-PW plus gap wedge. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING (2026) 7-iron?

The Cobra KING (2026) 7-iron has a loft of 27.5 degrees, a lie of 63.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

How does the Cobra KING differ from the KING MAX?

The KING has the more traditional shape, less offset and lofts about 2 degrees stronger: a 27.5-degree 7-iron and 42.5-degree pitching wedge against 29.5 and 44.5 on the KING MAX. Lengths are the same. Cobra aims the KING at faster swingers and the MAX at moderate swing speeds.

How do the Cobra KING lofts compare with the DS-ADAPT?

They are half a degree weaker from the 5-iron to the pitching wedge — the 7-iron is 27.5 degrees against 27 and the pitching wedge 42.5 against 42 — while the 4-iron, gap wedge and sand wedge are unchanged at 19, 48 and 54 degrees. Lies and lengths are the same.

What clubs come in a stock Cobra KING set?

Stock steel sets are 4-iron through pitching wedge or 5-iron through gap wedge, and graphite sets are 5-iron through pitching wedge plus gap wedge. The specification table also lists a 54-degree sand wedge.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Cobra KING (2026) is a current game improvement set in the Cobra range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Cobra DS-ADAPT. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
