About the Cobra F-Max Airspeed

The F-Max Airspeed was the lightest iron Cobra had made when it was announced on 12 November 2019, built for golfers with moderate swing speeds and at retail from January 2020. Total club weight dropped about 16 grams against the F-Max Superlite it replaced, through a 5-gram-lighter shaft, a lighter midsize grip and a lighter head, and the men's steel build swings at just D0. The cast cavity-back heads use a low-profile shape with a deep undercut cavity, progressive hosel lengths — shorter in the long irons to drop the centre of gravity, taller in the wedges — heel-biased weighting for a draw, and generous offset that tapers from 6.6 mm at the 4-iron to 1.0 mm at the sand wedge (converted to inches below). Lofts are weak by modern standards at 31.5 degrees for the 7-iron and 45 for the pitching wedge, in keeping with the easy-launch brief. The stock men's steel set was seven pieces, 5-PW plus gap wedge, with combo sets substituting hybrids for the long irons; MyGolfSpy ranked it the best super-game-improvement iron of 2020 and 2021, and Cobra replaced it with the Air-X in January 2022.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra F-Max Airspeed 7-iron?

The Cobra F-Max Airspeed 7-iron has a loft of 31.5 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

When were Cobra F-Max Airspeed irons released?

Cobra announced them on 12 November 2019 and they reached retail in January 2020. The Air-X replaced them in January 2022.

Who were the Cobra F-Max Airspeed irons designed for?

Golfers with moderate swing speeds. They were Cobra's lightest irons to date — about 16 grams lighter in total than the F-Max Superlite, with a D0 steel swing weight — with weak lofts, generous offset and heel-biased draw weighting for easy launch.

Finding a used set

The Cobra F-Max Airspeed ran from 2019 to 2022 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
