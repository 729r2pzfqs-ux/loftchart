Lofts at a glance

About the Cobra LTDx

The LTDx is Cobra's 2022 driver family — announced 19 January and at retail from 11 February 2022 — built around PWR-COR: a 14 g internal steel weight and 5 g external aluminium weight stacked low and forward behind the face, balanced by a 15 g fixed back weight on the core head, chasing "zero CG" bragging rights with near-neutral balance-point numbers. The core LTDx is the distance head, the LS the low-spin model for better players, and the Max the forgiveness/draw head whose movable ~10 g weight plus the MyFly8 draw settings stack up to roughly 18 yards of slice correction. The Aerojet replaced the family in 2023.

Head variants

The heads this family is sold in, with each one’s stock loft options.

LTDx9°, 10.5°, 12°
The core distance head: PWR-COR low-forward weighting plus a 15 g fixed back weight for ball speed with moderate forgiveness.
LTDx LS9°, 10.5°
The low-spin head for better players — no 12° option, flatter flight, and the stiffest stock shaft lineup in the family.
LTDx Max9°, 10.5°, 12°
The forgiveness and draw model: a movable ~10 g weight plays back (neutral, about +5 yards of draw) or heel (about +11 yards), and with the hosel's draw setting the correction reaches roughly 18 yards. A women's build was offered in 10.5° and 12°.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Cobra LTDx come in?

The LTDx and LTDx Max come in 9, 10.5 and 12 degrees; the LTDx LS in 9 and 10.5 only. The MyFly8 hosel extends each head 1.5 degrees either way.

What is the LTDx adjustment chart?

MyFly8 has eight settings: −1.5, −1, standard and +1 degrees in neutral versions, draw versions of −1, standard and +1, and a +1.5 top setting. The SmartPad sole keeps the face square at address in every setting.

How much draw correction does the LTDx Max offer?

Cobra quoted about 5 yards with the weight back, 11 yards with it in the heel, and up to roughly 18 yards with the heel weight combined with a Draw hosel setting.

Is the Cobra LTDx still in production?

No — it ran one cycle, from February 2022 until the Aerojet replaced it in 2023, so the used market is the source. Check which MyFly8 setting a used head is in before judging its loft.

Finding one used

The Cobra LTDx ran from 2022 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable driver may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
