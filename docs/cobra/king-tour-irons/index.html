About the Cobra KING Tour

The KING Tour is the first Cobra iron made by metal injection moulding, a process that packs powdered stainless into the mould under pressure and sinters it, giving a denser, softer-feeling head than a conventional casting and tighter tolerances than a forging. A high-density tungsten weight sits in the toe to put the centre of gravity behind the hitting area. Lofts are traditional — 33 degrees at the 7-iron, 45 at the pitching wedge — and the build is long, 37.50 inches at the 7-iron.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING Tour 7-iron?

The 2020 Cobra KING Tour 7-iron has a loft of 33 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.50 inches.

What is MIM in the Cobra KING Tour?

Metal injection moulding. Powdered stainless steel is injected into a mould under pressure and then sintered, which produces a denser head with tighter shape tolerances than casting and a softer feel than conventional stainless.

When were Cobra KING Tour irons made?

The MIM KING Tour launched in 2020. Cobra later reused the KING Tour name for a 2023 model with different specifications.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the Cobra KING Tour?

45 degrees, with a 50-degree gap wedge below it.

Finding a used set

The Cobra KING Tour ran from 2020 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
