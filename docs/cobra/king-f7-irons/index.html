About the Cobra KING F7

The KING F7 is Cobra's 2017 game-improvement iron and the standard-length version of a set that was also offered in ONE Length. It uses what Cobra called TECFLO construction, a mixed set in which the long irons are full hollow bodies, the mid irons are half hollow and the short irons and wedges are conventional cavity backs, all fronted by a PWRSHELL L-Cup face in high-strength 17-4 stainless steel. T.O.P. Technology removes mass from the centre of the head and redistributes it low and back. Lofts are strong, with a 30-degree 7-iron and a 45-degree pitching wedge, on a 37.25-inch steel 7-iron. The stock set is seven clubs, 5-iron through gap wedge, with 3-iron, 4-iron and sand wedge available to build custom seven- and eight-piece sets. Lengths and swing weights below are the steel-shaft values, and offsets are converted from Cobra's published millimetre figures.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING F7 7-iron?

The Cobra KING F7 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

What clubs come in a stock Cobra KING F7 set?

The stock set is seven clubs, 5-iron through gap wedge. Cobra also catalogued 3-iron, 4-iron and sand wedge heads for custom seven- and eight-piece sets.

What is TECFLO construction in the Cobra KING F7?

TECFLO is Cobra's mixed-set construction: full hollow long irons, half hollow mid irons, and conventional cavity-back short irons and wedges, so each part of the set is built for its own job.

Finding a used set

The Cobra KING F7 ran from 2016 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
