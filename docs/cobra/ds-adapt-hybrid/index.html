Hosel adjustment chart

FutureFit33 · loft ±2° · 33 settings.

Lie adjusts ±2° from stock, independently of loft. The SmartPad sole keeps the face visually square at address in every setting.

The same two-dial system as the DS-ADAPT drivers and fairways: 33 unique loft/lie settings with A1 the neutral stock position. Cobra publishes the code-by-code degree chart only as a graphic reached from a QR code on the hosel, so no position-by-position table is reproduced here. Each stock loft covers ±2°: the 19° 3-hybrid plays 17-21°, the 24° 5-hybrid 22-26°.

About the Cobra DS-ADAPT

The DS-ADAPT hybrid launched in January 2025 with the DS-ADAPT woods and was the first Cobra hybrid with the FutureFit33 hosel, which moves loft and lie independently up to 2° each way. One roughly 125 cc head covers five lofts from a 17° 2-hybrid to a 28° 6-hybrid. The OPTM hybrids replaced the line in January 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Cobra DS-ADAPT hybrid come in?

Five lofts: 17, 19, 21, 24 and 28 degrees — the 2-hybrid through 6-hybrid.

Is the Cobra DS-ADAPT hybrid adjustable?

Yes — it was Cobra's first hybrid with the FutureFit33 hosel, which moves loft and lie independently up to 2 degrees each way across 33 settings, so each head covers a 4 degree loft window.

What replaced the DS-ADAPT hybrid?

The Cobra OPTM hybrids, launched in January 2026, which carry the same 33-setting hosel system.

Buying one

Cobra has since introduced the Cobra OPTM above it in the range, so the DS-ADAPT is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and hybrids one generation back are the sweet spot of the used market. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
