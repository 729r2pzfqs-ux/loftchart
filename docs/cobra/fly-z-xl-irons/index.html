About the Cobra Fly-Z XL

The Fly-Z XL is the oversized, maximum-forgiveness member of Cobra's 2015 Fly-Z iron family. The 4 through 8 irons use a dual-cavity design whose hollow lower half drives the centre of gravity low and deep for easy launch, while the 9-iron through sand wedge switch to a deep perimeter undercut for accuracy; a Speed Channel trench behind the face perimeter thins the face for ball speed. Lofts are about a degree weaker than the standard Fly-Z — 31.5 degrees at the 7-iron, 45 at the pitching wedge — with generous progressive offset for a draw bias, and each club is built a quarter-inch longer than the Fly-Z. The stock set is eight clubs, 4-PW plus gap wedge, in steel; graphite was custom-order. Offsets below are converted from Cobra's published millimetre figures, and swing weights are the steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra Fly-Z XL 7-iron?

The Cobra Fly-Z XL 7-iron has a loft of 31.5 degrees, a lie of 62 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

What clubs come in a stock Cobra Fly-Z XL set?

The stock set is eight clubs, 4-iron through pitching wedge plus a gap wedge, in steel; graphite was a custom order. A 55-degree sand wedge was also catalogued.

What is the difference between the Fly-Z and the Fly-Z XL?

The XL is larger, longer and more forgiving: each club is about a quarter-inch longer, lofts run roughly a degree weaker, offset is greater, and the 4-8 irons use a hollow dual-cavity body where the standard Fly-Z uses an undercut cavity.

Finding a used set

The Cobra Fly-Z XL ran from 2015 to 2017 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
