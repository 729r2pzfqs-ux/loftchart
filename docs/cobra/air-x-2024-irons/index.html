About the Cobra Air-X (2024)

The second-generation Air-X is Cobra's lightweight easy-launch iron, launched in autumn 2023 and marketed as the 2024 AIR-X — some retailers list it as the AIR-X 2. Against the 2022 original the mid and short irons are about 1.5 degrees stronger, with a 30-degree 7-iron and a 43.5-degree pitching wedge, and offset grows slightly, tapering from 7.6 mm at the 4-iron to 2.0 mm at the sand wedge. An updated A.I.-designed H.O.T. Face variable-thickness pattern spreads ball speed across the face, and weight concentrated in the heel promotes a draw. The build stays ultra-light — a 97-gram FST Ultralite steel shaft or 55-gram Ultralite graphite — and Cobra publishes one length column for both shafts, so the lengths below apply to either build. Swing weights shown are the steel values (D0, D1 in the wedges; graphite swings at C6-C9). The stock steel set is seven clubs, 5-iron through gap wedge, right-hand only.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a 2024 Cobra Air-X 7-iron?

The 2024 Cobra Air-X 7-iron has a loft of 30 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches — 1.5 degrees stronger than the 2022 original.

Are the Cobra Air-X (2024) and the Air-X 2 the same irons?

Yes. Cobra launched the second-generation Air-X in autumn 2023 and markets it as the 2024 AIR-X; several retailers list the identical set under the name AIR-X 2.

How do the 2024 Air-X irons differ from the 2022 originals?

The mid and short irons are about 1.5 degrees stronger — a 30-degree 7-iron against 31.5 — with slightly more offset, and the face uses an updated A.I.-designed H.O.T. Face variable-thickness pattern. The ultra-lightweight build with 97-gram steel or 55-gram graphite shafts carries over.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Cobra Air-X (2024) is a current super game improvement set in the Cobra range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Cobra Air-X. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
