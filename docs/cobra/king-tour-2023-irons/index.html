About the Cobra King Tour

The 2023 King Tour is the technical cavity-back in Cobra's players range, launched at retail in February 2023 alongside the one-piece forged King CB and MB. It marks a construction change from the King MIM Tour it replaced: instead of metal injection moulding, each head goes through a five-step forging from 1025 carbon steel, the fifth step pressing the head at 2,000 tons and 700 degrees Celsius for a uniform grain structure. CNC undercuts in the back cavity run progressively deeper in the long irons, and a TPU insert and aluminium medallion damp vibration — technology the plainer CB and MB do without. The blade is slightly shorter than the King MIM Tour's and the build a quarter inch shorter through most of the set, with traditional lofts (32-degree 7-iron, 44-degree pitching wedge) and a stock KBS $-Taper 120 shaft. Heads run 3-iron through gap wedge, with 4-PW the standard set. A blacked-out King Tour Black Edition finish followed in 2024, and the model remained in Cobra's line into 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra King Tour (2023) 7-iron?

The 2023 Cobra King Tour 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 62.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

Is the 2023 Cobra King Tour a MIM iron?

No. Unlike the King MIM Tour it replaced, the 2023 King Tour is forged — a five-step process from 1025 carbon steel finishing with a 2,000-ton strike — with a TPU insert and aluminium medallion in the cavity.

How does the 2023 King Tour differ from the King MIM Tour?

Beyond the switch from metal injection moulding to forging, the lofts are one degree stronger through most of the set (32 versus 33 at the 7-iron), the build is a quarter inch shorter, the blade is slightly shorter, and the gap wedge is 48 rather than 50 degrees.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Cobra King Tour is a current players set in the Cobra range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Cobra King MIM Tour. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
