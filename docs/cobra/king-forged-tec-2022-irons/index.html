About the Cobra KING Forged Tec (2022)

The 2022 KING Forged Tec is a compact hollow-bodied players-distance iron: a forged 1025 carbon steel body with a forged PWRSHELL face cup, foam microspheres behind the face for sound, and a tungsten weight low in the head. The profile is deliberately small for the category, with a relatively thin top line. Lofts are strong — 29.5 degrees at the 7-iron and 44 at the pitching wedge — and the set is built long at 37.25 inches for the 7-iron.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING Forged Tec (2022) 7-iron?

The 2022 Cobra KING Forged Tec 7-iron has a loft of 29.5 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

Is the Cobra KING Forged Tec hollow?

Yes — a forged 1025 carbon steel body with a forged PWRSHELL face cup and foam microspheres inside to control the sound.

How strong are 2022 KING Forged Tec lofts?

Strong for a compact iron: 29.5 degrees at the 7-iron and 44 at the pitching wedge, about three and a half degrees stronger than a traditional players iron.

What is the stock shaft in the 2022 Cobra KING Forged Tec?

The KBS $-Taper Lite, at 115 grams in stiff and 110 grams in regular.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Cobra KING Forged Tec (2022) is a current players distance set in the Cobra range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Cobra KING Forged Tec. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
