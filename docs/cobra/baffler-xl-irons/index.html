About the Cobra Baffler XL

The Baffler XL brought Cobra's rail-soled Baffler utility concept to a full iron set aimed at maximum game improvement. An oversized face with large progressive offset produces a high, draw-biased flight, a deep undercut cavity pushes weight back for inertia, and the wide sole's oversized rails smooth turf interaction from any lie. Lofts are traditional for the class — 30.5 degrees at the 7-iron, 43.5 at the pitching wedge. Cobra's product page first appeared in November 2013 alongside the BiO Cell launch, selling as the 2014 season's entry line, so this record's 2013 is the announcement year. The stock set is eight clubs, 4-PW plus gap wedge, in steel; graphite was custom-order only. Cobra published a single length column covering both shaft materials and no offset figures. The Fly-Z XL replaced it in 2015.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra Baffler XL 7-iron?

The Cobra Baffler XL 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, a lie of 63 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What clubs come in a stock Cobra Baffler XL set?

The stock set is eight clubs, 4-iron through pitching wedge plus a 48.5-degree gap wedge, in steel; graphite was custom-order only. A 54-degree sand wedge was also catalogued.

What makes the Baffler XL different from other Cobra irons?

It carries the rail sole from Cobra's Baffler utility woods: oversized rails on a wide sole that improve turf interaction, combined with an oversized face and heavy offset for a high, draw-biased flight.

Finding a used set

The Cobra Baffler XL ran from 2013 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
