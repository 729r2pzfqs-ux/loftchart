About the Cobra KING Forged CB

Cobra's KING Forged CB is the cavity-back half of a pair sold with a matching muscleback, and Cobra let buyers order a full CB set, a full MB set or a split set — typically CB through the 6-iron and MB from the 7 down. Both are five-step forged from 1025 carbon steel, with a tungsten weight in the sole of the cavity-back heads. The set is built long by tour-iron standards, 37.5 inches at the 7-iron, and lofts are traditional at 34 degrees there.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING Forged CB 7-iron?

The Cobra KING Forged CB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.50 inches.

Can you mix Cobra KING Forged CB and MB irons?

Yes — Cobra sold the pair as a deliberate combo. A common build runs cavity backs through the 6-iron and musclebacks from the 7-iron down, and Cobra offered full CB, full MB or split sets.

When were Cobra KING Forged CB irons made?

Cobra and Rickie Fowler announced the KING Forged CB/MB pair on 30 October 2018, with retail availability from 2 November 2018; reviews commonly list them as 2019 irons.

Why are Cobra KING Forged CB irons so long?

Cobra builds them at 37.50 inches for the 7-iron, half an inch longer than most tour cavity backs, which is consistent across the KING forged line of that period.

Finding a used set

The Cobra KING Forged CB ran from 2018 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
