About the Cobra AMP Cell

The AMP Cell is Cobra's 2013 game-improvement iron, built with what Cobra called true metalwood face-weld construction: a thin, high-strength steel face welded to a softer 431 stainless steel body, used in the 4- through 7-irons, with a progressively more conventional cavity design through the short irons. E9 Face Technology gives the face a variable thickness pattern matched to typical impact dispersion, and AMP Cell weighting plus a V-Skid sole handle mass distribution and turf interaction. Lofts are strong for the period at 31 degrees in the 7-iron and 44 degrees in the pitching wedge, on a 37.25-inch 7-iron. Cobra listed heads from 3-iron through lob wedge; stock sets were sold as 4-PW or 5-SW. Swing weights below are the steel-shaft values — graphite builds run two points lighter.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra AMP Cell 7-iron?

The Cobra AMP Cell 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

What clubs come in a stock Cobra AMP Cell set?

Stock sets were sold as 4-iron through pitching wedge or 5-iron through sand wedge. Cobra also catalogued a 3-iron, gap wedge and lob wedge as separate heads.

How is the Cobra AMP Cell iron built?

The 4- through 7-irons use a metalwood-style face weld: a thin, high-strength steel face welded to a softer 431 stainless steel body, with E9 variable-thickness face geometry.

Finding a used set

The Cobra AMP Cell ran from 2012 to 2013 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
