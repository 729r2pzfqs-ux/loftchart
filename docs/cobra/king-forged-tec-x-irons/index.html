About the Cobra KING Forged Tec X

The KING Forged Tec X is the larger, more forgiving sibling of Cobra's 2022 KING Forged Tec: the same hollow-body, foam-filled construction with a thin forged PWRSHELL face, but with a 65-gram internal tungsten weight — 45 grams more than the standard Forged Tec — a slightly thicker top line, a wider sole and more offset. Cobra pitched it at players who want game-improvement distance and stability in a forged-looking head. Lofts are very strong, with a 27-degree 7-iron and a 42-degree pitching wedge, and the stock set is 4-PW or 5-GW. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING Forged Tec X 7-iron?

The Cobra KING Forged Tec X 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 63.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches — one of the strongest-lofted 7-irons of its era.

What is the difference between the KING Forged Tec and Forged Tec X?

Both are hollow, foam-filled irons with a forged PWRSHELL face. The Tec X carries a 65-gram tungsten weight (45 grams more than the standard Forged Tec), a thicker top line, a wider sole, more offset and stronger lofts — 27 degrees at the 7-iron against the standard model's 29.5.

Who is the Cobra KING Forged Tec X for?

Cobra aimed it at mid-to-high handicappers who want game-improvement forgiveness and distance in a forged-looking head; reviewers labelled it a 'players game-improvement' iron.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2022, the Cobra KING Forged Tec X is a current players distance set in the Cobra range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
