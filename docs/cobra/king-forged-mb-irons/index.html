About the Cobra KING Forged MB

The KING Forged MB is the muscle-back half of the KING Forged CB/MB pair Cobra announced with Rickie Fowler on 30 October 2018 (at retail from 2 November 2018, and often catalogued as a 2019 or 2020 iron). The design mimics Fowler's custom gamers: a compact players' shape, five-step forged from a single billet of 1025 carbon steel, with a CNC-milled face, tighter wedge-style groove spacing in the scoring clubs and a black DBM finish. The standard set ran cavity backs 4-6 and these musclebacks 7-PW, with a full MB set available in right hand and optional 2-iron, 3-iron and gap wedge heads. Lofts are traditional at 34 degrees in the 7-iron, but the build is long for a tour iron — 37.5 inches at the 7-iron with the stock Cobra Connect grip (0.25 inch shorter with a standard grip). Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING Forged MB 7-iron?

The Cobra KING Forged MB 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches with the stock Cobra Connect grip.

Can you buy a full set of Cobra KING Forged MB irons?

The standard retail set mixed cavity backs (4-6) with musclebacks (7-PW), but Cobra offered a full MB configuration in right hand, plus optional 2-iron, 3-iron and gap wedge heads.

When did the Cobra KING Forged MB come out?

Cobra and Rickie Fowler announced the KING Forged CB/MB pair on 30 October 2018 and it reached retail on 2 November 2018, so it is usually listed as a 2019 model.

Finding a used set

The Cobra KING Forged MB ran from 2018 to 2021 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
