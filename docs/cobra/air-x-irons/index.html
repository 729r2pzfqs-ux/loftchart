About the Cobra Air-X

The Air-X was Cobra's budget lightweight iron for golfers with moderate swing speeds, announced on 25 January 2022 and at retail from 28 January 2022 as the replacement for the F-Max Airspeed. The cavity-back heads carry weak lofts — a 31.5-degree 7-iron and a 45-degree pitching wedge — generous offset tapering from 6.74 mm at the 4-iron to 0.97 mm at the sand wedge, and heel-biased weighting for a draw. The lightweight brief runs through the whole build: a 97-gram FST Ultralite steel shaft, a 55-gram Ultralite graphite option and a 41-gram Lamkin REL grip, with the graphite set swinging at just C7.5 against D0 in steel. Cobra published one length column for both shafts, so the lengths below apply to either build; the swing weights shown are the steel values. The stock set was seven clubs, 5-iron through gap wedge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra Air-X 7-iron?

The 2022 Cobra Air-X 7-iron has a loft of 31.5 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

When were the original Cobra Air-X irons released?

Cobra announced them on 25 January 2022 and they reached retail on 28 January 2022. A second-generation Air-X, marketed as the 2024 model, replaced them in autumn 2023.

Who were the Cobra Air-X irons designed for?

Golfers with moderate swing speeds — Cobra targeted players with driver speeds under about 90 mph. The build is ultra-light, with a 97-gram steel or 55-gram graphite shaft and a 41-gram grip, plus weak lofts, generous offset and heel-biased draw weighting for easy launch.

Finding a used set

The Cobra Air-X ran from 2022 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
