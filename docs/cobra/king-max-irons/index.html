About the Cobra KING MAX

The KING MAX is the maximum-forgiveness half of the two-model KING iron family Cobra announced on 19 January 2026, at retail from 6 February 2026 at $999 in steel. Against the standard KING it has an oversized, confidence-inspiring shape, lofts about 2 degrees weaker — a 29.5-degree 7-iron and a 44.5-degree pitching wedge — and added offset with draw-biased weighting, aimed at players with average-to-moderate swing speeds and shallow attack angles. A 3D-printed internal medallion with intricate latticing frees mass to lower the centre of gravity, and the 360 SPEEDSHELL face with H.O.T. Face technology wraps the sole for more face flex. The stock steel set is 5-iron through gap wedge; graphite is sold as 5-PW plus gap wedge. Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING MAX 7-iron?

The Cobra KING MAX 7-iron has a loft of 29.5 degrees, a lie of 64 degrees and a length of 37.25 inches.

When were the Cobra KING MAX irons released?

Cobra announced the KING and KING MAX irons on 19 January 2026, and they reached retail on 6 February 2026 at $999 for a seven-piece steel set.

How does the KING MAX differ from the standard Cobra KING iron?

The KING MAX uses a larger head with a thicker topline and wider sole, lofts about 2 degrees weaker than the KING, and more offset with draw-biased weighting. It is aimed at golfers with average-to-moderate swing speeds who want maximum launch and forgiveness.

What clubs come in a stock Cobra KING MAX set?

The stock steel set is 5-iron through gap wedge; graphite sets are sold as 5-iron through pitching wedge plus gap wedge. A 4-iron and 56-degree sand wedge are catalogued as singles.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Cobra KING MAX is a current super game improvement set in the Cobra range, with no replacement announced. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
