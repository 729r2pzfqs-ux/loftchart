About the Cobra S9

The King Cobra S9 was Cobra's flagship game-improvement iron of the late Acushnet era, a multi-material design combining a 431 stainless steel body with a polymer topline and a urethane insert to raise inertia and push the centre of gravity low and back. Cobra's US site was already selling it in August 2006, and the line was refreshed for 2008 as the "2008 S9" with identical published specs — retailers therefore date it anywhere from 2006 to 2008. Lofts are moderately strong, with a 32.5-degree 7-iron and 44-degree pitching wedge, on generous offset. Sets were sold in men's, women's and seniors' versions; the men's specs are below, with the same lengths in steel and graphite. It was replaced by the Cobra S2 in 2009.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra S9 7-iron?

The King Cobra S9 7-iron has a loft of 32.5 degrees, a lie of 63 degrees and a length of 37 inches.

What year are the Cobra S9 irons?

Cobra was selling the S9 by August 2006 and refreshed the line as the '2008 S9' with identical specs, so listings date it anywhere from 2006 to 2008. The Cobra S2 replaced it in 2009.

What clubs were available in the Cobra S9 line?

Heads ran from the 3-iron through a 59-degree lob wedge, with a 49-degree gap and 54-degree sand wedge. Sets came in men's, women's and seniors' versions in steel or graphite.

Finding a used set

The Cobra S9 ran from 2006 to 2009 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
