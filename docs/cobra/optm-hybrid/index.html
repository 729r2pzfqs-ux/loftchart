About the Cobra OPTM

The OPTM hybrid launched in January 2026 with the OPTM drivers and fairways, replacing the DS-ADAPT hybrid. It is a single head design in four lofts from an 18° 3-hybrid to a 28° 6-hybrid, each carrying the FutureFit33 hosel with ±2° of loft and lie adjustment. Cobra stretched the footprint slightly longer and shallower than the DS-ADAPT to push MOI up, with a fixed back weight keeping the centre of gravity low — MyGolfSpy's 2026 test rated it the most forgiving hybrid of the year.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Cobra OPTM hybrid come in?

Four lofts: 18, 21, 24 and 28 degrees — the 3-hybrid through 6-hybrid.

Is the Cobra OPTM hybrid adjustable?

Yes — every loft carries the FutureFit33 hosel with 33 settings covering plus or minus 2 degrees of loft and lie, and the SmartPad sole keeps the face square in every setting.

What replaced the Cobra DS-ADAPT hybrid?

This one — the OPTM hybrid replaced the DS-ADAPT hybrid in January 2026, with a slightly longer and shallower head for more forgiveness.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Cobra OPTM is the current Cobra hybrid line as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Cobra DS-ADAPT. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above; the hosel settings extend each head beyond its stamped loft, so a loft between two stock options is usually reachable from either. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
