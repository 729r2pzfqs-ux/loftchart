About the Cobra KING TEC X (2024)

The KING TEC X replaced the KING Forged Tec X in October 2024 as the larger and stronger-lofted of Cobra's two TEC irons, a hollow body with a 70-gram tungsten weight inside the head. The loft, lie and length chart is unchanged from the model it replaced: 27 degrees at the 7-iron and 42 at the pitching wedge, with lies one degree more upright than the KING TEC. The stock set is 5-iron to gap wedge in steel. A ONE Length version was sold alongside it; figures here are the variable-length men's right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Cobra KING TEC X (2024) 7-iron?

The 2024 Cobra KING TEC X 7-iron has a loft of 27 degrees, a lie of 63.5 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.25 inches.

Did the lofts change from the KING Forged Tec X?

No. The 4-iron through gap wedge run 19, 21, 24, 27, 31.5, 36.5, 42 and 48 degrees on both.

Is the Cobra KING TEC X hollow?

Yes. Cobra describes a hollow body construction with an internal 70-gram tungsten weight.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the KING TEC X?

42 degrees, with a 48-degree gap wedge below it.

Production status

Introduced in 2024, the Cobra KING TEC X (2024) has no confirmed end date on file. It replaced the Cobra KING Forged Tec X. LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the Cobra catalogue — check with Cobra or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
