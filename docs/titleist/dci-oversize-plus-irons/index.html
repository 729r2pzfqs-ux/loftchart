About the Titleist DCI Oversize +

The DCI Oversize + is the standard, black-badged version of Titleist's 1996 second-generation oversize DCI, sold alongside the lightweight DCI Oversize Gold. The head is a large investment cast cavity back with a heavy offset ladder - .240 inches at the 1-iron and still .180 at the 7-iron - and lofts about a degree and a half stronger than the standard DCI of the era, with a 34.5 degree 7-iron and 47 degree pitching wedge. The scoring clubs are built progressively heavier, up to D8 at the sand wedge. Titleist catalogued it from a 1-iron through lob wedge; the oversize slot eventually passed to the 2001 DCI 822 OS.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist DCI Oversize + 7-iron?

The Titleist DCI Oversize + 7-iron is 34.5 degrees, with a 63 degree lie and a 36.75 inch standard steel length.

What is the difference between the DCI Oversize + and the DCI Oversize Gold?

The lofts and lies are identical. The black-badged Oversize + is the standard-weight version with Dynamic Gold steel and D2 swing weights, while the Gold is the lightweight build aimed at slower swing speeds, and the Gold carries a heavier offset ladder - .386 inches at the 1-iron against .240.

How many clubs were in the Titleist DCI Oversize + set?

Titleist catalogued it from a 1-iron through a 60 degree lob wedge, thirteen clubs, though typical retail sets were 3-PW.

Finding a used set

The Titleist DCI Oversize + ran from 1996 to 2001 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
