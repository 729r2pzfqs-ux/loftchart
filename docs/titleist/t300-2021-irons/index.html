About the Titleist T300 (2021)

The second-generation T300 is the game-improvement iron of Titleist's 2021 T-Series. It keeps the first generation's loft ladder unchanged — a 29-degree 7-iron and 43-degree pitching wedge — while adding roughly 40 percent more tungsten weighting and an updated Max Impact face support for higher launch and more forgiveness. The set runs 4-iron through pitching wedge plus two matching wedges at 48 and 53 degrees. It was replaced by the T350 in 2023.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a 2021 Titleist T300 7-iron?

The 2021 Titleist T300 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37-inch steel-shaft length.

What changed between the 2019 and 2021 Titleist T300?

Lofts are identical between the generations. The 2021 model adds about 40 percent more tungsten weighting and an updated Max Impact core for higher launch and more forgiveness, and its published lie angles run half a degree more upright in the 4 and 5 irons.

What wedges came with the 2021 Titleist T300 set?

Beyond the 43-degree pitching wedge, Titleist offered two matching wedges: W1 at 48 degrees and W2 at 53 degrees, both at a 64-degree lie.

Finding a used set

The Titleist T300 (2021) ran from 2021 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
