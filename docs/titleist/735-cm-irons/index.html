About the Titleist Forged 735.CM

The Forged 735.CM was Titleist's first off-the-shelf combo set, shipping to shops on 15 August 2005. The set blends seamlessly from cavity back long irons through shallow-cavity mid irons to pure muscleback short irons, with progressive blade heights, blade lengths, offset and center of gravity through the set. It was sold in two forgings: 1025 carbon steel with chrome plating, and unplated 410 stainless steel. Lofts are traditional - a 35 degree 7-iron and 47 degree pitching wedge - and Titleist offered the set as a 19 degree 2-iron plus 3-iron through pitching wedge, in right and left hand. The ZB of 2008 took over the blended-set slot when the AP1/AP2/ZB/ZM launch replaced Titleist's forged line.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist 735.CM 7-iron?

The Titleist Forged 735.CM 7-iron is 35 degrees, with a 63 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

Which clubs in the Titleist 735.CM set are cavity backs and which are blades?

The long irons are full cavity backs, the mid irons carry a shallow cavity and the short irons from the 8-iron down are pure musclebacks, blended progressively so the transition is seamless.

What is the difference between the chrome and stainless Titleist 735.CM versions?

The chrome version is forged from 1025 carbon steel and chrome plated for a softer feel; the stainless version is forged from unplated 410 stainless steel, which is more durable and slightly cheaper. Specs are the same.

Finding a used set

The Titleist Forged 735.CM ran from 2005 to 2008 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
