About the Titleist Vokey SM11

The SM11 is the 2026 generation of the Vokey Design wedge line, announced on 19 January 2026 and at retail worldwide from 20 February 2026. The matrix runs 27 loft/bounce/grind combinations across 44 to 60 degrees and six grinds (F, S, M, D, K, T), with the low-bounce 06K lob option in the line from launch and a new 44.10F pitching-wedge loft. Against the SM10 the high-bounce K lob models drop from 14 to 12 degrees of bounce, centre of gravity is matched across grinds within each loft, and the faces get three shot-specific groove shapes with around five percent more groove volume and a new directional face texture. Finishes are Tour Chrome, Nickel and a new Jet Black, with Raw available through custom order.

Grind options

The sole grinds this model is sold in, with the lofts each one is available at.

F Grind44°, 46°, 48°, 50°, 52°, 54°, 56°
Traditional full sole for full swings and square-face shots — the forgiving, confidence-first grind, and the only option at the strong lofts from 44 to 52 degrees.
S Grind54°, 56°, 58°, 60°
Full sole narrowed by a trailing-edge grind so it moves faster through the turf. For players who control loft with their hands ahead of or behind the ball, in neutral to firm conditions.
M Grind54°, 56°, 58°, 60°
Bob Vokey's favourite — heel, toe and trailing-edge relief for players who rotate the face open and closed to manufacture greenside shots.
D Grind54°, 56°, 58°, 60°
The player's high-bounce wedge. A forward bounce angle protects steeper swings in softer and grainier conditions, and opening the face lowers the leading edge.
K Grind58°, 60°
Wide full sole with enhanced camber, shaped for forgiveness. The 12K is the bunker club that resists digging in soft sand and turf; the low-bounce 06K brings the same shape to tighter, firmer lies.
T Grind58°, 60°
Low bounce with maximum heel, toe and trailing-edge relief so the club hugs the ground. For sharper shotmaking in firm conditions.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Titleist Vokey SM11 come in?

The Vokey SM11 is sold from 44 to 60 degrees in 2-degree steps — 27 loft, bounce and grind combinations in all. The 44-degree pitching wedge loft is new for SM11.

What grinds does the Vokey SM11 offer?

Six grinds: F (full sole, 44-56 degrees), S (narrowed full sole, 54-60), M (heel and toe relief, 54-60), D (player's high bounce, 54-60), K (wide high-bounce sole at 12 degrees plus the low-bounce 06K, 58-60) and T (low bounce, 58-60).

What bounce options does the SM11 have at 58 and 60 degrees?

Six at each loft: 4 degrees (T grind), 6 degrees (K grind), 8 degrees (M), 10 degrees (S) and 12 degrees in both the D and K grinds.

What changed from the Vokey SM10 to the SM11?

The low-bounce 06K lob grind joined the standard line, a 44-degree F grind was added, the high-bounce K models moved from 14 to 12 degrees of bounce, centre of gravity was matched across grinds at each loft, and the grooves were recut in three shot-specific shapes with more volume.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Titleist Vokey SM11 is a current tour wedge in the Titleist range as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Titleist Vokey SM10. New wedges are built to the loft, bounce and grind options in the chart above; a fitting can bend loft and lie from standard, so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Check the loft and bounce against the chart above before buying used — wedges are routinely bent from standard, and worn grooves cost spin long before a wedge looks worn.
