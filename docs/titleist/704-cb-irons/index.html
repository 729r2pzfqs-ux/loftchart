About the Titleist Forged 704.CB

The Forged 704.CB is the mid-handicap forged cavity back Titleist launched in 2004 and catalogued as its 2005 model, positioned to give serious and aspiring golfers a more forgiving route into forged irons than the compact 600-series blades while drawing on the playability of the earlier DCI models. Unusually for the era it is forged from stainless steel rather than carbon steel, so the heads are unplated and can be bent freely for custom fitting. Lofts are traditional with a 35 degree 7-iron and a 47 degree pitching wedge, and the manufacturer table ran from a 19.5 degree 2-iron through a 51 degree gap wedge. The Forged 755 replaced it in 2006.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist 704.CB 7-iron?

The Titleist Forged 704.CB 7-iron is 35 degrees, with a 63 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

Is the Titleist 704.CB forged from carbon steel?

No. It is forged from stainless steel, so the heads are unplated, more durable than chromed carbon steel and freely bendable for custom fitting.

What year is the Titleist 704.CB from?

It launched in 2004 and was sold as Titleist's 2005 model, remaining in the line until the Forged 755 replaced it in 2006.

Finding a used set

The Titleist Forged 704.CB ran from 2004 to 2006 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
