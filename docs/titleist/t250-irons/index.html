About the Titleist T250

The T250 is the new name for the players-distance slot in Titleist's 2025 T-Series, replacing the T200 when the line was announced in summer 2025 with pre-orders from 10 July and retail availability from 21 August 2025. The loft ladder is effectively the 2023 T200's — a 30.5-degree 7-iron, 43-degree pitching wedge and 48-degree W — with the 4-iron listed a degree stronger at 22 and a new 18-degree 2-iron added at the top of the range. The head moves to a face and body made entirely of high-strength steel, with a forged L-Face insert using a V-Taper design, improved Max Impact technology behind the face and split high-density tungsten weighting. Lie and length are shared per club number across the whole 2025 T-Series. The stock set is 4-PW with heads offered 2-iron through the 48-degree W; a separate T250 Launch Spec version with roughly four-degree-weaker lofts (35-degree 7-iron) and a T250-U utility iron sit alongside it and are not recorded in this table. Titleist does not publish offset, bounce or swing weight for the 2025 T-Series, so those keys are omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist T250 7-iron?

The Titleist T250 7-iron has a loft of 30.5 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37-inch standard steel length.

Did the T250 replace the Titleist T200?

Yes. When Titleist announced the 2025 T-Series it renamed the players-distance model T250, replacing the 2023 T200. The mid and short-iron lofts carry over — 30.5 degrees at the 7-iron and 43 at the pitching wedge — while the 4-iron is listed one degree stronger at 22 and an 18-degree 2-iron joins the range.

What is the Titleist T250 Launch Spec?

A higher-launching variant of the T250 with lighter heads and lofts roughly four degrees weaker — 35 degrees at the 7-iron and 47 at the pitching wedge — offered 5-iron through a 52-degree W for players who need more height and stopping power.

What clubs come in a stock Titleist T250 set?

The stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge, with a 2-iron, 3-iron and 48-degree W wedge available to extend it.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Titleist T250 is a current players distance set in the Titleist range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Titleist T200 (2023). New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
