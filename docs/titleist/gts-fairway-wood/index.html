Hosel adjustment chart

SureFit hosel · loft −0.75° to +1.5° · 16 settings.

Lie adjusts independently of loft, from 0.75° flat to 1.5° upright. Loft changes open or close the face slightly at address; SureFit's ring and cog adjust loft and lie independently, unlike single-axis sleeves.

About the Titleist GTS

The GTS fairway metals reached retail on 11 June 2026, replacing the GT fairways about ten months after the GTS drivers' debut cycle began. The crown composite now wraps around the body, and for the first time on a core Titleist fairway both heads carry a dual heel-toe sole weighting system of swappable flat weights for strike and shape bias. The GTS2 is the larger, shallower-faced head for sweepers, in five lofts from a 13.5° strong 3-wood (the one taller-faced head in the line); the GTS3 is the compact, deeper-faced player's head for steeper deliveries, and gains a 21° 7-wood the GT3 never had. Every head keeps the 16-setting SureFit hosel. No GTS hybrid exists — the GT hybrids carry on as Titleist's current hybrid line.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Titleist GTS fairway woods come in?

The GTS2 comes in 13.5, 15, 16.5, 18 and 21 degrees; the GTS3 in 15, 16.5, 18 and 21 — the 21 degree GTS3 7-wood is new, a loft the GT3 never offered.

What is the Titleist GTS fairway adjustment chart?

Every head uses the 16-setting SureFit hosel: loft from minus 0.75 to plus 1.5 degrees and lie from 0.75 degrees flat to 1.5 degrees upright, adjusted independently. A1 is standard for right-handers. Both heads add a dual heel-toe sole weighting system for strike and shape bias.

What is the difference between the GTS2 and GTS3 fairway woods?

The GTS2 is the larger head with a shallower face for players who sweep the ball; its 13.5 degree head keeps a taller face for spin control. The GTS3 is the compact, deeper-faced player's head for steeper angles of attack.

Is there a Titleist GTS hybrid?

No — as of October 2026 the GTS line covers drivers and fairways only, and the GT hybrids remain Titleist's current hybrid family.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Titleist GTS is the current Titleist fairway wood line as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Titleist GT. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above; the hosel settings extend each head beyond its stamped loft, so a loft between two stock options is usually reachable from either. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
