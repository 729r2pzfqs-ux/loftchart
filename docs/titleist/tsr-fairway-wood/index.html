Hosel adjustment chart

SureFit hosel · loft −0.75° to +1.5° · 16 settings.

Lie adjusts independently of loft, from 0.75° flat to 1.5° upright. Loft changes open or close the face slightly at address; SureFit's ring and cog adjust loft and lie independently, unlike single-axis sleeves.

About the Titleist TSR

The TSR fairway metals reached retail in late 2022 alongside the TSR drivers. The TSR2 is the core four-loft head; the TSR2+ is a single 13° tee-biased strong 3-wood with a taller face; the TSR3 is the compact player's head with a five-position SureFit adjustable CG track, sold down to 13.5°; and the lightweight TSR1 — added with the TSR1 driver in February 2023 — runs weaker lofts for moderate swing speeds. Every head carries the 16-setting SureFit hosel. The GT fairways replaced the line in August 2024.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Titleist TSR fairway woods come in?

The TSR2 comes in 15, 16.5, 18 and 21 degrees; the TSR2+ in a single 13; the TSR3 in 13.5, 15, 16.5 and 18; and the lightweight TSR1 in 15, 18, 20 and 23.

What is the difference between the TSR2 and TSR2+ fairway woods?

The TSR2+ is a single 13 degree head with a larger profile and taller face, built as a tee-biased strong 3-wood — closer to a mini driver in intent. The TSR2 is the conventional fairway line from 15 degrees up.

Is the Titleist TSR fairway wood adjustable?

Yes — every head uses the 16-setting SureFit hosel (loft minus 0.75 to plus 1.5 degrees, lie 0.75 flat to 1.5 upright, independently). The TSR3 adds a five-position sliding CG track for shape bias.

Finding one used

The Titleist TSR ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search the used listings on eBay. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable fairway wood may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
