Hosel adjustment chart

SureFit hosel · loft −0.75° to +1.5° · 16 settings.

Lie adjusts independently of loft, from 0.75° flat to 1.5° upright. Loft changes open or close the face slightly at address; SureFit's ring and cog adjust loft and lie independently, unlike single-axis sleeves.

About the Titleist TSR

The TSR is Titleist's 2022-2024 driver generation — the last all-titanium one before the thermoform-crowned GT. The TSR2 is the straight-distance head with a Multi-Plateau variable-thickness face that keeps speed on off-centre strikes; the TSR3 is the precision head with the five-position SureFit CG track; and the 430 cc TSR4 is the ultra-low-spin head whose front weight can be flipped rearward for a more forgiving profile. The ultralight TSR1 joined in February 2023 for swing speeds under about 90 mph. The TSR2, TSR3 and TSR4 reached shops in late September 2022 and the family was replaced by the GT in August 2024, making it a staple of the current used market.

Head variants

The heads this family is sold in, with each one’s stock loft options.

TSR19°, 10°, 12°
The ultra-lightweight aero head, about 40 g lighter than a standard build, for swing speeds under roughly 90 mph; high launch at a longer 45.75 inch build. Left-handed stock is 10° only.
TSR28°, 9°, 10°, 11°
The straight-distance head: high launch, low spin and maximum forgiveness from the Multi-Plateau VFT face.
TSR38°, 9°, 10°
The precision head for consistent strikers — mid launch, low spin, with the five-position SureFit CG track for bias tuning.
TSR48°, 9°, 10°
The compact spin-killer. Weight forward is maximum spin reduction; flipped rearward it plays closer to a "TSR3.5". The left-hand model arrived in spring 2023.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Titleist TSR come in?

The TSR2 comes in 8, 9, 10 and 11 degrees; the TSR3 and TSR4 in 8, 9 and 10; and the lightweight TSR1 in 9, 10 and 12.

What is the Titleist TSR SureFit chart?

The 16-setting SureFit hosel combines four loft positions (−0.75, standard, +0.75 and +1.5 degrees) with four lie positions (0.75 flat, standard, 0.75 upright and 1.5 upright). A1 is standard for right-handers; the left-hand chart mirrors with D4 as standard.

What is the difference between the TSR2 and TSR3?

The TSR2 is the forgiveness-first distance head with a fixed weight and a Multi-Plateau variable-thickness face; the TSR3 is the smaller precision head with the five-position SureFit CG track for heel-to-toe bias fitting.

Is the Titleist TSR still in production?

No — it ran from 2022 to 2024, when the GT replaced it, so the used market is the main source. Its SureFit sleeve interchanges with both older TSi/TS and newer GT/GTS shafts.

Finding one used

The Titleist TSR ran from 2022 to 2024 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search the used listings on eBay. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable driver may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
