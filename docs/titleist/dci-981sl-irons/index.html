About the Titleist DCI 981SL

The DCI 981SL is the superlight variant of the 1998 DCI 981, built for golfers with slower swing speeds. The whole set is assembled to C9 swing weights with lightweight shafts, and the heads carry even more offset than the already generous 981 - .300 inches at the 2-iron against .250 - with a wider sole and lower centre of gravity. Lofts run two degrees stronger than the standard 981, with a 33 degree 7-iron and a 45 degree pitching wedge, to convert the lighter build into distance. Titleist catalogued it from a 2-iron through a 57 degree lob wedge; the wedges are built heavier at D1 and D3.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist DCI 981SL 7-iron?

The Titleist DCI 981SL 7-iron is 33 degrees, with a 63 degree lie and a 36.75 inch length - two degrees stronger than the standard DCI 981.

What does SL mean on the Titleist DCI 981SL?

It marks the superlight version of the DCI 981: the set is built to C9 swing weights with lightweight shafts, stronger lofts and extra offset so golfers with slower swing speeds can keep their distance.

How does the DCI 981SL differ from the standard DCI 981?

Three ways: it is much lighter (C9 swing weights against D1), its lofts are about two degrees stronger through the set, and it carries more offset - .300 inches at the 2-iron against .250 - with a wider sole and lower centre of gravity.

Finding a used set

The Titleist DCI 981SL ran from 1998 to 2001 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
