Hosel adjustment chart

SureFit hosel · loft −0.75° to +1.5° · 16 settings.

Lie adjusts independently of loft, from 0.75° flat to 1.5° upright. Loft changes open or close the face slightly at address; SureFit's ring and cog adjust loft and lie independently, unlike single-axis sleeves.

About the Titleist GT

The GT hybrids followed the GT woods to retail in late 2024, with the lightweight GT1 joining in February 2025. The GT2 is the larger, easier-launching head in 18°, 21° and 24°; the GT3 is the compact player's head in 19°, 21° and 24° with an 11 g aft / 3 g forward interchangeable weight pair for flight tuning; and the GT1 is the lightweight high-launch model running 20° to 29° for moderate swing speeds. Every head carries the 16-setting SureFit hosel. The GTS generation has so far covered drivers and fairways only, so the GT hybrids remain Titleist's current line.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Titleist GT hybrids come in?

The GT2 comes in 18, 21 and 24 degrees; the GT3 in 19, 21 and 24; and the lightweight GT1 in 20, 23, 26 and 29.

Are the Titleist GT hybrids adjustable?

Yes — every head uses the 16-setting SureFit hosel, with loft from minus 0.75 to plus 1.5 degrees and lie from 0.75 degrees flat to 1.5 degrees upright, adjusted independently. The GT3 also swaps an 11 gram aft weight and 3 gram forward weight to tune flight.

What is the difference between the GT2 and GT3 hybrids?

The GT2 is the larger, higher-launching head starting at 18 degrees. The GT3 is the compact player's shape starting at 19 degrees, flighting lower, with the interchangeable sole weights the GT2 does not have.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2024, the Titleist GT is the current Titleist hybrid line as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Titleist TSR. New heads are sold in the stock lofts charted above; the hosel settings extend each head beyond its stamped loft, so a loft between two stock options is usually reachable from either. Before buying used, check the stamped loft against the chart above and ask which hosel setting it is in — an adjustable hybrid may be set a degree or more away from the number on the sole, and sleeve adapters are brand- and generation-specific.
