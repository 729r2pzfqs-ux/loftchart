About the Titleist T200 (2021)

The 2021 T200 is the second generation of Titleist's players-distance iron, redesigned around a cleaner, more compact look with a shorter blade length and a squarer topline than the 2019 original. It keeps the hollow-body construction with a forged SUP-10 face, Max Impact support and high-density tungsten, but the lofts back off a full degree in the middle of the set — a 31-degree 7-iron against the 2019 model's 30 — while the pitching wedge stays at 43 degrees. The stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge, with 17 and 20 degree T200 utility long irons sold separately for the top of the bag. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a 2021 Titleist T200 7-iron?

The 2021 T200 7-iron is 31 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37-inch standard steel length.

How does the 2021 T200 differ from the 2019 T200?

The 2021 model is more compact and a degree weaker in the middle of the set — a 31-degree 7-iron against 30, and a 35-degree 8-iron against 34 — while the 43-degree pitching wedge carries over.

Did the 2021 T200 come in long-iron lofts?

The stock set runs 4-PW, but Titleist offered 17-degree 2-iron and 20-degree 3-iron T200 utility builds separately for the top of the bag.

What shafts came stock on the 2021 T200?

True Temper AMT Black in steel and the Mitsubishi Tensei AV Blue AM2 in graphite.

Finding a used set

The Titleist T200 (2021) ran from 2021 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
