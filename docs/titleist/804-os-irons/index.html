About the Titleist 804.OS

The 804.OS is the oversize game-improvement member of Titleist's mid-2000s forged range, built with the same heat-treated forged stainless steel as the 704.CB so a larger, easier iron could keep a forged feel. The head carries a deep cavity, a wide sole and the heaviest offset Titleist published in the era - .300 inches at the 2-iron tapering to .120 at the gap wedge - with lofts of 34 degrees at the 7-iron and 46 at the pitching wedge. Titleist marketed it as a 2005-model-year club announced in late 2004, and catalogued it from a 2-iron through a 50 degree gap wedge, every club at D2. The forgiving end of the forged line passed to the 775.CB in 2006.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist 804.OS 7-iron?

The Titleist 804.OS 7-iron is 34 degrees, with a 63 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

Are the Titleist 804.OS irons forged or cast?

Forged. Titleist used the same heat-treated forged stainless steel as the 704.CB, which is why the model was marketed as 'Forged 804.OS' - unusual for an oversize game-improvement iron of the era.

What year did the Titleist 804.OS come out?

It reached shops in late 2004 as a 2005-model-year club and stayed in the line until the 775.CB replaced it in 2006.

Finding a used set

The Titleist 804.OS ran from 2004 to 2006 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
