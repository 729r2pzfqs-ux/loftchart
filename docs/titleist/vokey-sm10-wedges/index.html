About the Titleist Vokey SM10

The SM10 is the 2024 generation of the Vokey Design line, announced on 22 January 2024 and at retail worldwide from 8 March 2024. It launched with 25 loft/bounce/grind combinations across 46 to 62 degrees in six grinds (F, S, M, D, K, T); the low-bounce 58.06K and 60.06K lob models were added in March 2025, bringing the matrix to the 27 combinations charted here. Faces carry TX9 grooves cut individually by loft and finish over a high-frequency heat-treated impact area. Finishes are Tour Chrome, Jet Black and a Nickel finish new for this generation, with Raw available through WedgeWorks custom order. The SM11 replaced it at the top of the Titleist wedge catalogue in early 2026, making the SM10 the value buy while remaining stock lasts.

Grind options

The sole grinds this model is sold in, with the lofts each one is available at.

F Grind46°, 48°, 50°, 52°, 54°, 56°
All-purpose traditional full sole for full swings and square-face shots — the only option at the strong lofts from 46 to 52 degrees.
S Grind54°, 56°, 58°, 60°
Full sole narrowed by a trailing-edge grind. For neutral to firm conditions and players who control loft with their hands ahead of or behind the ball.
M Grind54°, 56°, 58°, 60°, 62°
Bob Vokey's favourite — rotate the face open and closed to manufacture shots. Suits shallower, sweeping swings.
D Grind54°, 56°, 58°, 60°
Heel, toe and trailing-edge grind with high measured bounce, for steeper attack angles and varied face positions.
K Grind58°, 60°
The highest-bounce wedge in the lineup and the ultimate bunker club — a wide full sole with enhanced camber for softer sand and turf. The 06K variant added in 2025 brings a 6-degree effective bounce version for shallow swings and firm turf.
T Grind58°, 60°
Low bounce with a narrow sole that hugs the ground and maximum heel, toe and trailing-edge relief. The ultimate shotmaking grind.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts does the Titleist Vokey SM10 come in?

The Vokey SM10 is sold from 46 to 62 degrees — 27 loft, bounce and grind combinations in all, including the low-bounce 58.06K and 60.06K lob models added in March 2025.

What grinds does the Vokey SM10 offer?

Six grinds: F (full sole, 46-56 degrees), S (narrowed full sole, 54-60), M (heel and toe relief, 54-62), D (player's high bounce, 54-60), K (wide high-bounce sole at 14 degrees plus the low-bounce 06K, 58-60) and T (low bounce, 58-60).

Is the Vokey SM10 still in production?

No — the SM11 replaced it as Titleist's current wedge line in early 2026. The SM10 remains widely available as closeout and used stock, and through the Titleist Certified pre-owned programme.

What is the difference between the SM10 K grind and 06K?

Both use the wide, cambered K sole. The standard K carries 14 degrees of bounce for soft sand and turf; the 06K, added in 2025, carries 6 degrees for shallow swings and firmer conditions.

Buying one

Titleist has since introduced the Titleist Vokey SM11 above it in the range, so the Vokey SM10 is at the end of its retail life rather than fully retired — closeout stock still turns up at retailers, and it is well represented in the used listings on eBay. Check the loft and bounce against the chart above before buying used — wedges are routinely bent from standard, and worn grooves cost spin long before a wedge looks worn.
