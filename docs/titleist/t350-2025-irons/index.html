About the Titleist T350 (2025)

The second-generation T350 is the game-improvement model of the 2025 T-Series, announced in summer 2025 with pre-orders from 10 July and retail availability from 21 August 2025. The loft ladder carries over from the 2023 T350 unchanged — a 29-degree 7-iron, 43-degree pitching wedge and wedges at 48 and 53 degrees — while the head is re-engineered with a high-strength steel face and body around a hollow construction, a forged L-Face insert with a multi-zone taper, improved Max Impact technology and split high-density tungsten perimeter weighting in a sleeker shape than before. Lie and length are shared per club number across the whole 2025 T-Series. Titleist sells it 5-iron through pitching wedge as stock, with a 4-iron and the two wedges (badged W and W2, recorded here as W48 and W53 to match the earlier T350 record) available to extend the set. Titleist does not publish offset, bounce or swing weight for the 2025 T-Series, so those keys are omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist T350 (2025) 7-iron?

The 2025 T350 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37-inch standard steel length.

Did the T350 lofts change for 2025?

No. The 2025 T350 keeps the 2023 ladder exactly, from 20 degrees at the 4-iron through the 29-degree 7-iron to the 48 and 53-degree wedges.

What is the difference between the T350 and the T250?

Both use a hollow high-strength steel body with a forged L-Face insert, but the T350 is the larger, more forgiving head with weaker lofts (29 degrees at the 7-iron against 30.5), a lower CG and higher launch, aimed at mid-to-high handicaps.

What clubs come in a stock Titleist T350 (2025) set?

The stock set is 5-iron through pitching wedge, with a 4-iron and 48 and 53-degree wedges available to extend it.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Titleist T350 (2025) is a current game improvement set in the Titleist range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Titleist T350. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
