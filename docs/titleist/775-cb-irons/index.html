About the Titleist Forged 775.CB

The Forged 775.CB is the largest and most forgiving iron of Titleist's 2006 forged line, announced alongside the 695.CB and 695.MB and in shops from spring 2006. It extends the blended-set concept of the 735.CM to an oversize chassis: multi-material channel back long irons with pronounced offset transition seamlessly to mid-size, more traditional short irons. Forged from 410 stainless steel rather than carbon steel, it is more durable and can be bent for custom fitting. Lofts are the strongest of the era's Titleist irons, with a 33 degree 7-iron and a 45 degree pitching wedge, and the stock set ran 3-iron through a 50 degree gap wedge - a ladder the AP1 that replaced it in 2008 kept unchanged.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist 775.CB 7-iron?

The Titleist Forged 775.CB 7-iron is 33 degrees, with a 63 degree lie and a 37 inch standard steel length.

Is the Titleist 775.CB forged from carbon steel?

No. It is forged from 410 stainless steel, which is harder and more durable than the 1025 carbon steel Titleist used elsewhere, and needs no chrome plating.

How much offset does the Titleist 775.CB have?

A lot for a Titleist forging: 0.295 inches at the 3-iron tapering to 0.095 inches at the gap wedge, roughly double the 695.CB and the most of any Titleist iron of its generation apart from the AP1 that replaced it.

Finding a used set

The Titleist Forged 775.CB ran from 2006 to 2008 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
