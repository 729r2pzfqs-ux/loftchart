About the Titleist 714 MB

The 714 MB is the 2014-model-year muscleback in Titleist's numbered line, forged from a block of 1025 carbon steel with tour-preferred constant blade lengths and minimal progressive offset. Lofts, lies, lengths and offsets are unchanged from the 712 MB, and Titleist publishes a progressive bounce for this generation running 2 degrees at the 3-iron to 8 at the pitching wedge. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist 714 MB 7-iron?

The Titleist 714 MB 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, a lie of 63.0 degrees, a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches and 5 degrees of bounce.

When were Titleist 714 MB irons made?

The 714 line was announced in 2013 as the 2014 range and was replaced by the 716 line in 2015.

Are 712 MB and 714 MB specs the same?

Yes for loft, lie, length and offset — both run 21 to 47 degrees from 3-iron to pitching wedge at identical lengths. The 714 generation is where Titleist published the progressive bounce figures for the muscleback.

How much offset does a Titleist 714 MB have?

0.125 inches at the 3-iron tapering to 0.075 inches at the pitching wedge — minimal and progressive, as Titleist describes it.

Finding a used set

The Titleist 714 MB ran from 2013 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
