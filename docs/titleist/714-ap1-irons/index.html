About the Titleist 714 AP1

AP1 is Titleist's game-improvement iron, and the 714 is the 2014-model-year version — a hollow-soled cast head with a large undercut cavity and a high-density tungsten weight low in the sole. Lofts are three degrees stronger than the 714 MB through the set, 32 degrees at the 7-iron against 35, and offset is roughly twice as much. The stock set runs down to two wedges, listed by Titleist as W and W2 at 48 and 52 degrees. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist 714 AP1 7-iron?

The Titleist 714 AP1 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, a lie of 63.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

Is the 714 AP1 the 2014 model?

Yes. Titleist announced the 714 line in 2013 and sold it as the 2014 range, so the same clubs are commonly listed as either 714 AP1 or AP1 (2014).

What is the difference between the Titleist AP1 and AP2?

AP1 is the game-improvement model — larger head, more offset and stronger lofts. The 714 AP1 7-iron is 32 degrees against the 714 AP2's 34, and offset at the 7-iron is 0.145 inches against roughly half that.

What wedges came with the 714 AP1?

Titleist catalogued two, a 48-degree W and a 52-degree W2, below the 44-degree pitching wedge.

Finding a used set

The Titleist 714 AP1 ran from 2013 to 2015 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
