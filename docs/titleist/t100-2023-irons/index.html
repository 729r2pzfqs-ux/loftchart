About the Titleist T100 (2023)

The third T100 keeps the tour chassis — a forged 1025 carbon steel body with a variable-thickness face insert and dual-density tungsten in the toe and heel — and refines the sole with more heel and toe relief. Lofts are traditional: 34 degrees at the 7-iron, 46 at the pitching wedge, one degree weaker than the T150 that sits beside it in the range. Offset runs from 3.4mm at the 3-iron down to 1.3mm at the gap wedge. Figures are the men's standard right-handed steel build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist T100 (2023) 7-iron?

The 2023 T100 7-iron has a loft of 34 degrees, a lie of 63.0 degrees and a steel-shaft length of 37.00 inches.

What is the difference between the T100 and the T150?

The T150 is the stronger, slightly more forgiving version of the same shape — its lofts run about one degree stronger through the set than the T100's, which is 34 degrees at the 7-iron.

When did the 2023 T100 come out?

2023, replacing the 2021 T100 as the third generation of Titleist's tour players iron.

What is the pitching wedge loft on the 2023 T100?

46 degrees, with a 50-degree gap wedge available below it.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Titleist T100 (2023) is a current players set in the Titleist range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Titleist T100 (2021). New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
