About the Titleist 620 MB

The 620 MB is Titleist's muscle back, a single-piece 1025 carbon forging with the least offset in the range — 2.3 mm at the 3-iron falling to under a millimetre at the pitching wedge. Lofts are the weakest Titleist sells, and identical to the 620 CB sold alongside it: a 35-degree 7-iron and a 47-degree pitching wedge, six degrees weaker at the 7-iron than the T200 of the same period. It is sold 4-PW as standard, with a 3-iron available.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist 620 MB 7-iron?

The Titleist 620 MB 7-iron has a loft of 35 degrees, a lie of 63.0 degrees and a length of 37.0 inches.

How weak are Titleist 620 MB lofts?

As weak as anything on sale. The 35-degree 7-iron matches the 620 CB and is six degrees weaker than the T200 distance iron Titleist sold in the same period.

When were Titleist 620 MB irons released?

In 2019, alongside the 620 CB.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2019, the Titleist 620 MB is a current blade set in the Titleist range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Titleist 718 MB. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
