About the Titleist T150 (2025)

The second-generation T150 is the faster players model of the 2025 T-Series, announced in summer 2025 with pre-orders from 10 July and retail availability from 21 August 2025. Its loft ladder carries over unchanged from the 2023 T150 — a 32-degree 7-iron and a 44-degree pitching wedge — but because the 2025 T100 went one degree stronger, the two models now sit a single degree apart instead of two. The forged head is slightly larger than the T100 and uses the same progressive dual-cavity construction with split high-density tungsten, extending the muscle channel through the 7-iron rather than only the long irons, with a variable face thickness design and more aggressive mid and short-iron grooves. Lie and length are shared per club number across the whole 2025 T-Series. Heads run 3-iron through a 48-degree W wedge (recorded here as GW to match the earlier T150 record), with 4-PW the stock set. Titleist does not publish offset, bounce or swing weight for the 2025 T-Series, so those keys are omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Titleist T150 (2025) 7-iron?

The 2025 T150 7-iron has a loft of 32 degrees, with a 63-degree lie and a 37-inch standard steel length.

Did the T150 lofts change for 2025?

No. The 2025 T150 keeps the 2023 ladder exactly — 19 degrees at the 3-iron, 32 at the 7-iron, 44 at the pitching wedge — but the gap to the T100 shrank to one degree because the 2025 T100 went a degree stronger.

What is the difference between the T150 and the T250?

The T150 is a forged players iron with a dual-cavity back and moderate forgiveness; the T250 is the larger players-distance model with a hollow high-strength steel body, a forged L-Face insert and stronger lofts (30.5 degrees at the 7-iron against 32).

What clubs come in a stock Titleist T150 (2025) set?

The stock set is 4-iron through pitching wedge, with a 3-iron and a 48-degree W wedge available to extend it.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2025, the Titleist T150 (2025) is a current players set in the Titleist range as of September 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Titleist T150. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
