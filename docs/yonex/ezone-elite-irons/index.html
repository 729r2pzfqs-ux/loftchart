About the Yonex EZONE Elite 4.0

The EZONE Elite 4.0 is the beginner- and high-handicapper-focused iron at the top of Yonex's game-improvement range, part of a full Elite 4.0 family (driver, fairway, hybrid, irons, putter) that reached UK retail on 1 March 2023. The cast SUS431 stainless head uses Yonex's Octaforce NEO construction - weight moved from high in the blade to a low, deep peripheral position - for a 10% larger sweet spot, a lower and deeper centre of gravity, and increased face flex for a higher, more forgiving launch than the mid-market EZONE GS i-Tech, though the two share a nearly identical loft ladder through the mid-irons. Sold in several stock configurations from 5-iron or 6-iron through pitching wedge, with AW and SW options, and built with Yonex's own lightweight EX-E04 graphite shaft in Super-Lite, Regular or Stiff flex as the sole stock offering.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Yonex EZONE Elite 4.0 7-iron?

The Yonex EZONE Elite 4.0 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 63 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

Who is the Yonex EZONE Elite 4.0 designed for?

Beginners and higher-handicap players. Yonex built the cast SUS431 stainless head around Octaforce NEO peripheral weighting for a larger sweet spot and higher, more forgiving launch than its mid-market EZONE GS i-Tech.

What shaft comes with the Yonex EZONE Elite 4.0?

Yonex's own lightweight EX-E04 graphite shaft, offered in Super-Lite, Regular or Stiff flex, paired with an Original Elastomer grip - there is no steel stock option.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Yonex EZONE Elite 4.0 is a current super game improvement set in the Yonex range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Yonex EZONE Elite 3.0. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
