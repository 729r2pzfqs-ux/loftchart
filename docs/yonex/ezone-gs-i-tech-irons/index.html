About the Yonex EZONE GS i-Tech

The EZONE GS i-Tech succeeded the EZONE GS as Yonex's international-market game-improvement iron, reviewed by Golf Monthly in February 2023. It keeps the GS line's four-piece graphite-hybrid head - a cast mild-carbon-steel body, a maraging steel face, a tungsten plate low in the head and Yonex's signature carbon graphite insert damping vibration between the metals - and adds a "G-Brid" face design that flexes inward at impact, cutting head weight by roughly 20% to lower the centre of gravity for a higher, faster launch, plus a Groove Flow Concept that angles the grooves rather than cutting them straight across for straighter ball flights. Lofts are essentially unchanged from the EZONE GS, with a 29-degree 7-iron and a 43-degree pitching wedge across a 4-iron-to-sand-wedge factory range. A six-piece set retailed at £749 in the UK with either Yonex's own EX-GO2 graphite or Nippon N.S.Pro Modus3 Tour 120 steel in the fitting matrix.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Yonex EZONE GS i-Tech 7-iron?

The Yonex EZONE GS i-Tech 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, a lie of 62.5 degrees and a length of 37.5 inches.

What does the G-Brid face do on the EZONE GS i-Tech?

It lets the face flex inward at impact, which Yonex says cuts head weight by about 20% and lowers the centre of gravity for a higher launch and more ball speed.

How does the EZONE GS i-Tech differ from the original EZONE GS?

The head keeps the same four-piece graphite-hybrid construction and a nearly identical loft ladder, but adds the flexing G-Brid face and an angled Groove Flow Concept aimed at straighter ball flights.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2023, the Yonex EZONE GS i-Tech is a current game improvement set in the Yonex range, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Yonex EZONE GS. New sets are built to the standard lofts, lies and lengths in the chart above, though a fitting can move any of them — so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against this chart before buying.
