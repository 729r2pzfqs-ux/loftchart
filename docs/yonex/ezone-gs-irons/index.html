About the Yonex EZONE GS

The EZONE GS was Yonex's game-improvement iron for international markets, on sale from 2021 until the EZONE GS i-Tech replaced it in 2023. The four-piece "graphite-hybrid" head combines a cast 8620 mild carbon steel body, an AM355 maraging steel face, a tungsten plate low in the head and a carbon graphite insert that Yonex - the Niigata carbon specialist - uses as a damper between the metals. Lofts are moderately strong with a 29-degree 7-iron and 43-degree pitching wedge, and the set runs 5-iron to sand wedge. Stock builds used Yonex's EX-330 graphite shaft with Nippon N.S.Pro 950GH steel as the custom option; the specs below are the UK retail chart for the stock build, and Yonex published no offset figures, so that key is omitted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Yonex EZONE GS 7-iron?

The Yonex EZONE GS 7-iron has a loft of 29 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37.25-inch stock length.

What is the graphite insert in the EZONE GS for?

Yonex sandwiches a carbon graphite insert between the maraging face and the steel body to damp vibration - the company is first a carbon-fibre specialist, and uses the insert to soften feel in an otherwise fast, cast head.

Who is the Yonex EZONE GS for?

It is a game-improvement iron for mid-to-high handicaps, pairing a 29-degree 7-iron with a lightweight stock graphite shaft and high launch.

Finding a used set

The Yonex EZONE GS ran from 2021 to 2023 and is no longer in production, so the used market is the only source. Search eBay, 2nd Swing, Golf Avenue and the PGA Tour Superstore trade-in listings, and check the loft and lie against the chart above before buying — sets this age have often been bent from standard.
