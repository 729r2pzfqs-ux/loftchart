Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the 0311 P GEN6 is 30° and the 0317 CB is 33°, a 3° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the 0311 P GEN6 should carry roughly 9 to 12 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the 0317 CB. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the 0317 CB averages 2.9° weaker, with the largest gap at the 5-iron (23° vs 27°). The pitching wedges are 44° and 46°; the stronger one leaves a 6° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the 0311 P GEN6 runs 4-iron to GW (8 clubs), the 0317 CB 3-iron to GW (9 clubs).

The 0311 P GEN6 is a players distance iron and the 0317 CB a players iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The 0311 P GEN6 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. The 0317 CB trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

The 0311 P GEN6 is listed as forged hollow body with an XCOR2 core and the 0317 CB as forged solid-body cavity back with CNC-milled face and back. The 0311 P GEN6's hollow body lets the face flex more and moves mass low and outward, usually giving higher launch and more ball speed on off-centre strikes than the 0317 CB, at some cost in feel.

Both sets were introduced in 2023, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: PXG designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The 0317 CB is the weaker-lofted set — up to 4° weaker than the 0311 P GEN6. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PXG 0311 P GEN6 stronger lofted than the 0317 CB?

Yes. The PXG 0311 P GEN6 7-iron is 30° against 33° for the 0317 CB, 3° stronger, which is worth roughly 9 to 12 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the PXG 0311 P GEN6 and 0317 CB?

At the 7-iron the difference is 3° (30° vs 33°). The largest gap on a shared club is 4° at the 5-iron, and all 8 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the PXG 0311 P GEN6 or the 0317 CB?

The PXG 0311 P GEN6. As a players distance iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. The 0317 CB is a players iron, better suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

Do the PXG 0311 P GEN6 and 0317 CB have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.5° lie and 37 inches long.
