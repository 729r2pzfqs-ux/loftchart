Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Kalea is 33° and the Kalea Premier is 32°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Kalea Premier should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Kalea. Across the 6 clubs both sets share, the Kalea Premier averages 1° stronger, with the largest gap at the 9-iron (42° vs 40°). The pitching wedges are 47° and 45°; the stronger one leaves a 5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Kalea 7-iron is 0.5 inches longer (36.75" vs 36.25").

The sets are built differently: the Kalea runs 6-iron to SW (6 clubs), the Kalea Premier 6-iron to SW (7 clubs).

Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The most forgiving category — very wide soles, maximum perimeter weighting and the strongest lofts. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The Kalea is listed as investment cast with Speed Pocket sole slots and the Kalea Premier as investment cast Cap Back design with Speed Pocket. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

There are 6 years between these sets (2016 and 2022), more than one design generation. Over that time irons moved toward stronger lofts, thinner and faster faces, hollow and multi-material heads and wider use of tungsten, so the newer set is not simply a better version of the older one: it may launch and spin quite differently. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 33° to 32°.

Which should you choose?

The Kalea Premier is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the Kalea on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the TaylorMade Kalea stronger lofted than the Kalea Premier?

No. The Kalea Premier is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 32° against 33° for the TaylorMade Kalea, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the TaylorMade Kalea and Kalea Premier?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (33° vs 32°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 9-iron, and all 6 shared clubs differ.

Are the TaylorMade Kalea and Kalea Premier aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the TaylorMade Kalea and Kalea Premier have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the TaylorMade Kalea is 61.75° lie and 36.75 inches, the Kalea Premier 61.75° and 36.25 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
