Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the 716 AP1 is 31° and the 718 AP1 is 30°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the 718 AP1 should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the 716 AP1. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the 718 AP1 averages 0.4° stronger, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (22° vs 21°).

The sets are built differently: the 716 AP1 runs 3-iron to W2 (10 clubs), the 718 AP1 4-iron to W2 (9 clubs).

Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Perimeter-weighted cavity backs with wider soles, more offset and stronger lofts, aimed at mid handicaps. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The 716 AP1 is listed as cast with an extreme 360-degree undercut cavity and the 718 AP1 as cast, hollow-body long irons with undercut cavity mid and short irons. The 718 AP1's hollow body lets the face flex more and moves mass low and outward, usually giving higher launch and more ball speed on off-centre strikes than the 716 AP1, at some cost in feel. Both use tungsten weighting to lower the centre of gravity and steady the head on off-centre strikes.

The 718 AP1 arrived 2 years after the 716 AP1, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 31° to 30°.

Which should you choose?

The 718 AP1 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 1° stronger than the 716 AP1 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Titleist 716 AP1 stronger lofted than the 718 AP1?

No. The 718 AP1 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 30° against 31° for the Titleist 716 AP1, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Titleist 716 AP1 and 718 AP1?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (31° vs 30°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 4-iron, and 8 of the 9 shared clubs differ.

Are the Titleist 716 AP1 and 718 AP1 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Titleist 716 AP1 and 718 AP1 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 63° lie and 37 inches long.
