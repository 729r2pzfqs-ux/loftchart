Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Baffler XL is 30.5° and the T-Rail is 30°, a 0.5° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the T-Rail should carry about 2 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Baffler XL. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the T-Rail averages 0.3° weaker, with the largest gap at the SW (54° vs 56°). The pitching wedges are 43.5° and 44°; the stronger one leaves a 6.5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the T-Rail 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (63° vs 62.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The most forgiving category — very wide soles, maximum perimeter weighting and the strongest lofts. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The Baffler XL is listed as cast deep-undercut cavity with oversized face and wide rail sole and the T-Rail as hollow-body cast iron-hybrid with baffler rail sole. The T-Rail's hollow body lets the face flex more and moves mass low and outward, usually giving higher launch and more ball speed on off-centre strikes than the Baffler XL, at some cost in feel.

There are 6 years between these sets (2013 and 2019), more than one design generation. Over that time irons moved toward stronger lofts, thinner and faster faces, hollow and multi-material heads and wider use of tungsten, so the newer set is not simply a better version of the older one: it may launch and spin quite differently. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 30.5° to 30°.

Which should you choose?

The T-Rail is the weaker-lofted set — up to 2° weaker than the Baffler XL. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Cobra Baffler XL stronger lofted than the T-Rail?

No. The T-Rail is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 30° against 30.5° for the Cobra Baffler XL, 0.5° stronger and worth about 2 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Cobra Baffler XL and T-Rail?

At the 7-iron the difference is 0.5° (30.5° vs 30°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the SW, and 5 of the 9 shared clubs differ.

Are the Cobra Baffler XL and T-Rail aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Cobra Baffler XL and T-Rail have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Cobra Baffler XL is 63° lie and 37.25 inches, the T-Rail 62.5° and 37.25 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
