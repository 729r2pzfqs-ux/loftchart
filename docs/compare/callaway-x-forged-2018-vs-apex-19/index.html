Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the X Forged (2018) is 33° and the Apex 19 is 30.5°, a 2.5° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Apex 19 should carry roughly 8 to 10 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the X Forged (2018). Across the 7 clubs both sets share, the Apex 19 averages 2.5° stronger, with the largest gap at the PW (46° vs 43°). The pitching wedges are 46° and 43°; the stronger one leaves a 7° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the X Forged (2018) runs 4-iron to PW (7 clubs), the Apex 19 3-iron to SW (10 clubs).

The X Forged (2018) is a players iron and the Apex 19 a players distance iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Apex 19 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. The X Forged (2018) trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

The X Forged (2018) is listed as triple-net forged and the Apex 19 as forged, multi-material. The Apex 19 adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

The Apex 19 came out 1 year after the X Forged (2018), but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The Apex 19 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 3° stronger than the X Forged (2018) on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Callaway X Forged (2018) stronger lofted than the Apex 19?

No. The Apex 19 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 30.5° against 33° for the Callaway X Forged (2018), 2.5° stronger and worth roughly 8 to 10 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Callaway X Forged (2018) and Apex 19?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2.5° (33° vs 30.5°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3° at the PW, and all 7 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Callaway X Forged (2018) or the Apex 19?

The Apex 19. As a players distance iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. The Callaway X Forged (2018) is a players iron, better suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

Do the Callaway X Forged (2018) and Apex 19 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 37 inches long.
