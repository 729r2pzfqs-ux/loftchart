What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the King Cobra Oversize is 32° and the King Cobra II Oversize is 32°, identical at the club most golfers compare on, although other clubs in the sets differ. Across the 11 clubs both sets share, the King Cobra II Oversize averages 0.4° weaker, with the largest gap at the PW (43° vs 45.5°). The pitching wedges are 43° and 45.5°; the stronger one leaves a 7° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the King Cobra Oversize runs 2-iron to SW (11 clubs), the King Cobra II Oversize 1-iron to LW (13 clubs).

Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Perimeter-weighted cavity backs with wider soles, more offset and stronger lofts, aimed at mid handicaps. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The King Cobra Oversize is listed as investment cast oversize cavity back and the King Cobra II Oversize as investment cast oversize cavity back with the Integrated Quad System. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The King Cobra II Oversize arrived 2 years after the King Cobra Oversize, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept.

Which should you choose?

The King Cobra II Oversize is the weaker-lofted set — up to 2.5° weaker than the King Cobra Oversize. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Cobra King Cobra Oversize stronger lofted than the King Cobra II Oversize?

Not at the 7-iron, where both are 32°. Other clubs in the sets differ, so compare the full table above.

What is the loft difference between the Cobra King Cobra Oversize and King Cobra II Oversize?

At the 7-iron the difference is 0° (32° vs 32°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2.5° at the PW, and 2 of the 11 shared clubs differ.

Are the Cobra King Cobra Oversize and King Cobra II Oversize aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Cobra King Cobra Oversize and King Cobra II Oversize have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 37 inches long.
