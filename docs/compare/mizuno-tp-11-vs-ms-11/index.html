Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the TP-11 is 37° and the MS-11 is 35°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the MS-11 should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the TP-11. Across the 11 clubs both sets share, the MS-11 averages 1.5° stronger, with the largest gap at the 5-iron (29.5° vs 27°). The pitching wedges are 49° and 48°; the stronger one leaves a 2° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the TP-11 runs 1-iron to SW (11 clubs), the MS-11 1-iron to SW (12 clubs).

The TP-11 is a game improvement iron and the MS-11 a blade iron, so they sit 3 steps apart on the forgiveness scale. The TP-11 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The MS-11 trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

The TP-11 is listed as forged shallow cavity back and the MS-11 as forged muscle back. The MS-11's muscle-back shape keeps mass directly behind the strike for feedback and control, where a cavity spreads it to the perimeter for forgiveness.

The MS-11 came out 1 year after the TP-11, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The MS-11 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2.5° stronger than the TP-11 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Mizuno TP-11 stronger lofted than the MS-11?

No. The MS-11 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 35° against 37° for the Mizuno TP-11, 2° stronger and worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Mizuno TP-11 and MS-11?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (37° vs 35°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2.5° at the 5-iron, and all 11 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Mizuno TP-11 or the MS-11?

The Mizuno TP-11. As a game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The MS-11 is a blade iron, better suited to low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.
