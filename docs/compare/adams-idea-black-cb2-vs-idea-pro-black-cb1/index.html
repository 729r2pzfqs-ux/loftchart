Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Idea Black CB2 is 33° and the Idea Pro Black CB1 is 34°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Idea Black CB2 should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Idea Pro Black CB1. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the Idea Pro Black CB1 averages 1° weaker, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (23° vs 24°). The pitching wedges are 45° and 46°; the stronger one leaves a 5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the Idea Black CB2 runs 3-iron to GW (9 clubs), the Idea Pro Black CB1 4-iron to GW (8 clubs).

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as forged. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

Both sets were introduced in 2010, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Adams designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The Idea Pro Black CB1 is the weaker-lofted set — up to 1° weaker than the Idea Black CB2. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Adams Idea Black CB2 stronger lofted than the Idea Pro Black CB1?

Yes. The Adams Idea Black CB2 7-iron is 33° against 34° for the Idea Pro Black CB1, 1° stronger, which is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the Adams Idea Black CB2 and Idea Pro Black CB1?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (33° vs 34°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 4-iron, and all 8 shared clubs differ.

Are the Adams Idea Black CB2 and Idea Pro Black CB1 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Adams Idea Black CB2 and Idea Pro Black CB1 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.25° lie and 37 inches long.
