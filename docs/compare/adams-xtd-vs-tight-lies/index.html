Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the XTD is 31° and the Tight Lies is 33°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the XTD should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Tight Lies. Across the 6 clubs both sets share, the Tight Lies averages 1.7° weaker, with the largest gap at the 7-iron (31° vs 33°). The pitching wedges are 44° and 45°; the stronger one leaves a 6° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the XTD 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (37.25" vs 37").

The sets are built differently: the XTD runs 3-iron to SW (10 clubs), the Tight Lies 6-iron to SW (6 clubs).

Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Perimeter-weighted cavity backs with wider soles, more offset and stronger lofts, aimed at mid handicaps. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as investment cast. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The Tight Lies arrived 1 year after the XTD, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 31° to 33°.

Which should you choose?

The Tight Lies is the weaker-lofted set — up to 2° weaker than the XTD. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Adams XTD stronger lofted than the Tight Lies?

Yes. The Adams XTD 7-iron is 31° against 33° for the Tight Lies, 2° stronger, which is worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the Adams XTD and Tight Lies?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (31° vs 33°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 7-iron, and all 6 shared clubs differ.

Are the Adams XTD and Tight Lies aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Adams XTD and Tight Lies have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Adams XTD is 62° lie and 37.25 inches, the Tight Lies 62° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
