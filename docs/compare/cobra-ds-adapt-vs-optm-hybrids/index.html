What the differences mean

Both families are sold in 1 head — Std for the DS-ADAPT, Std for the OPTM.

The DS-ADAPT is sold from 17° to 28°, the OPTM from 18° to 28°. Only the DS-ADAPT has a stock 17° and 19° option. Only the OPTM has a stock 18° option.

Adjustability is a wash: both use the same hosel system (FutureFit33, ±2° of loft, 33 settings), so a given head reaches the same effective loft window in either generation.

The OPTM arrived 1 year after the DS-ADAPT as its replacement. Hybrid generations usually carry over the loft ladder and move the technology — face design, adjustability, head options — so the older family is the value buy on the used market while the newer one holds retail price.

Which should you choose?

The OPTM is the newer generation, so it carries the current face technology and full retail availability; the DS-ADAPT is the value buy used or on closeout. If the head and loft you want is only in one lineup, that decides it — otherwise the newer family is the default at equal prices, and the older one the smarter buy at a real discount.

Both families are charted in full on their own pages: Cobra DS-ADAPT hybrid specs and Cobra OPTM hybrid specs.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Cobra DS-ADAPT and OPTM hybrids come in?

The Cobra DS-ADAPT is sold in stock lofts from 17 to 28 degrees and the OPTM from 18 to 28 degrees. They share 3 stock lofts.

Which has more head options, the Cobra DS-ADAPT or the OPTM?

Both families are sold in 1 head.

Are the Cobra DS-ADAPT and OPTM hybrids adjustable?

Yes — the Cobra DS-ADAPT uses FutureFit33, ±2° of loft, 33 settings, and the OPTM uses FutureFit33, ±2° of loft, 33 settings. Each model page carries the full setting-by-setting adjustment chart.
