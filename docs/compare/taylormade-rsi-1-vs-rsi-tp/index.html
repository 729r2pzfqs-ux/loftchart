Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the RSi 1 is 30.5° and the RSi TP is 34°, a 3.5° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the RSi 1 should carry roughly 10 to 14 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the RSi TP. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the RSi TP averages 3.2° weaker, with the largest gap at the 6-iron (26.5° vs 30°). The pitching wedges are 44° and 47°; the stronger one leaves a 6° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the RSi TP 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (63° vs 62.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the RSi 1 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (37" vs 36.75").

The sets are built differently: the RSi 1 runs 3-iron to LW (11 clubs), the RSi TP 2-iron to PW (9 clubs).

The RSi 1 is a game improvement iron and the RSi TP a players iron, so they sit 2 steps apart on the forgiveness scale. The RSi 1 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The RSi TP trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

The RSi 1 is listed as investment cast and the RSi TP as two-piece plasma-welded forged face and hosel with stainless back in the 3-7 irons, fully forged short irons. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

Both sets were introduced in 2014, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: TaylorMade designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The RSi TP is the weaker-lofted set — up to 3.5° weaker than the RSi 1. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the TaylorMade RSi 1 stronger lofted than the RSi TP?

Yes. The TaylorMade RSi 1 7-iron is 30.5° against 34° for the RSi TP, 3.5° stronger, which is worth roughly 10 to 14 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the TaylorMade RSi 1 and RSi TP?

At the 7-iron the difference is 3.5° (30.5° vs 34°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3.5° at the 6-iron, and all 8 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the TaylorMade RSi 1 or the RSi TP?

The TaylorMade RSi 1. As a game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The RSi TP is a players iron, better suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

Do the TaylorMade RSi 1 and RSi TP have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the TaylorMade RSi 1 is 63° lie and 37 inches, the RSi TP 62.5° and 36.75 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
