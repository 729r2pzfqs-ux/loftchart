Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the RAZR X Forged is 35° and the RAZR X Tour is 33°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the RAZR X Tour should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the RAZR X Forged. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the RAZR X Tour averages 1.6° stronger, with the largest gap at the 6-iron (31° vs 29°). The pitching wedges are 47° and 45°; the stronger one leaves a 5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the RAZR X Forged runs 2-iron to AW (10 clubs), the RAZR X Tour 3-iron to AW (9 clubs).

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The RAZR X Forged is listed as triple-net forged and the RAZR X Tour as investment cast. Forging, as in the RAZR X Forged, is generally credited with a softer feel; casting, as in the RAZR X Tour, allows more complex cavity and weighting shapes, which is why it dominates forgiving irons.

Both sets were introduced in 2011, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Callaway designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The RAZR X Tour is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the RAZR X Forged on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Callaway RAZR X Forged stronger lofted than the RAZR X Tour?

No. The RAZR X Tour is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 33° against 35° for the Callaway RAZR X Forged, 2° stronger and worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Callaway RAZR X Forged and RAZR X Tour?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (35° vs 33°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 6-iron, and all 9 shared clubs differ.

Are the Callaway RAZR X Forged and RAZR X Tour aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Callaway RAZR X Forged and RAZR X Tour have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 37 inches long.
