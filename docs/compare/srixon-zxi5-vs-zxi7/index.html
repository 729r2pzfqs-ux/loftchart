Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the ZXi5 is 31° and the ZXi7 is 32°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the ZXi5 should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the ZXi7. Across the 10 clubs both sets share, the ZXi7 averages 1° weaker, with the largest gap at the 9-iron (39° vs 41°). The pitching wedges are 44° and 46°; the stronger one leaves a 6° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The ZXi5 is a players distance iron and the ZXi7 a players iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The ZXi5 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. The ZXi7 trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

The ZXi5 is listed as forged multi-material and the ZXi7 as forged. Both use tungsten weighting to lower the centre of gravity and steady the head on off-centre strikes.

Both sets were introduced in 2024, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Srixon designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The ZXi7 is the weaker-lofted set — up to 2° weaker than the ZXi5. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Srixon ZXi5 stronger lofted than the ZXi7?

Yes. The Srixon ZXi5 7-iron is 31° against 32° for the ZXi7, 1° stronger, which is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the Srixon ZXi5 and ZXi7?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (31° vs 32°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 9-iron, and 8 of the 10 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Srixon ZXi5 or the ZXi7?

The Srixon ZXi5. As a players distance iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. The ZXi7 is a players iron, better suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

Do the Srixon ZXi5 and ZXi7 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 37.25 inches long.
