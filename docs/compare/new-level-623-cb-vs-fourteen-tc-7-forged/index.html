Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the New Level 623-CB is 34° and the Fourteen TC-7 Forged is 31°, a 3° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Fourteen TC-7 Forged should carry roughly 9 to 12 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the New Level 623-CB. Across the 7 clubs both sets share, the Fourteen TC-7 Forged averages 2.9° stronger, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (24° vs 21°). The pitching wedges are 46° and 44°; the stronger one leaves a 6° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Fourteen TC-7 Forged 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62° vs 61.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The New Level 623-CB is listed as forged cavity back and the Fourteen TC-7 Forged as one-piece forged undercut cavity back. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

Both sets were introduced in 2021, so they are direct rivals from the same generation of iron design.

Which should you choose?

The TC-7 Forged is the stronger-lofted set — up to 3° stronger than the 623-CB on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the New Level 623-CB stronger lofted than the Fourteen TC-7 Forged?

No. The Fourteen TC-7 Forged is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 31° against 34° for the New Level 623-CB, 3° stronger and worth roughly 9 to 12 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the New Level 623-CB and Fourteen TC-7 Forged?

At the 7-iron the difference is 3° (34° vs 31°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3° at the 4-iron, and all 7 shared clubs differ.

Are the New Level 623-CB and Fourteen TC-7 Forged aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the New Level 623-CB and Fourteen TC-7 Forged have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the New Level 623-CB is 62° lie and 37 inches, the Fourteen TC-7 Forged 61.5° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
