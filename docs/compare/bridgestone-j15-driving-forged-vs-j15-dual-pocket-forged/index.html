Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the J15 Driving Forged is 32° and the J15 Dual Pocket Forged is 31°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the J15 Dual Pocket Forged should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the J15 Driving Forged. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the J15 Dual Pocket Forged averages 0.5° stronger, with the largest gap at the 7-iron (32° vs 31°). The pitching wedges are 46° and 45°; the stronger one leaves a 5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The J15 Driving Forged is listed as two-piece forged with a hidden internal cavity and the J15 Dual Pocket Forged as two-piece forged with a Turbo Rubber Design perimeter cavity. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

Both sets were introduced in 2015, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Bridgestone designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The J15 Dual Pocket Forged is the stronger-lofted set — up to 1° stronger than the J15 Driving Forged on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged stronger lofted than the J15 Dual Pocket Forged?

No. The J15 Dual Pocket Forged is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 31° against 32° for the Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged and J15 Dual Pocket Forged?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (32° vs 31°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 7-iron, and 4 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Are the Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged and J15 Dual Pocket Forged aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Bridgestone J15 Driving Forged and J15 Dual Pocket Forged have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.5° lie and 37 inches long.
