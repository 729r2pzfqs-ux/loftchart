Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Elyte HL is 30° and the Quantum Max OS is 29°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Quantum Max OS should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Elyte HL. Across the 10 clubs both sets share, the Quantum Max OS averages 1° stronger, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (20° vs 19°). The pitching wedges are 43° and 42°; the stronger one leaves a 8° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Elyte HL 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (37.5" vs 37.25").

The Elyte HL is a game improvement iron and the Quantum Max OS a super game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Quantum Max OS is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Elyte HL trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

The Elyte HL is listed as Speed Frame cast body with an Ai10x face and a tri-sole design and the Quantum Max OS as two-piece Modern 360° Undercut cavity body with a Progressive Tri-Sole and urethane microspheres. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The Quantum Max OS came out 1 year after the Elyte HL, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The Quantum Max OS is the stronger-lofted set — up to 1° stronger than the Elyte HL on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Callaway Elyte HL stronger lofted than the Quantum Max OS?

No. The Quantum Max OS is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 29° against 30° for the Callaway Elyte HL, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Callaway Elyte HL and Quantum Max OS?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (30° vs 29°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 4-iron, and all 10 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Callaway Elyte HL or the Quantum Max OS?

The Quantum Max OS. As a super game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Callaway Elyte HL is a game improvement iron, better suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

Do the Callaway Elyte HL and Quantum Max OS have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Callaway Elyte HL is 62.25° lie and 37.5 inches, the Quantum Max OS 62.25° and 37.25 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
