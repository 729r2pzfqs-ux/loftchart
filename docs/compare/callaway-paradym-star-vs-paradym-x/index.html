Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Paradym Star is 30° and the Paradym X is 27.5°, a 2.5° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Paradym X should carry roughly 8 to 10 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Paradym Star. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the Paradym X averages 2.8° stronger, with the largest gap at the 9-iron (40° vs 36°). The pitching wedges are 45° and 41°; the stronger one leaves a 9° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Paradym Star 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62° vs 62.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the Paradym Star 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (37.25" vs 37").

The sets are built differently: the Paradym Star runs 5-iron to SW (8 clubs), the Paradym X 4-iron to SW (10 clubs).

The Paradym Star is a super game improvement iron and the Paradym X a game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Paradym Star is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Paradym X trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

The Paradym Star is listed as hollow body with a forged 455 face cup and the Paradym X as investment cast, hollow body. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help. The Paradym X adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

Both sets were introduced in 2023, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Callaway designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The Paradym X is the stronger-lofted set — up to 4° stronger than the Paradym Star on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Callaway Paradym Star stronger lofted than the Paradym X?

No. The Paradym X is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 27.5° against 30° for the Callaway Paradym Star, 2.5° stronger and worth roughly 8 to 10 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Callaway Paradym Star and Paradym X?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2.5° (30° vs 27.5°). The largest gap on a shared club is 4° at the 9-iron, and 7 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Callaway Paradym Star or the Paradym X?

The Callaway Paradym Star. As a super game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Paradym X is a game improvement iron, better suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

Do the Callaway Paradym Star and Paradym X have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Callaway Paradym Star is 62° lie and 37.25 inches, the Paradym X 62.5° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
