Lofts compared

What the differences mean

Every club the two sets share has exactly the same loft, starting with a 32° 7-iron. On paper there is no distance or trajectory difference: with the same swing, a 7-iron from either set should launch and carry about the same. The real differences are in how the heads are built, how they look at address and how they feel at impact, none of which a spec table can show. Lie angles and lengths match as well, so any fitting adjustment made to one set would carry straight over to the other.

Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The most forgiving category — very wide soles, maximum perimeter weighting and the strongest lofts. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The Hot Launch E523 is listed as hollow-body iron-wood with perimeter weighting, Houdini Sole and concave body shaping and the Hot Launch E524 as hollow-body iron-wood with Houdini Sole. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help.

The Hot Launch E524 arrived 2 years after the Hot Launch E523, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept.

Which should you choose?

The Hot Launch E523 and Hot Launch E524 share the same lofts on every club on file, so the difference is in head construction, feel and forgiveness rather than trajectory.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Tour Edge Hot Launch E523 stronger lofted than the Hot Launch E524?

No. Both have a 32° 7-iron and the same loft on every club they share, so neither is stronger lofted. The difference is in construction and feel.

What is the loft difference between the Tour Edge Hot Launch E523 and Hot Launch E524?

There is none. Every club both sets include has the same loft, including the 32° 7-iron.

Are the Tour Edge Hot Launch E523 and Hot Launch E524 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Tour Edge Hot Launch E523 and Hot Launch E524 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.5° lie and 37.25 inches long.
