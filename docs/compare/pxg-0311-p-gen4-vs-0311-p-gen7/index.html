Lofts compared

What the differences mean

Every club the two sets share has exactly the same loft, starting with a 30° 7-iron. On paper there is no distance or trajectory difference: with the same swing, a 7-iron from either set should launch and carry about the same. The real differences are in how the heads are built, how they look at address and how they feel at impact, none of which a spec table can show. Lie angles and lengths match as well, so any fitting adjustment made to one set would carry straight over to the other.

The sets are built differently: the 0311 P GEN4 runs 3-iron to GW (9 clubs), the 0311 P GEN7 4-iron to GW (8 clubs).

Both are players distance irons, built for roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. Forged or multi-material heads that keep a compact look while adding face technology and stronger lofts for distance. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The 0311 P GEN4 is listed as forged hollow body with an XCOR core and the 0311 P GEN7 as forged hollow body with a welded face. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help. The 0311 P GEN7 adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

The 0311 P GEN7 came out 3 years after the 0311 P GEN4, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The 0311 P GEN4 and 0311 P GEN7 share the same lofts on every club on file, so the difference is in head construction, feel and forgiveness rather than trajectory.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PXG 0311 P GEN4 stronger lofted than the 0311 P GEN7?

No. Both have a 30° 7-iron and the same loft on every club they share, so neither is stronger lofted. The difference is in construction and feel.

What is the loft difference between the PXG 0311 P GEN4 and 0311 P GEN7?

There is none. Every club both sets include has the same loft, including the 30° 7-iron.

Are the PXG 0311 P GEN4 and 0311 P GEN7 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players distance irons, built for roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the PXG 0311 P GEN4 and 0311 P GEN7 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.5° lie and 37 inches long.
