Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the G Le4 is 29.5° and the G740 is 28°, a 1.5° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the G740 should carry roughly 4 to 6 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the G Le4. Across the 6 clubs both sets share, the G740 averages 3.8° stronger, with the largest gap at the PW (46.5° vs 40°). The pitching wedges are 46.5° and 40°; the stronger one leaves a 10° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the G Le4 7-iron sits 1° flatter (62° vs 63°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the G740 7-iron is 0.5 inches longer (36.5" vs 37").

The sets are built differently: the G Le4 runs 6-iron to SW (7 clubs), the G740 5-iron to 56W (9 clubs).

Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The most forgiving category — very wide soles, maximum perimeter weighting and the strongest lofts. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The G Le4 is listed as investment cast and the G740 as investment cast cavity back. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

Both sets were introduced in 2026, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Ping designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The G740 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 6.5° stronger than the G Le4 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Ping G Le4 stronger lofted than the G740?

No. The G740 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 28° against 29.5° for the Ping G Le4, 1.5° stronger and worth roughly 4 to 6 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Ping G Le4 and G740?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1.5° (29.5° vs 28°). The largest gap on a shared club is 6.5° at the PW, and all 6 shared clubs differ.

Are the Ping G Le4 and G740 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Ping G Le4 and G740 have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Ping G Le4 is 62° lie and 36.5 inches, the G740 63° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
