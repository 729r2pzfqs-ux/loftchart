Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Hot Launch E524 is 32° and the Hot Launch X525 is 29°, a 3° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Hot Launch X525 should carry roughly 9 to 12 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Hot Launch E524. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the Hot Launch X525 averages 2.4° stronger, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (23° vs 20°). The pitching wedges are 44° and 43°; the stronger one leaves a 7° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Hot Launch X525 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62.5° vs 62°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The sets are built differently: the Hot Launch E524 runs 3-iron to AW (9 clubs), the Hot Launch X525 4-iron to AW (8 clubs).

Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The most forgiving category — very wide soles, maximum perimeter weighting and the strongest lofts. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The Hot Launch E524 is listed as hollow-body iron-wood with Houdini Sole and the Hot Launch X525 as hollow-body iron-wood with perimeter weighting, Houdini Sole and an ultra-shallow face. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help.

The Hot Launch X525 arrived 1 year after the Hot Launch E524, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 32° to 29°.

Which should you choose?

The Hot Launch X525 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 3° stronger than the Hot Launch E524 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Tour Edge Hot Launch E524 stronger lofted than the Hot Launch X525?

No. The Hot Launch X525 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 29° against 32° for the Tour Edge Hot Launch E524, 3° stronger and worth roughly 9 to 12 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Tour Edge Hot Launch E524 and Hot Launch X525?

At the 7-iron the difference is 3° (32° vs 29°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3° at the 4-iron, and all 8 shared clubs differ.

Are the Tour Edge Hot Launch E524 and Hot Launch X525 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Tour Edge Hot Launch E524 and Hot Launch X525 have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Tour Edge Hot Launch E524 is 62.5° lie and 37.25 inches, the Hot Launch X525 62° and 37.25 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
