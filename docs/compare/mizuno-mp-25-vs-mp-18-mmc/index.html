Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the MP-25 is 34° and the MP-18 MMC is 32°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the MP-18 MMC should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the MP-25. Across the 7 clubs both sets share, the MP-18 MMC averages 1.6° stronger, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (24° vs 22°).

The sets are built differently: the MP-25 runs 3-iron to PW (8 clubs), the MP-18 MMC 4-iron to PW (7 clubs).

The MP-25 is a players distance iron and the MP-18 MMC a players iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The MP-25 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. The MP-18 MMC trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

The MP-25 is listed as Grain Flow Forged with a micro-slot behind the face and the MP-18 MMC as forged. The MP-18 MMC adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

The MP-18 MMC came out 2 years after the MP-25, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The MP-18 MMC is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the MP-25 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Mizuno MP-25 stronger lofted than the MP-18 MMC?

No. The MP-18 MMC is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 32° against 34° for the Mizuno MP-25, 2° stronger and worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Mizuno MP-25 and MP-18 MMC?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (34° vs 32°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 4-iron, and 6 of the 7 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Mizuno MP-25 or the MP-18 MMC?

The Mizuno MP-25. As a players distance iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. The MP-18 MMC is a players iron, better suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

Do the Mizuno MP-25 and MP-18 MMC have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 61.5° lie and 36.75 inches long.
