Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the ZXi4 is 28.5° and the ZXi5+ is 29.5°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the ZXi4 should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the ZXi5+. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the differences roughly cancel out, with the largest gap at the 7-iron (28.5° vs 29.5°).

The sets are built differently: the ZXi4 runs 4-iron to SW (9 clubs), the ZXi5+ 3-iron to SW (10 clubs).

The ZXi4 is a game improvement iron and the ZXi5+ a players distance iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The ZXi4 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The ZXi5+ trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head.

The ZXi4 is listed as hollow body with a forged face and third-generation MainFrame milling and the ZXi5+ as forged hollow body. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help. The ZXi5+ adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

The ZXi5+ arrived 2 years after the ZXi4, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 28.5° to 29.5°.

Which should you choose?

The ZXi5+ is the weaker-lofted set — up to 1° weaker than the ZXi4. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Srixon ZXi4 stronger lofted than the ZXi5+?

Yes. The Srixon ZXi4 7-iron is 28.5° against 29.5° for the ZXi5+, 1° stronger, which is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the Srixon ZXi4 and ZXi5+?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (28.5° vs 29.5°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 7-iron, and 3 of the 9 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Srixon ZXi4 or the ZXi5+?

The Srixon ZXi4. As a game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The ZXi5+ is a players distance iron, better suited to roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head.

Do the Srixon ZXi4 and ZXi5+ have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 37.25 inches long.
