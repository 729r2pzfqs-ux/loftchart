Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the P7MB (2023) is 34° and the P7CB is 33°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the P7CB should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the P7MB (2023). Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the P7CB averages 0.7° stronger, with the largest gap at the 9-iron (42.5° vs 41°). The pitching wedges are 47° and 46°; the stronger one leaves a 4° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the P7MB (2023) runs 3-iron to PW (8 clubs), the P7CB 3-iron to GW (9 clubs).

The P7MB (2023) is a blade iron and the P7CB a players iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The P7CB is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The P7MB (2023) trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

The P7MB (2023) is listed as forged and the P7CB as forged with co-forged tungsten. The P7CB adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

The P7CB came out 2 years after the P7MB (2023), but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The P7CB is the stronger-lofted set — up to 1.5° stronger than the P7MB (2023) on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the TaylorMade P7MB (2023) stronger lofted than the P7CB?

No. The P7CB is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 33° against 34° for the TaylorMade P7MB (2023), 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the TaylorMade P7MB (2023) and P7CB?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (34° vs 33°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1.5° at the 9-iron, and 5 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the TaylorMade P7MB (2023) or the P7CB?

The P7CB. As a players iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The TaylorMade P7MB (2023) is a blade iron, better suited to low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Do the TaylorMade P7MB (2023) and P7CB have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.5° lie and 37 inches long.
