Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Kalea Premier is 32° and the Stealth HD is 30°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Stealth HD should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Kalea Premier. Across the 7 clubs both sets share, the Stealth HD averages 1.4° stronger, with the largest gap at the 7-iron (32° vs 30°). The pitching wedges are 45° and 44.5°; the stronger one leaves a 5.5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Kalea Premier 7-iron sits 1.8° flatter (61.75° vs 63.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the Stealth HD 7-iron is 1 inches longer (36.25" vs 37.25").

The sets are built differently: the Kalea Premier runs 6-iron to SW (7 clubs), the Stealth HD 5-iron to LW (9 clubs).

Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The most forgiving category — very wide soles, maximum perimeter weighting and the strongest lofts. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The Kalea Premier is listed as investment cast Cap Back design with Speed Pocket and the Stealth HD as investment cast Cap Back design, draw-biased. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The Stealth HD came out 1 year after the Kalea Premier, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The Stealth HD is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the Kalea Premier on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the TaylorMade Kalea Premier stronger lofted than the Stealth HD?

No. The Stealth HD is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 30° against 32° for the TaylorMade Kalea Premier, 2° stronger and worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the TaylorMade Kalea Premier and Stealth HD?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (32° vs 30°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 7-iron, and all 7 shared clubs differ.

Are the TaylorMade Kalea Premier and Stealth HD aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the TaylorMade Kalea Premier and Stealth HD have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the TaylorMade Kalea Premier is 61.75° lie and 36.25 inches, the Stealth HD 63.5° and 37.25 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
