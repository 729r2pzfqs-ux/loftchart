Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Idea a12 is 32° and the Idea (2023) is 30°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Idea (2023) should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Idea a12. Across the 4 clubs both sets share, the Idea (2023) averages 1.1° stronger, with the largest gap at the 7-iron (32° vs 30°). The pitching wedges are 44° and 44.5°; the stronger one leaves a 6° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Idea a12 7-iron sits 0.2° flatter (62.25° vs 62.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the Idea a12 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (37.5" vs 37.25").

The sets are built differently: the Idea a12 runs 7-iron to LW (7 clubs), the Idea (2023) 4-iron to AW (8 clubs).

The Idea a12 is a super game improvement iron and the Idea (2023) a game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Idea a12 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Idea (2023) trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

The Idea a12 is listed as investment cast and the Idea (2023) as hollow body. The Idea (2023)'s hollow body lets the face flex more and moves mass low and outward, usually giving higher launch and more ball speed on off-centre strikes than the Idea a12, at some cost in feel.

There are 12 years between these sets (2011 and 2023), more than one design generation. Over that time irons moved toward stronger lofts, thinner and faster faces, hollow and multi-material heads and wider use of tungsten, so the newer set is not simply a better version of the older one: it may launch and spin quite differently. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 32° to 30°.

Which should you choose?

The Idea (2023) is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the Idea a12 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Adams Idea a12 stronger lofted than the Idea (2023)?

No. The Idea (2023) is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 30° against 32° for the Adams Idea a12, 2° stronger and worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Adams Idea a12 and Idea (2023)?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (32° vs 30°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 7-iron, and all 4 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Adams Idea a12 or the Idea (2023)?

The Adams Idea a12. As a super game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Idea (2023) is a game improvement iron, better suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

Do the Adams Idea a12 and Idea (2023) have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Adams Idea a12 is 62.25° lie and 37.5 inches, the Idea (2023) 62.5° and 37.25 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
