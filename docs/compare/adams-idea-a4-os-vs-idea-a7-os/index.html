Lofts compared

What the differences mean

Every club the two sets share has exactly the same loft, starting with a 32° 7-iron. On paper there is no distance or trajectory difference: with the same swing, a 7-iron from either set should launch and carry about the same. The real differences are in how the heads are built, how they look at address and how they feel at impact, none of which a spec table can show.

The sets are built differently: the Idea Tech a4OS runs 3H to PW (8 clubs), the Idea a7 OS 6-iron to LW (8 clubs).

Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The most forgiving category — very wide soles, maximum perimeter weighting and the strongest lofts. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The Idea Tech a4OS is listed as cast and the Idea a7 OS as investment cast. The Idea a7 OS's hollow body lets the face flex more and moves mass low and outward, usually giving higher launch and more ball speed on off-centre strikes than the Idea Tech a4OS, at some cost in feel.

The Idea a7 OS arrived 1 year after the Idea Tech a4OS, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept.

Which should you choose?

The Idea Tech a4OS and Idea a7 OS share the same lofts on every club on file, so the difference is in head construction, feel and forgiveness rather than trajectory.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Adams Idea Tech a4OS stronger lofted than the Idea a7 OS?

No. Both have a 32° 7-iron and the same loft on every club they share, so neither is stronger lofted. The difference is in construction and feel.

What is the loft difference between the Adams Idea Tech a4OS and Idea a7 OS?

There is none. Every club both sets include has the same loft, including the 32° 7-iron.

Are the Adams Idea Tech a4OS and Idea a7 OS aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are super game improvement irons, built for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Adams Idea Tech a4OS and Idea a7 OS have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.25° lie and 37.5 inches long.
