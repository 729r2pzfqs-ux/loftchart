Lofts compared

What the differences mean

Every club the two sets share has exactly the same loft, starting with a 30° 7-iron. On paper there is no distance or trajectory difference: with the same swing, a 7-iron from either set should launch and carry about the same. The real differences are in how the heads are built, how they look at address and how they feel at impact, none of which a spec table can show.

The sets are built differently: the TW757 Vx runs 4-iron to PW (7 clubs), the TW767 Vx 4-iron to A (8 clubs).

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as one-piece forged cavity back with a tungsten toe weight. Both use tungsten weighting to lower the centre of gravity and steady the head on off-centre strikes.

The TW767 Vx arrived 2 years after the TW757 Vx, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept.

Which should you choose?

The TW757 Vx and TW767 Vx share the same lofts on every club on file, so the difference is in head construction, feel and forgiveness rather than trajectory.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Honma TW757 Vx stronger lofted than the TW767 Vx?

No. Both have a 30° 7-iron and the same loft on every club they share, so neither is stronger lofted. The difference is in construction and feel.

What is the loft difference between the Honma TW757 Vx and TW767 Vx?

There is none. Every club both sets include has the same loft, including the 30° 7-iron.

Are the Honma TW757 Vx and TW767 Vx aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Honma TW757 Vx and TW767 Vx have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Honma TW757 Vx is 61.5° lie and 37.125 inches, the TW767 Vx 61.5° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
