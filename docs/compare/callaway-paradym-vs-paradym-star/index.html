Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Paradym is 29° and the Paradym Star is 30°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Paradym should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Paradym Star. Across the 7 clubs both sets share, the Paradym Star averages 1.9° weaker, with the largest gap at the 9-iron (37° vs 40°). The pitching wedges are 42° and 45°; the stronger one leaves a 8° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Paradym Star 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62.5° vs 62°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the Paradym Star 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (37" vs 37.25").

The sets are built differently: the Paradym runs 4-iron to GW (9 clubs), the Paradym Star 5-iron to SW (8 clubs).

The Paradym is a game improvement iron and the Paradym Star a super game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Paradym Star is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Paradym trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

The Paradym is listed as investment cast, hollow body and the Paradym Star as hollow body with a forged 455 face cup. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help. The Paradym adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

Both sets were introduced in 2023, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Callaway designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The Paradym Star is the weaker-lofted set — up to 3° weaker than the Paradym. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Callaway Paradym stronger lofted than the Paradym Star?

Yes. The Callaway Paradym 7-iron is 29° against 30° for the Paradym Star, 1° stronger, which is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the Callaway Paradym and Paradym Star?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (29° vs 30°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3° at the 9-iron, and 6 of the 7 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Callaway Paradym or the Paradym Star?

The Paradym Star. As a super game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Callaway Paradym is a game improvement iron, better suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

Do the Callaway Paradym and Paradym Star have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Callaway Paradym is 62.5° lie and 37 inches, the Paradym Star 62° and 37.25 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
