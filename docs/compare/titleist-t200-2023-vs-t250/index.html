What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the T200 (2023) is 30.5° and the T250 is 30.5°, identical at the club most golfers compare on, although other clubs in the sets differ. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the differences roughly cancel out, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (21° vs 22°).

The sets are built differently: the T200 (2023) runs 4-iron to W (8 clubs), the T250 2-iron to W (10 clubs).

Both are players distance irons, built for roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. Forged or multi-material heads that keep a compact look while adding face technology and stronger lofts for distance. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The T200 (2023) is listed as forged face with hollow-body, Max Impact internal support and the T250 as hollow-body, high-strength steel with a forged L-Face insert. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help. Both use tungsten weighting to lower the centre of gravity and steady the head on off-centre strikes.

The T250 arrived 2 years after the T200 (2023), a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept.

Which should you choose?

The T250 is the weaker-lofted set — up to 1° weaker than the T200 (2023). That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Titleist T200 (2023) stronger lofted than the T250?

Not at the 7-iron, where both are 30.5°. Other clubs in the sets differ, so compare the full table above.

What is the loft difference between the Titleist T200 (2023) and T250?

At the 7-iron the difference is 0° (30.5° vs 30.5°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 4-iron, and 1 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Are the Titleist T200 (2023) and T250 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players distance irons, built for roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Titleist T200 (2023) and T250 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 63° lie and 37 inches long.
