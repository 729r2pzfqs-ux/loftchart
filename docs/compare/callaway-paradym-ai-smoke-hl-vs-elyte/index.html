Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Paradym Ai Smoke HL is 30° and the Elyte is 29°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Elyte should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Paradym Ai Smoke HL. Across the 10 clubs both sets share, the Elyte averages 1° stronger, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (21° vs 19°). The pitching wedges are 43° and 42°; the stronger one leaves a 8° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Paradym Ai Smoke HL 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62° vs 62.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the Paradym Ai Smoke HL 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (37.5" vs 37.25").

The Paradym Ai Smoke HL is a super game improvement iron and the Elyte a game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Paradym Ai Smoke HL is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Elyte trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

The Paradym Ai Smoke HL is listed as cast body with an Ai Smart Face and a hollow long-iron construction and the Elyte as Speed Frame cast body with a forged face cup and urethane microspheres. The Paradym Ai Smoke HL's hollow body lets the face flex more and moves mass low and outward, usually giving higher launch and more ball speed on off-centre strikes than the Elyte, at some cost in feel. Both use tungsten weighting to lower the centre of gravity and steady the head on off-centre strikes.

The Elyte came out 1 year after the Paradym Ai Smoke HL, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The Elyte is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the Paradym Ai Smoke HL on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke HL stronger lofted than the Elyte?

No. The Elyte is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 29° against 30° for the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke HL, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke HL and Elyte?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (30° vs 29°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 4-iron, and 7 of the 10 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke HL or the Elyte?

The Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke HL. As a super game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Elyte is a game improvement iron, better suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

Do the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke HL and Elyte have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke HL is 62° lie and 37.5 inches, the Elyte 62.5° and 37.25 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
