Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the X-18 is 33° and the X-Tour is 35°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the X-18 should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the X-Tour. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the X-Tour averages 1.2° weaker, with the largest gap at the 6-iron (29° vs 31°). The pitching wedges are 46° and 47°; the stronger one leaves a 4° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the X-Tour 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62.5° vs 62°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The sets are built differently: the X-18 runs 2-iron to LW (12 clubs), the X-Tour 2-iron to PW (9 clubs).

The X-18 is a game improvement iron and the X-Tour a players iron, so they sit 2 steps apart on the forgiveness scale. The X-18 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The X-Tour trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

The X-18 is listed as investment cast and the X-Tour as forged. Forging, as in the X-Tour, is generally credited with a softer feel; casting, as in the X-18, allows more complex cavity and weighting shapes, which is why it dominates forgiving irons.

The X-Tour came out 1 year after the X-18, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The X-Tour is the weaker-lofted set — up to 2° weaker than the X-18. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Callaway X-18 stronger lofted than the X-Tour?

Yes. The Callaway X-18 7-iron is 33° against 35° for the X-Tour, 2° stronger, which is worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the Callaway X-18 and X-Tour?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (33° vs 35°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 6-iron, and 7 of the 9 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Callaway X-18 or the X-Tour?

The Callaway X-18. As a game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The X-Tour is a players iron, better suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

Do the Callaway X-18 and X-Tour have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Callaway X-18 is 62.5° lie and 37 inches, the X-Tour 62° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
