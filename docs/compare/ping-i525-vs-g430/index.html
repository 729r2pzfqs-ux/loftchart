Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the i525 is 30.5° and the G430 is 29°, a 1.5° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the G430 should carry roughly 4 to 6 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the i525. Across the 7 clubs both sets share, the G430 averages 2.3° stronger, with the largest gap at the PW (45° vs 41°). The pitching wedges are 45° and 41°; the stronger one leaves a 9° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the i525 runs 3-iron to UW (9 clubs), the G430 4-iron to LW (11 clubs).

The i525 is a players distance iron and the G430 a game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The G430 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The i525 trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head.

The i525 is listed as hollow body and the G430 as investment cast. The i525's hollow body lets the face flex more and moves mass low and outward, usually giving higher launch and more ball speed on off-centre strikes than the G430, at some cost in feel. The i525 adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

The G430 came out 1 year after the i525, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The G430 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 4° stronger than the i525 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Ping i525 stronger lofted than the G430?

No. The G430 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 29° against 30.5° for the Ping i525, 1.5° stronger and worth roughly 4 to 6 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Ping i525 and G430?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1.5° (30.5° vs 29°). The largest gap on a shared club is 4° at the PW, and all 7 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Ping i525 or the G430?

The G430. As a game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The Ping i525 is a players distance iron, better suited to roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head.

Do the Ping i525 and G430 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 37 inches long.
