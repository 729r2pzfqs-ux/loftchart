Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast is 31° and the Elyte Max Fast is 30°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Elyte Max Fast should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the Elyte Max Fast averages 0.9° stronger, with the largest gap at the 5-iron (25° vs 23°). The pitching wedges are 44° and 43°; the stronger one leaves a 7° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Elyte Max Fast 7-iron sits 0.2° flatter (62.5° vs 62.25°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Perimeter-weighted cavity backs with wider soles, more offset and stronger lofts, aimed at mid handicaps. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as investment cast, multi-material. The Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast adds tungsten weighting, which lowers the centre of gravity and raises stability on mis-hits, helping the long irons launch.

The Elyte Max Fast arrived 1 year after the Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 31° to 30°.

Which should you choose?

The Elyte Max Fast is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast stronger lofted than the Elyte Max Fast?

No. The Elyte Max Fast is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 30° against 31° for the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast and Elyte Max Fast?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (31° vs 30°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 5-iron, and 6 of the 9 shared clubs differ.

Are the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast and Elyte Max Fast aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast and Elyte Max Fast have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Callaway Paradym Ai Smoke Max Fast is 62.5° lie and 37.5 inches, the Elyte Max Fast 62.25° and 37.5 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
