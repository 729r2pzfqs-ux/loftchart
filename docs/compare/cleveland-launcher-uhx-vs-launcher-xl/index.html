Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Launcher UHX is 30° and the Launcher XL is 29°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Launcher XL should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Launcher UHX. Across the 7 clubs both sets share, the Launcher XL averages 0.6° stronger, with the largest gap at the 7-iron (30° vs 29°). The pitching wedges are 44° and 43°; the stronger one leaves a 7° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the Launcher UHX runs 4-iron to PW (7 clubs), the Launcher XL 4-iron to DW (8 clubs).

The Launcher UHX is a game improvement iron and the Launcher XL a super game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Launcher XL is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Launcher UHX trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

The Launcher UHX is listed as progressive set — hollow-body long irons (4-7), cavity-back short irons (8-PW) and the Launcher XL as hollow long irons, cavity-back short irons. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help.

The Launcher XL arrived 3 years after the Launcher UHX, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 30° to 29°.

Which should you choose?

The Launcher XL is the stronger-lofted set — up to 1° stronger than the Launcher UHX on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Cleveland Launcher UHX stronger lofted than the Launcher XL?

No. The Launcher XL is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 29° against 30° for the Cleveland Launcher UHX, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Cleveland Launcher UHX and Launcher XL?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (30° vs 29°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 7-iron, and 4 of the 7 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Cleveland Launcher UHX or the Launcher XL?

The Launcher XL. As a super game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Cleveland Launcher UHX is a game improvement iron, better suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

Do the Cleveland Launcher UHX and Launcher XL have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 63° lie and 37 inches long.
