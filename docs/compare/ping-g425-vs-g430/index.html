Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the G425 is 30° and the G430 is 29°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the G430 should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the G425. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the G430 averages 1.4° stronger, with the largest gap at the PW (44.5° vs 41°). The pitching wedges are 44.5° and 41°; the stronger one leaves a 9° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the G425 runs 4-iron to LW (10 clubs), the G430 4-iron to LW (11 clubs).

Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Perimeter-weighted cavity backs with wider soles, more offset and stronger lofts, aimed at mid handicaps. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as investment cast. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The G430 arrived 2 years after the G425, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 30° to 29°.

Which should you choose?

The G430 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 3.5° stronger than the G425 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Ping G425 stronger lofted than the G430?

No. The G430 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 29° against 30° for the Ping G425, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Ping G425 and G430?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (30° vs 29°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3.5° at the PW, and 7 of the 9 shared clubs differ.

Are the Ping G425 and G430 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Ping G425 and G430 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 37 inches long.
