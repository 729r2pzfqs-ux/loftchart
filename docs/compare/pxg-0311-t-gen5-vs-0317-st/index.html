Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the 0311 T GEN5 is 32° and the 0317 ST is 33°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the 0311 T GEN5 should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the 0317 ST. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the 0317 ST averages 0.9° weaker, with the largest gap at the 5-iron (25° vs 27°).

The sets are built differently: the 0311 T GEN5 runs 4-iron to GW (8 clubs), the 0317 ST 3-iron to GW (9 clubs).

The 0311 T GEN5 is a players iron and the 0317 ST a blade iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The 0311 T GEN5 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The 0317 ST trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

The 0311 T GEN5 is listed as forged hollow body with an XCOR2 core and the 0317 ST as triple-forged solid muscle-back blade with CNC-milled face and back. The 0311 T GEN5's hollow body lets the face flex more and moves mass low and outward, usually giving higher launch and more ball speed on off-centre strikes than the 0317 ST, at some cost in feel. The 0317 ST's muscle-back shape keeps mass directly behind the strike for feedback and control, where a cavity spreads it to the perimeter for forgiveness.

Both sets were introduced in 2022, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: PXG designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The 0317 ST is the weaker-lofted set — up to 2° weaker than the 0311 T GEN5. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PXG 0311 T GEN5 stronger lofted than the 0317 ST?

Yes. The PXG 0311 T GEN5 7-iron is 32° against 33° for the 0317 ST, 1° stronger, which is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the PXG 0311 T GEN5 and 0317 ST?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (32° vs 33°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 5-iron, and 7 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the PXG 0311 T GEN5 or the 0317 ST?

The PXG 0311 T GEN5. As a players iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The 0317 ST is a blade iron, better suited to low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Do the PXG 0311 T GEN5 and 0317 ST have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.5° lie and 37 inches long.
