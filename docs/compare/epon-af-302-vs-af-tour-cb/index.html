Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the AF-302 is 34° and the AF-Tour CB is 34°, identical at the club most golfers compare on, although other clubs in the sets differ. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the AF-Tour CB averages 0.4° weaker, with the largest gap at the 3-iron (20° vs 21°).

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The AF-302 is listed as forged one-piece cavity back (S20C round-bar billet, ultra-precision multi-stage forging) and the AF-Tour CB as forged one-piece half-cavity (S20C billet). With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

There are 5 years between these sets (2010 and 2015), more than one design generation. Over that time irons moved toward stronger lofts, thinner and faster faces, hollow and multi-material heads and wider use of tungsten, so the newer set is not simply a better version of the older one: it may launch and spin quite differently.

Which should you choose?

The AF-Tour CB is the weaker-lofted set — up to 1° weaker than the AF-302. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Epon AF-302 stronger lofted than the AF-Tour CB?

Not at the 7-iron, where both are 34°. Other clubs in the sets differ, so compare the full table above.

What is the loft difference between the Epon AF-302 and AF-Tour CB?

At the 7-iron the difference is 0° (34° vs 34°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 3-iron, and 3 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Are the Epon AF-302 and AF-Tour CB aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Epon AF-302 and AF-Tour CB have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 36.5 inches long.
