Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Eye 2 is 36° and the ISI is 34.5°, a 1.5° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the ISI should carry roughly 4 to 6 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Eye 2. Across the 10 clubs both sets share, the ISI averages 1.9° stronger, with the largest gap at the SW (57.5° vs 52°). The pitching wedges are 50.5° and 47°; the stronger one leaves a 3° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Eye 2 7-iron sits 1° flatter (62.5° vs 63.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The sets are built differently: the Eye 2 runs 3-iron to LW (10 clubs), the ISI 3-iron to LW (11 clubs).

Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Perimeter-weighted cavity backs with wider soles, more offset and stronger lofts, aimed at mid handicaps. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as investment cast. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

There are 14 years between these sets (1982 and 1996), more than one design generation. Over that time irons moved toward stronger lofts, thinner and faster faces, hollow and multi-material heads and wider use of tungsten, so the newer set is not simply a better version of the older one: it may launch and spin quite differently. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 36° to 34.5°.

Which should you choose?

The ISI is the stronger-lofted set — up to 5.5° stronger than the Eye 2 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Ping Eye 2 stronger lofted than the ISI?

No. The ISI is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 34.5° against 36° for the Ping Eye 2, 1.5° stronger and worth roughly 4 to 6 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Ping Eye 2 and ISI?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1.5° (36° vs 34.5°). The largest gap on a shared club is 5.5° at the SW, and 9 of the 10 shared clubs differ.

Are the Ping Eye 2 and ISI aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Ping Eye 2 and ISI have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Ping Eye 2 is 62.5° lie and 36.75 inches, the ISI 63.5° and 36.75 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
