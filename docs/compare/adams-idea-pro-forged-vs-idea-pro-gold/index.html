What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Idea Pro Forged is 34° and the Idea Pro Gold is 34°, identical at the club most golfers compare on, although other clubs in the sets differ. Across the 6 clubs both sets share, the differences roughly cancel out, with the largest gap at the 5-iron (26° vs 27°). Beyond loft, the Idea Pro Forged 7-iron sits 1° flatter (61.5° vs 62.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as forged. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The Idea Pro Gold arrived 2 years after the Idea Pro Forged, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept.

Which should you choose?

The Idea Pro Gold is the weaker-lofted set — up to 1° weaker than the Idea Pro Forged. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Adams Idea Pro Forged stronger lofted than the Idea Pro Gold?

Not at the 7-iron, where both are 34°. Other clubs in the sets differ, so compare the full table above.

What is the loft difference between the Adams Idea Pro Forged and Idea Pro Gold?

At the 7-iron the difference is 0° (34° vs 34°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 5-iron, and 1 of the 6 shared clubs differ.

Are the Adams Idea Pro Forged and Idea Pro Gold aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Adams Idea Pro Forged and Idea Pro Gold have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Adams Idea Pro Forged is 61.5° lie and 37 inches, the Idea Pro Gold 62.5° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
