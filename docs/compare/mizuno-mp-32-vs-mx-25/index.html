Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the MP-32 is 35° and the MX-25 is 34°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the MX-25 should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the MP-32. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the MX-25 averages 0.6° stronger, with the largest gap at the 6-iron (31° vs 30°). The pitching wedges are 47° and 46°; the stronger one leaves a 4° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the MP-32 runs 2-iron to PW (9 clubs), the MX-25 3-iron to PW (8 clubs).

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The MP-32 is listed as forged and the MX-25 as Grain Flow Forged with a CNC-milled sole cavity. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The MX-25 came out 3 years after the MP-32, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The MX-25 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 1° stronger than the MP-32 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Mizuno MP-32 stronger lofted than the MX-25?

No. The MX-25 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 34° against 35° for the Mizuno MP-32, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Mizuno MP-32 and MX-25?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (35° vs 34°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1° at the 6-iron, and 5 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Are the Mizuno MP-32 and MX-25 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Mizuno MP-32 and MX-25 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 61.5° lie and 36.75 inches long.
