Options per loft

What the differences mean

The Opus SP offers the wider selection: 24 loft/bounce/grind combinations against 11 for the Opus SP+. The Opus SP also has the deeper grind catalogue: 5 sole grinds against 3.

The Opus SP is sold from 48° to 64°, the Opus SP+ from 54° to 60°. Only the Opus SP comes in 48°, 50°, 52°, 62°, and 64°.

The scoring lofts are where bounce and grind choice matters most: at 56° the Opus SP offers 4 options to 3; at 58° the Opus SP offers 5 options to 3; at 60° the Opus SP offers 5 options to 3. More options at a degree means more ways to match the sole to your turf and swing, which is what a wedge fitting actually selects.

The two lines launched 1 year apart (2025 and 2026), so they compete head-to-head rather than one replacing the other.

Which should you choose?

Neither is the better wedge outright — both are current tour-style designs. The Opus SP offers the wider option matrix, which matters if you want an unusual bounce or grind at your loft; otherwise choose on feel, finish and price at the combination you actually play.

Both option charts are reproduced in full on their own pages: Callaway Opus SP specs and Callaway Opus SP+ specs.
