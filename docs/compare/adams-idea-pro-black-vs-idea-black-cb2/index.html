Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Idea Pro Black is 35° and the Idea Black CB2 is 33°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Idea Black CB2 should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the Idea Pro Black. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the Idea Black CB2 averages 1.6° stronger, with the largest gap at the 6-iron (31° vs 29°). The pitching wedges are 47° and 45°; the stronger one leaves a 5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Idea Black CB2 7-iron sits 0.2° flatter (62.5° vs 62.25°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The Idea Pro Black is a blade iron and the Idea Black CB2 a players iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Idea Black CB2 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The Idea Pro Black trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Both heads are listed as forged. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The Idea Black CB2 came out 1 year after the Idea Pro Black, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The Idea Black CB2 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the Idea Pro Black on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Adams Idea Pro Black stronger lofted than the Idea Black CB2?

No. The Idea Black CB2 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 33° against 35° for the Adams Idea Pro Black, 2° stronger and worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Adams Idea Pro Black and Idea Black CB2?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (35° vs 33°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 6-iron, and 8 of the 9 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Adams Idea Pro Black or the Idea Black CB2?

The Idea Black CB2. As a players iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The Adams Idea Pro Black is a blade iron, better suited to low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Do the Adams Idea Pro Black and Idea Black CB2 have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Adams Idea Pro Black is 62.5° lie and 37 inches, the Idea Black CB2 62.25° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
