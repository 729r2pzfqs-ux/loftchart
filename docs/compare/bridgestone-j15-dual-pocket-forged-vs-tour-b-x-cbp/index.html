Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the J15 Dual Pocket Forged is 31° and the Tour B X-CBP is 31°, identical at the club most golfers compare on, although other clubs in the sets differ. Across the 7 clubs both sets share, the Tour B X-CBP averages 0.8° stronger, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (22.5° vs 21°). The pitching wedges are 45° and 44°; the stronger one leaves a 6° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the Tour B X-CBP 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62.5° vs 62°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The sets are built differently: the J15 Dual Pocket Forged runs 3-iron to PW (8 clubs), the Tour B X-CBP 4-iron to PW (7 clubs).

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The J15 Dual Pocket Forged is listed as two-piece forged with a Turbo Rubber Design perimeter cavity and the Tour B X-CBP as forged body with press-formed high-strength steel face, pocket cavity. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The Tour B X-CBP arrived 3 years after the J15 Dual Pocket Forged, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept.

Which should you choose?

The Tour B X-CBP is the stronger-lofted set — up to 1.5° stronger than the J15 Dual Pocket Forged on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Bridgestone J15 Dual Pocket Forged stronger lofted than the Tour B X-CBP?

Not at the 7-iron, where both are 31°. Other clubs in the sets differ, so compare the full table above.

What is the loft difference between the Bridgestone J15 Dual Pocket Forged and Tour B X-CBP?

At the 7-iron the difference is 0° (31° vs 31°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1.5° at the 4-iron, and 5 of the 7 shared clubs differ.

Are the Bridgestone J15 Dual Pocket Forged and Tour B X-CBP aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Bridgestone J15 Dual Pocket Forged and Tour B X-CBP have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Bridgestone J15 Dual Pocket Forged is 62.5° lie and 37 inches, the Tour B X-CBP 62° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
