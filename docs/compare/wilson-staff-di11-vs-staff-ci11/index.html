Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the Staff Di11 is 32° and the Staff Ci11 is 32°, identical at the club most golfers compare on, although other clubs in the sets differ. Across the 10 clubs both sets share, the Staff Ci11 averages 0.9° weaker, with the largest gap at the 3-iron (18° vs 20°). The pitching wedges are 43° and 44°; the stronger one leaves a 7° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Perimeter-weighted cavity backs with wider soles, more offset and stronger lofts, aimed at mid handicaps. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The Staff Di11 is listed as cast cavity back and the Staff Ci11 as investment cast. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The Staff Ci11 came out 1 year after the Staff Di11, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The Staff Ci11 is the weaker-lofted set — up to 2° weaker than the Staff Di11. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Wilson Staff Di11 stronger lofted than the Staff Ci11?

Not at the 7-iron, where both are 32°. Other clubs in the sets differ, so compare the full table above.

What is the loft difference between the Wilson Staff Di11 and Staff Ci11?

At the 7-iron the difference is 0° (32° vs 32°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 3-iron, and 7 of the 10 shared clubs differ.

Are the Wilson Staff Di11 and Staff Ci11 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are game improvement irons, built for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Wilson Staff Di11 and Staff Ci11 have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62° lie and 37.5 inches long.
