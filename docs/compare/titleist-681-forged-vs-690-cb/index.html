Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the 681 Forged is 36° and the 690.CB is 35°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the 690.CB should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the 681 Forged. Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the 690.CB averages 1.1° stronger, with the largest gap at the PW (49° vs 47°). The pitching wedges are 49° and 47°; the stronger one leaves a 3° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the 690.CB 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62° vs 61.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The sets are built differently: the 681 Forged runs 1-iron to PW (10 clubs), the 690.CB 2-iron to W (10 clubs).

The 681 Forged is a blade iron and the 690.CB a players iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The 690.CB is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The 681 Forged trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

The 681 Forged is listed as forged blade and the 690.CB as forged cavity back. The 681 Forged's muscle-back shape keeps mass directly behind the strike for feedback and control, where a cavity spreads it to the perimeter for forgiveness.

The 690.CB arrived 1 year after the 681 Forged, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 36° to 35°.

Which should you choose?

The 690.CB is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the 681 Forged on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Titleist 681 Forged stronger lofted than the 690.CB?

No. The 690.CB is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 35° against 36° for the Titleist 681 Forged, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Titleist 681 Forged and 690.CB?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (36° vs 35°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the PW, and all 9 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Titleist 681 Forged or the 690.CB?

The 690.CB. As a players iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The Titleist 681 Forged is a blade iron, better suited to low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Do the Titleist 681 Forged and 690.CB have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Titleist 681 Forged is 62° lie and 36.75 inches, the 690.CB 61.5° and 36.75 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
