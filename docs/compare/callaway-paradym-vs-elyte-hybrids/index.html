What the differences mean

The Elyte family is the broader line: 3 heads against 2 — Std and X for the Paradym, Std, X, and Max Fast for the Elyte.

The Paradym is sold from 18° to 30°, the Elyte from 18° to 39°. Only the Paradym has a stock 30° option. Only the Elyte has a stock 19°, 22°, 23°, 25°, 26°, 29°, 31°, 35°, and 39° option.

Adjustability is a wash: both use the same hosel system (OptiFit hosel, −1° to +2° of loft, 8 settings), so a given head reaches the same effective loft window in either generation.

The two lines launched 2 years apart (2023 and 2025), so they compete head-to-head rather than one replacing the other.

Which should you choose?

Neither family is better outright — match the head to your shot shape and forgiveness needs first, then pick the stock loft nearest your launch window and fine-tune with the hosel. Feel, sound and price at your spec are the real tiebreakers.

Both families are charted in full on their own pages: Callaway Paradym hybrid specs and Callaway Elyte hybrid specs.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Callaway Paradym and Elyte hybrids come in?

The Callaway Paradym is sold in stock lofts from 18 to 30 degrees and the Elyte from 18 to 39 degrees. They share 4 stock lofts.

Which has more head options, the Callaway Paradym or the Elyte?

The Elyte: 3 head variants against 2.

Are the Callaway Paradym and Elyte hybrids adjustable?

Yes — the Callaway Paradym uses OptiFit hosel, −1° to +2° of loft, 8 settings, and the Elyte uses OptiFit hosel, −1° to +2° of loft, 8 settings. Each model page carries the full setting-by-setting adjustment chart.
