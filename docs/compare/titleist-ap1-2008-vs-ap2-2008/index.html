Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the AP1 (2008) is 33° and the AP2 (2008) is 35°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the AP1 (2008) should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the AP2 (2008). Across the 9 clubs both sets share, the AP2 (2008) averages 1.6° weaker, with the largest gap at the 6-iron (29° vs 31°). The pitching wedges are 45° and 47°; the stronger one leaves a 5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The AP1 (2008) is a game improvement iron and the AP2 (2008) a players iron, so they sit 2 steps apart on the forgiveness scale. The AP1 (2008) is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The AP2 (2008) trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

The AP1 (2008) is listed as cast, multi-material dual cavity and the AP2 (2008) as forged, multi-material dual cavity. Forging, as in the AP2 (2008), is generally credited with a softer feel; casting, as in the AP1 (2008), allows more complex cavity and weighting shapes, which is why it dominates forgiving irons. Both use tungsten weighting to lower the centre of gravity and steady the head on off-centre strikes.

Both sets were introduced in 2008, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Titleist designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The AP2 (2008) is the weaker-lofted set — up to 2° weaker than the AP1 (2008). That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Titleist AP1 (2008) stronger lofted than the AP2 (2008)?

Yes. The Titleist AP1 (2008) 7-iron is 33° against 35° for the AP2 (2008), 2° stronger, which is worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the Titleist AP1 (2008) and AP2 (2008)?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (33° vs 35°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 6-iron, and all 9 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Titleist AP1 (2008) or the AP2 (2008)?

The Titleist AP1 (2008). As a game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The AP2 (2008) is a players iron, better suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps.

Do the Titleist AP1 (2008) and AP2 (2008) have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 63° lie and 37 inches long.
