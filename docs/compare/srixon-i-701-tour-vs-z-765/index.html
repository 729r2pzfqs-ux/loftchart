Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the I-701 Tour is 34° and the Z 765 is 32°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the Z 765 should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the I-701 Tour. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the Z 765 averages 1.5° stronger, with the largest gap at the 4-iron (24° vs 22°). Beyond loft, the I-701 Tour 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (61.5° vs 62°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the Z 765 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (36.75" vs 37").

The sets are built differently: the I-701 Tour runs 3-iron to PW (8 clubs), the Z 765 3-iron to AW (9 clubs).

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

The I-701 Tour is listed as forged cavity back and the Z 765 as forged. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

There are 9 years between these sets (2007 and 2016), more than one design generation. Over that time irons moved toward stronger lofts, thinner and faster faces, hollow and multi-material heads and wider use of tungsten, so the newer set is not simply a better version of the older one: it may launch and spin quite differently. Here the 7-iron loft moved from 34° to 32°.

Which should you choose?

The Z 765 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the I-701 Tour on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Srixon I-701 Tour stronger lofted than the Z 765?

No. The Z 765 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 32° against 34° for the Srixon I-701 Tour, 2° stronger and worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Srixon I-701 Tour and Z 765?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (34° vs 32°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the 4-iron, and 7 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Are the Srixon I-701 Tour and Z 765 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Srixon I-701 Tour and Z 765 have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Srixon I-701 Tour is 61.5° lie and 36.75 inches, the Z 765 62° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
