Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the G440 is 29° and the G740 is 28°, a 1° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the G740 should carry roughly 3 to 4 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the G440. Across the 8 clubs both sets share, the G740 averages 1.1° stronger, with the largest gap at the PW (42° vs 40°). The pitching wedges are 42° and 40°; the stronger one leaves a 10° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the G440 7-iron sits 1° flatter (62° vs 63°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The sets are built differently: the G440 runs 4-iron to 56W (10 clubs), the G740 5-iron to 56W (9 clubs).

The G440 is a game improvement iron and the G740 a super game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The G740 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The G440 trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

The G440 is listed as investment cast and the G740 as investment cast cavity back. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The G740 came out 1 year after the G440, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The G740 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the G440 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Ping G440 stronger lofted than the G740?

No. The G740 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 28° against 29° for the Ping G440, 1° stronger and worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Ping G440 and G740?

At the 7-iron the difference is 1° (29° vs 28°). The largest gap on a shared club is 2° at the PW, and 7 of the 8 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Ping G440 or the G740?

The G740. As a super game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Ping G440 is a game improvement iron, better suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

Do the Ping G440 and G740 have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Ping G440 is 62° lie and 37 inches, the G740 63° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
