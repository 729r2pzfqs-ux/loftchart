Lofts compared

Which should you choose?

The JPX-921 Hot Metal Pro is the stronger-lofted set — up to 1° stronger than the JPX-919 Hot Metal Pro on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.
