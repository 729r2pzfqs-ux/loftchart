What the differences mean

The Titleist Vokey SM9 offers the wider selection: 25 loft/bounce/grind combinations against 17 for the Ping Glide 4.0. The Titleist Vokey SM9 also has the deeper grind catalogue: 7 sole grinds against 4.

The Ping Glide 4.0 is sold from 46° to 60°, the Titleist Vokey SM9 from 46° to 62°. Only the Titleist Vokey SM9 comes in 48° and 62°.

The scoring lofts are where bounce and grind choice matters most: at 56° the Titleist Vokey SM9 offers 4 options to 3; at 58° the Titleist Vokey SM9 offers 5 options to 4; at 60° the Titleist Vokey SM9 offers 6 options to 4. More options at a degree means more ways to match the sole to your turf and swing, which is what a wedge fitting actually selects.

Both lines were introduced in 2022, so this is a choice between two current designs rather than an upgrade.

Which should you choose?

Neither is the better wedge outright — both are current tour-style designs. The Titleist Vokey SM9 offers the wider option matrix, which matters if you want an unusual bounce or grind at your loft; otherwise choose on feel, finish and price at the combination you actually play.

Both option charts are reproduced in full on their own pages: Ping Glide 4.0 specs and Titleist Vokey SM9 specs.
