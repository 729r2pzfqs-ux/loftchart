Lofts compared

What the differences mean

Every club the two sets share has exactly the same loft, starting with a 34° 7-iron. On paper there is no distance or trajectory difference: with the same swing, a 7-iron from either set should launch and carry about the same. The real differences are in how the heads are built, how they look at address and how they feel at impact, none of which a spec table can show.

The sets are built differently: the Idea Pro Gold runs 5-iron to PW (6 clubs), the Idea Pro Black CB1 4-iron to GW (8 clubs).

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as forged. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

The Idea Pro Black CB1 arrived 2 years after the Idea Pro Gold, a typical one-generation refresh. Changes over that span are usually incremental: refined face thickness, revised weighting, updated looks and small loft tweaks rather than a new concept.

Which should you choose?

The Idea Pro Gold and Idea Pro Black CB1 share the same lofts on every club on file, so the difference is in head construction, feel and forgiveness rather than trajectory.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Adams Idea Pro Gold stronger lofted than the Idea Pro Black CB1?

No. Both have a 34° 7-iron and the same loft on every club they share, so neither is stronger lofted. The difference is in construction and feel.

What is the loft difference between the Adams Idea Pro Gold and Idea Pro Black CB1?

There is none. Every club both sets include has the same loft, including the 34° 7-iron.

Are the Adams Idea Pro Gold and Idea Pro Black CB1 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Adams Idea Pro Gold and Idea Pro Black CB1 have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Adams Idea Pro Gold is 62.5° lie and 37 inches, the Idea Pro Black CB1 62.25° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
