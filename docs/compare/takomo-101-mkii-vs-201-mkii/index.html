Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the 101 MKII is 29° and the 201 MKII is 31°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the 101 MKII should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the 201 MKII. Across the 6 clubs both sets share, the 201 MKII averages 2.5° weaker, with the largest gap at the 8-iron (33° vs 36°). The pitching wedges are 43° and 46°; the stronger one leaves a 7° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the 201 MKII 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62.5° vs 62°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The sets are built differently: the 101 MKII runs 5-iron to GW (7 clubs), the 201 MKII 4-iron to PW (7 clubs).

The 101 MKII is a game improvement iron and the 201 MKII a players distance iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The 101 MKII is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The 201 MKII trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head.

The 101 MKII is listed as cast hollow body with variable-thickness face and the 201 MKII as cast hollow body with machined variable-thickness face. Both use hollow-body heads, so both trade a little feel for face flex and launch help.

Both sets were introduced in 2026, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Takomo designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The 201 MKII is the weaker-lofted set — up to 3° weaker than the 101 MKII. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Takomo 101 MKII stronger lofted than the 201 MKII?

Yes. The Takomo 101 MKII 7-iron is 29° against 31° for the 201 MKII, 2° stronger, which is worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry with the same swing.

What is the loft difference between the Takomo 101 MKII and 201 MKII?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (29° vs 31°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3° at the 8-iron, and all 6 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Takomo 101 MKII or the 201 MKII?

The Takomo 101 MKII. As a game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps. The 201 MKII is a players distance iron, better suited to roughly 5 to 15 handicaps who want extra distance from a compact head.

Do the Takomo 101 MKII and 201 MKII have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Takomo 101 MKII is 62.5° lie and 37 inches, the 201 MKII 62° and 37 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
