Lofts compared

Which should you choose?

The Staff Model XB (2026) is the stronger-lofted set — up to 2° stronger than the Staff Model CB (2026) on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.
