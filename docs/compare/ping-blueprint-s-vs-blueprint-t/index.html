Lofts compared

What the differences mean

Every club the two sets share has exactly the same loft, starting with a 33° 7-iron. On paper there is no distance or trajectory difference: with the same swing, a 7-iron from either set should launch and carry about the same. The real differences are in how the heads are built, how they look at address and how they feel at impact, none of which a spec table can show.

The Blueprint S is a players iron and the Blueprint T a blade iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The Blueprint S is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The Blueprint T trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Both heads are listed as forged. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

Both sets were introduced in 2024, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Ping designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The Blueprint S and Blueprint T share the same lofts on every club on file, so the difference is in head construction, feel and forgiveness rather than trajectory.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Ping Blueprint S stronger lofted than the Blueprint T?

No. Both have a 33° 7-iron and the same loft on every club they share, so neither is stronger lofted. The difference is in construction and feel.

What is the loft difference between the Ping Blueprint S and Blueprint T?

There is none. Every club both sets include has the same loft, including the 33° 7-iron.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Ping Blueprint S or the Blueprint T?

The Ping Blueprint S. As a players iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The Blueprint T is a blade iron, better suited to low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Do the Ping Blueprint S and Blueprint T have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Ping Blueprint S is 62° lie and 37 inches, the Blueprint T 62° and 36.75 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
