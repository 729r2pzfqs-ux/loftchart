Lofts compared

What the differences mean

Every club the two sets share has exactly the same loft, starting with a 34° 7-iron. On paper there is no distance or trajectory difference: with the same swing, a 7-iron from either set should launch and carry about the same. The real differences are in how the heads are built, how they look at address and how they feel at impact, none of which a spec table can show. Lie angles and lengths match as well, so any fitting adjustment made to one set would carry straight over to the other.

The 301 CB is a players iron and the 301 MB a blade iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The 301 CB is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The 301 MB trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Both heads are listed as one-piece forged. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

Both sets were introduced in 2023, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Takomo designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The 301 CB and 301 MB share the same lofts on every club on file, so the difference is in head construction, feel and forgiveness rather than trajectory.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Takomo 301 CB stronger lofted than the 301 MB?

No. Both have a 34° 7-iron and the same loft on every club they share, so neither is stronger lofted. The difference is in construction and feel.

What is the loft difference between the Takomo 301 CB and 301 MB?

There is none. Every club both sets include has the same loft, including the 34° 7-iron.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Takomo 301 CB or the 301 MB?

The Takomo 301 CB. As a players iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The 301 MB is a blade iron, better suited to low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Do the Takomo 301 CB and 301 MB have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 62.5° lie and 37 inches long.
