Lofts compared

Which should you choose?

The Eye 2 Plus is the stronger-lofted set — up to 4.5° stronger than the Eye 2 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.
