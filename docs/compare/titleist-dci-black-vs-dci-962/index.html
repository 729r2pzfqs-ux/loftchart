What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the DCI Black is 36° and the DCI 962 is 36°, identical at the club most golfers compare on, although other clubs in the sets differ. Across the 13 clubs both sets share, the DCI 962 averages 0.3° weaker, with the largest gap at the 2-iron (18° vs 19.5°). Beyond loft, the DCI Black 7-iron sits 1.5° flatter (61.5° vs 63°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander; the DCI 962 7-iron is 0.25 inches longer (36.5" vs 36.75").

Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Small-to-mid cavity backs with traditional lofts, modest offset and workable shaping for low-handicap players. Because they target the same golfer, the decision comes down to the specific loft, feel and shaping differences rather than who the sets are made for.

Both heads are listed as investment cast, perimeter weighted cavity back. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

There are 4 years between these sets (1993 and 1997), more than one design generation. Over that time irons moved toward stronger lofts, thinner and faster faces, hollow and multi-material heads and wider use of tungsten, so the newer set is not simply a better version of the older one: it may launch and spin quite differently.

Which should you choose?

The DCI 962 is the weaker-lofted set — up to 1.5° weaker than the DCI Black. That trades raw distance for a steeper landing angle and more predictable stopping power into greens.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Titleist DCI Black stronger lofted than the DCI 962?

Not at the 7-iron, where both are 36°. Other clubs in the sets differ, so compare the full table above.

What is the loft difference between the Titleist DCI Black and DCI 962?

At the 7-iron the difference is 0° (36° vs 36°). The largest gap on a shared club is 1.5° at the 2-iron, and 4 of the 13 shared clubs differ.

Are the Titleist DCI Black and DCI 962 aimed at the same golfer?

Yes. Both are players irons, built for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. Choose on feel, looks and gapping, ideally after hitting both.

Do the Titleist DCI Black and DCI 962 have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Titleist DCI Black is 61.5° lie and 36.5 inches, the DCI 962 63° and 36.75 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
