Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the TA1 Form Forged is 36° and the TA7 is 33.5°, a 2.5° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the TA7 should carry roughly 8 to 10 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the TA1 Form Forged. Across the 10 clubs both sets share, the TA7 averages 1.9° stronger, with the largest gap at the 8-iron (40° vs 37°). The pitching wedges are 48° and 45°; the stronger one leaves a 5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping. Beyond loft, the TA1 Form Forged 7-iron sits 0.5° flatter (62° vs 62.5°), which nudges start direction slightly right for a right-hander.

The sets are built differently: the TA1 Form Forged runs 1-iron to PW (10 clubs), the TA7 1-iron to LW (13 clubs).

The TA1 Form Forged is a blade iron and the TA7 a players iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The TA7 is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The TA1 Form Forged trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

The TA1 Form Forged is listed as forged muscle back and the TA7 as cavity back with micro-cavity topline. The TA1 Form Forged's muscle-back shape keeps mass directly behind the strike for feedback and control, where a cavity spreads it to the perimeter for forgiveness.

The TA7 came out 2 years after the TA1 Form Forged, but it is a different line rather than a direct replacement, so the two were designed for different golfers as well as different seasons.

Which should you choose?

The TA7 is the stronger-lofted set — up to 3° stronger than the TA1 Form Forged on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged stronger lofted than the TA7?

No. The TA7 is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 33.5° against 36° for the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged, 2.5° stronger and worth roughly 8 to 10 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged and TA7?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2.5° (36° vs 33.5°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3° at the 8-iron, and all 10 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged or the TA7?

The TA7. As a players iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to roughly scratch to 10 handicaps. The Cleveland TA1 Form Forged is a blade iron, better suited to low single-digit handicaps who strike the ball consistently.

Do the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged and TA7 have the same lie and length?

Not exactly. At the 7-iron the Cleveland TA1 Form Forged is 62° lie and 36.75 inches, the TA7 62.5° and 36.75 inches. Both can be bent or built to your own fit.
