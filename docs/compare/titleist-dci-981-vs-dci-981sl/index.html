Lofts compared

What the differences mean

At the 7-iron, the DCI 981 is 35° and the DCI 981SL is 33°, a 2° gap. Each degree of loft is worth roughly 3 to 4 yards of carry at the same swing speed, so the DCI 981SL should carry roughly 6 to 8 yards further with that club, on a slightly lower, shallower flight than the DCI 981. Across the 12 clubs both sets share, the DCI 981SL averages 1.2° stronger, with the largest gap at the SW (55° vs 52°). The pitching wedges are 47° and 45°; the stronger one leaves a 5° jump to a 50° gap wedge, which matters when planning wedge gapping.

The sets are built differently: the DCI 981 runs 1-iron to LW (13 clubs), the DCI 981SL 2-iron to LW (12 clubs).

The DCI 981 is a game improvement iron and the DCI 981SL a super game improvement iron, so they sit one step apart on the forgiveness scale. The DCI 981SL is the more forgiving design, with a wider sole, more offset and more perimeter weighting for high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The DCI 981 trades some of that for a more compact head, thinner topline and more control over flight and shape, which suits roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

The DCI 981 is listed as investment cast, perimeter weighted and the DCI 981SL as investment cast, progressive cavity back. With the build method this similar, differences in feel come down to head size, sole width, topline and offset rather than how the heads are made.

Both sets were introduced in 1998, so this is a choice between two models from the same lineup rather than an upgrade: Titleist designed them to sit side by side for different players.

Which should you choose?

The DCI 981SL is the stronger-lofted set — up to 3° stronger than the DCI 981 on matching clubs. Expect longer carry per numbered iron but a flatter descent angle; gapping at the wedge end is where that shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Titleist DCI 981 stronger lofted than the DCI 981SL?

No. The DCI 981SL is the stronger-lofted set: its 7-iron is 33° against 35° for the Titleist DCI 981, 2° stronger and worth roughly 6 to 8 yards of extra carry.

What is the loft difference between the Titleist DCI 981 and DCI 981SL?

At the 7-iron the difference is 2° (35° vs 33°). The largest gap on a shared club is 3° at the SW, and 11 of the 12 shared clubs differ.

Which is better for higher handicap golfers, the Titleist DCI 981 or the DCI 981SL?

The DCI 981SL. As a super game improvement iron it is the more forgiving design, suited to high handicaps, beginners and slower swing speeds. The Titleist DCI 981 is a game improvement iron, better suited to roughly 10 to 25 handicaps.

Do the Titleist DCI 981 and DCI 981SL have the same lie and length?

At the 7-iron, yes: both are 63° lie and 36.75 inches long.
