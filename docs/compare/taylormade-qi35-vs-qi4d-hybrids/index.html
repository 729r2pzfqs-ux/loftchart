What the differences mean

Both families are sold in 3 heads — Rescue, Max Rescue, and Max Lite Rescue for the Qi35, Rescue, Max Rescue, and Max Lite Rescue for the Qi4D.

The Qi35 is sold from 17° to 35°, the Qi4D from 17° to 34°. Only the Qi35 has a stock 27°, 31°, and 35° option. Only the Qi4D has a stock 26°, 30°, and 34° option.

The hosels differ: the Qi35 uses 3° Loft Sleeve (Qi35 Rescue only), ±1.5° of loft, 12 settings; the Qi4D uses 3° Loft Sleeve (Qi4D Rescue only), ±1.5° of loft, 12 settings. The full setting-by-setting charts are on each model's own page.

The Qi4D arrived 1 year after the Qi35 as its replacement. Hybrid generations usually carry over the loft ladder and move the technology — face design, adjustability, head options — so the older family is the value buy on the used market while the newer one holds retail price.

Which should you choose?

The Qi4D is the newer generation, so it carries the current face technology and full retail availability; the Qi35 is the value buy used or on closeout. If the head and loft you want is only in one lineup, that decides it — otherwise the newer family is the default at equal prices, and the older one the smarter buy at a real discount.

Both families are charted in full on their own pages: TaylorMade Qi35 hybrid specs and TaylorMade Qi4D hybrid specs.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the TaylorMade Qi35 and Qi4D hybrids come in?

The TaylorMade Qi35 is sold in stock lofts from 17 to 35 degrees and the Qi4D from 17 to 34 degrees. They share 6 stock lofts.

Which has more head options, the TaylorMade Qi35 or the Qi4D?

Both families are sold in 3 heads.

Are the TaylorMade Qi35 and Qi4D hybrids adjustable?

Yes — the TaylorMade Qi35 uses 3° Loft Sleeve (Qi35 Rescue only), ±1.5° of loft, 12 settings, and the Qi4D uses 3° Loft Sleeve (Qi4D Rescue only), ±1.5° of loft, 12 settings. Each model page carries the full setting-by-setting adjustment chart.
