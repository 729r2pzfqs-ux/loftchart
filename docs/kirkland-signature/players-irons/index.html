About the Kirkland Signature Player's

The Kirkland Signature Player's irons are Costco's house-brand iron set, sold only as a fixed seven-club box - 4-iron through pitching wedge - at $499 when it debuted in December 2023 (Costco's online price later rose to $549.99). The head is a hollow-body players distance design with a stainless steel body, an injected urethane insert behind the face and an internal tungsten weight, in a mirror-chrome finish whose shaping drew immediate comparisons to TaylorMade's P790. Configuration options are minimal: right handed only, a True Temper Elevate 115 steel shaft in regular or stiff flex, and a Lamkin Crossline 360 grip, with a two-sets-per-member purchase limit. Lofts are moderately strong, with a 31-degree 7-iron and 45-degree pitching wedge. Costco publishes only the shaft, grip and set composition, not a per-club table, so the loft, lie and length figures below come from Driving Range Heroes' review of a retail set, with the 31-degree 7-iron independently confirmed by Golf Monthly and Today's Golfer. The initial run sold out within hours and the set has restocked intermittently since.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loft of a Kirkland Signature Player's 7-iron?

The Kirkland Signature Player's 7-iron has a loft of 31 degrees, with a 62.5-degree lie and a 37-inch steel shaft - confirmed independently by Driving Range Heroes, Golf Monthly and Today's Golfer.

What clubs come in the Kirkland Signature Player's iron set?

It is a fixed seven-club set: 4-, 5-, 6-, 7-, 8- and 9-irons plus a 45-degree pitching wedge. There are no individual clubs, no left-handed option and only two configurations - regular or stiff flex in the True Temper Elevate 115 steel shaft.

How much do the Kirkland Signature Player's irons cost?

The set launched at $499 including shipping in December 2023 and sold out within hours; Costco's online price was later listed at $549.99. Costco applies a two-sets-per-member limit.

Are the Kirkland Signature Player's irons like the TaylorMade P790?

They resemble it: both are hollow-body players distance irons with a polymer/urethane fill and tungsten weighting. In Today's Golfer robot-assisted testing the Kirkland gave up about 2.9 mph of ball speed and 7 yards of 7-iron carry to the 2023 P790, at roughly a third of the price.

Production status

Introduced in 2023, the Kirkland Signature Player's has no confirmed end date on file. LoftChart records a discontinuation year only where a source dates it, and none has been found for this set, so it may or may not still be in the Kirkland Signature catalogue — check with Kirkland Signature or a retailer before counting on a new set. Used sets appear on eBay, 2nd Swing and Golf Avenue, and should be measured against the chart above before buying, since irons are routinely bent from standard.
