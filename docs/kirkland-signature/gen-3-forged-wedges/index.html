About the Kirkland Signature Gen 3 Forged

Costco's third-generation Kirkland Signature wedges, the first forged set in the line, reached the USGA conforming list in April 2026 and went on sale in June 2026 at 199.99 dollars for the three-wedge set. The set is sold only as a trio — 52, 56 and 60 degrees — with a sandblasted raw face replacing the milled face of the first two generations, progressive centre of gravity placement, a Kirkland Signature wedge-flex steel shaft and a Lamkin Crossline 360 grip, right-handed only at retail. Costco publishes only the lofts and construction; the bounce figures charted here are from independent review measurements, and no lie angle or length has been published, so treat those columns as unverified.

Frequently asked questions

What lofts do the Kirkland Signature Gen 3 wedges come in?

The set is sold only as a trio: a 52-degree gap wedge, a 56-degree sand wedge and a 60-degree lob wedge, for 199.99 dollars. There are no individual lofts, grinds or bounce options.

Are the Kirkland Gen 3 wedges forged?

Yes — the third generation moved to forged mild carbon steel with a sandblasted raw face, where the first two generations were cast with milled faces.

What bounce do the Kirkland Gen 3 wedges have?

Costco does not publish bounce figures. Independent review measurements put them at roughly 10 degrees on the 52 and 56 and 8 degrees on the 60, in line with the Gen 2 set.

Where it sits in the lineup

Introduced in 2026, the Kirkland Signature Gen 3 Forged is a current tour wedge in the Kirkland Signature range as of October 2026, with no replacement announced. It replaced the Kirkland Signature Gen 2. New wedges are built to the loft, bounce and grind options in the chart above; a fitting can bend loft and lie from standard, so confirm the numbers on the order sheet. Check the loft and bounce against the chart above before buying used — wedges are routinely bent from standard, and worn grooves cost spin long before a wedge looks worn.
